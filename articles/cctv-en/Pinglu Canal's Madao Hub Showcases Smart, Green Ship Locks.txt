A new gateway from China's inland waterways to the sea!

The Madao hub on the Pinglu Canal in Guangxi features giant water-saving locks and smart monitoring systems.

The 134.2-km canal will connect China's inland waterways with the Beibu Gulf, creating a shorter, more efficient route to the sea.
