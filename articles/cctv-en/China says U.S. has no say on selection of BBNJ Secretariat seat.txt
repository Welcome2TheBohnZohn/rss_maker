China says U.S. has no say on selection of BBNJ Secretariat seat

ARTICLE TEXT:
BEIJING, Nov. 25 (Xinhua) -- A Chinese spokesperson said on Wednesday that the United States, which is neither a state party to the United Nations Convention on the Law of the Sea nor to the Biodiversity Beyond National Jurisdiction (BBNJ) Agreement, has no say on the selection of the BBNJ Secretariat seat.

Guo Jiakun, the spokesperson for the Chinese foreign ministry, made the remarks when asked to comment on a statement issued by the U.S. State Department welcoming the joint bid by Chile and Belgium to host the Secretariat of the BBNJ Agreement.

"We call on all parties to stay vigilant against attempts to politicize the bidding and create confrontation in the process," said Guo.

He stressed that China has made clear its position on the Belgium-Chile proposal, adding that this joint candidature is problematic in at least six aspects, namely, it contravenes the stipulations of the BBNJ Agreement, procedural justice, established international practice, equitable geographical distribution, fair competition and the original commitment.

"According to the BBNJ Agreement, the seat of the Secretariat, the specific modalities for the operation of the Clearing-House Mechanism (CHM), including its installation, as well as where the subsidiary bodies hold their meetings shall all be determined by the Conference of the Parties, and the CHM shall be managed by the Secretariat," said Guo.

These are the consensus as well as rights and responsibilities of all Parties to the Agreement, which no country has the right or is in any position to violate, he added.

"No one shall trade under the table what should be collectively determined by all Parties, let alone coerce or hijack other Parties into accepting their proposal," Guo noted.

As one of the first signatories and Parties to the Agreement and the largest assessed-contributor to its budget, China stands ready to work with the international community to uphold the original purpose of the Agreement and keep the selection of the Secretariat seat proceeding in the right direction, so that the BBNJ Agreement can truly serve the shared interests of humanity, said Guo.
