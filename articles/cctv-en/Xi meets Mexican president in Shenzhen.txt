SHENZHEN, Nov. 17 (Xinhua) -- Chinese President Xi Jinping on Tuesday met with Mexican President Claudia Sheinbaum Pardo in Shenzhen.

The city in south China's Guangdong Province will host the 33rd Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation (APEC) Economic Leaders' Meeting, scheduled for Nov. 18-19.
