TITLE:
China firmly opposes US's additional tariffs on China, other economies

PUBLISHED:
2026-11-26

SOURCE:
CCTV English

CATEGORY:
Authoritative PRC Media

URL:
https://english.cctv.com/2026/11/26/ARTI97dHNd9zaOKudcJZn6Pq261126.shtml

ARTICLE TEXT:
China's Ministry of Commerce said on Thursday that China firmly opposes the US Section 301 investigation under the pretext of "overcapacity," saying that the investigation is a typical unilateralist act that severely disrupts the international economic order and violates World Trade Organization rules.

Ministry spokesperson He Yadong made the remarks in reference to the US Trade Representative's recently announced findings of a Section 301 investigation into "overcapacity" and its proposed measures to impose additional tariffs on 14 economies, including China.

China urges the US to correct its erroneous practices and return to the right track of resolving issues through dialogue and consultation, He said, adding that China will closely monitor developments and reserves the right to take necessary measures to safeguard its legitimate rights and interests.
