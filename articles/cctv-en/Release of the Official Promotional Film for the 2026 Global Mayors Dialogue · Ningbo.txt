The 2026 Global Mayors Dialogue · Ningbo will be held from November 2 to 5. The official city image film for the event, Where All Rivers Merge into the Sea, has been released. From the first wooden paddle that heralded the beginning of China's maritime civilization to the world's largest port, Ningbo, under the theme of "Openness and Connectivity · Shared Opportunities," engages in dialogue with the world.
