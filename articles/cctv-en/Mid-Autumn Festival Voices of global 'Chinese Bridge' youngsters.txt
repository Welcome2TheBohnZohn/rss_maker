Ahead of the 2026 Mid-Autumn Festival, the candidates of the 19th "Chinese Bridge"—Chinese Proficiency Competition for Foreign Secondary School Students traveled to Beijing and Tianjin. Through contests and cultural study tours, they explored China and experienced the enduring charm of Mid-Autumn. Known as the global "Olympics" of Chinese learning, "Chinese Bridge" brings us voices from Mandarin enthusiasts across five continents, sharing what the festival means to them.
