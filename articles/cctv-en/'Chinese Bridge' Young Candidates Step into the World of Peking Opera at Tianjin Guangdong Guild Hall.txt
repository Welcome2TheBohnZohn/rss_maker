On September 19, 2026, the candidates of the 19th "Chinese Bridge"—Chinese Proficiency Competition for Foreign Secondary School Students visited the Tianjin Guangdong Guild Hall. Immersed in the rich heritage of this century-old opera house, they embarked on a remarkable journey to explore Peking Opera, experiencing the depth and charm of traditional Chinese culture up close.
