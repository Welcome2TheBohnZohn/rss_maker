BEIJING, Nov. 16 (Xinhua) -- Chinese President Xi Jinping on Monday congratulated Zeljka Cvijanovic, Denis Becirovic and Darijana Filipovic on their assuming office as members of the Presidency of Bosnia and Herzegovina (BiH).

Noting that BiH is an important partner of China in Southeast Europe, Xi said that in recent years, bilateral relations have maintained steady and healthy development, political mutual trust has grown increasingly strong, and fruitful cooperation has been achieved in various fields.

Xi said he attaches great importance to the development of China-BiH relations and stands ready to work with the members of the Presidency to deepen the traditional friendship and mutually beneficial cooperation between the two countries, steer bilateral ties to a higher level, and bring greater benefits to the peoples of both countries.
