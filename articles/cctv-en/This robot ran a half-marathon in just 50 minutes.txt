At a half-marathon event in Beijing, China, humanoid robots raced alongside thousands of human runners in separate lanes. One autonomous robot, Lightning, finished in just 50 minutes and 26 seconds—faster than the men’s half-marathon world-record time at the time. A remarkable test of speed, balance and endurance.
