SHANGHAI, Nov. 14 (Xinhua) -- China's independently developed 10-tonne electric commercial aircraft CE-25A completed its maiden piloted flight on Saturday, according to the Commercial Aircraft Corporation of China (COMAC).

The aircraft carried two pilots and one flight-test engineer during the flight in Binzhou, east China's Shandong Province. The aircraft operated normally, and all performance indicators met expectations, COMAC said.

The aircraft completed its first unmanned autonomous flight on Feb. 2 this year.

Developed jointly by COMAC, Chinese battery maker CATL, Shanghai Jiao Tong University and other partners, the CE-25A has completed 19 unmanned autonomous flight validations.

The Civil Aviation Administration of China has accepted COMAC's application for a type certificate.
