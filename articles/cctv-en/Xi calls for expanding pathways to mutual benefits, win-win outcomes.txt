SHENZHEN, Nov. 18 (Xinhua) -- Chinese President Xi Jinping on Wednesday called on the Asia-Pacific to expand pathways to mutual benefits and win-win outcomes to prosper together.

Xi made the remarks while delivering a keynote speech at the APEC CEO Summit 2026 in Shenzhen, south China's Guangdong Province.

Efforts should be made to eliminate trade and investment barriers to foster an open Asia-Pacific economy, while promoting inclusive and balanced development to ensure both efficiency and fairness, Xi said.
