Premier Li: China ready to strengthen cooperation with ADB on regional development

ARTICLE TEXT:
Chinese Premier Li Qiang meets with Asian Development Bank (ADB) President Masato Kanda at the Great Hall of the People in Beijing, the capital of China, November 26, 2026. /Xinhua

China stands ready to work with the Asian Development Bank (ADB) to strengthen cooperation in areas such as regional integration, connectivity and emerging industries, and jointly promote regional development and prosperity, Chinese Premier Li Qiang said on Thursday.

Li made the remarks during his meeting with ADB President Masato Kanda in Beijing.

Noting that growing instability and uncertainty in the international situation in recent years have taken a toll on the Asian economy, Li said all parties need to further strengthen regional cooperation.

Chinese President Xi Jinping has called for building an Asia-Pacific community characterized by solidarity and mutual assistance, openness and integration, enterprise and innovation, as well as inclusive development, at last week's APEC Economic Leaders' Meeting, Li said, adding that China stands ready to work closely with all parties in the region to turn this inspiring vision into reality.

The ADB has long been an important promoter of regional economic integration, and China will continue to support the bank's relevant work, further elevate bilateral cooperation and jointly promote regional development and prosperity, Li said.

Against complex changes in the external environment this year, China's economy has stood firm under pressure and maintained steady progress, injecting much-needed stability and certainty into the Asian and global economies, Li noted.

China will continue to promote high-quality development, accelerate the unleashing of demand in its supersized market, deepen the integration of technological and industrial innovation, and maintain the strong momentum of high-quality, innovation-driven growth, thereby contributing to regional development, he said.

China stands ready to work with the ADB to implement the new country partnership strategy, strengthen cooperation in areas such as regional integration, connectivity and emerging industries, improve the quality and efficiency of bilateral cooperation, and better achieve mutually beneficial and win-win outcomes, Li said.

Kanda congratulated China on its economic resilience and sustained growth amid global economic uncertainties, as well as its remarkable progress in fostering new growth drivers.

Noting that this year marks the 40th anniversary of cooperation between the ADB and China, Kanda said the two sides have made substantial progress in cooperation over the past four decades across a wide range of areas, including infrastructure development, poverty reduction, environmental protection and climate action, and have established a platform for South-South cooperation.

The ADB stands ready to work with China to effectively implement the new country partnership strategy, support Chinese modernization and the implementation of China's 15th Five-Year Plan (2026-2030), and elevate the partnership between the ADB and China to a higher level, Kanda said.
