SHENZHEN, Nov. 19 (Xinhua) -- Chinese President Xi Jinping on Thursday said that China-U.S. relations have generally maintained a stable and positive trajectory, injecting stability and certainty into the world.

Xi made the remarks when meeting with U.S. President Donald Trump in Shenzhen.

Xi said that he and Trump had jointly explored the right way for China and the United States, as two major countries, to get along, established a new positioning of a constructive China-U.S. relationship of strategic stability and given it new meaning based on respect, fairness and reciprocity.

The new positioning has been welcomed by the people of both China and the United States, as well as by the international community, he added.

Xi stressed that building a constructive China-U.S. relationship of strategic stability is an unprecedented and pioneering endeavor, adding that the key to sustaining the current positive momentum lies in taking concrete action to implement the consensus reached by the two heads of state.

On the Taiwan question, the U.S. side should do the right thing and avoid major disruptions to China-U.S. relations, Xi said.

Trump said he and Xi have jointly guided the development of U.S.-China relations, and both sides should maintain the positive momentum of the stable development in bilateral relations.

The United States does not support "Taiwan independence," Trump said.
