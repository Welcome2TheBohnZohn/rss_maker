China and India are neighbors that cannot move away

Zhou Mi, Senior research fellow, Chinese Academy of International Trade and Economic Cooperation, said, "China and India are both important historical countries. The two have close interactions in the thousands of years, which have created so many fortunes for the world. So China and India as neighbors are not able to move away from each other."
