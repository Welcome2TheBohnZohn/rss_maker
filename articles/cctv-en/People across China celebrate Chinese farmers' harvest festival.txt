BEIJING, Sept. 23 (Xinhua) -- People across China on Wednesday held various activities to celebrate the ninth Chinese farmers' harvest festival.

The festival is the first national festival created specifically for the country's farmers. This year's festival falls on Wednesday.

Launched in 2018, the festival coincides with the Autumnal Equinox each year, which is one of the 24 solar terms of the Chinese lunisolar calendar and usually falls between Sept. 22 and 24 during the country's agricultural harvest season.
