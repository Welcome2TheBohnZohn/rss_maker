How do you put ancient treasures back together after thousands of years? At China's Sanxingdui archaeological site, conservators combine meticulous hands-on restoration with 3D scanning and digital modeling to study broken bronze artifacts and help reveal the extraordinary craftsmanship of ancient Shu civilization.
