TITLE:
How can Ningxia wine make a lasting impression on the world?

ARTICLE TEXT:
"We don't need to be like everybody else." At the 47th World Congress of Vine and Wine in Yinchuan, Ningxia, the OIV President highlighted the unique stories that China and Ningxia have to tell.

From the achievements of a young wine region to the terroir between the Helan Mountains and the Yellow River—and a memorable glass of Marselan—international guests shared what stood out to them about Ningxia. Their reflections point to a way forward: build on the region's strengths and let the world discover its distinctive wines.
