ARTICLE TEXT:
Three decades, three meetings. In 2001, by the Huangpu River, Shanghai hosted APEC's first handshake with the new century. In 2014, by the shores of Yanqi Lake, Beijing lit up the dream of shared growth. In 2026, on the shores of the South China Sea, Shenzhen opens its arms once again — in the name of innovation. From Shanghai to Beijing to Shenzhen, the cities have changed, but the conviction has not: openness, cooperation and win-win progress remain this land's enduring answer to the Asia-Pacific. Three cities, one goal. This time — see you in Shenzhen.
