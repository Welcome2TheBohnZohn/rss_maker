SHENZHEN, Nov. 19 (Xinhua) -- Chinese President Xi Jinping on Thursday made four points on how major countries should get along with each other and jointly safeguard world peace.

Major countries should uphold mutual respect, peaceful coexistence, win-win cooperation, and dialogue and consultation, Xi said at a summit with Russian President Vladimir Putin and U.S. President Donald Trump in Shenzhen, south China's Guangdong Province.
