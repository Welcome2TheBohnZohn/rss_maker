TITLE:
New growth drivers lift China's industrial profits 16% in first 10 months of 2026

ARTICLE TEXT:
Profits of China's industrial firms above designated size rose 16.0% year-on-year to 6.74 trillion yuan ($1 trillion) in the first ten months of 2026, maintaining double-digit growth since the start of the year, data from the National Bureau of Statistics (NBS) showed on Friday.

The growth rate accelerated by 0.5 percentage points from the January-September period, as emerging growth drivers continued to gain momentum. In October alone, industrial profits climbed 20.1% year-on-year, up 5.7 percentage points from September.

By sector, profits in mining rose 38.8% and manufacturing increased 17.5% in the first 10 months, while those in the production and supply of electricity, heat, gas and water fell 14.8%.

The electronics sector was a major driver of profit growth, with earnings doubling year-on-year during the January-October period. Rising demand for AI computing power continued to boost profits in memory chips and related manufacturing industries, contributing 9.7 percentage points to the growth in total industrial profits, said Yu Weining, chief statistician of the NBS.

High-tech manufacturing also continued to outperform the broader industrial sector, with profits rising 50.9% year-on-year, 34.9 percentage points faster than the overall industrial growth rate, Yu said.

The expansion of large-scale intelligent computing centers drove profits in optical fiber and optical cable manufacturing up 6.7 times and 1.5 times, respectively. Meanwhile, continued development of the aerospace industry lifted profits in spacecraft and launch vehicle manufacturing by 63.2%, while those in aerospace-related equipment manufacturing rose 41.7%.
