HANGZHOU, Nov. 20 (Xinhua) -- The International Network for Silk, the first global scientific organization in the textile sector to be headquartered in China, was launched on Friday to strengthen cooperation across the global silk industry, organizers said.

It has more than 100 member entities from over 20 countries and regions, covering major silk-producing and silk-consuming countries worldwide.

The body will operate as a global platform for dialogue, resource sharing and integrated development. It aims to deepen global cooperation in the silk industry, and support sericulture and silk sector development in developing and less-developed regions.

Silk production remains culturally and economically significant for China, which is among the world's largest producers and exporters of raw silk and finished silk goods.

The launch comes after China issued its first national guideline in September to boost the high-quality development of time-honored industries including silk, targeting stronger internationalization and higher positions in global value chains.

The country's first national exposition of time-honored industries kicked off in the east China city of Hangzhou on Friday, showcasing heritage sectors such as silk, tea, porcelain, brewing, traditional Chinese medicine, and arts and crafts.
