WASHINGTON, Nov. 20 (Xinhua) -- U.S. President Donald Trump on Friday hailed his "unprecedented" trilateral summit with Chinese President Xi Jinping and Russian President Vladimir Putin as one of the most significant international meetings in years.

"We had one of the most momentous international summits in many years. That was the first ever trilateral meeting between the leaders of the United States, Russia and China," Trump told a press conference at the White House. "And the relationship was fantastic between the three of us."

"The result of this historic summit was a first-of-its-kind trilateral peace declaration," Trump said. "The United States, Russia and China formally agreed that nuclear conflict must never be allowed to happen."

Trump also described the APEC Economic Leaders' Meeting in Shenzhen, south China's Guangdong Province, as "very successful."

At the trilateral summit in Shenzhen on Thursday, the three leaders had an in-depth exchange of views on maintaining world peace and promoting strategic stability in major country relations. Following the summit, the three countries released the China-Russia-U.S. Heads of State Declaration toward Peace.
