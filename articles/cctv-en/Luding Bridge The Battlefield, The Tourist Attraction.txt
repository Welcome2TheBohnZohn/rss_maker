ARTICLE TEXT:
In May 1935, 22 Chinese Red Army soldiers crawled across the 13 iron chains and reached the other end of the bridge among enemy's gunfire. Ninety years later, the bridge has become a tourist attraction. At the 90th anniversary of the victory of Chinese Red Army's Long March, CCTV.com reporter comes to the bridge to retell its story.
