TITLE:
Pinglu Canal opens, creating a new gateway to the sea for southwest China

PUBLISHED:
2026-09-18

SOURCE:
CCTV English

CATEGORY:
Authoritative PRC Media

URL:

ARTICLE TEXT:
On September 16, the Pinglu Canal officially opens to navigation. This 134.2-kilometer canal connects the main channel of the Xijiang River with the Beibu Gulf, giving southwest China a more direct route to the sea.
