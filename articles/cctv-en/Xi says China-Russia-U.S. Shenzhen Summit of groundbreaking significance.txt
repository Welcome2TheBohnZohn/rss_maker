SHENZHEN, Nov. 19 (Xinhua) -- Chinese President Xi Jinping on Thursday said that the first-ever face-to-face meeting between the Chinese, Russian and U.S. heads of state is of groundbreaking significance.

Xi made the remarks at a summit with Russian President Vladimir Putin and U.S. President Donald Trump in Shenzhen, south China's Guangdong Province.
