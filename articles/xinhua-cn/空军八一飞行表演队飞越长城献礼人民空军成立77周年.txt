TITLE:
空军八一飞行表演队飞越长城献礼人民空军成立77周年

PUBLISHED:
2026-11-06

SOURCE:
Xinhua Chinese

CATEGORY:
Authoritative PRC Media

URL:
https://www.news.cn/photo/20261106/e7c6610eb42942aeba15d49841b5343d/c.html
