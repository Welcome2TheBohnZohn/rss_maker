中国海警舰艇编队9月10日在我钓鱼岛及其附属岛屿领海内维权巡航
China Coast Guard Task Group Conducts a Rights-protection Patrol in the Territorial Waters of China's Diaoyu Dao and Its Affiliated Islands on September 10
9月10日，中国海警登步舰编队在我钓鱼岛及其附属岛屿领海内维权巡航。这是中国海警依法开展的维权巡航活动。
On September 10, the China Coast Guard (CCG) task group led by CCGS Dengbu conducted a rights-protection patrol in the territorial waters of China's Diaoyu Dao and its affiliated islands. The patrol was a rights-protection operation carried out by the CCG in accordance with the law.
