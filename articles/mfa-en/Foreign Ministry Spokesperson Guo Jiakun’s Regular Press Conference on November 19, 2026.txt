Shenzhen TV: This morning, the 33rd APEC Economic Leaders’ Meeting was successfully held in Shenzhen. Could you share more details?
Guo Jiakun: This morning, President Xi Jinping chaired the 33rd APEC Economic Leaders’ Meeting and delivered important remarks in Shenzhen.
President Xi Jinping noted that the launch of APEC more than 30 years ago started a remarkable journey toward building an Asia-Pacific community. over three decades and more, the Asia-Pacific region has witnessed progress with each passing day, and created the Asia-Pacific miracle of rapid economic growth. In the face of new opportunities and challenges, we must strive to build an Asia-Pacific community characterized by solidarity and mutual assistance, openness and integration, enterprise and innovation, as well as inclusive development.
President Xi Jinping stressed that China stands ready to work with all parties to build the four key pillars of an Asia-Pacific community: strengthen the pillar of economy and trade by aligning various regional economic and trade agreements and enhancing policy dialogue on the FTAAP; upgrade the pillar of connectivity by improving the infrastructure network, harmonizing policies and regulations, and facilitating travel for business, tourism and other purposes; foster the pillar of innovation by strengthening digital infrastructure development and encouraging the broad application of AI; enhance the pillar of development by promoting regional cooperation on energy and food security, achieving sustainable economic growth, supporting livelihood improvements, and investing in the people.
President Xi announced new measures China would take to support Asia-Pacific cooperation, including providing financial support for APEC’s practical cooperation, establishing a Center for Asia-Pacific Community Studies in Shenzhen, and setting up cooperation institutions in such areas as digital technology, cross-border data flow, clean energy, and the development of SMEs.
The meeting adopted by consensus the 2026 APEC leaders’ declaration, which sets out the Shenzhen Outlook on building an Asia-Pacific community, and three outcome documents on trade, connectivity and innovation.
AFP: Some South Korean media outlets have reported today that there are no signs of North Korean leader Kim Jong Un visiting China. Can the foreign ministry give us an indication of whether a meeting between Kim and U.S. President Donald Trump in China is being arranged?
Guo Jiakun: I do not have information on that.
Asahi Shimbun: Regarding the arrangements for the APEC events, the pared-down welcome accorded to the Japanese Prime Minister has been interpreted by some as a snub and drawn quite some attention. What’s China’s consideration behind such arrangements?
Guo Jiakun: As the host, China makes appropriate protocol arrangements for all participants in accordance with international practice.
AFP: Will the Chinese leader hold a meeting with Japanese Prime Minister Takaichi on the sidelines of the APEC summit?
Guo Jiakun: Our position on China-Japan relations is clear.
