Shenzhen TV: Recently, Vice President of the Swiss Federal Council and Foreign Minister Ignazio Cassis paid a visit to China and had the fifth round of China-Switzerland Foreign Ministers’ Strategic Dialogue with Member of the Political Bureau of the CPC Central Committee and Minister of Foreign Affairs Wang Yi. Could you please share more information about this visit?
Guo Jiakun: Switzerland is China’s important innovative strategic partner in Europe. The China-Switzerland relationship has always been at the forefront of China’s relationships with Western countries, and has become a good example for cooperation between countries with different social systems and at different development stages. On the occasion of the 10th anniversary of the China-Switzerland innovative strategic partnership, the President and the Vice President of the Swiss Federal Council paid visits to China within a month. These visits have shown high political trust of the two countries and forged broad consensus for cooperation.
The Foreign Ministers’ Strategic Dialogue is an effective mechanism where Chinese and Swiss diplomatic authorities strengthen policy synergy and coordination on bilateral and multilateral affairs. Regarding the fifth round of this dialogue, China has released information. The two sides agreed to follow the important common understandings reached by the leaders, step up high-level engagements, uphold mutual respect, mutual trust, openness and cooperation, improve and upgrade cooperation in free trade, finance, innovation, education, people-to-people exchanges and other areas, strengthen communication on global AI governance and other issues, deepen multilateral coordination, safeguard international fairness and justice, and contribute to peace, stability, prosperity and progress of the world.
Bloomberg: NVIDIA Corp.’s board has reinstated Jensen Huang less than two weeks after he left under a cloud of scandal. President Trump had pressed for the move and the board cited the U.S government’s input when making the announcement. Does the foreign ministry have any comment on Huang’s return?
Guo Jiakun: We have no comment on this.
AFP: On Monday, the United States added Singapore-based Megaspeed International to a trade blacklist that bars it from purchasing advanced chips. This is related to the NVIDIA news because the alleged spy Jensen Huang had an affair with was an executive at Megaspeed. Regarding this trade blacklist, does the Chinese foreign ministry have a comment or response?
Guo Jiakun: We have made clear China’s position on the relevant issue more than once. I have nothing further to add.
Bloomberg: It’s reported that the Solomon Islands has informed China it is terminating the landmark security agreement when it expires. Can the foreign ministry confirm this and does the ministry have any comment about the report that the Solomon Islands will sign a new deal with Australia?
Guo Jiakun: China always follows the principle of mutual respect and equality in developing relations with the Solomon Islands. There is no element of geopolitical rivalry or bloc confrontation in cooperation between China and Pacific island countries, including the Solomon Islands. Our cooperation does not target any third party and is not subject to third-party influence. The international community should pool efforts to help Pacific island countries realize sustainable development on the basis of respecting their independence, putting development first, and upholding openness and inclusiveness. Relevant cooperation should not target any third party.
AFP: Yesterday, Ukraine claimed that it was behind strikes on two Russian vessels off the coast of Libya. Does China have a comment on this latest development of war between Russia and Ukraine?
Guo Jiakun: I’m not familiar with the specifics.
China’s position on the Ukraine crisis has been consistent and clear.
Bloomberg: We saw that a Pakistani Air Force pilot will blast off on China’s Shenzhou-24 spacecraft on Wednesday, becoming the first foreign astronaut in the history of China’s manned space program. We are wondering if the foreign ministry has any comment.
Guo Jiakun: The joint mission of the Pakistani astronaut and the Chinese crew to Tiangong space station will mark the first of its kind in the history of China’s manned space program. The Pakistani astronaut will be the first visiting foreign astronaut to China’s space station. This year marks the 75th anniversary of China-Pakistan diplomatic ties. The joint mission will be yet another important milestone in the all-weather strategic cooperative partnership. We look forward to their exciting journey into space. We wish the mission a full success.
