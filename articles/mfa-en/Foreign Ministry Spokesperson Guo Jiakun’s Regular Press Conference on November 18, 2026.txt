CCTV: The 2026 APEC CEO Summit is held in Shenzhen today, which is attended by many state leaders and people from the business communities. Can you share more details about this event?
Guo Jiakun: This morning, President Xi Jinping attended the opening ceremony of the 2026 APEC CEO Summit in Shenzhen and delivered a keynote speech, in which he made four propositions for jointly building an Asia-Pacific community and bringing prosperity for all in the region.
First, upholding peace and stability. We should uphold the postwar international order and promote an equality-based and orderly multipolar world and economic globalization that is inclusive and brings benefit to all. We should resolve differences through dialogue and consultation, and overcome challenges through solidarity and cooperation. This is the way to cement the foundation for sustaining peace and stability in the Asia-Pacific.
Second, building bridges of opening up and cooperation. We should firmly safeguard the WTO-centered, rules-based multilateral trading system, support free and open trade and investment, and keep global industrial and supply chains stable and unimpeded. We should work steadily toward a Free Trade Area of the Asia-Pacific to pave the way for greater economic integration in the region.
Third, boosting innovation to drive growth. We should forge science, technology and innovation partnerships, deepen cooperation in digital economy and AI, and promote green and low-carbon transition. By doing so, we can turn strengths in innovation into drivers of growth, and provide strong impetus for economic growth in the Asia-Pacific.
Fourth, expanding our ways for mutual benefits and win-win outcomes. We should remove trade and investment barriers to build an open Asia-Pacific economy. At the same time, we should promote development that is balanced and delivers benefit to all to ensure both efficiency and fairness. We should follow a people-centered development philosophy, and ensure that development fruits are shared by all economies and all groups and communities. China supports the establishment of a World Association for Bay Areas Economic Cooperation to help explore new ways for stronger economic cooperation in the Asia-Pacific.
President Xi pointed out that China’s economy maintains a strong momentum of high-quality, innovation-driven growth, demonstrating great resilience and vitality. A blueprint for China’s development, the 15th Five-Year Plan also provides a list of opportunities for promoting economic growth in the Asia-Pacific and the wider world. China will provide global business communities with a super-sized open market, a promising land for development, and an environment that enables mutually reinforcing cooperation. Asia-Pacific business communities are more than welcome to continue to get actively involved in China’s reform and opening up and modernization drive, and share in the opportunities created by Chinese modernization.
AFP: Regarding tomorrow’s trilateral meeting between the three presidents of China, Russia and the U.S., will it cover Ukraine? Trump said before leaving for Asia that the leaders would talk about Ukraine. So would China shut down any discussion of Ukraine if it comes up?
Guo Jiakun: The three heads of state will have an in-depth exchange of views on important issues bearing on global peace. We will release information about the summit in a timely manner. Please check back for updates.
The Hindu: I know you said previously that China hopes the meeting will focus on promoting strategic stability among the major powers. I was just wondering could you elaborate if are there any expectations of concrete outcomes that China hopes to see?
Guo Jiakun: China has shared information on the China-Russia-U.S. Shenzhen Summit. The three heads of state will have an in-depth exchange of views on important issues bearing on global peace. We will release information about the summit in a timely manner. Please check back for updates.
AFP: The interim CEO of NVIDIA told shareholders yesterday that it was no longer shipping licensed Hopper data center products to China, which means that the H200 chips that were previously allowed into China under a deal by Washington are no longer being sent over. Does China have a comment on this?
Guo Jiakun: We have made clear more than once our principled position on U.S. chip exports to China.
