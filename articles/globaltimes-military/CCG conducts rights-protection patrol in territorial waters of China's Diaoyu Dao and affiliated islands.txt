TITLE:
CCG conducts rights-protection patrol in territorial waters of China's Diaoyu Dao and affiliated islands

PUBLISHED:
2026-10-10 18:18:00

SOURCE:
Global Times Military

CATEGORY:
Military

URL:
https://www.globaltimes.cn/page/202610/1371948.shtml

ARTICLE TEXT:
On October 10, the China Coast Guard (CCG) task group led by CCGS Xiamen conducted a rights-protection patrol in the territorial waters of China's Diaoyu Dao and its affiliated islands, according to WeChat account of CCG.
The patrol was a rights-protection operation carried out by the CCG in accordance with the law.
