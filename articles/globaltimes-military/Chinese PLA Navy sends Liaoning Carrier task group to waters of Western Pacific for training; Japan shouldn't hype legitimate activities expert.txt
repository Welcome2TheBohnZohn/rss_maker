TITLE:
Chinese PLA Navy sends Liaoning Carrier task group to waters of Western Pacific for training; Japan shouldn't hype legitimate activities: expert

ARTICLE TEXT:
The Liaoning aircraft carrier participates in a dual-aircraft-carrier formation drill on October 21, 2024. Photo: VCG
The Chinese PLA Navy on Wednesday sent the 
Liaoning Carrier task group to the waters of the Western Pacific to conduct training, which is organized in accordance with annual routine training plan, aiming to test and improve the group's realistic training level and continuously enhance the capabilities to execute missions. 
The move is in compliance with relevant international law and practices and not directed against any specific country or target, the PLA Navy said in a statement.
On Wednesday evening, Japan's Ministry of Defense claimed that it had spotted a total of four vessels, the 
Liaoning
, two Type 055 large destroyers 
Anshan (Hull 103) and 
Wuxi (Hull 104), and Type 052D guided-missile destroyer 
Huainan (Hull 123), transiting southeast through the waters between Okinawa's main island and Miyako Island, heading toward the Pacific Ocean.
Carrier-based fighter aircraft from the Liaoning were seen taking off and landing in waters approximately 250 km south of Ishigaki Island, Japan's Defense Ministry claimed in a statement.
Zhang Junshe, a Chinese military affairs expert, told the Global Times that PLA warships, including aircraft carriers, regularly conduct far seas exercises in the Western Pacific, but Japan frequently hypes such activities.
These far seas exercises conform to the international law and common practices, and the Japanese side should not hype such legitimate activities, Zhang said.
