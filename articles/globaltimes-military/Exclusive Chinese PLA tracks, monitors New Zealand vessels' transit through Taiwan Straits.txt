Exclusive: Chinese PLA tracks, monitors New Zealand vessels’ transit through Taiwan Straits
                        
                        
                            By Global Times Published: Sep 20, 2026 01:15 PM 
                            
                                
                            
                         
                     
                    
                         Chinese military sources told the Global Times that New Zealand's frigate HMNZS Te Mana and replenishment ship HMNZS Aotearoa transited the Taiwan Straits on September 18. The Chinese People's Liberation Army tracked and monitored the vessels and maintained vigilance throughout their passage, with the situation fully under control.
