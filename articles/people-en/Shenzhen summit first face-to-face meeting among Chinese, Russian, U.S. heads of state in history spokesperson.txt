Shenzhen summit first face-to-face meeting among Chinese, Russian, U.S. heads of state in history: spokesperson
 (Xinhua) 16:28, November 16, 2026
BEIJING, Nov. 16 (Xinhua) -- The meeting of Chinese, Russian, U.S. heads of state in China's Shenzhen will be the first face-to-face meeting among the top leaders of the three countries in history, Chinese Foreign Ministry spokesperson Mao Ning said on Monday.
Mao made the remarks when asked to answer a related query, saying that the heads of state will have an in-depth exchange of views on maintaining world peace and promoting strategic stability in major country relations.
Chinese President Xi Jinping will hold a China-Russia-U.S. summit with Russian President Vladimir Putin and U.S. President Donald Trump in Shenzhen on Nov. 19, a Chinese foreign ministry spokesperson announced on Monday morning.
