Full attendance exhibitors at 9th CIIE
People visit the booth of Johnson & Johnson, a nine-year attendee of the China International Import Expo (CIIE), at the exhibition area of Medical Equipment and Health Care Products during the ninth CIIE in east China's Shanghai, Nov. 7, 2026. (Xinhua/Chen Haoming)
SHANGHAI, Nov. 7 (Xinhua) -- Running from Nov. 5 to 10, this year's China International Import Expo (CIIE) has attracted 4,142 overseas exhibitors, and nearly 200 of the participants have maintained full attendance at the CIIE for nine consecutive years, reflecting China's firm commitment to high-level opening-up.
