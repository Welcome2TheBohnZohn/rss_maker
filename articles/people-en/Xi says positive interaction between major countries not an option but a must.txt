Xi says positive interaction between major countries not an option but a must
SHENZHEN, Nov. 19 (Xinhua) -- Chinese President Xi Jinping on Thursday said that positive interaction between major countries is "not optional, but a must."
Xi made the remarks at a summit with Russian President Vladimir Putin and U.S. President Donald Trump in Shenzhen, south China's Guangdong Province.
"Humanity, living in the same global village, is a community with a shared future where we all stand to benefit or lose together. In this global village, major countries shoulder special responsibilities," he said.
In recent years, China has been promoting the China-Russia comprehensive strategic partnership of coordination for the new era, and actively developing the constructive China-U.S. relationship of strategic stability, with a view to fostering major-country dynamics featuring peaceful coexistence, overall stability, and balanced development to the benefit of each other and the world at large, he said.
Noting that transformation not seen in a century is accelerating across the globe, Xi said the world is not peaceful and testing times reveal responsibilities on one's shoulders.
As major countries and permanent members of the United Nations Security Council, China, Russia and the United States should set good examples, work together to meet global challenges, jointly uphold world peace and stability, and make greater contributions to the progress of humanity.
