Xi says China, Australia should be mutually trusted partners
SHENZHEN, Nov. 20 (Xinhua) -- Chinese President Xi Jinping said Friday that China and Australia should be cooperative partners who trust each other and help each other succeed, as the two countries have no historical grievances or fundamental conflicts of interests.
China-Australia relations are stable at present, said Xi when meeting with Australian Prime Minister Anthony Albanese in Shenzhen, south China's Guangdong Province.
Xi said that in a volatile and turbulent world, the two countries should enhance mutual trust, accommodate each other's core interests and major concerns, and keep to the right direction of friendship and cooperation.
He called for deepening mutually beneficial cooperation in pursuit of the two countries' respective development goals, advancing traditional cooperation in agriculture, energy and minerals, and expanding cooperation in emerging areas such as the green economy, new energy, the digital industry, artificial intelligence and climate change response.
Efforts should be made to promote friendship between the two countries through expanded people-to-people exchanges, Xi said, noting that the two sides had agreed to designate 2027 as the China-Australia Year of Tourism.
He also emphasized the need to strengthen multilateral coordination in addressing global challenges, advance the building of an Asia-Pacific community, and jointly uphold the international system with the United Nations at its core and the international order based on international law.
