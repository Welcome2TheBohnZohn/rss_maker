Xi urges all-out rescue, relief efforts after mudslide hits border port in Xizang
 (Xinhua) 16:21, August 26, 2026
		
		
BEIJING, Aug. 26 (Xinhua) -- Chinese President Xi Jinping has ordered all-out efforts to search and rescue people who are missing after a mudslide in Nepal caused casualties at Gyirong border port in southwest China's Xizang Autonomous Region on Wednesday morning.
