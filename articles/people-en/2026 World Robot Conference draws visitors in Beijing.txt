2026 World Robot Conference draws visitors in Beijing
People visit the 2026 World Robot Conference in Beijing, capital of China, Aug. 22, 2026. The conference, which runs from Wednesday through Sunday, has drawn more than 300 exhibitors from 26 countries, with over 3,000 products on display, including more than 300 debuts. Many people visited the conference on Saturday, the first open day of the event. (Xinhua/Chen Yehua)
