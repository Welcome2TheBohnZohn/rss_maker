CPC Central Committee to hold press conference on plenary session
 (Xinhua) 08:09, October 30, 2026
BEIJING, Oct. 29 (Xinhua) -- The Central Committee of the Communist Party of China (CPC) will hold a press conference on Friday on the guiding principles from the fifth plenary session of the 20th CPC Central Committee.
The press conference, scheduled to start at 10 a.m., will be broadcast live by China Media Group, and on major news websites including people.com.cn, xinhuanet.com and china.com.cn.
