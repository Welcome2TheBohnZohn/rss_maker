Trailer: China's Answer to Global Poverty Reduction
 (People's Daily Online) 09:32, October 11, 2026
Ice-and-snow tourism is activating local resources. Ethnic cultures are stepping out from the mountains. Ecological governance is renewing the land.
Follow Xi, a video column produced by People's Daily Online, has launched the documentary series "China's Answer to Global Poverty Reduction", chronicling China's efforts to reduce poverty through development — tailored, in each place, to local conditions. The transformation of ordinary people's lives is the most convincing proof. China's exploration also offers lessons for global poverty reduction.
