28th China Hi-Tech Fair kicks off in Shenzhen
This photo taken on Nov. 26, 2026 shows a scene of the 28th China Hi-Tech Fair (CHTF) in Shenzhen, south China's Guangdong Province. The three-day event kicked off at the Shenzhen World Exhibition & Convention Center (Bao'an) on Thursday. This year's CHTF features a total exhibition area of 400,000 square meters and attracts approximately 5,000 companies and institutions from over 120 countries and regions. (Xinhua/Liang Xu)
