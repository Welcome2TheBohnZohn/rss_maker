Three high-speed railways open to traffic in E China's Anhui
The bullet train No. G9528 stops at Lixin Railway Station on the Huaibei-Fuyang high-speed railway in east China's Anhui Province, Nov. 27, 2026. Three high-speed railways, namely Chaohu-Ma'anshan, Huaibei-Bengbu and Huaibei-Fuyang high-speed railway, in Anhui opened to traffic on Friday, bringing the operating mileage of high-speed rail in the Yangtze River Delta to over 8,500 kilometers.
