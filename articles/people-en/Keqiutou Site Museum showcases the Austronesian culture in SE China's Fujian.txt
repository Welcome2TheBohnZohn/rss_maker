Keqiutou Site Museum showcases the Austronesian culture in SE China's Fujian
A girl visits the exhibition "Born by the Sea - Pingtan Prehistoric Culture" at the Keqiutou Site Museum in Pingtan, southeast China's Fujian Province, Aug. 20, 2026. (Xinhua/Du Xiaoyi)
PINGTAN, Aug. 21 (Xinhua) -- Keqiutou Site Museum, a branch of the Chinese Archaeological Museum, is built around the Keqiutou ruins in Pingtan, southeast China's Fujian Province. It serves as an important platform for the study and presentation of Austronesian culture and for the display of the prehistoric culture of Pingtan.
