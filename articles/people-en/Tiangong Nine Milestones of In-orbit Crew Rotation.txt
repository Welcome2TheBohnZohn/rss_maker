Tiangong: Nine Milestones of In-orbit Crew Rotation
By Zhou Jielu, Jiang Jie, Shi Jing (People's Daily Online) 14:28, November 26, 2026
China's Tiangong space station celebrated its first in-orbit crew rotation on November 30, 2022, when the Shenzhou-14 crew handed over the station to their Shenzhou-15 counterparts. Since then, the station has completed nine crew rotations. The arrival of the Shenzhou-24 crew on Wednesday marked the latest milestone, bringing the station its first foreign astronaut.
