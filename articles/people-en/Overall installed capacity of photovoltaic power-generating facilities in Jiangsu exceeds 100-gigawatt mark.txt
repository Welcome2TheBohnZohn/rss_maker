Overall installed capacity of photovoltaic power-generating facilities in Jiangsu exceeds 100-gigawatt mark
An aerial drone photo taken on Aug. 19, 2026 shows a photovoltaic power project in Taizhou, east China's Jiangsu Province. The overall installed capacity of photovoltaic power-generating facilities in Jiangsu has exceeded 100-gigawatt mark to reach 100.08 gigawatts by the end of July 2026, according to the State Grid Jiangsu Electric Power Company.
