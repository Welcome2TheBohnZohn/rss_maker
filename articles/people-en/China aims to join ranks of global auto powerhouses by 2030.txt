China aims to join ranks of global auto powerhouses by 2030
 (Xinhua) 10:18, September 11, 2026
		
		
BEIJING, Sept. 11 (Xinhua) -- China aims to join the ranks of the world's automotive powerhouses by 2030, with stronger advantages across the entire industrial chain of intelligent connected new energy vehicles and large-scale application of vehicles equipped with autonomous driving functions, according to an industry development plan released Friday.
