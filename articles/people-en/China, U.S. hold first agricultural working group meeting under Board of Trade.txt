China, U.S. hold first agricultural working group meeting under Board of Trade
 (Xinhua) 14:59, November 25, 2026
BEIJING, Nov. 25 (Xinhua) -- China and the United States held the first meeting of their agricultural working group under the China-U.S. Board of Trade in Beijing from Monday to Tuesday, according to China's Ministry of Commerce.
Guided by the important consensus reached by the two heads of state, the two sides held candid, in-depth and constructive discussions on issues including agricultural product regulation, market access and agricultural trade, the ministry said.
Both sides will continue to strengthen communication through the working group and work toward addressing each other's concerns in the agricultural sector to promote sound, stable and sustainable development of China-U.S. economic and trade relations, it added.
