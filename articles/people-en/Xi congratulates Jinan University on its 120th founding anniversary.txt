Xi congratulates Jinan University on its 120th founding anniversary
BEIJING, Nov. 16 (Xinhua) -- Chinese President Xi Jinping on Monday sent a congratulatory letter to Jinan University, celebrating its 120th founding anniversary.
Xi, also general secretary of the Communist Party of China Central Committee and chairman of the Central Military Commission, extended congratulations to the faculty, students and alumni of the university.
Xi urged the university to carry forward its patriotic traditions and distinctive educational features, and to foster more young talent among compatriots in Hong Kong, Macao and Taiwan and overseas Chinese.
He also called on the university to carry forward and promote Chinese culture and advance friendly exchanges between China and the world.
The university was asked to improve its education and teaching quality and make new contributions to pooling strength among overseas Chinese and advancing Chinese modernization.
Founded in 1906, the university in south China's Guangdong Province has, over the past 120 years, nurtured a wealth of outstanding talent for Hong Kong, Macao and Taiwan, as well as for more than 170 countries.
