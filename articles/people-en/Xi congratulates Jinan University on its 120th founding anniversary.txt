Xi congratulates Jinan University on its 120th founding anniversary
 (Xinhua) 10:21, November 16, 2026
BEIJING, Nov. 16 (Xinhua) -- Chinese President Xi Jinping on Monday sent a congratulatory letter to Jinan University, celebrating its 120th founding anniversary.
Xi, also general secretary of the Communist Party of China Central Committee and chairman of the Central Military Commission, extended congratulations to the faculty, students and alumni of the university.
