ARTICLE TEXT:
Located at the confluence of the Yangtze River and the Qingyi River, Wuhu in east China's Anhui Province has long been shaped by the culture of southern Anhui and built on a solid industrial foundation. As one of China's major industrial cities, Wuhu has undergone a remarkable transformation from traditional manufacturing to emerging modern industries.
