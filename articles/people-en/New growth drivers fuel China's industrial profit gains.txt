New growth drivers fuel China's industrial profit gains
BEIJING, Nov. 27 (Xinhua) -- Profits of China's industrial firms above designated size rose 16 percent year on year in the first 10 months of 2026, official data showed Friday, as new growth drivers gathered momentum.
The growth rate accelerated 0.5 percentage points from the January-September period, according to the National Bureau of Statistics (NBS). In October alone, profits surged 20.1 percent year on year, accelerating 5.7 percentage points from September.
By sector, mining profits jumped 38.8 percent and manufacturing profits rose 17.5 percent, while profits in the production and supply of electricity, heat, gas and water fell 14.8 percent.
The electronics sector was a standout driver, with profits increasing 100 percent year on year in the first 10 months and contributing 9.7 percentage points to overall industrial profit growth, as surging demand for AI computing power fueled profits in memory chips and related manufacturing. Within computer manufacturing, profits of computer makers surged 410 percent and those of computer peripheral makers jumped 200 percent.
High-tech manufacturing also shone, with profits up 50.9 percent year on year in the first 10 months, outpacing overall industrial growth by 34.9 percentage points. Driven by large-scale construction of intelligent computing centers, profits of optical fiber and optical cable makers surged 670 percent and 150 percent, respectively.
The profit margin on operating revenue stood at 5.69 percent, up 0.45 percentage points year on year.
