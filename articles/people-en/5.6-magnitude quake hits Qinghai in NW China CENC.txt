5.6-magnitude quake hits Qinghai in NW China: CENC
 (Xinhua) 10:26, November 22, 2026
BEIJING, Nov. 21 (Xinhua) -- A 5.6-magnitude earthquake jolted Delingha City in northwest China's Qinghai Province at 6:45 p.m. on Saturday (Beijing Time), according to the China Earthquake Networks Center (CENC).
The epicenter was monitored at 37.14 degrees north latitude and 96.62 degrees east longitude. The earthquake struck at a depth of 10 km, the center said.
