Exoskeletons power new hiking experience in scenic areas in E China's Anhui
HUANGSHAN, Oct. 5 (Xinhua) -- The Qiyun Mountain scenic area offers exoskeleton robot rentals during the National Day holiday to help visitors hike with less effort.
The lightweight, easy-to-use devices sense body movements, providing uphill assistance and downhill cushioning to protect knees and reduce leg strain.
With a growing number of hikers in recent years, the area now offers over 20 such robots, using technology to deliver a smarter mountain climbing experience.
