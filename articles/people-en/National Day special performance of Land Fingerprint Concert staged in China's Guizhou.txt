National Day special performance of Land Fingerprint Concert staged in China's Guizhou
People watch the Land Fingerprint Concert in Dafang County of southwest China's Guizhou Province, Oct. 1, 2026. The Shexiang ancient town in Dafang County staged its National Day special performance of the Land Fingerprint Concert on Thursday, attracting lots of visitors. The second special performance is scheduled to be held on Oct. 5.
As a resettlement spot for poverty alleviation relocation, Shexiang ancient town has held various events to boost the local economy including village markets, singing performances and torch festivals. Since the debut of the Land Fingerprint Concert in August, the town has witnessed a surge in visitor numbers and fresh impetus for the economic development of surrounding areas. (Xinhua/Yang Wenbin)
