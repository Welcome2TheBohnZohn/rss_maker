Sweet cherries ride wave of China's booming market demands
Cherries are seen at a cherry orchard in Curico province, central Chile, Nov. 14, 2026. Chile is a major global exporter of cherries, and China is the largest overseas market for Chilean cherries. According to data from Chile's Ministry of Agriculture, about 569,000 tonnes of cherries were exported in the 2025/2026 season, of which about 87 percent went to China.
In recent years, against the backdrop of deepening economic cooperation in the Asia-Pacific region, China and Chile, both the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation (APEC) economies, have continued to advance economic and trade cooperation. Since 2018, Chile has operated a direct shipping route to China known as the "cherry express," significantly shortening the transit time.
This year marks the APEC "China Year," and the 33rd APEC Economic Leaders' Meeting was held in Shenzhen from November 18 to 19. Taking this as an opportunity, China and Chile will further strengthen cooperation on trade facilitation and agricultural trade. The cherries crossing the Pacific Ocean have become a vivid epitome of the deepening economic and trade cooperation between China and Chile. (Xinhua/Zhou Jiayi)
