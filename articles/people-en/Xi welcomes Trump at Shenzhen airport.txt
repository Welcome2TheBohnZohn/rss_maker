SHENZHEN, Nov. 18 (Xinhua) -- Chinese President Xi Jinping on Wednesday welcomed U.S. President Donald Trump at Shenzhen Bao'an International Airport in south China's Guangdong Province.
Trump is in Shenzhen to attend the 33rd Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation (APEC) Economic Leaders' Meeting, scheduled for Nov. 18-19.
Amid welcome music, Xi and Trump shook hands warmly. Honor guards of the Chinese People's Liberation Army presented arms to salute, and a girl handed the U.S. leader a bouquet.
The national flags of China and the United States fluttered in the wind as a military band played the national anthems of the two countries, and fighter jets flew overhead in salute. The two heads of state waved to a welcoming young crowd.
Xi recalled his state visit to the United States in September this year, when the U.S. presidential couple came to the airport in person and hosted a grand welcome ceremony, noting that China is a nation with a tradition of courtesy that values reciprocating goodwill.
Extending his welcome to Trump for visiting China again, Xi said that he looks forward to conducting in-depth exchanges with the U.S. president on major issues concerning bilateral relations, Asia-Pacific cooperation and the global landscape to advance a constructive China-U.S. relationship of strategic stability based on respect, fairness and reciprocity.
Trump thanked Xi for greeting him in person at the airport and for the special arrangements by the Chinese side. He said it is a great pleasure to visit China again, calling China a great country and Xi a great leader.
Trump said that every meeting with Xi had been very pleasant, and he believes that the visit will further advance bilateral relations and achieve complete success.
