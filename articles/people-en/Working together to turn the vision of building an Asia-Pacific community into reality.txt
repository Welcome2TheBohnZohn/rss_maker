ARTICLE TEXT:
Working together to turn the vision of building an Asia-Pacific community into reality
Photo shows the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation (APEC)-themed flower beds on a street in Shenzhen, south China's Guangdong province. (Photo/Weng Qiyu)
Along Shenzhen Bay in Shenzhen, south China's Guangdong province, calm waters stretch along broad, open shores. On Nov. 19, the 33rd Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation (APEC) Economic Leaders' Meeting was held in the city, bringing together participants to discuss ways to advance Asia-Pacific cooperation and promote regional development.
Chinese President Xi Jinping chaired the meeting and delivered a speech, proposing to work together to build the "four key pillars" of an Asia-Pacific community -- economy and trade, connectivity, innovation, and development.
He charted the course toward an Asia-Pacific community characterized by solidarity and mutual assistance, openness and integration, enterprise and innovation, as well as inclusive development.
Drawing on lessons from the past helps pave the way for a new journey ahead. Since its establishment more than three decades ago, APEC has kept pace with the times and steered the process of Asia-Pacific economic integration. Over the years, the Asia-Pacific region has witnessed progress with each passing day, and created the Asia-Pacific miracle of rapid economic growth.
Today, changes unseen in a century are unfolding at an accelerating pace across the globe. Innovation is burgeoning across sciences, technologies, and industries. In the face of new opportunities and challenges, how can the region write a new chapter of the Asia-Pacific miracle?
Taking into account the broader historical trends and the demands of the times, Xi pointed out that building an Asia-Pacific community requires commitment to solidarity and mutual assistance, openness and integration, enterprise and innovation, and inclusive development.
These four principles outline the vision for an Asia-Pacific community from four dimensions: relations among economies, the path to development, the driving force for progress, and the values that underpin the community.
They provide fundamental guidance for building an Asia-Pacific community and ensuring steady, sustained progress in regional cooperation.
Turning this vision into reality calls for concrete action, resolve, and a strong sense of responsibility.
"China is an advocate, promoter, and forerunner of Asia-Pacific economic cooperation. We would like to work with all parties to pool wisdom and synergy in building the four key pillars of an Asia-Pacific community," Xi said in his speech.
He called on APEC economies to strengthen the pillar of economy and trade. "The Free Trade Area of the Asia-Pacific (FTAAP) is a shared vision of APEC members. It is important to make steady progress in realizing this vision and setting a higher standard for openness," he said.
He called for APEC economies to upgrade the pillar of connectivity and strengthen physical connectivity by improving the infrastructure network, deepen institutional connectivity by harmonizing policies and regulations, and enhance people-to-people connectivity by facilitating travel for business, tourism and other purposes, further strengthening the ties that underpin regional economic integration.
APEC economies should foster the pillar of innovation and make the digital economy and artificial intelligence (AI) the engines of innovation-driven growth, Xi said.
He also urged APEC economies to enhance the pillar of development and strengthen capacity building, achieve sustainable economic growth, and tap into the full potential of talent across the Asia-Pacific.
At the meeting, Xi also announced a series of new measures China will take to support Asia-Pacific cooperation.
Addressing both immediate concerns and long-term trends, these proposals and measures demonstrate China's commitment to translating its vision for an Asia-Pacific community into concrete results and reflect its sense of responsibility as a major country.
China is rooted in the Asia-Pacific and has consistently supported regional cooperation.
Since assuming the role of host of APEC 2026, China has held more than 300 meetings and events in 16 cities, attracting over 10,000 representatives from APEC member economies. Their active participation has helped ensure that the APEC "China Year" deliver meaningful outcomes.
Various parties have spoken highly of the fruitful results achieved during the APEC "China Year," expressing their readiness to deepen cooperation across various fields, jointly address risks and challenges, and promote APEC's further development, thereby contributing to peace, stability, and prosperity in the region and the world.
With firm confidence, a clear sense of direction, and a commitment to delivering results, economies across the region have come together to build an Asia-Pacific community.
China stands ready to work with all parties to remain committed to this overarching goal and advance cooperation across all fields with a strong sense of mission. Together, they can deliver more tangible outcomes from Asia-Pacific cooperation and write a new chapter in the Asia-Pacific miracle.
