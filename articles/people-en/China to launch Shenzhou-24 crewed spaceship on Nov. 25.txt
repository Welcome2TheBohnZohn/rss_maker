China to launch Shenzhou-24 crewed spaceship on Nov. 25
 (Xinhua) 09:12, November 24, 2026
JIUQUAN, Nov. 24 (Xinhua) -- China's Shenzhou-24 crewed spaceship is scheduled to be launched at 8:18 a.m. (Beijing Time) Wednesday from the Jiuquan Satellite Launch Center in northwest China, the China Manned Space Agency announced on Tuesday.
