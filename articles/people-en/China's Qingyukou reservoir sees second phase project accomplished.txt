China's Qingyukou reservoir sees second phase project accomplished
This aerial drone photo taken on Oct. 30, 2026 shows the construction site of Qingyukou reservoir in Bazhong, southwest China's Sichuan Province. In addition to flood control purposes, the Qingyukou reservoir also plays a critical role in water supply and hydro-power generation. The reservoir, which is constructed in three phases, saw its second phase project accomplished on Friday. (Xinhua/Wang Xi)
