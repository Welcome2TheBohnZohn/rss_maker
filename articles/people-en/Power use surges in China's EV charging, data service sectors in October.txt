Power use surges in China's EV charging, data service sectors in October
BEIJING, Nov. 23 (Xinhua) -- Electricity consumption by China's electric vehicle charging and internet data service sectors soared in October, highlighting robust demand from the country's fast-growing new-energy vehicle and digital industries, official data showed Monday.
Power consumption by charging and battery-swapping services reached 18.6 billion kilowatt-hours in October, surging 59.4 percent year on year, while that of internet data services jumped 34.7 percent to 9.9 billion kilowatt-hours, according to the National Energy Administration.
NEVs accounted for 64.3 percent of new car sales last month, up from 61.6 percent in September, while the cumulative share for the first 10 months climbed to 54.8 percent, according to the China Association of Automobile Manufacturers.
China's total electricity consumption for October stood at 870.9 billion kilowatt-hours, an increase of 1.6 percent year on year.
Driven by rapid growth in electricity consumption by new business forms, power use in the tertiary industry grew the fastest among all industries, said Wu Liqiang, an official with the China Electricity Council.
Electricity consumption by the tertiary industry rose 6.3 percent year on year to 171.4 billion kilowatt-hours in October. Electricity consumption by the primary industry stood at 12.4 billion kilowatt-hours, up 3.3 percent year on year, while power use by the secondary industry edged up 1.8 percent to 579 billion kilowatt-hours.
Notably, power consumption by high-tech and equipment manufacturing industries climbed 6.5 percent year on year to 111.1 billion kilowatt-hours in October.
In October, power use in high energy-consuming manufacturing sector posted negative year-on-year growth for three consecutive months, said Wu.
In the first 10 months of 2026, China's total electricity consumption rose 3.7 percent year on year to 8.94 trillion kilowatt-hours. Electricity use by charging and battery-swapping services surged 55.9 percent in this period, while that of internet data services increased by 40.8 percent.
According to an annual development report on China's electric power industry released by the China Electricity Council in July, total electricity consumption nationwide in 2026 is expected to increase by 5 percent to 6 percent year on year.
