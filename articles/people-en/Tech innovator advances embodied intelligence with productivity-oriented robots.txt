Tech innovator advances embodied intelligence with productivity-oriented robots
Guo Yandong poses for photos with a robot at Ai² Robotics in Nanshan District of Shenzhen, south China's Guangdong Province, Nov. 12, 2026. During the 2026 APEC Economic Leaders' Week in Shenzhen, seven AlphaBot robots stationed at the media center provided services for Chinese and foreign journalists, operating reliably and serving drinks over seven consecutive days.
These robots are from Ai² Robotics, a robotics startup founded in early 2023 in Shenzhen by Guo Yandong. Guo believes that humanoid robots must be deployed in real scenarios for practical assignments. With this end goal in mind, he has oriented Ai² Robotics toward developing productivity-focused intelligent robots. The AlphaBot robots are now in service across Shenzhen for airport, industrial, urban governance and emergency-response tasks. Guo's vision of robots as the fourth-generation intelligent terminals following personal computers, mobile phones and smart vehicles is steadily taking shape. (Xinhua/Liang Xu)
