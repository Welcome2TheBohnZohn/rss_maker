Magnificent aerial views of red-hued Dahong Mountain in N China's Inner Mongolia
Photo shows an aerial view of Dahong Mountain in Naomugeng Sumu, Siziwang Banner, Ulanqab, north China's Inner Mongolia Autonomous Region. (Photo/Lyu Haojun)
With vibrant colors and rugged terrain, Dahong Mountain stands out against the surrounding grasslands, looking like a red ribbon across the vast grassland in Naomugeng Sumu, Siziwang Banner, Ulanqab, north China's Inner Mongolia Autonomous Region.
Stretching about 15 kilometers from north to south and 10 kilometers from east to west, the distinctive tableland features red conglomerate rocks shaped by prolonged weathering and erosion, creating a landscape reminiscent of Danxia landforms.
