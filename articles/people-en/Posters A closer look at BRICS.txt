Posters: A closer look at BRICS
 (People's Daily Online) 14:37, September 12, 2026
		
		
Editor's note:
The 18th BRICS Summit is being held in New Delhi, India, from Sept. 12 to 13.
Over the past two decades, BRICS has grown into a major platform for cooperation among emerging markets and developing countries and a primary channel for strengthening solidarity and cooperation among Global South nations.
How did the BRICS family take shape? What gives greater BRICS cooperation its growing weight and influence? This series of posters takes a closer look at the BRICS cooperation mechanism.
