ARTICLE TEXT:
Shoulder the responsibilities of major countries and safeguard world peace
On Nov. 19, Chinese President Xi Jinping held a China-Russia-U.S. summit with Russian President Vladimir Putin and U.S. President Donald Trump in Shenzhen, south China's Guangdong province.
The historic trilateral summit marked the first-ever face-to-face meeting between the Chinese, Russian, and U.S. heads of state, making it a groundbreaking event that will go down in history.
Following the summit, the three countries released the China-Russia-U.S. Heads of State Declaration toward Peace, expressing their commitment to maintaining strategic stability and international security.
Against a backdrop of global turbulence and uncertainty, the summit further underscored the three countries' resolve to uphold dialogue and manage differences.
It helped build consensus on maintaining strategic stability among major countries and safeguarding world peace, while sowing the seeds of building consensus through dialogue, taking responsibilities in meeting challenges, and promoting peace through cooperation.
Spanning approximately 90 minutes, the summit drew worldwide attention and reflected the international community's strong desire for peace and stability.
Today, changes unseen in a century are accelerating across the globe, geopolitical rivalries are intensifying, and localized conflicts are occurring frequently. With humanity sharing a common destiny, positive interaction among major countries is no longer optional -- it is imperative.
Whether in global development, food security, energy security, climate governance, public health cooperation, or global artificial intelligence (AI) governance, coordination and cooperation among major countries are indispensable.
Conflicts and disputes continue to erupt around the world, with one hotspot flaring up as another subsides. The international community expects major countries to do more to promote reconciliation and peace talks.
As major countries and permanent members of the United Nations Security Council, China, Russia, and the U.S. share common interests in strengthening communication, managing differences, and working together to address global challenges. These expectations align closely with broader international sentiment.
At the summit, Xi made four points on how major countries could find the right way to get along and jointly underpin world peace, calling for mutual respect, peaceful coexistence, win-win cooperation, and dialogue and consultation.
Respect is a fundamental principle in major-country interactions. China, Russia, and the U.S. have different national conditions and should respect one another, seek common ground while reserving differences, and pursue shared development. They should respect one another's sovereignty and territorial integrity, the social systems and development paths of their own choice, and their respective core interests and major concerns, while adhering to the principle of non-interference in other countries' internal affairs.
Peaceful coexistence is the bottom line for relations among major countries. China, Russia and the U.S. should be partners and friends. They should not go for conflict or confrontation among them, and avoid falling into the "Thucydides Trap." They should bear in mind that a nuclear war cannot be won and must never be fought, and work together to safeguard the postwar international order and uphold the purposes of the UN Charter.
Win-win cooperation is another imperative. China, Russia and the U.S. have different development endowments and strong economic complementarity. They can leverage their respective strengths and reinforce one another with what one has to offer. They should firmly oppose decoupling and disruption of industrial and supply chains, and work together to maintain stable and unimpeded energy industrial and supply chains.
The three countries should also remain committed to dialogue and consultation, so as to reduce misjudgments, properly manage differences and prevent frictions from escalating.
These four points build on one another, charting a course for major countries to pursue peaceful coexistence and development. Their significance extends far beyond the three countries.
China adheres to the path of peaceful development. In recent years, China has been promoting the China-Russia comprehensive strategic partnership of coordination for the new era and actively developing the constructive China-U.S. relationship of strategic stability, with a view to fostering major-country dynamics featuring peaceful coexistence, overall stability and balanced development to the benefit of each other and the world at large.
The summit fully demonstrated China's efforts to promote coordination and positive interaction among major countries, as well as its sincerity in managing differences through dialogue and promoting peace and pursuing win-win outcomes through cooperation.
"The leaders of these three countries, which are among the most important players on the world stage and are permanent members of the UN Security Council, decided to use the multilateral setting of the APEC summit in Shenzhen to meet face to face, discuss their main concerns, engage in constructive dialogue on issues of common interest, reduce the risk of unintended conflicts arising from strategic miscalculations and curb tensions among the great powers. That in itself is an important diplomatic achievement," said a scholar quoted in an article on the website of Russia's TASS news agency.
The U.S. magazine Foreign Affairs observed that the meeting between the three leaders could pave the way for great-power cooperation across ideological lines and stabilize a fragmented world.
These international assessments echo China's position and underscore the timeliness and significance of the summit.
Viewed from a broad historical perspective, the summit carries even deeper significance for our times.
Major countries have always played a uniquely important role in maintaining global strategic stability, safeguarding regional peace, and promoting common development for humanity. Peace among major countries brings stability to the world, while rivalry and confrontation among them can only lead to chaos.
More than 80 years ago, China, Russia and the U.S., as key anti-fascist forces in the world, fought shoulder to shoulder and achieved great victories, winning peace and tranquility for the world.
The lessons of history remain relevant today: cooperation is the way to jointly withstand risks, while confrontation only breeds disaster.
The world today is far from peaceful, with turbulence and conflict intertwined. It is the call of our times and a mission entrusted by history for China, Russia and the U.S. to demonstrate a sense of responsibility, shoulder the responsibilities befitting major countries, and lead and advance multipolarity and greater democracy in international relations.
Putin said safeguarding the purposes and principles of the UN Charter and upholding international peace are their shared responsibilities, and they should adhere to the principles of sovereign equality and noninterference in internal affairs, jointly safeguard energy and food security, and strengthen international cooperation on AI governance.
Trump said that the summit is a historic event of great importance, drawing global attention. Noting that dialogue is essential to maintaining stability and achieving peace, Trump said all issues should be resolved through dialogue.
Some international commentators have simplistically labeled the summit as an example of "great-power condominium" or "Yalta 2.0."
Such interpretations essentially apply outdated notions of power politics and the division of spheres of influence to a new form of interaction among major countries in the new era. This misreading ignores both the profound changes in the international landscape and the widespread aspiration of countries around the world to participate in global affairs on an equal footing.
The trend toward a multipolar world is irreversible, and bloc confrontation offers no way forward. All parties should take a realistic approach, pave the way for multilateral cooperation, promote common development, and find the right way for major countries to get along in the new era.
Only by recognizing this can the true value of the summit be understood: it was not about a "deal" among major countries, but rather a new attempt by them to address differences through dialogue instead of confrontation.
As long as all parties work toward the same goal and continue to act on the consensus reached, the seeds sown at the trilateral summit will eventually take root, sprout, bloom, and bear fruit.
China stands ready to work with Russia and the U.S. to implement the consensus reached by the three leaders, continue to strengthen communication, meet global challenges, safeguard world peace and stability, and jointly make the world a better place.
