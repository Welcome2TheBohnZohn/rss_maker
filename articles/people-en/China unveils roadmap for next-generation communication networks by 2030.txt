China unveils roadmap for next-generation communication networks by 2030
 (Xinhua) 10:56, November 26, 2026
BEIJING, Nov. 26 (Xinhua) -- Chinese authorities on Thursday jointly released an implementation plan for the development of next-generation communication networks, eyeing to build such networks with comprehensive coverage and leading performance by 2030.
The plan aims to advance 6G network development at an appropriate time and basically complete the construction of a low-Earth-orbit satellite internet system by 2030.
It also aims to promote the large-scale development of integrated networks such as the Industrial Internet and the Internet of Vehicles, significantly improve smart development and further strengthen the industrial ecosystem.
