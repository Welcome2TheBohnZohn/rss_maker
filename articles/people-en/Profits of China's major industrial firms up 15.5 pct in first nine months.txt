Profits of China's major industrial firms up 15.5 pct in first nine months
 (Xinhua) 10:51, October 27, 2026
BEIJING, Oct. 27 (Xinhua) -- Profits of China's industrial firms above designated size increased 15.5 percent year on year during the first nine months of 2026, data from the National Bureau of Statistics showed on Tuesday.
