2026 World Robot Conference opens in Beijing, 311 new products unveiled
Children inspect a robot on display at the 2026 World Robot Conference on Aug. 19, 2026 in Beijing. (People's Daily Online/Weng Qiyu)
The 2026 World Robot Conference, themed "Human-Robot Symbiosis, Production-Demand Convergence," opened in the Beijing Economic-Technological Development Area (Yizhuang) on Aug. 19, 2026. Co-hosted by the Chinese Institute of Electronics and the World Robot Cooperation Organization, the conference invited 30 international supporting organizations and drew 373 exhibiting companies showcasing more than 3,000 innovative products, with over 17,000 contestants taking part in related competitions. A total of 311 new products were unveiled at the conference.
