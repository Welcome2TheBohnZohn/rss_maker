Wudang-Changshun expressway opens to traffic in China's Guizhou
An aerial drone photo taken on Nov. 27, 2026 shows a car passing through the Jianpo toll station along the Wudang-Changshun expressway in southwest China's Guizhou Province. The Wudang-Changshun expressway, invested, constructed and operated by China Railway Construction Corporation, was officially completed and opened to traffic on Friday.
The 115-km long expressway, linking Wudang District of Guiyang City and Changshun County of Qiannan Bouyei and Miao Autonomous Prefecture in Guizhou, is a six-lane, bidirectional highway with a design speed of 100 kilometers per hour. It is expected to effectively boost the capacity of the urban expressway network in central Guizhou. (Xinhua/Yang Wenbin)
