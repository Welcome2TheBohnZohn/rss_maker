Autumn colors transform landscape in Huma county, NE China's Heilongjiang Province
Photo shows a stunning autumn view of Huma county, nestled at the eastern foot of the Greater Khingan Mountains in northeast China's Heilongjiang Province.
Autumn sets the Greater Khingan Mountains ablaze with color, and Huma county — nestled at the eastern foot of the range in northeast China's Heilongjiang Province — offers spectacular views of its forests and waterways.
In Oupu township, the mountains are covered in vibrant autumn foliage. Red, gold, and green leaves form layers of color across the hills, a striking contrast against the deep green of the forest.
The clear, winding river that runs through the valleys reflects the colorful hills along its banks, bringing the autumn scenery of the mountains and water together.
As breezes move through the forest, the changing light and swaying leaves add movement to the vivid landscape.
