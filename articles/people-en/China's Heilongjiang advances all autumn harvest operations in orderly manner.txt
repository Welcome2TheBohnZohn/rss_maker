China's Heilongjiang advances all autumn harvest operations in orderly manner
An aerial drone photo taken on Sept. 8, 2026 shows a harvester working in a rice field of Youyi Farm Co., Ltd. under Beidahuang Group in Shuangyashan, northeast China's Heilongjiang Province. As the farm's 550,000 mu (about 36,667 hectares) of rice paddies in the hinterland of the Sanjiang Plain enter the harvest season, the Heilongjiang Reclamation Area is advancing all autumn harvest operations in an orderly manner. (Xinhua/Wang Song)
