China's import expo reports over 85 bln USD in intended deals
 (Xinhua) 15:54, November 10, 2026
SHANGHAI, Nov. 10 (Xinhua) -- The ninth China International Import Expo (CIIE) concluded in Shanghai on Tuesday, with one-year intended deals reaching a record high of 85.45 billion U.S. dollars, up 2.3 percent from the previous edition.
First held in 2018, the CIIE is the world's first national-level expo dedicated to imports.
