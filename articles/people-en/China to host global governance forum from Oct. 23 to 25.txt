China to host global governance forum from Oct. 23 to 25
 (Xinhua) 13:20, October 16, 2026
BEIJING, Oct. 16 (Xinhua) -- China will host the 2026 Global Governance Forum (Xiongan) from Oct. 23 to 25 in Xiongan New Area, north China's Hebei Province, according to the Chinese People's Institute of Foreign Affairs.
