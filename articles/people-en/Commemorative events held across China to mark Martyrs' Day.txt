Commemorative events held across China to mark Martyrs' Day
Students present flowers to martyrs at a martyrs' cemetery in Binzhou City, east China's Shandong Province, Sept. 30, 2026. (Photo by Jia Haining/Xinhua)
Commemorative events were held across China to mark Martyrs' Day in recent days. Observed on Sept. 30, one day before National Day, the day commemorates those who gave their lives for national independence and prosperity.
Students salute to a monument at a martyrs' cemetery in Binhai New Area of Tianjin, north China, Sept. 30, 2026. (Photo by Du Penghui/Xinhua)
Veterans salute to a monument for martyrs at a martyrs' cemetery in Suihua City, northeast China's Heilongjiang Province, Sept. 30, 2026. (Photo by Liu Huapeng/Xinhua)
Students clean martyrs' graves at a martyrs' cemetery in Lianyungang City, east China's Jiangsu Province, Sept. 29, 2026. (Photo by Wang Jianmin/Xinhua)
Students take part in a martyr commemorative event in Qionghai City, south China's Hainan Province, Sept. 30, 2026. (Xinhua/Guo Cheng)
Students take part in a commemorative event in front of a monument for martyrs in Haikou, south China's Hainan Province, Sept. 30, 2026. (Xinhua/Yang Guanyu)
Students sing the national anthem during a commemorative event at a martyrs' cemetery in Tengchong City, southwest China's Yunnan Province, Sept. 30, 2026. (Photo by Gong Zujin/Xinhua)
Students present flowers to martyrs' sculptures at Xishan National Forest Park in Beijing, capital of China, Sept. 30, 2026. (Xinhua/Yin Gang)
Students present flowers to martyrs at a martyrs' cemetery in Shangqiu City, central China's Henan Province, Sept. 30, 2026. (Photo by Li Heng/Xinhua)
