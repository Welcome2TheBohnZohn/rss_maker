Xi pays tribute to national heroes on Martyrs' Day
 (Xinhua) 10:18, September 30, 2026
		
		
BEIJING, Sept. 30 (Xinhua) -- Chinese President Xi Jinping and other Party and state leaders attended a ceremony to present flower baskets to fallen national heroes at Tian'anmen Square in central Beijing on Wednesday morning.
The event was held to mark Martyrs' Day.
