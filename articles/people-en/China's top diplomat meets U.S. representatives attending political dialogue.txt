China's top diplomat meets U.S. representatives attending political dialogue
Wang Yi, a member of the Political Bureau of the Communist Party of China Central Committee and director of the Office of the Central Commission for Foreign Affairs, meets with U.S. representatives attending the U.S.-China Political Dialogue 2026 in Beijing, capital of China, Nov. 13, 2026. (Xinhua/Tian Yu)
BEIJING, Nov. 13 (Xinhua) -- China's top diplomat Wang Yi on Friday met with U.S. representatives attending the U.S.-China Political Dialogue 2026 in Beijing.
Wang, a member of the Political Bureau of the Communist Party of China (CPC) Central Committee and director of the Office of the Central Commission for Foreign Affairs, said that China and the United States should clarify the goals of their joint efforts, expand the areas of mutually beneficial cooperation, consolidate the foundation of bilateral relations, and be committed to maintaining world peace and stability.
The two sides should uphold mutual respect, peaceful coexistence and win-win cooperation, and implement the consensus reached by the two heads of state on building a constructive relationship of strategic stability on the basis of respect, fairness and reciprocity, Wang noted.
The representative of the U.S. side said that people-to-people and cultural exchanges, especially those between young people and students, are conducive to enhancing friendship and mutual trust between the two peoples, adding that they are grateful for the invitation for 100,000 young Americans to visit China for exchanges and study in the coming five years, and are willing to make efforts to promote the healthy and stable development of U.S.-China relations.
