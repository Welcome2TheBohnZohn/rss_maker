China adds Kyrgyzstan, Vietnam to 240-hour visa-free transit program
 (Xinhua) 10:21, August 20, 2026
		
		
BEIJING, Aug. 20 (Xinhua) -- China has added Kyrgyzstan and Vietnam to its 240-hour visa-free transit program, bringing the total number of countries eligible for the policy to 57, immigration authorities announced on Thursday.
