Cangfang Town in China's Henan enters citrus harvest season
Farmers load harvested citrus fruits onto a truck in Cangfang Town of Xichuan County, central China's Henan Province, Nov. 18, 2026. Cangfang Town of Xichuan County has entered its citrus harvest season. Leveraging its soil, water and climate advantages, the town has gradually developed large-scale citrus cultivation. The citrus planting area in Cangfang Town has exceeded 20,000 mu (about 1,333.33 hectares), making citrus one of the town's signature pillar industries. (Photo by Kong Weijia/Xinhua)
