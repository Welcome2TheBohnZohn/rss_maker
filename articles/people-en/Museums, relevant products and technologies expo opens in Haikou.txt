Museums, relevant products and technologies expo opens in Haikou
An exhibit is displayed at the 11th Chinese Museums and Relevant Products and Technologies Exposition in Haikou, south China's Hainan Province, Nov. 13, 2026. (Xinhua/Pu Xiaoxu)
HAIKOU, Nov. 13 (Xinhua) -- The 11th Chinese Museums and Relevant Products and Technologies Exposition, with the participation of over 600 museums and some 200 cultural and museum-related enterprises, opened in Haikou on Friday.
