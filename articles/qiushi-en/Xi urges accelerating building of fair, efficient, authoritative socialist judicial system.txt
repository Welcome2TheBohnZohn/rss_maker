Xi urges accelerating building of fair, efficient, authoritative socialist judicial system
BEIJING -- Chinese President Xi Jinping has urged efforts to speed up the building of a fair, efficient and authoritative socialist judicial system and safeguard high-quality development and high-level security with high-quality judicial services.
Xi, also general secretary of the Communist Party of China (CPC) Central Committee and chairman of the Central Military Commission, made the remarks in a recent instruction on the work related to the people's courts.
People's courts at all levels have provided a strong judicial guarantee for reform, development and stability since the 18th National Congress of the CPC in 2012, Xi said.
He called for efforts to deepen the reform of the judicial system and regulate the exercise of judicial power.
In the process, Xi stressed the need to fully implement the thought on socialist rule of law with Chinese characteristics for the new era, and to adhere to the leadership of the CPC, a people-centered approach, the path of socialist rule of law with Chinese characteristics, and the independent and impartial exercise of adjudicatory power in accordance with the law.
Efforts should be made to build strong and capable teams of loyal, upright and responsible personnel, Xi said, urging new and greater contributions to advancing the Peaceful China Initiative and the rule of law to a higher level, and to advancing Chinese modernization.
Xi's instruction was conveyed at a national conference on the work of the people's courts held in Beijing on Monday and Tuesday.
Chen Wenqing, a member of the Political Bureau of the CPC Central Committee and head of the Commission for Political and Legal Affairs of the CPC Central Committee, attended and addressed the event.
Chen said each stage of judicial services, from case filing and trials to enforcement and litigation-related petitions, should follow the principle of serving the people to safeguard fairness and justice.
The conference was presided over by Zhang Jun, president of the Supreme People's Court.
