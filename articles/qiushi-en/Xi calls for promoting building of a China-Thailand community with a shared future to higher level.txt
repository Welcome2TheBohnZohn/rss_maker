SHENZHEN -- Chinese President Xi Jinping on Friday called for promoting the building of a China-Thailand community with a shared future to a higher level.
China and Thailand should continuously enhance political mutual trust and strengthen solidarity and coordination, Xi said when meeting with Thai Prime Minister Anutin Charnvirakul in Shenzhen, south China's Guangdong Province.
The two countries should step up exchanges between governments, political parties, legislatures and at other levels, and safeguard their common strategic interests, Xi stressed.
He called on the two sides to make good use of cooperation mechanisms in economy and trade, science and technology, and the digital economy, expand and strengthen traditional cooperation in agriculture and connectivity, and cultivate and strengthen new growth areas of cooperation such as artificial intelligence, new energy vehicles, aviation and aerospace, disaster prevention, mitigation and relief, and water resources management.
The two sides should strengthen cooperation in culture, education, and tourism, Xi added.
He also urged intensifying efforts to crack down on illegal and criminal activities such as online gambling and telecom fraud.
The two sides should strengthen multilateral coordination and work together to safeguard the common interests of the Global South, Xi said.
China is ready to play a positive role in bringing about an early turnaround in Thailand-Cambodia relations, Xi said.
For his part, Anutin said Thailand is ready to continue to strengthen high-level exchanges with China and enhance cooperation in areas such as trade, investment, science and technology, industrial and supply chains, and connectivity.
Thailand will resolutely crack down on cross-border crimes including online gambling and telecom fraud, and protect the safety of Chinese citizens in Thailand, he said, adding that the country welcomes more Chinese visitors.
Anutin attended the 33rd APEC Economic Leaders' Meeting in Shenzhen, held from Wednesday to Thursday.
