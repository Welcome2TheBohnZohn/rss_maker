Xi urges advancing building of Party regulations, institutions
BEIJING -- Chinese President Xi Jinping has urged continuous and high-quality advancement in the building of regulations and institutions of the Communist Party of China (CPC), and upholding rule-based governance as the basic approach to governing the Party.
Xi, also general secretary of the CPC Central Committee and chairman of the Central Military Commission, made the remarks in a recent instruction on the work related to Party regulations and institutions.
Xi said the CPC has made solid efforts and achieved notable achievements in strengthening Party regulations and institutions over the past five years.
To provide institutional guarantees for persevering with full and rigorous Party self-governance and ensuring the Party remains the strong leadership core in building socialism with Chinese characteristics, Xi urged enhanced efforts in the systematic integration, education and enforcement of Party institutions, as well as in improving the alignment and coordination of Party rules and regulations with state laws and regulations.
Xi's instruction was conveyed at a national meeting on the work related to Party regulations held in Beijing on Tuesday and Wednesday.
