TITLE:
APEC media center opens, offers reporters tech tools

ARTICLE TEXT:
The media center for the 2026 APEC Economic Leaders' Week opened on Wednesday, offering nearly 5,000 Chinese and foreign journalists reporting facilities, artificial intelligence-powered production tools and cultural experiences.
Set up at the Shenzhen Convention and Exhibition Center in Shenzhen, Guangdong province, the facility is spread over 15,000 square meters and is divided into functional zones, providing services such as free internet access, media workstations and technical support for reporters covering the meetings. It also features news conference halls and an international broadcasting center.
The 33rd Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation Economic Leaders' Meeting is scheduled for Nov 18 and 19 in the southern Chinese tech hub.
A key feature of the media center is its digital intelligence integration. It is home to the nation's first Harmony-OS smart studio, which is equipped with XR, or extended reality, virtual recording technology and 4K ultrahigh-definition signal transmission capabilities. Intelligent editing tools are also available to help journalists quickly edit and produce content in multiple languages, significantly enhancing publication efficiency.
Technology has also been integrated into hospitality services. At the Journalists' Home, robots prepare coffee and serve ice cream, while delivery robots bring drinks and snacks.
During the APEC Economic Leaders' Week, which will be held from Friday to Nov 19 in Shenzhen, the media center will host a variety of events, including official news briefings and city tours.
