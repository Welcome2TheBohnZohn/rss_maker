TITLE:
Talks begin for economic partnership pact with Indonesia

PUBLISHED:
2026-11-21

SOURCE:
China Daily English

CATEGORY:
Authoritative PRC Media

URL:
https://www.chinadaily.com.cn/a/202611/21/WS6b00e44fe4b03ebf8519dccc.html

ARTICLE TEXT:
President Xi Jinping and Indonesian President Prabowo Subianto announced on Friday the start of negotiations to reach a bilateral comprehensive economic partnership agreement.
During their meeting, Xi said the two countries should strengthen strategic communication and solidarity, deepen all-round strategic cooperation, consolidate the foundation of political trust and jointly guard against and respond to external risks.
He called on the two countries to expand mutually beneficial cooperation, advance major projects such as the Jakarta-Bandung High-Speed Railway, and deepen cooperation in energy and mineral resources, artificial intelligence, the digital economy and clean energy.
He also called for efforts to actively build a blue partnership and reach an all-round, high-standard comprehensive economic partnership agreement at an early date.
China is willing to strengthen coordination and cooperation with Indonesia within multilateral frameworks, including the United Nations, Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation and China-ASEAN, Xi said.
Prabowo noted that he and Xi have met four times in recent years to push for significant progress in bilateral relations, and stressed that the strategic partnership between Indonesia and China is stronger than ever before.
Indonesia firmly adheres to the one-China policy and recognizes Taiwan as a province of the People's Republic of China, Prabowo said.
He thanked China for its longstanding support for Indonesia's economic and social development, adding that his country is willing to continuously deepen cooperation with China.
He said Indonesia welcomes Chinese enterprises to invest and do business, and vowed to provide a reliable, stable and predictable business environment.
