Chinese travelers in France urged to stay vigilant
China's Foreign Ministry and its diplomatic missions in France have renewed their safety advisory for Chinese citizens traveling to the country following large-scale demonstrations and violent clashes in several French cities.
Mass protests have recently erupted in dozens of major and medium-sized cities across France, including Paris, Marseille and Lyon, with incidents of violence and clashes between protesters and police.
The ministry and Chinese diplomatic missions in France are urging Chinese citizens who are planning to visit the country to remain vigilant, closely monitor local developments and take necessary safety precautions.
Chinese citizens are also advised to avoid areas where demonstrations are taking place and to contact local police and Chinese diplomatic missions for assistance in the event of an emergency.
