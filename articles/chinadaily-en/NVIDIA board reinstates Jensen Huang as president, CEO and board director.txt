TITLE:
NVIDIA board reinstates Jensen Huang as president, CEO and board director

ARTICLE TEXT:
SAN FRANCISCO -- US chipmaker NVIDIA announced Monday that Jensen Huang has been reinstated as the company's president and chief executive officer (CEO), as well as a member of its board of directors, with immediate effect.
In a statement, Nvidia said its board voted unanimously to reinstate Huang following a series of discussions and meetings with him. The board said it was satisfied with Huang's candor and ability to lead the company.
The board also said it had received input from senior levels of the US government and, based on that input, was confident that Huang's reinstatement posed no national security risk.
"The Board believes Jensen is the right person to lead NVIDIA, and that his reinstatement is in the best interests of our stockholders, employees, partners and customers," said Harvey C. Jones, chair of the board.
"We have every confidence in Jensen and look forward to working with him as he resumes his leadership of the company," he said.
"I am honored to return to NVIDIA and lead one of the most consequential technology companies in the world at the beginning of a new industrial revolution," Huang said, cited by the statement.
Huang stepped down on Nov 12 amid mounting pressure over his extramarital affairs. Chief Financial Officer (CFO) Colette Kress was appointed as interim CEO. Under the latest decision, Kress will continue to serve as CFO, while Jones will remain board chairman.
"The NVIDIA Board of Directors has made the correct and proper decision to return Jensen Huang to the position of CEO," US President Donald Trump said on Monday following NVIDIA's announcement.
