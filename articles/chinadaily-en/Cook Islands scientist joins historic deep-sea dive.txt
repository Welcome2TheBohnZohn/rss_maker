TITLE:
Cook Islands scientist joins historic deep-sea dive

PUBLISHED:
2026-11-24

SOURCE:
China Daily English

CATEGORY:
Authoritative PRC Media

ARTICLE TEXT:
A scientist from the Cook Islands has become the first person from her country to directly observe the deep-sea seabed within its exclusive economic zone, joining a dive aboard China's manned submersible Jiaolong in the Manihiki fracture zone on Nov 21 local time, the Ministry of Natural Resources said on Tuesday.
Chloe Wragg joined Chinese scientists Yang Yifan and Zhao Shengya aboard the submersible to conduct an environmental survey. The mission successfully achieved its objectives, collecting valuable deep-sea environmental data and samples.
The successful dive highlights the blue partnership between China and the Cook Islands and further strengthens their cooperation in deep-sea science and technology, the ministry said.
Following the dive, Wragg expressed her excitement at witnessing the milestone firsthand. She said the Cook Islands has long been at the forefront of deep-sea scientific research and that the dive demonstrated the country's determination and commitment to deepening its understanding of its own seabed.
She also expressed her gratitude for the opportunity to work with colleagues from the National Deep Sea Center and the Second Institute of Oceanography, both under the Ministry of Natural Resources.
Wragg said the expedition reflected the longstanding friendship and scientific ties between the two countries, as well as their close relations and extensive exchanges.
The expedition marks the second joint marine scientific mission between China and the Cook Islands, following their first such mission in 2025.
During the current expedition, Jiaolong is scheduled to conduct five dives in the Cook Islands' exclusive economic zone. The mission aims to help the country fill gaps in its basic deep-sea data and train its first group of local researchers in crewed submersible operations through hands-on experience.
