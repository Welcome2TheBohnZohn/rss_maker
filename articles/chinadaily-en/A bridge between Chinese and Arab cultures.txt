      With ties between China and Arab countries growing ever closer, language is playing an increasingly important role in connecting people and creating new opportunities. In Shishi, Fujian province, a free Arabic language program is helping Chinese merchants overcome language barriers and expand their reach into Arab markets. Across the Arab world, growing access to Chinese language education is opening new pathways to education, employment and deeper cultural exchange. Language is building bridges, turning dialogue into understanding and connections into lasting friendships.
