China's major stock indexes surge, chip shares rally

ARTICLE TEXT:
BEIJING -- China's major stock indexes surged on Friday, with strong gains in sectors across the semiconductor supply chain.
The benchmark Shanghai Composite Index reclaimed the 3,600-point mark, closing 3.73 percent higher to end the day at 3,606.49 points.
The Shenzhen Component Index closed 7.24 percent higher at 10,814.02 points.
The ChiNext Index, tracking China's Nasdaq-style board of growth enterprises, surged 11.61 percent, while the STAR Composite Index, which reflects the performance of stocks on China's sci-tech innovation board, jumped 14.21 percent.
Semiconductor and electronics stocks led the rally, alongside electronic chemicals makers and stocks linked to China's national semiconductor fund. Shares tied to co-packaged optics (CPO) and high-bandwidth memory (HBM) also posted strong gains, while banking shares were among the laggards.
A State Council executive meeting on Friday stressed that the capital market is vital to overall economic development, calling for efforts to strengthen counter-cyclical adjustments and tackle critical reform tasks at the same time.
The meeting also pointed to the need to improve market-stabilizing mechanisms, give full play to existing policies, and mull and introduce new measures to promote steady and sound development of the capital market.
According to the meeting, efforts should be made to encourage listed companies to pay more dividends and step up share repurchases, give greater play to state-backed funds in steadying the market, and optimize the quasi-"stabilization fund" function of the state-owned Central Huijin Investment Ltd. to provide sufficient liquidity.
The meeting also underlined the need to improve policies to support and guide medium- and long-term funds such as insurance funds, enterprise annuities, and social security funds into the market, and strengthen the capacity to guard against and respond to imported risks.
