TITLE:
Stronger mutual trust with Australia emphasized

ARTICLE TEXT:
President Xi Jinping said on Friday that China and Australia should be cooperative partners who trust each other and help each other succeed, as the two countries have no historical grievances or fundamental conflicts of interests.
He made the remarks when meeting with Australian Prime Minister Anthony Albanese in Shenzhen, Guangdong province, where they attended the recently concluded 33rd Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation Economic Leaders' Meeting.
Noting that China-Australia relations are stable at present, Xi said that both countries should take the occasion of the 55th anniversary of diplomatic relations next year as an opportunity to promote greater development of bilateral ties in the spirit of equality, seek common ground while reserving differences, and pursue mutually beneficial cooperation.
Amid a complex international landscape, the two countries should enhance mutual trust, view each other rationally, accommodate each other's core interests and major concerns, and keep to the direction of friendship and cooperation, he said.
Xi called for further advancing cooperation in traditional sectors, including agriculture, energy and minerals, and enhancing partnership in emerging fields such as the green economy, new energy, digital industry, artificial intelligence and climate response, in order to jointly share opportunities and pursue common development.
Economic and trade cooperation remains the ballast of China-Australia relations. China has been Australia's largest trading partner, source of imports and export market for 17 consecutive years. The two countries have had a long and highly productive partnership in iron ore and steel.
According to the General Administration of Customs, bilateral trade reached approximately $206.58 billion in 2025, with China's exports to Australia worth $76.33 billion, a year-on-year increase of 7.9 percent. In the first eight months of 2026, the total trade in goods between China and Australia was worth $176.9 billion, up 34.8 percent year-on-year.
While emphasizing the importance of promoting the countries' friendship by expanding cultural and people-to-people exchanges, Xi said that China and Australia should strengthen multilateral coordination to address global challenges.
The two countries should advance the building of the Asia-Pacific community, and jointly safeguard the international system with the United Nations at its core and the international order underpinned by international law, he said.
Albanese said the improvement and development of Australia-China relations serve the interests of both sides, and have brought tangible benefits to the two peoples.
He reiterated Australia's commitment to the one-China policy, saying that his country is willing to maintain close high-level exchanges with China, strengthen practical cooperation in trade, investment, AI and other fields, and deepen cultural and people-to-people exchanges.
Australia is ready to work with China on mutual respect and proper handling of differences, so that their relationship can become a model of constructive ties between countries with different systems, he said.
Albanese congratulated China on successfully hosting the APEC Economic Leaders' meeting, saying that in the current turbulent world, China serves as an anchor of stability. He also congratulated China on hosting the China-Russia-US summit, which he said is of great importance to global and regional peace and stability.
Following his meeting with Xi, Albanese flew to Beijing to attend the 11th China-Australia Annual Leaders' Meeting with Premier Li Qiang.
During that meeting, Li said China is willing to work with Australia to expand the scale of bilateral trade, optimize its structure, and further enhance trade and investment liberalization and facilitation to achieve more tangible outcomes.
A statement on joint outcomes of the meeting was issued on Friday. The two countries agreed to designate 2027 as the China-Australia Year of Tourism.
This is the third visit to China made by Albanese since he first took office as Australia's prime minister in May 2022.
On Friday, Albanese also met in Beijing with Zhao Leji, chairman of the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress.
