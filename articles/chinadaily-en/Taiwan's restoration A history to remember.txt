Never to be undermined: Remembering Taiwan's return to China
        Share - WeChat
        
        
      
Oct 25 marks the Commemoration Day of Taiwan's Restoration. Eighty-one years ago, Taiwan returned to China after half a century of Japanese colonial rule. "The restoration of Taiwan was achieved through the sacrifices of the Chinese people," Yang Yizhou, vice-chairman of the All-China Federation of Taiwan Compatriots, said. "We will never allow this outcome to be taken away from anyone else, nor be undermined."
