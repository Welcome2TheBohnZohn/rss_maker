At the 2026 Global Mayors Dialogue · Ningbo, mayors from New Zealand, Lebanon and Mauritius shared their impressions of the city and reflected on the importance of friendly exchanges and mutual learning between cities. From cultural exchanges to youth engagement, cities are strengthening ties through open dialogue, deepening understanding and moving forward together.
