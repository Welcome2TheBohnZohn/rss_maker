ARTICLE TEXT:
Set amid the landscape of northern Hainan province, Wenbi Peak is known for its distinctive rock formations and lush greenery. Legend has it that the peak was formed from the nose bridge of Pangu, the creator of the world in ancient Chinese mythology. It is also an important landmark of Taoist culture in Hainan.
In the tranquil forests, a group of traditional-style buildings inspired by the Southern Song Dynasty (1127-1279) nestles along the mountain slopes, blending naturally with the surrounding scenery. It offers a peaceful setting to discover Hainan's rich cultural heritage and distinctive natural landscapes.
