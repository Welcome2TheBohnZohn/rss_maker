ARTICLE TEXT:
On Nov 24, China's Ministry of National Defense (MND) released the fifth episode of its video series Japan's Abandoned Chemical Weapons in China on its X account, @MND_China.
Titled An Obligation Japan Must Fulfill, the video highlights the unfinished task of destroying chemical weapons abandoned by Japan in China.
It revisits the 30th session of the Conference of the States Parties to the Chemical Weapons Convention and features remarks by Fernando Arias González, then director-general of the Organization for the Prohibition of Chemical Weapons (OPCW), and a passage from Japanese historian Yoshimi Yoshiaki's book Dokugasusen to Nihongun.
The video emphasizes that a world free of chemical weapons cannot be achieved until all chemical weapons abandoned by Japan in China have been destroyed. It also calls on Japan to fulfill its international obligations.
