TITLE:
Pakistani astronaut to become first foreign visitor to China's Tiangong space station

PUBLISHED:
2026-11-24

SOURCE:
China Daily English

CATEGORY:
Authoritative PRC Media

URL:

ARTICLE TEXT:
China's Tiangong space station is set to welcome its first foreign astronaut — Pakistani astronaut Muhammad Zeeshan Ali, who will also become the first Pakistani citizen to travel beyond Earth's atmosphere.
Ali, a wing commander in the Pakistan Air Force, is scheduled to fly to the orbital outpost with two Chinese astronauts — Major General Tang Hongbo, commander of the Shenzhou XXIV mission, and Colonel Wu Yi, a spaceflight engineer. Their spacecraft is scheduled to lift off aboard a Long March 2F rocket at 8:18 am on Wednesday from the Jiuquan Satellite Launch Center in northwestern China, according to Zhang Jingbo, a senior engineer and spokesman for the China Manned Space Agency.
At a news conference on Tuesday morning at the Jiuquan spaceport, Zhang said propellant loading was scheduled to begin at the launch tower later that day and that other prelaunch preparations were proceeding in an orderly manner.
