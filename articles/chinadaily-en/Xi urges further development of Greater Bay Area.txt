TITLE:
Xi urges further development of Greater Bay Area

ARTICLE TEXT:
President Xi Jinping has called for efforts to break new ground in the high-quality development of the Guangdong-Hong Kong-Macao Greater Bay Area, highlighting the important mission of Guangdong province in effectively implementing the national strategy on promoting the region's integrated development.
Xi, who is also general secretary of the Communist Party of China Central Committee and chairman of the Central Military Commission, made the remarks on Monday during an inspection tour of Zhongshan, Guangdong province.
The trip was Xi's first domestic inspection tour after the fifth plenary session of the 20th CPC Central Committee, a key Party meeting, was held in late October. He underlined the need to fully and faithfully implement the decisions and plans made at the session and better promote the integrated development of the east and west banks of the Pearl River Estuary.
Xi inspected the Shenzhen-Zhongshan Link, a mega cross-sea passage in South China that features two bridges, two artificial islands and an underwater tunnel.
Spanning 24 kilometers, the link connects Zhongshan and the technology hub of Shenzhen — which lie on opposite sides of the Pearl River Estuary — slashing travel time from around two hours to about 30 minutes.
At the west artificial island of the link, Xi learned about the construction and operation of the large-scale transportation project in the Greater Bay Area, another such project following the Hong Kong-Zhuhai-Macao Bridge, which is approximately 31 km south of the link.
He said that to develop the Greater Bay Area, the first priority is to promote transportation integration.
Noting that the Shenzhen-Zhongshan Link has played a pioneering role in promoting the integrated development of the east and west banks of the Pearl River Estuary, he emphasized the importance of managing and operating this project well to better serve the development of the Greater Bay Area.
The Greater Bay Area, which encompasses the Hong Kong and Macao special administrative regions and nine cities in Guangdong, covers 56,000 square km. It achieved a total economic output of over 15 trillion yuan ($2.25 trillion) in 2025. China aims to develop the area into a world-class city cluster, a global technology and innovation center and a livable business-friendly location.
While visiting the Future Technology Center of the Bay Area in Zhongshan's Cuiheng new district, Xi observed the development achievements of the city in modern industries such as biomedicine, electronic information and high-end equipment, as well as the display of traditional industrial products such as furniture.
He learned about local efforts in undertaking industrial transfers, promoting the transformation and upgrading of traditional industries and developing emerging industries, while inquiring about the performance and market prospects of innovative products.
Xi emphasized that building the Greater Bay Area is a major strategic plan of the CPC Central Committee. Guangdong shoulders an important mission, and cities on both sides of the Pearl River Estuary, including Guangzhou, Shenzhen, Zhuhai, Dongguan and Zhongshan, should play a leading role, he said.
Xi expressed the hope that Zhongshan will seize the opportunities, take reform and innovation as the fundamental driving force, improve regional cooperation mechanisms, foster a favorable business environment, and make great efforts to promote the deep integration of technological innovation and industrial innovation for new progress in high-quality development.
Speaking to representatives of small and medium-sized enterprises and sci-tech personnel, Xi encouraged them to make greater contributions to the development of Guangdong and the Greater Bay Area and China's greater self-reliance and strength in science and technology.
This year marks the 160th anniversary of Sun Yat-sen's birth. During the inspection tour, Xi also visited the Museum of Dr. Sun Yat-sen in Cuiheng village, where Sun was born.
He said that Sun made immortal contributions to national independence, social progress and people's well-being.
He called for emulating, inheriting and carrying forward Sun's legacy to inspire all Chinese people at home and abroad to unite and strive to build a great country and realize national rejuvenation through Chinese modernization.
