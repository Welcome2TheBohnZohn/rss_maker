A Memory in Black and White
Pandas, people and five decades of shared moments

      Giant pandas from China and generations of people in the United States have shared memories spanning more than five decades. A first arrival, a child's eager gaze, a cub taking its first steps, a farewell and a new beginning — these photographs capture more than fleeting moments. They show how a simple encounter with a panda can become a memory carried from one generation to the next.
