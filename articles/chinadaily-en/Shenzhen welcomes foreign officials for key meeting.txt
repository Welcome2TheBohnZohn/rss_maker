TITLE:
Shenzhen welcomes foreign officials for key meeting

ARTICLE TEXT:
Mexican President Claudia Sheinbaum Pardo arrived in Shenzhen, in South China's Guangdong province, on Monday, marking the start of a series of arrivals by leaders of the economies for the 2026 APEC Economic Leaders' Meeting.
This is Sheinbaum's first trip to China since assuming office in 2024, with four cabinet ministers accompanying her.
Sheinbaum is also scheduled to hold talks with President Xi Jinping during her stay in the city, focusing on technology, renewable energy, environmental cooperation, and scientific development.
It is reported that the objectives of her visit are to deepen economic, trade and industrial cooperation with China and lay the groundwork for hosting APEC meetings in 2028.
Papua New Guinean Prime Minister James Marape and Canadian Prime Minister Mark Carney also arrived in Shenzhen on Monday. More leaders from APEC economies are expected to arrive in the following days ahead of the meeting.
Wang Junsheng, a researcher at the Chinese Academy of Social Sciences' National Institute of International Strategy, said that the enthusiasm among APEC member economies for this year's meeting reflects a shared desire for development and closer regional cooperation, particularly at a time of mounting economic uncertainty and rising trade protectionism.
Economic ties among the 21 APEC members are deeply intertwined, Wang said, with China serving as the largest trading partner for 13 of them.
In the first three quarters of 2026, eight of China's top 10 trading partners were APEC economies.
Given that the Asia-Pacific accounts for more than 60 percent of global GDP, closer economic cooperation and regional integration are crucial not only to the development of individual economies but also to regional and global prosperity, Wang said.
China has long been a strong advocate and active practitioner of openness and cooperation, Wang noted.
Restrictions removed
In November 2024, China removed all market access restrictions on foreign investment in the manufacturing sector, further opening its market and promoting deeper integration between domestic and international markets.
The move has created greater opportunities for foreign businesses to invest in China, expand cooperation and benefit from the country's development, Wang said.
The strong interest in this year's meeting also reflects high expectations for China as the host economy, Wang said, adding that many APEC members are looking to China to play a greater role in promoting free trade, advancing regional economic integration and creating new opportunities for shared growth.
