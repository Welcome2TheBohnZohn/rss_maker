ARTICLE TEXT:
Nestled in the southern reaches of the Taihang Mountains, Yuntai Mountain is renowned for its mountain scenery, water landscapes, and spectacular geological formations. Sheer cliffs face each other across deep gorges, while waterfalls, springs, streams, and pools weave through the landscape, with red rock contrasting with clear green-blue waters. Among the scenic area's highlights, Red Stone Gorge stands out for its magnificent Danxia landforms.
In autumn, a climb up Yuntai Mountain offers sweeping views of the Taihang peaks, with forests taking on rich seasonal hues and the landscape revealing a fresh beauty.
