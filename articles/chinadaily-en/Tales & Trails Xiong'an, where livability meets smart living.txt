From a plot of land to a blueprint, and from a blueprint to a thriving city, Xiong'an New Area in Hebei province is turning its vision into reality.
A city designed for better living: parks and green spaces are becoming part of everyday life, from Chunfang Garden to the Yannan Embankment along Baiyang Lake.
A city built for the future: smart mobility is making daily life more convenient, while research and innovation across Beijing, Tianjin, and Hebei are creating new opportunities for growth.
Join China Daily host Erik Nilsson to explore Xiong'an and experience the comfort and convenience of a city shaping the future.
