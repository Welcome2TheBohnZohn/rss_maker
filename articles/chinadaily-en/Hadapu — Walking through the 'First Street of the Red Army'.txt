Step into Hadapu and walk along its flagstone streets, where old buildings and courtyards preserve traces of the past. Known as the "First Street of the Chinese Workers' and Peasants' Red Army", the historic street offers a glimpse into the lives of Red Army soldiers during the Long March. Today, local specialties such as "Red Army pastry" offer another way to experience its history. Walk along the flagstone streets of Hadapu and retrace the footsteps of the Red Army.
