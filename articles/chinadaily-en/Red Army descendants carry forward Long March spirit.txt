More than 90 years ago, the main force of the Central Red Army left Ruijin in Jiangxi province to start the Long March, an epic 12,500-kilometer trek. The smoke of war has long faded, but stories of faith, sacrifice and determination live on. Descendants of Red Army veterans carry their ancestors' spiritual torch, passing down memories of the Long March across generations.
