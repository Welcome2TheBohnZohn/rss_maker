China claims women's 3,000m relay gold in Seoul

ARTICLE TEXT:
SEOUL -- China won the women's 3,000m relay at the Seoul stop of the 2026-2027 ISU Short Track World Tour on Saturday, claiming its first gold of the season on the circuit.
Zhang Chutong put China in front at the start, and the team led through the first half of the race. Canada overtook China during a relay exchange but fell in the closing laps, allowing China to take the title in 4 minutes, 12.679 seconds. South Korea finished second, and the Individual Neutral Athletes (AIN2) team took third.
