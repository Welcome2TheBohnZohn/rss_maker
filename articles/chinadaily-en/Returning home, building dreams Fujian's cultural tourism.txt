      On Fujian's coast, some return from afar while others cross mountains and seas to put down roots. Local resident Zheng Xianshi preserves the warmth of his hometown through a family-style homestay, while Wang Fayu, a Taiwan compatriot, relocated his entire family to Pingtan, bringing with him authentic Taiwan flavors that he shares with the community. Together, their choices paint a harmonious picture of Fujian's cultural tourism industry.
