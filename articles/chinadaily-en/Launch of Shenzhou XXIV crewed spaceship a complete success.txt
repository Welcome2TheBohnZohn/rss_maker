TITLE:
Launch of Shenzhou XXIV crewed spaceship a complete success

PUBLISHED:

SOURCE:

CATEGORY:
Authoritative PRC Media

URL:

ARTICLE TEXT:
China's Shenzhou XXIV crew is on its way to the Tiangong space station. A Long March 2F rocket lifted off from the Jiuquan Satellite Launch Center at 8:18 am Beijing time on Nov 25, sending the spacecraft into orbit. On board are Chinese astronauts Tang Hongbo and Wu Yi and Pakistani astronaut Muhammad Zeeshan Ali, who will become the first foreign astronaut to visit Tiangong.
