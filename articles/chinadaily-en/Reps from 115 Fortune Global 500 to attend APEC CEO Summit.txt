Reps from 115 Fortune Global 500 to attend APEC CEO Summit

ARTICLE TEXT:
SHENZHEN -- Representatives from 115 Fortune Global 500 companies will attend the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation CEO Summit 2026, the China Council for the Promotion of International Trade said Sunday.
US companies make up the largest group among participating companies from economies other than China, accounting for nearly 20 percent of the total, said Li Qingshuang, vice-chairperson of the CCPIT, at a news conference.
Heads of several international organizations, as well as leaders of business associations from the United States, Russia, Japan and other economies, will also attend the summit, Li said.
