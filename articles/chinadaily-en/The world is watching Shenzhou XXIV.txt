Pakistan's Muhammad Zeeshan Ali has become the first foreign astronaut aboard China's Tiangong space station, where he will carry out experiments developed by scientists in Pakistan.
The joint mission marks a milestone in China-Pakistan space cooperation and reflects China's openness to working with partners around the world on space exploration.
