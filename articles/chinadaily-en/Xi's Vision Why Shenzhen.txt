In December 2012, Xi Jinping chose Shenzhen for his first local inspection tour as China's top leader, reaffirming the country's commitment to reform and opening-up, and calling for bold exploration and innovation.
Today, Shenzhen attracts entrepreneurs, engineers and scientists from around the world. Why Shenzhen? China Daily host Dylan Walker visits the city to hear the stories of the people who helped build it, and entrepreneurs from around the world, exploring what continues to drive Shenzhen's remarkable capacity for innovation and growth.
