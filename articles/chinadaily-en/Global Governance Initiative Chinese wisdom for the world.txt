      To commemorate the first anniversary of the Global Governance Initiative, Mushahid Hussain Sayed, chairman of the Pakistan-China Institute, shared his insights with China Daily on how the Global Governance Initiative, which upholds multilateralism, sovereign equality and people-centered development, is resonating with the shared aspirations of the Global South and garnering broad international recognition.
"The Global Governance Initiative provides a pathway to development, pathway to moving forward, and pathway to building a better, more equitable and a world based on rule of law," Sayed remarked.
