Why did representatives from 115 Fortune Global 500 companies come to Shenzhen?
They were among the business leaders gathered for the APEC CEO Summit on Nov 17-18, and one big question was where to find new opportunities.
China is already a major market, but as I heard at the summit, global companies are now looking beyond sales to China's technology, talent and innovation.
