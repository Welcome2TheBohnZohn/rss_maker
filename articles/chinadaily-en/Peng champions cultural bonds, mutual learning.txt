Peng champions cultural bonds, mutual learning

ARTICLE TEXT:
China is willing to enhance cultural exchanges and mutual learning with the rest of the world, Peng Liyuan, wife of President Xi Jinping, said on Thursday while experiencing a cultural showcase with the spouses of leaders of Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation economies in Shenzhen, Guangdong province.
Peng warmly greeted the guests at the Shenzhen Bay Culture Square, and they watched festive and vibrant Xingshi and Yingge dance performances presented by a group of children.
Xingshi, which literally means awakening the lion, is a southern-style lion dance, while Yingge is an artful fusion of dance, drama and martial arts that has long been popular in Guangdong's Chaoshan region, comprising the cities of Shantou, Chaozhou and Jieyang. Both dances are listed as China's national intangible cultural heritage.
Peng and the guests also toured an exhibition showcasing efforts to preserve and pass on local intangible cultural heritage. They viewed with interest exquisite handicraft items, enjoyed an artistic performance featuring Lingnan culture, and experienced a digital presentation of local arcade streets.
Lingnan culture — an important part of Chinese culture — embodies the wisdom of harmonious coexistence between humanity and nature, Peng told the guests, adding that China is willing to enhance cultural exchanges and mutual learning with the rest of the world.
The guests spoke highly of the long history and richness of traditional Chinese culture and the achievements made in advancing Chinese modernization. They also expressed their willingness to enhance exchanges and cooperation with China.
At the end of their visit, Peng and the guests, who included the wives of the leaders of Chile, Vietnam, the Republic of Korea and the Philippines, posed for a group photo.
