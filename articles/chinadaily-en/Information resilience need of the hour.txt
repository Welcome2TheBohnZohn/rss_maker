Former Kyrgyz ambassador: Information resilience need of the hour
        Share - WeChat
        
        
      
      
      Artificial intelligence is making disinformation appear increasingly convincing. Text, images and videos can now be harder than ever to distinguish from the real thing.
What does this mean for the Shanghai Cooperation Organization, which brings together 10 member states representing more than 3 billion people?
Kairat Osmonaliev, former ambassador extraordinary and plenipotentiary of the Kyrgyz Republic to the Republic of Azerbaijan and concurrently to Georgia, shared his answer in an interview with China Daily's Global Lens: the key is not to establish a common mechanism for controlling information, but to build a system of information resilience among member states, thereby safeguarding the information security of the internet.
