TITLE:
Send-off ceremony held for Shenzhou XXIV astronauts

PUBLISHED:
2026-11-25

SOURCE:

ARTICLE TEXT:
The three astronauts of the Shenzhou XXIV mission, including the first foreign national to be launched by a Chinese rocket, have headed to the launch tower at the Jiuquan Satellite Launch Center in northwestern China's Gobi Desert, after attending a send-off ceremony early on Wednesday morning.
At the ceremony in front of their residential building starting at 5:28 am local time, mission commander Major General Tang Hongbo, spaceflight engineer Colonel Wu Yi, and Wing Commander Muhammad Zeeshan Ali, a Pakistani astronaut and payload specialist in the mission, all in their spacesuits, reported to a high-ranking official that they were ready for the coming mission and asked for permission to embark on the flight.
The official granted ceremonial departure clearance to the Shenzhou XXIV crew, who greeted and saluted all participants in the brief event, before boarding a coach to the launch tower. Many Jiuquan spaceport workers and residents stood along the road and hailed the astronauts as their vehicle passed.
The upcoming mission marks the first joint flight involving Chinese and foreign astronauts in the history of China's manned space program. Ali will also become the first foreigner to visit China's Tiangong space station.
The Long March 2F rocket tasked with transporting the astronauts to orbit stands at 58.3 meters and weighs nearly 500 metric tons. The model has carried out all of China's 17 manned flights so far.
