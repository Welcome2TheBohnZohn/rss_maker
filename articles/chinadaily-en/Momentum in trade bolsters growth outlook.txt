TITLE:
Momentum in trade bolsters growth outlook

PUBLISHED:
2026-11-10

SOURCE:

ARTICLE TEXT:
China's foreign trade growth, building on a steady climb up the export value chain and a massive domestic market that is opening wider to imports, is delivering a crucial boost to economic growth in the final stretch of the year, experts and executives said.
The country's pursuit of more balanced foreign trade promises greater opportunities for global businesses, while reinforcing China's role as an anchor of stability for the world economy, they added.
Their comments came as the General Administration of Customs said on Tuesday that the total value of China's goods imports and exports reached 44.81 trillion yuan ($6.7 trillion) in the first 10 months of 2026, up 20 percent year-on-year.
Exports helped fuel overall trade growth, with shipments rising 17.6 percent year-on-year to 25.99 trillion yuan over the period. Exports of mechanical and electrical products grew at a faster pace, up 25.2 percent year-on-year to 16.81 trillion yuan.
Behind much of China's export strength this year is the global boom in artificial intelligence investment, which has provided a growing boost to shipments of chips and related products, said Feng Lin, executive director of research at Orient Golden Credit Rating International.
"A steadily improving export mix is also becoming an important driver of export growth," she said, noting that years of manufacturing upgrades continue to bolster exports of high-tech goods.
That industrial progress has gradually shifted China's manufacturing edge from low labor costs toward "a combination of strengths in supply chains, technological innovation and production efficiency", said Luo Zhiheng, chief economist at Yuekai Securities.
Inside the workshops of Kyocera Document Technology (Dongguan) Co, an office equipment manufacturer in Guangdong province, a batch of newly developed multifunction printers awaits shipment to Southeast Asia, according to Huangpu Customs in Guangdong.
Yuan Xiji, a company executive, explained how the company climbs up the value chain. "We used to focus on basic assembly and processing, but have since become a research and development center."
Luo, the economist, said the gains from exports also feed through to the wider economy, as stronger demand brings more orders to manufacturers, spurs production and helps underpin the recovery.
Foreign trade's contribution to growth is reflected in data from the National Bureau of Statistics, which showed that net exports of goods and services added 1.2 percentage points to GDP growth in the first three quarters. Exports also generate substantial investment and consumption, said Shan Hui, chief China economist at Goldman Sachs.
Meanwhile, robust import growth is also emerging as a stabilizing force, as the rapid expansion of high-tech industries fuels demand in the domestic market.
According to the General Administration of Customs, total goods imports grew 23.6 percent year-on-year to 18.82 trillion yuan in the first 10 months, with the value of integrated circuit imports surging 58 percent.
In October alone, total imports climbed 34.7 percent year-on-year to 2.08 trillion yuan.
"We expect imports in US dollar terms to maintain strong year-on-year growth of around 30 percent in November," said Feng of Orient Golden Credit Rating International.
The upbeat outlook comes as the world's second-largest importer seeks to promote "balanced development of imports and exports", a goal set out in China's 15th Five-Year Plan (2026-30).
"The Chinese market is an anchor of resilience for the global economy," said Wang Xuekun, head of the Chinese Academy of International Trade and Economic Cooperation.
Beyond its vast market, China is also encouraging open innovation, with its technological advances providing fresh impetus for global development, Wang said.
Xiao Ping, the China and Asia regional hub manager for IEC LV Motors at ABB Group's motion business, said, "China's industrial capabilities and supply chain advantages are irreplaceable."
The Swiss company sees considerable opportunities for energy-efficient cooling systems in China and overseas markets as AI drives growing demand for computing infrastructure worldwide, Xiao said.
