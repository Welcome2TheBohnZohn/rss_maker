TITLE:
Li calls for boosting ASEAN links

ARTICLE TEXT:
Premier Li Qiang has called on China and its Southeast Asian neighbors to take the entry into force of the China-ASEAN Free Trade Area 3.0 Upgrade Protocol as an opportunity to deepen industrial and supply chain cooperation and boost connectivity.
Li made the remarks at the 29th China-ASEAN Summit in Manila, the Philippines, on Monday. The gathering announced that the China-ASEAN FTA 3.0 Upgrade Protocol will enter into force on Dec 1.
The two sides should accelerate infrastructure connectivity, or "hard connectivity", in areas such as railways and highways, and strengthen "soft connectivity" in mechanisms, standards and rules, Li said.
China has been ASEAN's largest trading partner for 17 consecutive years, while ASEAN has been China's largest trading partner for six consecutive years.
From January through July this year, the total value of bilateral trade reached $744.41 billion, a year-on-year increase of 24.7 percent, according to the Ministry of Commerce.
The ministry said on Monday that the upgrade "will foster a regional market and industrial and supply chain systems that are more open, convenient, secure, stable and predictable for all enterprises in the region amid an uncertain global economic and trade environment".
Xu Liping, director of the Chinese Academy of Social Sciences' Center for Southeast Asian Studies, said that the upgrade of the China-ASEAN FTA will "significantly accelerate the pace of institutional opening-up on both sides".
"Both sides are actively leading the shaping of international rules in emerging areas such as the digital economy, the green economy, e-commerce supply chain connectivity and consumer protection, further boosting two-way growth in investment and trade," he said.
Stronger AI cooperation
As part of the fruitful outcomes of Monday's gathering, the meeting also decided that 2027 will be the China-ASEAN Year of artificial intelligence cooperation.
The meeting issued outcome documents covering topics such as the fifth anniversary of the establishment of the China-ASEAN comprehensive strategic partnership, the founding of an energy cooperation partnership and the deepening of forestry cooperation, according to the Foreign Ministry.
China applauds the establishment of a China-ASEAN mechanism for energy ministers' meetings and stands ready to work with ASEAN to successfully hold the China-ASEAN Year of AI cooperation next year, advance "AI plus" cooperation and create a strong engine for high-quality regional economic development, Li said.
He also expressed China's readiness to continue contributing to the China-ASEAN Cooperation Fund and strengthen cooperation in areas including poverty reduction, disaster prevention and mitigation, food security, and maternal and child health.
At the meeting, Li urged China and ASEAN countries to protect and develop their common home, injecting greater stability and certainty into the development and prosperity of the global village.
He called on the two sides to strengthen the foundations for peace and security by properly handling differences through dialogue and consultation, jointly opposing external interference and safeguarding regional peace and stability.
On the South China Sea issue, Li said that China looks forward to the early conclusion of the Code of Conduct in the South China Sea, which will provide a solid institutional guarantee for lasting peace and stability in the South China Sea.
Attending leaders of ASEAN countries also voiced readiness for the early conclusion of the Code and jointly securing the region's peace and stability. They appreciated China's consistent support for ASEAN's centrality in the region and the country's commitment to the peaceful resolution of disputes, the Foreign Ministry said in a news release.
As this year marks the fifth anniversary of the establishment of the China-ASEAN comprehensive strategic partnership, Li said that these extraordinary five years and the hard-won achievements offer valuable experience and useful lessons.
Both sides need to carry forward their tradition of friendship and mutual assistance, uphold the right path of openness and win-win cooperation, and keep pace with the trend of innovation and development, he said.
