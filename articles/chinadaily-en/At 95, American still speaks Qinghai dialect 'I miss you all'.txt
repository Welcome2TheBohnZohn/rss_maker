ARTICLE TEXT:
At 95, American David Plymire still speaks with a distinct Qinghai accent. He spent 17 years in Huangyuan county, Qinghai province, and considers it as his hometown. Recently, China Daily reporters visited him in Springfield, Missouri. In this video, he greeted his followers in China and expressed how much he misses his old friends in Huangyuan. （Reporter: Mingmei Li, Video: Chelsea Ge)
