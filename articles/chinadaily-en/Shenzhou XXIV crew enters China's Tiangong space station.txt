TITLE:
Shenzhou XXIV crew enters China's Tiangong space station

ARTICLE TEXT:
JIUQUAN -- The Shenzhou XXIV crew, comprising Chinese astronauts Tang Hongbo and Wu Yi, and Pakistani astronaut Muhammad Zeeshan Ali, entered China's Tiangong space station on Wednesday.
Serving as a payload specialist, Ali has made history by becoming the first foreigner to board Tiangong.
The astronaut trio took off at 8:18 am (Beijing Time) the same day aboard China's Shenzhou XXIV spaceship from the Jiuquan Satellite Launch Center in Northwest China.
The Shenzhou XXIII crew in orbit opened the hatch of the space station at 2:35 pm and welcomed the new arrivals, according to the China Manned Space Agency (CMSA).
After entering the space station, the Shenzhou XXIV crew met with the three Shenzhou XXIII astronauts, who have been in orbit for about six months. The two crews then took group pictures of the ninth space get-together in China's aerospace history. They will now start a new in-orbit handover round.
Ali will join in the crew's routine work and focus on joint China-Pakistan space science experiments while in orbit, primarily installing experiment samples and units, and carrying out in-orbit experiments, CMSA spokesman Zhang Jingbo revealed at a news conference on Tuesday.
After completing a short-term joint flight, Ali will return to Earth aboard the Shenzhou XXIII spacecraft alongside two Shenzhou XXIII crew members. The other two Shenzhou XXIV astronauts will stay in orbit for roughly six months along with Shenzhou XXIII astronaut Zhu Yangzhu, who has already spent six months in orbit and will remain aboard Tiangong to complete a year-long stay experiment.
