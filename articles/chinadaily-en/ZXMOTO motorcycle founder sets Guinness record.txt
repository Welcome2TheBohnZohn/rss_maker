Zhang Xue, the founder of Chinese motorcycle manufacturer ZXMOTO, set a new Guinness World Record on Sunday in his hometown of Mayang Miao autonomous county, Huaihua, Central China's Hunan province.
Riding a modified MX450 across open water for 40.74 kilometers, Zhang set a new record for the greatest distance traveled over open water by a modified motorcycle, surpassing the previous record of 31.8 km.
