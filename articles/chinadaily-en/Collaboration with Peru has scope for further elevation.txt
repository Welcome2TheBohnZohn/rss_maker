TITLE:
Collaboration with Peru has scope for further elevation

ARTICLE TEXT:
President Xi Jinping called on China and Peru on Tuesday to strengthen cooperation in infrastructure, energy and resources, and tap the potential for collaboration in emerging areas such as new energy and artificial intelligence to achieve shared prosperity.
Xi made the remarks during a meeting with Peruvian President Keiko Fujimori in Shenzhen, Guangdong province.
Noting that this year marks the 55th anniversary of diplomatic relations between the two countries, he said China has been Peru's largest trading partner and largest export destination for 12 consecutive years.
He noted that the Chancay Port, built with the participation of Chinese enterprises, has given a strong boost to economic and trade cooperation between Asia and Latin America.
China is ready to work with Peru to take their comprehensive strategic partnership to new heights and bring greater benefits to both peoples, Xi said.
He called for closer friendly exchanges between the two countries' governments, legislative bodies, political parties and at the subnational level to strengthen political mutual trust.
The two sides should continue unlocking the benefits from the protocol upgrading the bilateral free trade agreement, which took effect on Nov 1, he said.
The two countries should strengthen multilateral cooperation and enhance communication and coordination within frameworks including the United Nations and the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation to uphold true multilateralism and free trade, and jointly safeguard international fairness and justice, he added.
Fujimori said Peru firmly adheres to the one-China principle and hopes to expand exchanges between the two peoples and advance cooperation in trade, investment and infrastructure.
