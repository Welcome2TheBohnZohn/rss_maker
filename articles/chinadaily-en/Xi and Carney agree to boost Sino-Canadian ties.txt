TITLE:
Xi and Carney agree to boost Sino-Canadian ties

PUBLISHED:
2026-11-18

SOURCE:
China Daily English

CATEGORY:
Authoritative PRC Media

URL:
https://www.chinadaily.com.cn/a/202611/18/WS6afce446e4b03ebf8519d02c.html

ARTICLE TEXT:
President Xi Jinping called on China and Canada to build on the momentum of improving bilateral ties, deepen cooperation in traditional and emerging sectors and expand people-to-people exchanges during talks with Canadian Prime Minister Mark Carney on Tuesday.
Carney arrived in the coastal city of Shenzhen, South China's Guangdong province, on Monday, for his second visit to China this year as prime minister, following his first in January.
Xi said improving and developing relations between China and Canada, both important Asia-Pacific countries, serve their peoples' interests and contribute to world peace, stability, development and prosperity.
The course of bilateral relations has underscored the importance of mutual respect, equality and mutual benefit, and the seeking of common ground while reserving differences, he said.
To develop bilateral ties, Xi called on the two sides to build on the current momentum and strengthen political mutual trust.
Through constructive dialogue and communication, the two sides should foster a more accurate understanding of each other's development paths and policy intentions, deepen their understanding of and respect for each other's core interests and major concerns, and properly manage and resolve differences to jointly keep bilateral relations on the right course, he said.
Xi urged closer alignment to deepen cooperation in traditional areas such as trade and energy and expand cooperation in emerging sectors including the green economy, drawing on complementary strengths for mutual benefit.
Carney said bilateral ties had been on the right track since the new strategic partnership was established earlier this year. Substantial trade growth and progress across sectors demonstrated that cooperation could deepen on the basis of mutual respect, he said.
