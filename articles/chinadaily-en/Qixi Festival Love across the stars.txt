      Join us in celebrating the Qixi Festival. Often referred to as Chinese Valentine's Day, this cherished tradition tells the timeless love story of the Cowherd and the Weaver Girl, symbolizing the power of love and the beauty of reunion as they meet across the Milky Way.
In this video, let’s witness the intricate crafts and sweet Qiaoguo pastries that embody heartfelt emotions and the threads of fate that connect us all. Let the Milky Way stand as a testament to love that grows stronger with each passing year.
