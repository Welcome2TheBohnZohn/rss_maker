Flag-raising ceremony inspires Hong Kong and Macao delegates
        Share - WeChat
        
        
      
      
      About 1,200 delegates from Hong Kong and Macao, including students, officials, and teachers, attended the national flag-raising ceremony at Tiananmen Square in Beijing on Sunday as part of a five-day "Salute the National Flag" activity aimed at deepening patriotic education.
Liang Kaicheng, a student from Macao's Fong Chong School of Taipa, said, "I'm really excited. It's my first time in Beijing and my first time attending the national flag-raising ceremony at Tiananmen Square. It is nothing like watching it back at school. Seeing our national flag rise slowly in the capital is something to be proud of."
Kuang Chun-wan, a student from Hong Kong's Pui Kiu Middle School, expressed his excitement and deep emotion after witnessing the ceremony.
