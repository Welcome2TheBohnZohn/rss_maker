      What do Chinese students bring to British universities? What does a one-year master's degree offer? And what role can universities play in facilitating exchanges and cooperation when debate about China grows more heated?
In an exclusive interview with China Daily, Michael Spence, president and provost of University College London, discusses Chinese students, the value of a British university education, and the importance of international academic ties. And he explains why, at a time of growing uncertainty, he has chosen to speak out in support of engagement with China.
