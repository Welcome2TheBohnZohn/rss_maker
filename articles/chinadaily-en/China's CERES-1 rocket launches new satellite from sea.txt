RIZHAO, Shandong -- China launched a rocket from the ocean waters near the city of Rizhao in East China's Shandong province on Wednesday, placing a new satellite into planned orbit.
The CERES-1 Y8 carrier rocket blasted off at 11:39 am (Beijing Time).
The Taiyuan Satellite Launch Center carried out this offshore launch.
