TITLE:
Mutual respect enables stable partnership with Chile

SOURCE:

ARTICLE TEXT:
President Xi Jinping said on Tuesday that China and Chile should work together to be partners of mutual respect and trust, pioneers of all-round cooperation, defenders of the common interests of developing countries and promoters of China-Latin America cooperation.
Xi made the remarks during a meeting with Chilean President Jose Antonio Kast in Shenzhen.
He said that the China-Chile relationship has withstood the test of the changes of the international landscape, maintaining long-term healthy and stable development and standing at the forefront of China's relations with South American countries.
The most important experience drawn from the growth of China-Chile ties is that both countries respect each other, treat each other as equals and adhere to mutual respect and seek win-win results, he added.
Xi underlined the need for both countries to continue to support each other on issues concerning their core interests and major concerns, strengthen exchanges at all levels and in various fields, and promote exchanges on governance experience.
Noting that China and Chile have complementary economic structures and a strong foundation for cooperation, he called on both sides to strengthen the alignment of development strategies through high-quality Belt and Road cooperation, enhance collaboration in traditional fields.
He also expressed the country's willingness to work with Latin American and Caribbean countries to inject more certainty into peace and development in the region.
Kast said that for many years, China has been Chile's largest trading partner and the largest export destination for its agricultural products and minerals, and cooperation with China has provided important opportunities for Chile's economic and social development.
