TITLE:
In conversation with Cui Tiankai: US domestic noise should not derail China-US ties

PUBLISHED:

ARTICLE TEXT:
Before heading to Shenzhen to cover the 33rd APEC Economic Leaders' Meeting, we attended the US-China Political Dialogue 2026 held in Beijing. There, we spoke with Cui Tiankai, former Chinese ambassador to the US, about the key issues shaping China-US relations.
Here are some of his key quotes from the interview:
• The US ought to focus on fixing its own problems, rather than always looking elsewhere.
• There are various noises and events inside the US. Some have absolutely nothing to do with China and shouldn't derail China-US ties.
• The Pacific is vast enough for China and the US to grow together, and for the whole Asia-Pacific to thrive side by side. If the US joins us [China] and regional nations in this endeavor, we can achieve so much.
