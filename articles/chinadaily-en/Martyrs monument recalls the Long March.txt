Gaohunao Martyrs Monument — 90 years of the Long March

As China marks the 90th anniversary of the victory of the Long March, tributes have poured in at the Gaohunao Red Army Martyrs Monument in Guangchang county, Jiangxi province.
The monument recalls one of the Long March's most tragic battles. In 1934, Red Army soldiers held the line for 41 days against far larger enemy forces, winning a critical window that enabled the Central Red Army's strategic transfer.
