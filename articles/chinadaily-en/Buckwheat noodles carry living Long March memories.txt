      Came for the noodles. Stayed for the history.
In Wuqi county, Shaanxi province, a veteran treated a group of visiting college students from Beijing to local lamb chopped buckwheat noodles — and with the meal came 90-year-old memories of the Long March. Some dishes come with more than flavor; they carry a living legacy.
Chen Siyang, Gong Shaojun, Yao Fuxiang, Qiu Tong and Liu Yuting contributed to this story.
