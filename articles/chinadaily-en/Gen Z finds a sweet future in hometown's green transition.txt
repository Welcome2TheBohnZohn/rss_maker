Gen Z returnee finds a sweet future in hometown's green transition
At the southern foot of the Jinggang Mountains in Ji'an, Jiangxi province, the buzz of bees is becoming part of a new soundscape.
Tianhe town once relied on coal mining as the backbone of the local economy. Today, as the town looks for greener ways forward, a young beekeeper is betting on an old family trade — with some new ideas of his own.
Mao Yuanxiang, a Gen Z returnee, could have started his career in Shenzhen after graduating from university with a degree in cost engineering. Instead, he returned home.
He chose beekeeping, an industry his family has been involved in for generations, and set about giving it a modern twist.
Working with universities, Mao has introduced smart beehives and explored the development of honey-based drinks. He is also planning a bee-themed park where visitors can learn about beekeeping and the natural environment behind it.
His story is part of a broader transformation taking place across Jinggangshan.
Known as the cradle of the Chinese revolution, Jinggangshan is where Chinese revolutionaries explored a new path. The experience gave rise to the Jinggangshan Spirit, commonly described as a spirit of firm faith and hard work, seeking truth from facts and forging new paths, relying on the people and striving for victory.
