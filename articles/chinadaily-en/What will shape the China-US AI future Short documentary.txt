ARTICLE TEXT:
The US is betting big on AI. Tech giants are pouring hundreds of billions of dollars into chips, data centers, and power — all to build the world's most advanced models and keep their weights closed.
But China's open-weight breakthroughs are challenging that playbook.
From DeepSeek to Moonshot AI's Kimi K3, Chinese developers are closing the gap at a fraction of the cost and releasing the weights of the world's leading models for developers worldwide to build on.
