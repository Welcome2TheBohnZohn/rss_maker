Pakistani minister: China's space mission shows its inclusive approach

ARTICLE TEXT:
The successful launch of Shenzhou XXIV crewed spacecraft has captured global attention. Pakistani astronaut Muhammad Zeeshan Ali has become the first foreign astronaut to visit China's space station.
In an interview with China Daily, Pakistan's Minister for Planning, Development and Special Initiatives Ahsan Iqbal said the mission demonstrates China's inclusive approach to development. By sharing advanced technologies, China can help improve people's lives across the Global South while paving the way for future international partnerships in space exploration.
