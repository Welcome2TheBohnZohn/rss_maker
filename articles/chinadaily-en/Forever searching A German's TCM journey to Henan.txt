At 20, a young German man named Wuming bought a one-way ticket to Henan, China. He never expected to put down roots here and find his calling in traditional Chinese medicine.
He once read Jia Dao's poem For an Absent Recluse as a tale of regret. Today, he sees it differently: the journey matters more than the destination, and the answers we seek may already lie within us.
