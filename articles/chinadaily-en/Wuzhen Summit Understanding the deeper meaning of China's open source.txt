What do you think "open source" means? At China's first open-source-themed ecosystem experience center at the 2026 World Internet Conference Wuzhen Summit, the answer becomes strikingly clear: it's not about a few controlling the technology — it's about co-creation and shared benefits. AI large models, spatial intelligence, intelligent agents… these terms that sound hardcore are turning into new experiences that ordinary people can participate in.
