Openness, innovation and cooperation urged

ARTICLE TEXT:
President Xi Jinping highlighted on Wednesday the emergence of Shenzhen, Guangdong province, the host city of the 33rd Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation Economic Leaders' Meeting, from a fishing village to a metropolitan city, saying that the story of Shenzhen is a microcosm of China's reform and opening-up and a shining example of Asia-Pacific cooperation.
Xi made the remarks in a speech delivered at the banquet that he and his wife, Peng Liyuan, hosted to welcome guests attending the APEC Economic Leaders' Meeting.
"I have often said that to see over a few thousand years of China, go to Xi'an; to see 500 years of China, go to Beijing; to see 100 years of China, go to Shanghai. Tonight, let me add this: To see 50 years of China, come to Shenzhen," he said, adding that Shenzhen is a place with a big story to tell.
Xi recalled that in 1978, when he first visited Shenzhen, it was still a small fishing village. More than 40 years have passed, and the city is filled with high-rises and bustling traffic — a transformation nothing short of miraculous, he said.
Xi highlighted that openness is in Shenzhen's DNA, innovation is the wellspring of its vitality and cooperation is the source of its strength.
Growing from attracting foreign investment and technology to actively shaping the international rules, Shenzhen today has trade links spanning the globe and welcomes an endless stream of businesspeople and travelers, serving as a window of high-standard opening-up in China, he said.
Xi noted that Shenzhen has developed from a city of export processing to an independent research and development hub, and from being the "world factory" to becoming a "city of innovation".
Today's Shenzhen files up to 20,000 international patent applications annually, with advanced manufacturing and modern service industries thriving side by side, placing it at the forefront of cluster-based innovation in China, he said.
He highlighted the inclusiveness of the city and quoted the people of Shenzhen as saying, "Once here, Shenzhener forever."
The city opens its minds and arms to go-getters from around the world and brings together talent from over 100 countries and regions, making it a vibrant hub of win-win cooperation between China and the rest of the world, Xi said.
He emphasized that since its founding, APEC has been marked by openness, innovation and cooperation.
From the first handshake in Seattle, the United States, to the historic gathering by Beijing's Yanqi Lake, and from the solemn commitment to the Bogor Goals to the broad vision of building an Asia-Pacific community — APEC has remained attuned to the pulse of the times, spearheaded economic integration in the Asia-Pacific region, and helped to make the region the most dynamic in the global economy and a vital engine of world growth, Xi said.
Xi noted that since becoming the APEC chair, China has hosted more than 300 meetings and events across 16 cities in the course of the year. He commended the active participation of the member economies, saying that they have contributed to significant outcomes during the APEC China Year.
He expressed his expectation for the 33rd APEC Economic Leaders' Meeting to be a venue to discuss steps to expand mutual openness, explore pathways to innovation-driven growth, extend opportunities for result-oriented cooperation across various fields, and strive to deliver more tangible results on the ground.
"I look forward to discussing with you the great endeavor of Asia-Pacific cooperation and advancing our shared cause of Asia-Pacific development," he said.
After the banquet, Xi and Peng, along with the guests, watched a cultural performance.
