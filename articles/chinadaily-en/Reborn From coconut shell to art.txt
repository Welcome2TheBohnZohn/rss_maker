      In Hainan, humble coconut shells get a serious glow-up - carved into speakers, storage boxes, vases and intricate works of art.
Once a royal tribute, coconut carving has come a long way. Today, these shells are crafted into unique gifts. From coconut to artwork, that's quite a transformation!
