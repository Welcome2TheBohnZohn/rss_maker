TITLE:
Xi: Head-of-state diplomacy 'irreplaceable' for China-US ties

PUBLISHED:
2026-11-20

SOURCE:

ARTICLE TEXT:
President Xi Jinping highlighted on Thursday the "irreplaceable" role of head-of-state diplomacy in guiding relations between China and the United States, urging concrete actions to sustain the current momentum of stable and positive growth in the relationship.
Xi made the remarks during his meeting with US President Donald Trump in Shenzhen, Guangdong province, on the sidelines of the 33rd Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation Economic Leaders' Meeting.
Both sides considered the meeting to be positive and constructive, according to a statement released after the meeting.
The two sides agreed to map out an action plan for building a constructive relationship of strategic stability, implement the consensus reached by the two presidents, and build a constructive China-US relationship of strategic stability on the basis of respect, fairness and reciprocity, the statement said.
This was the third meeting between Xi and Trump this year. The two presidents also met in Beijing in May, when Trump made a state visit to China, and in Washington in September, when Xi made a state visit to the US.
Xi said that over the past few months, he and Trump have been working together to explore the right path for China and the US, the two major countries, to get along well with each other.
The new vision of building a constructive China-US relationship of strategic stability based on respect, fairness and reciprocity has been well recognized by the peoples of both countries and welcomed by the international community, he said.
Xi noted that the overall stability and positive momentum of China-US relations have brought tangible benefits to the people of the two countries, and injected stability and certainty into the world.
He expressed his hope to continue working with Trump to jointly steer the giant ship of China-US relations in the right direction toward the future.
Xi emphasized that building a constructive China-US relationship of strategic stability is an unprecedented, groundbreaking endeavor that requires an enterprising spirit, hard and solid efforts, and the ability to get things done.
To sustain the current momentum of stable and positive growth in China-US relations, the key is to take concrete action to implement the consensus reached by the two heads of state, he said, adding that communication between the working teams on both sides also needs to keep pace.
Xi stressed that it is important to make a longer list of cooperation and a shorter list of problems. Differences that cannot be resolved for the time being should be managed in a constructive way to prevent them from escalating, he said.
On the Taiwan question, Xi said the US should keep doing the right thing and avoid major setbacks in China-US relations.
He called on the two sides to strengthen communication on economic and trade matters, address each other's concerns and work toward mutual benefit.
Cooperation between the two countries on counternarcotics, repatriation of illegal immigrants, and combating telecommunications and online fraud has yielded positive results, Xi said, adding that it can be further deepened based on respect, fairness and reciprocity.
He also underlined the need for China and the US to strengthen communication and coordination to guard against security risks of artificial intelligence and ensure that AI always remains under human control.
Trump said he and Xi have jointly guided the development of US-China relations, adding that both leaders have maintained close contact, with their friendship growing stronger.
Trade and investment ties between the two countries have continued to grow, and cooperation on fentanyl and other issues has yielded results, Trump noted.
Both sides should maintain the momentum of steady development of bilateral relations, and strengthen cooperation in areas such as farm products, energy and super intelligence, and fairly address each other's concerns, Trump said.
He told Xi that the US does not support "Taiwan independence".
Trump congratulated China on hosting a successful APEC Economic Leaders' Meeting, adding that both countries need to work together to lead and promote world peace and stability.
The two presidents also discussed regional and international issues of mutual interest.
He Yadong, spokesman for the Chinese Ministry of Commerce, announced on Thursday that the agricultural working group under the China-US Board of Trade will soon hold its first meeting.
The two countries' economic and trade teams have maintained communication at various levels, and the two sides held the first meeting of the Board of Trade under the China-US economic and trade consultation mechanism via video link on Nov 13, the spokesman said at a regular news briefing in Beijing.
