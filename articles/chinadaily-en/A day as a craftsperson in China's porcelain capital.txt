What's it like to be a porcelain craftsperson for a day in Taoxichuan, Jingdezhen, Jiangxi province? Transformed from the former Universe Porcelain Factory, Taoxichuan keeps Jingdezhen's centuries-old ceramic heritage alive. From shaping clay to painting blue-and-white designs, every step brings this timeless craft to life. Watch the video and experience it firsthand.
