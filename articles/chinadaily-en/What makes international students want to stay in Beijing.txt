At the 2nd Beijing International Gathering for Youth in Beijing's Shougang Park on Nov 10, more than 600 young people from China and abroad explored career opportunities and local culture. From professional ambitions and everyday acts of kindness to a young Brazilian's journey from student to entrepreneur, discover their stories of building a future in Beijing.
