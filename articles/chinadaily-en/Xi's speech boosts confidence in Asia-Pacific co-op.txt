President Xi Jinping delivered a keynote speech at the opening ceremony of the APEC CEO Summit 2026 in Shenzhen on Wednesday. International business leaders said his speech charted the course for greater openness, deeper cooperation and shared prosperity in the Asia-Pacific, while boosting global businesses' confidence in tapping into opportunities in China and expanding partnerships.
