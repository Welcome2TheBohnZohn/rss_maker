ARTICLE TEXT:
Before heading to Shenzhen to cover APEC, China Daily reporter Zhao Manfeng received three postcards. Following the postmarks, she set out to find their senders: Jeong Songhwa from South Korea, Ma Junbang from China, and Truong Le Na from Vietnam — representing APEC's previous, current and next host economies. 
Using their cameras to connect people across borders, the three young content creators explore opportunities in Shenzhen.
From Gyeongju to Shenzhen to Phu Quoc, the APEC journey is opening doors for young people across the Asia-Pacific.
In Shenzhen, they are making connections, pursuing opportunities and turning their dreams into reality.
