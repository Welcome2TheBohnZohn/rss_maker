Chinese volleyball icon Lang Ping receives FIVB's highest distinction

ARTICLE TEXT:
BEIJING -- Chinese volleyball legend Jenny Lang Ping and four other outstanding international volleyball figures have been awarded the Volleyball Order, FIVB's highest distinction, during the world volleyball governing body's 40th World Congress in Punta Cana, the Dominican Republic on Saturday.
The FIVB described Lang as "a global volleyball icon whose achievements as both a player and coach have inspired generations." Known as the "Iron Hammer," Lang helped China claim Olympic gold at Los Angeles 1984 before guiding the women's national team to another triumph as head coach at Rio 2016. She also led the USA women's team to silver at Beijing 2008. Inducted into the International Volleyball Hall of Fame in 2002, she received the prestigious IOC Coaches Lifetime Achievement Award in 2025.
In her acceptance speech, Lang said: "Thanks to the FIVB for inviting me, and for awarding me the Volleyball Order. As I stand here today, I honestly don't feel that I am standing here alone. I feel my coaches, my teammates, my players, and everyone I've worked with over the years standing right here with me."
Lang recalled that since she started playing volleyball in Beijing when she was fourteen, she has spent fifty years in the game, and every step of the way, there was a team beside her. She said she wanted to share the honor with all of them.
"To my family, my friends, and all the fans who have supported me along the way, thank you from the bottom of my heart. You celebrated with me and stood by me through the hardest times. You gave me strength," she said.
"When I look back, I remember the people, the friendships, and the dreams we shared. And if I could do it all over again, I'd choose volleyball every time," Lang added.
"Looking ahead, I have two hopes. I hope to see many more women coaches stand on this stage. And I hope more people around the world get to play volleyball, cheer for it, and fall in love with it."
The other recipients were Doug Beal (United States), who coached the US men's team to Olympic gold in 1984; Craig Carracher (Australia), who has contributed to the development of volleyball and beach volleyball; Mireya Luis (Cuba), a three-time Olympic champion and two-time world champion; and Ruedi Kunz (Switzerland), who has played a longstanding role in organizing the Gstaad beach volleyball tournament.
The Volleyball Order is the FIVB's highest distinction, recognizing individuals who have made an exceptional and lasting contribution to the growth and advancement of volleyball and beach volleyball worldwide.
