TITLE:
Steady growth trajectory driven by innovation

ARTICLE TEXT:
October's economic data point to a steadily improving Chinese economy that is undergoing structural upgrading despite global uncertainties. In particular, double-digit growth in high-tech manufacturing and a surge in investment in intellectual property products highlight the resilience and vibrancy of the world's second-largest economy.
The Chinese economy grew 4.6 percent year-on-year in the first three quarters, with the October data suggesting this steady trajectory remains intact. The essence of the country's economic strength lies in the shift from traditional growth drivers to new and innovative engines.
Last month, the value added of high-tech manufacturing rose by 17.6 percent year-on-year, picking up pace from September and reflecting the rapid industrial upgrading underway across the country.
In a breakdown, the production of 3D-printing equipment, lithium-ion batteries and industrial robots surged by 85.9 percent, 30 percent and 26 percent year-on-year, respectively, while the output of new energy vehicles increased by 17.1 percent. As traditional factor-driven expansion gives way to technology-led and advanced manufacturing growth, it enables the country's economy to better withstand external shocks and internal pressures, laying a solid foundation for long-term sustainable growth.
Wang Guanhua, a spokesman for the National Bureau of Statistics, noted on Monday that the transition from traditional to new growth drivers is the result of the country's persistent efforts over many years to promote high-quality development, which will provide stronger support for the economy going forward.
Moreover, increasing investment in intellectual property products — up by 9.2 percent in the first 10 months of the year compared with the same period last year — suggests that companies are prioritizing innovation and future productivity through R&D, software and databases rather than physical assets. Its share of total investment rose by 2.5 percentage points year-on-year, effectively promoting scientific and technological innovation. As market resources continuously flow into research and technological breakthroughs, innovation is becoming a systematic driving force that boosts industrial upgrading. It is releasing China's economic vitality and fueling high-quality development.
From a foreign trade perspective, as global demand for smart and green goods expands, China's high-tech and green products have shown strong growth momentum. In October, exports of both high-tech products and the "new three" products — namely photovoltaic products, lithium-ion batteries and electric vehicles — all increased by more than 50 percent year-on-year.
Additionally, strong import growth has become further evidence of the sizable domestic demand. China's imports surged by 23.6 percent in the first 10 months of the year, with October alone witnessing a year-on-year increase of 34.7 percent. The upgrading of consumption demand and industrial modernization have boosted the demand for quality goods, advanced equipment and technological products from global markets. Against the backdrop of sluggish global growth and complex external challenges, China's expanding imports deliver tangible opportunities that can help propel a global recovery, positioning the country as a key stabilizer of the world economy.
Factors that previously dragged on the economy are also showing signs of improvement. For instance, the inventory of newly built commercial housing has continued to shrink, thanks mainly to supportive government measures. By the end of October, the floor area of unsold newly built commercial housing had decreased by 1.7 percent year-on-year, a decline 0.2 percentage points larger than at the end of September, marking eight consecutive months of decline. The government will continue to manage new housing supply and reduce inventory to ensure the healthy development of the market.
The long-term positive fundamentals of China's economy remain unchanged. With continuous structural reform, strengthened innovation capacity and expanding domestic market momentum, China is on track to meet its full-year growth target of 4.5 to 5 percent. This will also inject strong vitality into global growth.
