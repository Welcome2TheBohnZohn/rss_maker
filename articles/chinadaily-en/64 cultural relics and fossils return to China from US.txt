      The United States returned 64 Chinese cultural relics and paleontological fossils to China last week. The returned items, seized by US law enforcement agencies, include the torso of a bodhisattva statue from the Tianlongshan Grottoes, as well as statues, terracotta figures, dinosaur skeleton fossils, and other items. The US side said that China-US cooperation on the return of cultural relics reflects the friendship and mutual respect between the two countries.
