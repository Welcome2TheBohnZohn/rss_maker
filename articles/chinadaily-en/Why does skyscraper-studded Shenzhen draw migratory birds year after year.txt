TITLE:
Why does skyscraper-studded Shenzhen draw migratory birds year after year?

ARTICLE TEXT:
Every autumn and winter, large numbers of migratory birds arrive in Shenzhen, either to rest over the winter or pass through on their journeys. What makes this modern metropolis such a reliable, welcoming stop for these avian travelers returning like clockwork each year?
China Daily reporter Yan An ventured into the mangrove forests of Shenzhen Bay with Zhang Gaofeng, secretary-general of the Shenzhen Bird Watching Society, to find the answer.
