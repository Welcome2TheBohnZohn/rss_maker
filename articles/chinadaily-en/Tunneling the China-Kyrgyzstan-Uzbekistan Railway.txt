Built meter by meter: Tunneling the China-Kyrgyzstan-Uzbekistan Railway
        Share - WeChat
        
        
      
      
      "A 12.4-kilometer tunnel is built meter by meter." At the Naryn No 1 Tunnel, a key section of the China-Kyrgyzstan-Uzbekistan Railway, China Daily reporter Peng Yixuan speaks with Chinese frontline builders in Kyrgyzstan about life on location and the railway construction process. Against harsh mountain conditions, these workers spare no effort in constructing this critical cross-border rail link. Connecting three nations, the railway boosts regional connectivity and serves as a great example of Belt and Road win-win cooperation.
