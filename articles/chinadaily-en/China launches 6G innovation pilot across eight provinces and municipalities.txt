TITLE:
China launches 6G innovation pilot across eight provinces and municipalities

ARTICLE TEXT:
China's top industry regulator launched a ministry-province collaborative pilot program for 6G innovation and development in Shanghai on Tuesday, with Beijing, Hebei, Shanghai, Jiangsu, Zhejiang, Anhui, Hubei, and Guangdong approved to carry out 6G pilot work.
The launch marks a key step in China's push to advance 6G development from technology research to coordinated regional experimentation, laying the groundwork for the country's participation in global 6G standardization and industrial deployment.
The pilot program, initiated by the Ministry of Industry and Information Technology, focuses on utilizing local conditions within the national-level framework.
Key tasks include strengthening research on 6G-related industries such as new-type terminals, key chips and components, instruments, and satellite internet, as well as on potential 6G application scenarios including immersive communications, industrial manufacturing, the low-altitude economy, and the marine economy.
The initiative aims to form a nationally coordinated development pattern for 6G.
Research on 6G technical standards has also entered a sprint phase, according to the IMT-2030 (6G) Promotion Group, China's top-level coordination platform for 6G research and development.
Under the timeline set by international standards organizations, the first version of 6G international standards is expected to be released in the first half of 2029, while basic research on 6G standards is scheduled to be completed in the first half of 2027.
