A new chapter begins: Giant pandas return to Zoo Atlanta

ARTICLE TEXT:
Two giant pandas from China, Ping Ping and Fu Shuang, made their public debut at Zoo Atlanta on Nov 6, ending a two-year absence of giant pandas from the city. Their arrival opens a new chapter in giant panda conservation, building on 25 years of collaboration between Zoo Atlanta and its Chinese partners. Visitors welcomed the pair, while zoo officials highlighted the role of pandas in promoting wildlife conservation and cultural exchange. (Reporter: May Zhou, Video: Chelsea Ge)
