Xi hosts welcome banquet for guests attending APEC meeting

ARTICLE TEXT:
SHENZHEN -- Chinese President Xi Jinping and his wife, Peng Liyuan, on Wednesday hosted a banquet in Shenzhen, South China's Guangdong province, to welcome guests who are here to attend the 33rd Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation (APEC) Economic Leaders' Meeting.
"On behalf of the Chinese government and people, I wish to extend a warm welcome to all the distinguished guests," Xi said in his toast.
Openness is deeply rooted in Shenzhen's DNA and innovation drives the city's vitality, while cooperation is where its strength comes from, Xi said, adding that the success of Shenzhen is also a microcosm of China's reform and opening-up, as well as an example of fruitful cooperation across the Asia-Pacific.
Noting that openness, innovation and cooperation have been the distinctive features of APEC since its establishment, Xi said APEC has grasped the pulse of the times and guided the process of regional economic integration, making the Asia-Pacific the most dynamic region and a vital growth engine of the world economy.
"At the economic leaders' meeting tomorrow, we will discuss measures to expand two-way opening-up, explore paths of innovation-driven growth and unfold more opportunities of practical cooperation in various fields," Xi said.
