      As autumn arrives, North China's Inner Mongolia autonomous region transforms into a breathtaking palette of gold, orange, and green. From vast grasslands to colorful forests, tranquil lakes, picturesque wetlands, and rugged hills, the region offers a spectacular escape for autumn travelers.
Hulunbuir
Golden grasslands stretch beneath wide-open skies, with the winding Moergele River adding a graceful touch to the landscape. Take a scenic drive along road G331, explore birch forests and wetlands, or meet reindeer at the Aoluguya Ewenki Reindeer Tribe.
