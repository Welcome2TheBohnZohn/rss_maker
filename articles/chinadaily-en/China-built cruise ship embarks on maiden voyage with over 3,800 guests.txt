China-built cruise ship embarks on maiden voyage with over 3,800 guests

ARTICLE TEXT:
The Adora Flora City, China's second domestically built large cruise ship, embarked on its maiden commercial voyage with more than 3,800 guests on Sunday, marking a significant step toward the large-scale operation of the ship.
Departing from the Nansha International Cruise Home Port in Guangzhou, the capital of Guangdong province, the cruise ship is set to visit Hong Kong and Hue, Vietnam, on a 6-day, 5-night journey.
