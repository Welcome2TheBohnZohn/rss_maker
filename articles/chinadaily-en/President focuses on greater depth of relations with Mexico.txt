TITLE:
President focuses on greater depth of relations with Mexico

PUBLISHED:
2026-11-18

SOURCE:
China Daily English

CATEGORY:
Authoritative PRC Media

URL:

ARTICLE TEXT:
China stands ready to work with Mexico to steer bilateral ties toward high-quality development and jointly promote an Asia-Pacific that is prosperous and stable, open and interconnected, universally beneficial and inclusive, united and mutually supportive, President Xi Jinping said on Tuesday.
In talks with Mexican President Claudia Sheinbaum Pardo in Shenzhen, Guangdong province, Xi stressed joint efforts to work toward a fairer and more equitable global governance system and contribute to world peace and common development.
Xi said that advancing China-Mexico relations to a higher level and expanding cooperation in both scope and depth is of great significance to the development of both countries and greater solidarity among Global South nations.
The president called on China and Mexico to deepen political mutual trust, strengthen exchanges and cooperation at all levels and across various fields, and jointly ensure the success of celebrations next year marking the 55th anniversary of diplomatic relations.
He urged both sides to pursue mutually beneficial cooperation, better align their development strategies and expand collaboration in infrastructure, science and technology, clean energy, culture and tourism.
Xi also called for closer coordination at the United Nations and other multilateral platforms to uphold fairness and justice and safeguard the basic norms governing international relations.
Sheinbaum said that Mexico firmly adheres to the one-China principle and attaches great importance to its relations with China.
Mexico, which will host the APEC meetings in 2028, is willing to strengthen multilateral coordination with China, uphold multilateralism and safeguard the common interests of the Global South, she added.
