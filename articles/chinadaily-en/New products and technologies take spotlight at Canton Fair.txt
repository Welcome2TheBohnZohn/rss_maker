The 140th edition of the China Import and Export Fair, also known as the Canton Fair, kicked off in Guangzhou, South China's Guangdong province, on Thursday. Focusing on new, green, and smart products, the fair showcases a wide range of cutting-edge technologies, drawing exhibitors and buyers from around the world.
