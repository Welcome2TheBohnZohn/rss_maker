Fish have returned to the Huangqihai Lake in Ulaanqab, Inner Mongolia autonomous region, which once dried up, highlighting the results of ecological restoration.
On Oct 16, President Xi Jinping visited the lake and commended local ecological restoration efforts. Ban Xiuping, Party chief of Qahar Right Wing Front Banner, said Xi's recognition has given local officials greater confidence and motivation to continue their work.
