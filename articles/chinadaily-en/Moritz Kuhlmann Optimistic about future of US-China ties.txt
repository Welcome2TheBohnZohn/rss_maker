      Young sinologist Moritz Kuhlmann said President Xi Jinping's "50,000 in Five Years" initiative is a "beautiful initiative", stressing the importance of young Americans experiencing China firsthand. He added that the US and China can start with concrete cooperation and gradually expand it, expressing optimism about the future of bilateral ties.
