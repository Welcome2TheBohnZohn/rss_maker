ARTICLE TEXT:
One of China's four famous classical gardens, Lingering Garden was first built in 1593 during the Ming Dynasty (1368-1644) and is regarded as a masterpiece of garden art in Jiangnan (the southern regions of the lower reaches of the Yangtze River). Its elegant layout combines graceful pavilions, winding covered walkways, tranquil waters, and striking rockeries. Within a compact space, the garden evokes an expansive natural landscape, capturing the classical ideal of finding the beauty of mountains and forests within an urban setting.
As you wander through the garden, the scenery unfolds with every step. Architecture and nature blend seamlessly, revealing the refined charm and timeless elegance of Suzhou's classical gardens.
