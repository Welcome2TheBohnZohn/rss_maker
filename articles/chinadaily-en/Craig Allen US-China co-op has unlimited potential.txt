      After attending the third China-US Track 1.5 Dialogue in Beijing on Tuesday, Craig Allen, former president of the US-China Business Council, said there were very good outcomes from US President Donald Trump's meeting with President Xi Jinping on May 14. He said the two countries can make significant progress together in areas such as AI and healthcare, adding that the potential for US-China cooperation is unlimited.
