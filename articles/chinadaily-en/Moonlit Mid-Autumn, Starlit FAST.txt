      Today marks the 10th anniversary of the completion of the Five-hundred-meter Aperture Spherical radio Telescope, or FAST, coinciding with this year's Mid-Autumn Festival. As we admire the moon, FAST continues to "listen" to the stars, commemorating a decade of capturing whispers from billions of light-years away. Happy 10th birthday, China's FAST!
