      Join China Daily reporter Zhao Manfeng at the 13th Beijing Xiangshan Forum, where experts and officials called for greater trust, dialogue and cooperation amid growing uncertainties in the international environment.
Over the 20 years since its founding, the Beijing Xiangshan Forum has played an important role in building consensus on security, promoting defense cooperation and safeguarding regional peace and stability.
Themed "Build Consensus on Stability, Advance Security Governance Together," this year's forum has brought together about 2,000 guests, including government officials, experts, scholars, and observers from more than 100 countries, regions, and international organizations.
