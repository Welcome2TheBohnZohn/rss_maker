What makes Linyi stand out to international businesses?
Business delegates from Nigeria, Ghana and Australia shared their insights into the city's markets and business environment at the fourth Linyi Trade City International Sourcing Conference, which is running from Oct 28 to 30 in Linyi, Shandong province.
Boasting a diverse product portfolio, one-stop sourcing services and solid industrial strengths, Linyi presents abundant business prospects. The delegates identified room for deeper cooperation across building materials, technology and other sectors.
