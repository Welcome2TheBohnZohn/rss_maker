      Foreign Minister Wang Yi will visit the Republic of Korea and Indonesia from Wednesday to Saturday, a Foreign Ministry spokesperson announced on Wednesday.
In Indonesia, Wang will chair the first Conference of China-Indonesia Comprehensive Strategic Dialogue Mechanism, the spokesperson said.
He will also attend the Second Ministerial Meeting of China-Indonesia Joint Foreign and Defense Ministerial Dialogue together with Defense Minister Dong Jun, the spokesperson added.
