      Foreign vloggers from six countries joined journalists from Chinese and foreign media outlets to visit Pingyao Ancient City in Jinzhong, Shanxi province, on Sunday.
The visit was part of "Loyalty and Honor Without Borders", a global online outreach campaign on Guan Gong culture, jointly hosted by the Cyberspace Administration of Shanxi province, China Daily Website, and the Yuncheng municipal government. The four-day cultural journey is dedicated to offering participants an immersive encounter with the spirit of loyalty and righteousness embodied by Guan Gong, one that transcends linguistic and geographic barriers.
