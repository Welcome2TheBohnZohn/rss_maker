      Following the Global Development Initiative, the Global Security Initiative and the Global Civilization Initiative, President Xi Jinping proposed the Global Governance Initiative last year. The four global initiatives, each with a distinct focus, are gaining more global consensus, injecting greater stability and certainty to the world. Together, they offer a window into China's vision and efforts to build a better world for all.
