      President Xi Jinping has just wrapped up his state visit to the United States. During the visit, the two sides added new substance to the vision for the China-US relationship and agreed to jointly build a constructive relationship of strategic stability based on respect, fairness and reciprocity. Experts believe that the China-US summit will have a far-reaching significance and a positive impact on world peace, stability and global economic growth.
