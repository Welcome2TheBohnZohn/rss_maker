ARTICLE TEXT:
The Shenzhou XXIV crewed spaceship blasted off from Jiuquan on Wednesday morning and successfully entered orbit, carrying Tang Hongbo, Wu Yi and Pakistani astronaut Muhammad Zeeshan Ali to China's Tiangong space station.
The mission marks the first joint flight involving Chinese and foreign astronauts in the history of China's manned space program, and the first time a foreign astronaut has visited the Tiangong space station.
