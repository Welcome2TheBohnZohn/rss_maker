TITLE:
Intl network launched to streamline silk standards

ARTICLE TEXT:
The world's first international science and technology organization in the textile sector to be headquartered in China was formally established in Hangzhou, Zhejiang province, on Friday, marking a new step in China-led international efforts to promote cooperation on silk industry standards, technology and development.
With more than 100 member organizations from over 20 countries and regions, the agenda of the China-initiated International Network for Silk covers the entire silk value chain, including sericulture resource development, production and processing, and trade and investment, according to official information released during the founding ceremony.
"Under the framework of the network, we should build a public service platform to share information, match supply and demand, enhance exchanges and advance cooperation, as well as to promote practical collaboration across the silk value chain," Vice-Premier Zhang Guoqing said on Friday during the ceremony.
Efforts should be made to strengthen mutual recognition of standards, align rules and promote industrial coordination in the silk sector, while building an integrated innovation ecosystem, he said.
Zhang also called for greater cultural exchanges, including cross-border showcases of silk-making techniques, visits by inheritors of intangible cultural heritage and joint projects by designers from different countries.
China's position as a major player in the global silk industry provides an important foundation for the initiative. The country accounted for about 75 percent of global cocoon production and more than 60 percent of raw silk exports by the end of 2025, according to the Ministry of Commerce.
