Yakou village in Zhongshan, Guangdong province, South China, has become a social media sensation for its unique scenery, where the sea meets endless rice paddies. China Daily reporter Yan An met South African vlogger Lizzy during her visit to the village, where she enjoyed train-themed homestays and sweeping views of the rice fields. "This place is really special!" she said.
