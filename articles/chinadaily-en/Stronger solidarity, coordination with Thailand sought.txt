TITLE:
Stronger solidarity, coordination with Thailand sought

PUBLISHED:
2026-11-21

SOURCE:
China Daily English

CATEGORY:
Authoritative PRC Media

URL:
https://www.chinadaily.com.cn/a/202611/21/WS6b00e33be4b03ebf8519dcc6.html

ARTICLE TEXT:
President Xi Jinping called on China and Thailand to cultivate and strengthen new growth areas of cooperation to jointly build a regional hub for technological innovation and industrial cooperation.
He made the remarks during a meeting with Thai Prime Minister Anutin Charnvirakul, adding that China and Thailand should enhance political mutual trust and strengthen solidarity and coordination.
China firmly supports Thailand in achieving national stability and development, Xi said. He expressed readiness to work with Thailand to advance the building of a China-Thailand community with a shared future and contribute more to regional peace, stability and prosperity.
Xi called for closer exchanges between governments, parties and legislative bodies, greater sharing of governance experience, and efforts to safeguard their common strategic interests.
He called on both sides to better align their strategies, make full use of cooperation mechanisms and strengthen cooperation in established areas. He also urged stronger cooperation in culture, education and tourism. Both sides should intensify efforts to crack down on illegal and criminal activities such as online gambling and telecom fraud, he said.
China is ready to play a positive role in bringing about an early turnaround in Thailand-Cambodia relations, Xi said.
Anutin said Thailand firmly adheres to the one-China principle and is ready to continue to strengthen high-level exchanges with China and enhance cooperation in areas such as trade and investment.
Thailand will resolutely crack down on cross-border crimes, protect the safety of Chinese citizens in Thailand, and welcome more Chinese tourists, he added.
