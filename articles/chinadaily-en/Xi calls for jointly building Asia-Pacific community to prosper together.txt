Xi calls for jointly building Asia-Pacific community to prosper together

ARTICLE TEXT:
SHENZHEN -- President Xi Jinping pledged on Wednesday that China is willing to work with all parties to forge ahead with resolve, jointly build an Asia-Pacific community and bring prosperity for all.
Addressing the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation (APEC) CEO Summit 2026 in the southern metropolis of Shenzhen, Xi called on business communities in the region to uphold the entrepreneurial spirit and make greater contributions to economic integration in the Asia-Pacific.
Under the theme "To Prosper and Win the Future Together," the two-day meeting has attracted more than 2,000 representatives from 21 APEC economies and 13 non-APEC economies, including Britain, France, Germany and Switzerland.
"The Asia-Pacific is our home; it is here we jointly pursue development and cooperation," Xi said.
Born amid the waves of economic globalization, APEC has been one of the world's most dynamic engines of growth. Today, the APEC economies are home to about 40 percent of the world's population, and account for more than 60 percent of global GDP and nearly half of global trade.
As the world becomes more turbulent and the global economy lacks growth momentum, the Chinese president said the region must answer the "hard questions of our times": to open doors or close doors, work together or against each other, adapt to change or stick to old ways?
In his response, Xi put forward proposals on how the region can prosper together, calling on APEC economies to uphold peace and stability, build bridges of opening-up and cooperation, boost innovation to drive growth and expand ways for mutual benefits and win-win outcomes.
All parties in the region must "uphold the postwar international order," he said, calling on them to resolve differences through dialogue and consultation and address challenges through solidarity and coordination.
