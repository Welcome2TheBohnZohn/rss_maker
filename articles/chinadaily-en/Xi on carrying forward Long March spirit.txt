This year marks the 90th anniversary of the victory of the Long March, which lasted from 1934 to 1936. The arduous journey helped lay the foundation for the eventual victory of the Chinese revolution. President Xi Jinping has always stressed the importance of carrying forward the spirit of the Long March, which has become a powerful spiritual force inspiring the Chinese people to overcome difficulties and advance from victory to victory.
