ARTICLE TEXT:
"I was the last one to join the program. But now I can confidently say that from that last one to becoming the first one to be in space." Pakistani astronaut Muhammad Zeeshan Ali is preparing for the Shenzhou XXIV mission after completing a year-long training program in just seven months. Starting from scratch, he learned Chinese and can now read through the entire mission checklist. For Ali, learning Chinese is more than mastering a language — it's a way to connect with the Chinese people and understand their hearts.
