'Auntie Basketball' team scores cultural points on US tour
Guizhou's community-based players use sport to foster exchanges
By LIU BOQIAN and YANG JUN in Guiyang | China Daily
Updated: 2026-11-26 09:07

Players from two "Auntie Basketball" teams compete in the championship battle in Danjiang town, Leishan county, Guizhou province, on Aug 9. The county-level "Village Cup" women's basketball league concluded after nearly two months of competition, featuring 33 village teams from nine towns and drawing thousands of visitors to the area. LIANG WEN/FOR CHINA DAILY
Loud cheers and applause echoed through the gymnasium at Crimson Cliffs High School in Washington City, Utah, as a group of women from Guizhou province faced the school's girls' basketball team in a friendly match during a recent trip to the United States.
Yang Xiaohui (left), a member from the delegation, talks in Mandarin with students from Crimson Cliffs High School in Washington City, Utah, on Oct 2. CHINA DAILY
The visitors were members of the "Auntie Basketball" delegation, a grassroots women's basketball team from Qiandongnan Miao and Dong autonomous prefecture in Guizhou. More than 20 members of the delegation traveled to various communities from Sept 28 to Oct 6, visiting states, including California, Nevada, and Utah.
