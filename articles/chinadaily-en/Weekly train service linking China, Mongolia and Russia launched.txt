Weekly train service linking China, Mongolia and Russia launched

ARTICLE TEXT:
A passenger train departed Beijing for Irkutsk, Russia, on Wednesday morning, marking the launch of a weekly rail service linking China, Mongolia and Russia.
The V3 train left Beijing Railway Station at 7:27 am and is scheduled to arrive in Ulaanbaatar, the capital of Mongolia, on Thursday afternoon before reaching Irkutsk on Friday afternoon.
The 2,666-kilometer journey takes about 55 hours. The train stops at cities and towns in all three countries, including Jining and Ereenhot in China, Ulaanbaatar in Mongolia and Ulan-Ude in Russia.
