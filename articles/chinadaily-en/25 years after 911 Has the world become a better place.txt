      On September 11, 2001, the devastating attacks claimed nearly 3,000 lives in an instant. That day, the world mourned together with the United States.
Twenty-five years later, as we remember those lost, we cannot help but ask: where has this tragedy ultimately led the United States and the rest of the world?
