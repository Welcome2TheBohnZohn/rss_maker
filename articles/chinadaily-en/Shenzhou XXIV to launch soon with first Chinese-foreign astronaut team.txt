TITLE:
Shenzhou XXIV to launch soon with first Chinese-foreign astronaut team

PUBLISHED:
2026-11-17

SOURCE:
China Daily English

CATEGORY:
Authoritative PRC Media

URL:
https://www.chinadaily.com.cn/a/202611/17/WS6afc3b49e4b03ebf8519cf74.html

ARTICLE TEXT:
China's next manned mission, the Shenzhou XXIV, is scheduled to be launched in the near future to transport three astronauts to the Tiangong space station, according to the China Manned Space Agency.
The Shenzhou XXIV crew spacecraft and its carrier, a Long March 2F rocket, were moved to the service tower on Tuesday at the Jiuquan Satellite Launch Center in northwestern China's Gobi Desert, the agency said in a brief news release, adding that launch facilities at the spaceport "are in good condition."
The release noted that the Shenzhou XXIV spacecraft and the rocket will undergo final functional checks in the next several days, and the launch will take place in due course.
The upcoming mission is set to mark the first joint flight involving Chinese and foreign astronauts in the history of China's manned space program, as a Pakistani astronaut has been selected to work as the payload specialist in the Shenzhou XXIV crew and spend a multi-day journey inside the Tiangong, before returning to Earth with two Chinese astronauts from the Shenzhou XXIII team.
