TITLE:
Anhui adds three high-speed rail lines

PUBLISHED:

ARTICLE TEXT:
Three high-speed railways opened in East China's Anhui province on Friday, expanding the high-speed rail network in the Yangtze River Delta region to more than 8,500 kilometers.
The Chaohu-Ma'anshan, Huaibei-Bengbu and Huaibei-Fuyang high-speed railways together add about 364.5 km of new mainline tracks with a design speed of 350 km per hour, according to a press release by the China Railway Shanghai Group on Friday.
The Shanghai group is responsible for administering railways in the YRD region, comprising the city of Shanghai and the provinces of Jiangsu, Zhejiang and Anhui.
Accounting for nearly a quarter of the country's gross domestic product in 2025, the region is one of China's most dynamic economic powerhouses.
With the opening of the new lines, Anhui's high-speed rail mileage has reached 3,300 km, the longest in the YRD region, said the Shanghai group.
Five counties along the routes — Hexian county of Ma'anshan, Suixi county of Huaibei, Guzhen county of Bengbu, and Mengcheng and Lixin counties of Bozhou — are connected to the high-speed rail network for the first time.
In the initial phase of operation starting Friday morning, the new railways will operate 72 scheduled bullet trains daily.
Entrepreneur Duan Yanling, who lives in Huaibei city, frequently travels to Shanghai to visit her son and to Hefei for her business.
Previously, high-speed trains had to pass by Xuzhou in the neighboring Jiangsu province to link Huaibei with Hefei and Shanghai.
"With the new lines, my travel times to Shanghai and Hefei have both been reduced by about an hour, and I have more options to schedule my travel," she said.
The Shanghai group stated that in the first quarter of next year, the frequency and reach of train services on the three rail lines will be increased to better meet the public's travel needs.
Anhui has 16 prefecture-level cities, all of which were connected by high-speed rail by 2019, according to the provincial government.
In China's administrative divisions, each prefecture-level city oversees several counties and county-level cities.
Anhui has 59 such counties and county-level cities. As of Friday, 44 of these regions in the province have been connected to the high-speed rail network. Several more are expected to join the network by 2027.
There are a total of 15 stations along the three new lines, including 10 newly built stations.
One of the new stations, the Suzhouxi Railway Station on the Huaibei-Bengbu high-speed railway, can accommodate up to 1,300 people at peak times, according to Zhang Xupeng, chief engineer of the Suzhouxi Railway Station project from China Railway No 10 Engineering Group.
Local tourism authorities expect to attract more visitors with easier transport mobility.
Ma'anshan has launched a campaign offering residents from the provincial capital of Hefei free admission to 18 local State-owned tourist attractions in the city in December, according to the local government.
The city is also collaborating with local businesses to offer package deals and discounts.
The Chaohu-Ma'anshan high-speed rail line includes the world's largest-span three-tower cable-stayed bridge — the Ma'anshan Yangtze River rail-cum-road bridge.
The Huaibei-Bengbu high-speed rail line features 23 bridges, including the largest railway bridge spanning the Huaihe River, according to the China Railway Shanghai Group.
