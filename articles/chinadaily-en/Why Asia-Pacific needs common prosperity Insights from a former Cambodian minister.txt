Why Asia-Pacific needs common prosperity? Insights from a former Cambodian minister

ARTICLE TEXT:
In a recent interview with China Daily's Global Lens, Pou Sothirak, Cambodia's former minister of industry, mines and energy, said that the 2026 APEC Economic Leaders' Meeting offers an important opportunity for regional economies to deepen cooperation and share development opportunities.
Although Cambodia is not an APEC member, Sothirak expressed high expectations for the upcoming meeting in Shenzhen.
He stressed that regional cooperation should go beyond expanding trade and attracting investment. It should also help smaller economies strengthen their technological and industrial capabilities, enabling them to benefit more fully from regional economic integration. China can play a constructive role in this process through technology sharing, skills development and improved market access.
The APEC leaders' meeting, which will be held in Shenzhen, is expected to bring new momentum for regional economies to collaborate amid challenging times. By embracing openness and strengthening cooperation, Asia-Pacific economies can turn shared challenges into opportunities and work toward common prosperity.
