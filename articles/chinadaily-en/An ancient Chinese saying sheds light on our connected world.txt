      What kind of world do we want to create? To explore this question, we begin with an ancient Chinese saying that has traveled across more than 1,600 years of history. “志合者不以山海为远” means "No mountain or ocean can distance people who have shared aspirations." The message behind the saying continues to resonate in today's interconnected world.
