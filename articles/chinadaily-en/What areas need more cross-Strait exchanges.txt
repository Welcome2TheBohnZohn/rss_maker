What areas need more cross-Strait exchanges?

The complementary strengths of industries and supply chains on both sides of the Taiwan Strait can create greater opportunities for Taiwan's economic growth. "After future reunification across the Strait, with access to the Chinese mainland market, Taiwan's economy would no longer be constrained by external forces," said Zhong Houtao, a professor at the University of International Relations.
