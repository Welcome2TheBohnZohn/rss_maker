Xi attends BRICS summit in New Delhi
NEW DELHI -- Chinese President Xi Jinping attended here on Saturday the 18th BRICS Summit.
---Xi says BRICS cooperation mechanism has become world's most influential platform for cooperation among emerging markets, developing countries
---Xi says BRICS countries should firmly stand on right side of history
---Xi calls on BRICS to pioneer in innovative development
---Xi urges BRICS to play pioneering role in maintaining peace, development
---Xi warns against war in Middle East and Gulf region
---Xi says China to work with BRICS members to play due role in achieving peace, tranquility in Middle East and Gulf region
---Xi calls on BRICS to pioneer in boosting mutual learning among civilizations
---Xi says BRICS should pioneer in improving global governance
---Xi urges BRICS countries to strengthen cooperation in AI
---Xi proposes initiative to promote new industrialization through AI among BRICS countries
---Xi calls on BRICS to oppose Cold War mentality, bloc confrontation
---Xi calls on BRICS to oppose violations of other countries' sovereignty, interference in their internal affairs
---Xi calls on BRICS to uphold UN authority
---Xi calls on BRICS to reject all forms of protectionism
---Xi calls on BRICS to lead global governance in cyberspace, outer space, deep sea, and amplify voice of Global South
