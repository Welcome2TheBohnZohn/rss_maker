Editor's note: President Xi Jinping held six bilateral talks with leaders attending the APEC Economic Leaders' Meeting in Shenzhen, Guangdong province, on Tuesday.
President Xi Jinping called on Tuesday for China and Vietnam to enhance mutual trust and solidarity, deepen comprehensive cooperation, and jointly safeguard peace and stability and promote development and prosperity.
Xi, who is also general secretary of the Communist Party of China Central Committee, made the remarks during a meeting with General Secretary of the Communist Party of Vietnam Central Committee and Vietnamese President To Lam in Shenzhen.
He described China and Vietnam as "comrades and brothers", saying that "the rougher the seas, the more we must pull together".
While calling for promoting the alignment of their interests, Xi underlined the need for both countries to boost connectivity, advance the early establishment of smart ports and cross-border economic cooperation zones, expand collaboration in digital transformation and green, low-carbon development, and explore partnership in applications of artificial intelligence.
He called on both sides to properly manage their differences in the South China Sea issue, steadily advance maritime cooperation, and strengthen cooperation within frameworks such as the United Nations, China-ASEAN cooperation, and Lancang-Mekong cooperation.
Xi also expressed China's willingness to strengthen mutual learning with Vietnam in development experiences, firmly support each other, and deepen exchanges on party self-supervision and self-governance.
To Lam expressed his congratulations on the successful convening of the fifth plenary session of the 20th CPC Central Committee.
He said that Vietnam highly values its relationship with China, and regards it as its most important strategic partner.
In the current complex international situation, Vietnam is willing to strengthen solidarity and cooperation with China to aid each other's development, he added.
