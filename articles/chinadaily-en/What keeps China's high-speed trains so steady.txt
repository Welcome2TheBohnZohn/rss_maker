      A cup of tea can stand upright on a Chinese high-speed train. But the real story isn't the cup. It's the wheel beneath the train.
At 350 km/h, the wheel diameter tolerance is tighter than a human hair. Every wheel has its own digital ID.
This National Day holiday, millions are traveling home. These wheels carry us safely to our loved ones.
