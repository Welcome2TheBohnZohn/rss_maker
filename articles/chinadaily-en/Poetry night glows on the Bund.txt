As part of the 2026 Lu Xun Literature Week, the "People's poetry: Lu Xun Literary Prize Bund poetry night" took place on Aug 29 at No 1 the Bund, Shanghai. Poets from across the country gathered alongside local Shanghai poets for an enchanting evening of verse along the waterfront.
