TITLE:
Closer cooperation with Malaysia to bring more benefits

PUBLISHED:
2026-11-21

SOURCE:
China Daily English

CATEGORY:
Authoritative PRC Media

URL:
https://www.chinadaily.com.cn/a/202611/21/WS6b00e5f1e4b03ebf8519dcd7.html

ARTICLE TEXT:
President Xi Jinping called on China and Malaysia to deepen strategic coordination and jointly advance global governance reform to safeguard the common interests of developing countries during a meeting with Malaysian Prime Minister Anwar Ibrahim on Friday.
Xi recalled that he and Anwar reached important consensus on building a high-level strategic China-Malaysia community with a shared future in April last year.
China is ready to work with Malaysia to uphold the original aspiration of establishing diplomatic ties, foster greater momentum for cooperation and further advance the building of a China-Malaysia community with a shared future to bring greater benefits to both peoples, Xi said.
He emphasized that bilateral relations enjoy a solid political foundation, distinct strengths in cooperation and broad prospects for development.
China and Malaysia should maintain high-level strategic communication and support each other in safeguarding their respective national sovereignty, security and development interests, Xi said. The two sides should also ensure high-quality development and operation of key projects, expand agricultural trade, and broaden cooperation in tourism, education and other areas, he added.
Xi called for joint efforts to advance the reform of global governance, strengthen coordination and cooperation within multilateral frameworks, and safeguard common interests.
Anwar said the Taiwan question is China's internal affair, and Malaysia firmly adheres to the one-China policy.
He said Malaysia is ready to strengthen strategic coordination with China, give full play to the role of bilateral cooperation mechanisms, ensure the sound operation of key infrastructure projects and enhance cooperation in emerging areas such as artificial intelligence.
