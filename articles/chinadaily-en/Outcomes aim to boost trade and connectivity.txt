Outcomes aim to boost trade and connectivity

ARTICLE TEXT:
The Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation economic leaders have agreed on measures to facilitate cross-border trade, strengthen regional connectivity and harness emerging technologies to promote employment and prosperity.
The measures are set out in three documents adopted at the 33rd APEC Economic Leaders' Meeting, which concluded in Shenzhen, Guangdong province, on Thursday.
All three outcome documents support efforts to realize the APEC Putrajaya Vision 2040 goal of building an open, dynamic, resilient and peaceful Asia-Pacific community.
The APEC Trade Digitalization Cooperation Framework (2027-2030) aims to promote cross-border trade and investment, as advances in digital technology continue to profoundly reshape international trade and the way it is managed.
The framework promotes policy exchange and the use of digital technologies to streamline trade procedures.
To reduce clearance costs and time, the framework encourages economies to make electronic trade administration forms available to the public and to recognize electronically submitted forms as legally equivalent to paper versions.
It also sets out plans for cooperation to facilitate cross-border data flows and strengthen business and consumer trust in digital transactions. The framework acknowledges the importance of data protection and privacy.
The APEC Connectivity Blueprint addresses the movement of goods, services, capital and people across the region.
Building on cooperation under the 2015-25 blueprint, it encourages economies to advance physical, institutional and people-to-people connectivity toward a seamlessly and comprehensively connected and integrated Asia-Pacific region.
The blueprint encourages economies to develop, maintain and upgrade quality infrastructure. To reduce institutional barriers to trade and investment, it also promotes customs modernization, cooperation on technical standards and good regulatory practices.
The blueprint also encourages efforts to address supply chain chokepoints, enhance resilience and minimize disruptions. To strengthen people-to-people connectivity, it promotes business mobility through the APEC Business Travel Card, alongside exchanges in education and vocational training.
The meeting also endorsed the APEC Statement on Harnessing Digital and Emerging Technologies for Employment and Prosperity in the Asia-Pacific Region. The statement calls for using digital and emerging technologies to promote high-quality and full employment and accelerate the digital transformation of small and medium-sized enterprises.
It emphasizes capacity building, wider digital access, and digital literacy and skills training to help workers and businesses adapt.
