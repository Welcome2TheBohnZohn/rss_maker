      Trade relations between China and the European Union are feeling the heat following the release of the latest customs data in mid-July.
In the first half of 2026, China's trade surplus in goods with the EU climbed to a record high.
Yet the broader picture tells a more nuanced story. For example, a considerable share of China-EU trade is generated by European companies operating in China, with 40 percent of their output exported back to Europe, leaving China with the surplus and the EU with the profits.
