      Just as seedlings grow into a forest, young people involved in the "50,000 in Five Years" initiative proposed by President Xi Jinping can become a force for positive change. American friend Ronald Sakolsky shared this message with students in Inner Mongolia, drawing on his friendship with Chinese tree planter Yin Yuzhen to inspire young people to believe in themselves and be the change they wish to see in the world.
