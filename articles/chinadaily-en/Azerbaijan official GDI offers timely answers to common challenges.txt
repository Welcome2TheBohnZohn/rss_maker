Azerbaijan official: GDI offers timely answers to common challenges

ARTICLE TEXT:
In an exclusive interview with China Daily, Elchin Amirbayov, special representative of President Ilham Aliyev of Azerbaijan, stated that the Global Development Initiative (GDI) is important and timely.
During his visit to China from Nov 16 to 20 at the invitation of the Chinese People's Institute of Foreign Affairs, he said that amid multiple challenges, including geopolitical turbulence, development imbalances, and climate change, the GDI places development at the core of the global agenda and offers equal development opportunities for all countries.
Amirbayov welcomed Chinese enterprises to Azerbaijan to take part in bilateral cooperation in areas such as renewable energy and to help turn the UN Sustainable Development Goals from blueprints into reality.
