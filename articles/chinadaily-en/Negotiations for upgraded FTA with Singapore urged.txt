TITLE:
Negotiations for upgraded FTA with Singapore urged

PUBLISHED:
2026-11-21

SOURCE:
China Daily English

CATEGORY:
Authoritative PRC Media

URL:
https://www.chinadaily.com.cn/a/202611/21/WS6b00e228e4b03ebf8519dcbe.html

ARTICLE TEXT:
President Xi Jinping on Friday called on China and Singapore to launch negotiations for an upgraded Version 4.0 of their free trade agreement and deliver signature cooperation outcomes in areas such as the digital economy, green development and artificial intelligence.
While meeting with Singaporean Prime Minister Lawrence Wong, Xi stressed the need for both sides to strengthen strategic communication, enhance solidarity and coordination, and continuously elevate the all-round high-quality future-oriented partnership to new heights.
China and Singapore should be sincere friends who trust one another, consolidate political mutual trust, safeguard common interests and support each other in pursuing development paths suited to their respective national conditions, Xi said.
China and Singapore should be development partners leading in innovation, Xi said, urging the two countries to make sustained efforts in advancing high-quality Belt and Road cooperation.
China and Singapore should be friendly neighbors who learn from each other, deepen exchanges on governance and inter-party cooperation, and expand people-to-people exchanges, he added.
Xi called on both countries to serve as progressive forces in upholding international fairness and justice, strengthen coordination and cooperation within multilateral frameworks and keep East Asian cooperation on the right track.
Wong noted that cooperation between Singapore and China continues to deepen, and the all-round partnership remains robust.
Singapore firmly adheres to the one-China policy and opposes "Taiwan independence", he stressed.
He said Singapore attaches great importance to its partnership with China and is willing to strengthen exchanges at all levels.
