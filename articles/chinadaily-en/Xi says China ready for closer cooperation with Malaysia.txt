TITLE:
Xi says China ready for closer cooperation with Malaysia

PUBLISHED:
2026-11-20

SOURCE:
China Daily English

CATEGORY:
Authoritative PRC Media

URL:

ARTICLE TEXT:
SHENZHEN -- China stands ready to foster greater momentum for cooperation with Malaysia to bring more benefits to people of both countries, President Xi Jinping said Friday.
China-Malaysia relations have maintained high-level development since the two sides reached important consensus on building a high-level strategic China-Malaysia community with a shared future and ushered in a new "Golden 50 Years" for bilateral ties in April last year, he said.
Xi made the remarks when meeting with Malaysian Prime Minister Anwar Ibrahim in Shenzhen, South China's Guangdong province.
China and Malaysia should maintain high-level strategic communication and support each other in safeguarding their respective national sovereignty, security and development interests, Xi said at the meeting.
The two sides should also ensure high-quality development and operation of key projects, expand and improve agricultural product trade, and broaden cooperation in tourism, education and other areas, he added.
Xi called for joint efforts to advance the reform of global governance, strengthen coordination and cooperation within multilateral frameworks such as APEC, and safeguard the common interests of developing countries.
Anwar said the Taiwan question is China's internal affair, and Malaysia firmly adheres to the One-China principle.
He said Malaysia is ready to strengthen strategic coordination with China, give full play to the role of bilateral cooperation mechanisms, ensure the sound operation of key infrastructure projects, and enhance cooperation in emerging areas such as artificial intelligence.
Malaysia will also promote inter-civilization exchanges and dialogue, step up coordination and collaboration in international affairs, and keep elevating the level of Malaysia-China relations, Anwar said.
Anwar attended the 33rd APEC Economic Leaders' Meeting held from Wednesday to Thursday in Shenzhen.
