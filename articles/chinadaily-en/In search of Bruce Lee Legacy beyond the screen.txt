ARTICLE TEXT:
What makes Bruce Lee an icon whose influence still resonates more than five decades after his death? To mark the 86th anniversary of his birth, we revisit his legacy through the eyes of three people inspired by his life and philosophy.
Joseph Clarke, an Irish Jeet Kune Do practitioner and young Sinologist who recently relocated to China; American filmmaker and kung fu expert Laurence Brahm; and Wang Zhipeng, chairman of the Beijing Wing Chun Martial Arts Association, share their insights into Lee's martial arts philosophy, his Wing Chun roots and the challenges he faced in Hollywood.
From his famous "Be water" philosophy to his belief in breaking free from rigid styles, Lee championed adaptability, self-expression and constant self-discovery. Drawing on both Eastern and Western traditions, he used martial arts to challenge stereotypes and bridge cultures. His legacy lives on, inspiring new generations to think beyond convention and forge their own paths.
