Greece and China, as two ancient civilizations, have long engaged in exchanges in ideas, art and trade, said Evgenios Kalpyris, Greek ambassador to China, in an interview with China Daily, adding that culture acts as a powerful medium for civilizational dialogue, creating an unbiased platform for mutual learning.
In his view, Greece and China serve as bridges between the East and the West. Various cultural activities help strengthen people-to-people ties and carry forward the legacy of dialogue between the two civilizations. Watch now!
