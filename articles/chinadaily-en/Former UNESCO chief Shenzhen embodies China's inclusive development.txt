ARTICLE TEXT:
The 33rd APEC Economic Leaders' Meeting will be held in Shenzhen from Nov 18 to 19. Irina Bokova, former director-general of UNESCO, told China Daily that Shenzhen's transformation from a fishing village into a bustling metropolis with universities, technology, culture, economy, and finance is remarkable. As a green city and a city of learning, Shenzhen exemplifies China's inclusive development. Amid global turbulence, Bokova hopes this APEC meeting will foster dialogue, dispel mistrust and bring economies together to tackle world challenges. Watch the video!
