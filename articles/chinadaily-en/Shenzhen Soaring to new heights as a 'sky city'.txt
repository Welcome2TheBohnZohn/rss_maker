      Shenzhen is rapidly evolving into a "sky city", where the low-altitude economy is seamlessly integrated into everyday life. Drones swiftly deliver packages, efficiently perform traffic patrols, and transport medical supplies across the city.
At the heart of this transformation is Bao'an district, an innovation hub that is generating new jobs and fueling economic growth. With its drones making a global impact, Shenzhen is embracing openness and innovation, marking just the beginning of its journey as a "sky city".
