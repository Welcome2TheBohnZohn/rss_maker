ARTICLE TEXT:
SHENZHEN -- President Xi Jinping on Wednesday welcomed US President Donald Trump at Shenzhen Bao'an International Airport in South China's Guangdong province.
Trump is in Shenzhen to attend the 33rd Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation Economic Leaders' Meeting, scheduled for Nov 18-19.
Amid welcome music, Xi and Trump shook hands warmly. Honor guards of the Chinese People's Liberation Army presented arms to salute, and a girl handed the US leader a bouquet.
The national flags of China and the United States fluttered in the wind as a military band played the national anthems of the two countries, and fighter jets flew overhead in salute. The two heads of state waved to a welcoming young crowd.
Xi recalled his state visit to the United States in September this year, when the US presidential couple came to the airport in person and hosted a grand welcoming ceremony, noting that China is a nation with a tradition of courtesy that values reciprocating goodwill.
