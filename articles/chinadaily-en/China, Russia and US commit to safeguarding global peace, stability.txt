TITLE:
China, Russia and US commit to safeguarding global peace, stability

PUBLISHED:
2026-11-20

SOURCE:

CATEGORY:
Authoritative PRC Media

ARTICLE TEXT:
The leaders of China, Russia and the United States stressed their shared commitment to maintaining strategic stability and international security at a historic trilateral summit on Thursday, pledging to advance United Nations reform, increase energy supplies and hold talks on reducing nuclear risks.
President Xi Jinping, Russian President Vladimir Putin and US President Donald Trump made the commitment in the China-Russia-US Heads of State Declaration toward Peace, issued following their unprecedented trilateral meeting in Shenzhen, Guangdong province.
"The three heads of state reaffirmed that a nuclear war cannot be won and must never be fought," the declaration said, adding that the three countries will conduct a dialogue on reducing nuclear risks and preventing the proliferation of nuclear weapons.
As permanent members of the UN Security Council, the three countries will remain committed to reforming the UN so that it can respond more effectively to global challenges and conform to its fundamental mission of international peace and security.
On energy security, the three countries announced plans to increase the production and supply of refined petroleum products, while reaffirming the importance of maintaining stable global energy supplies.
The declaration also addressed the jurisdiction of international judicial institutions. The three leaders agreed that the International Criminal Court and other similar institutions must not abuse their jurisdiction.
Xi, while presiding over the summit, described the first-ever gathering of the three countries' top leaders as "groundbreaking". He said history has shown that peace among major countries brings stability to the world, while their contention would only lead to chaos in the world.
He stressed that humanity, living in the same global village, is "a community with a shared future where we all stand to benefit or lose together".
In this global village, major countries shoulder special responsibilities, with their positive interaction being "not optional, but a must", he said.
Xi called on the three countries to set good examples, work together to meet global challenges, jointly uphold world peace and stability, and make a greater contribution to the progress of humanity.
The Chinese president made a four-point proposal on how major countries could find the right way to get along and jointly underpin world peace — staying committed to mutual respect, peaceful coexistence, win-win cooperation, and dialogue and consultation.
On mutual respect, he highlighted the need for the three countries to respect one another's sovereignty and territorial integrity, social systems and development paths of their own choice, and their respective core interests and major concerns.
On peaceful coexistence, Xi said China, Russia and the US should be partners and friends and avoid conflict or confrontation.
The Thucydides Trap is by no means a destiny, he added.
Turning to economic cooperation, Xi highlighted the complementary strengths of the three economies and urged them to expand trade and economic ties rather than pursue decoupling or disruptions to supply chains.
On dialogue and consultation, he urged joint efforts from the three countries to maintain regular high-level exchanges, keep dialogue channels open at all levels, and establish crisis communication and prevention mechanisms, to ensure that "misjudgments occur less, differences can be managed, and frictions will not escalate".
The international community expects major countries to do more to promote peace talks, Xi pointed out, while stressing that China, Russia and the US should continue to encourage parties to a conflict to sit down and talk, and settle disputes through political and diplomatic means.
Wrapping up the gathering, Xi described the summit as "positive, constructive and fruitful", saying the three countries share the same view on safeguarding world peace.
As long as dialogue continues and the door to engagement remains open, there is hope for peace, Xi said, while expressing confidence that the seeds planted at the Shenzhen summit would take root and bear fruit.
Putin emphasized the importance of sovereign equality, noninterference in internal affairs and finding mutually acceptable solutions through dialogue and cooperation.
Russia stands ready to work with China and the US on the basis of mutual respect and equality, peacefully manage differences, promote free and open international trade, and strengthen cooperation on energy and food security and global governance of artificial intelligence, he said.
Trump told the two leaders that the three nuclear powers bear an important responsibility for maintaining global peace and stability, stressing the need to prevent nuclear proliferation and avoid the catastrophic consequences of nuclear weapons.
The joint declaration from the China-Russia-US summit indicated that the unprecedented gathering has been successful and has yielded significant outcomes, said Wu Xinbo, dean of the Institute of International Studies at Fudan University.
Wu said the four-point proposal put forward by Xi is crucial not only to shaping a new type of major-country relations, but also to guiding relations among countries more broadly in a changing international landscape.
He rejected comparisons between the Shenzhen summit and the 1945 Yalta Conference, arguing that the two gatherings differed fundamentally in their purposes.
The Yalta Conference was primarily concerned with the postwar division of spheres of influence among major powers, whereas the Shenzhen summit sought to strengthen cooperation among the three countries to promote global peace, security, stability, development and governance, he said.
Zhu Feng, dean of the School of International Studies at Nanjing University, said the summit and its declaration reflected a new approach to addressing the challenges of the times.
The declaration's central message was that major countries share responsibility for safeguarding world peace, without which sustainable development cannot be achieved, Zhu said. He described the summit as a major breakthrough in head-of-state diplomacy, saying it had helped the three countries establish a greater degree of common understanding about their responsibilities in managing international affairs.
