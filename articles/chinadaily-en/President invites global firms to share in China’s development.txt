TITLE:
President invites global firms to share in China’s development

PUBLISHED:

ARTICLE TEXT:
President Xi Jinping invited businesses worldwide on Wednesday to share in China's development, pledging to open its vast market further, create more opportunities for companies to innovate and grow, and offer investors a more stable and predictable business environment.
Xi made the remarks when delivering a keynote speech at the APEC CEO Summit 2026 in Shenzhen, South China's Guangdong province. Chilean President Jose Antonio Kast, Malaysian Prime Minister Anwar Ibrahim and representatives of 115 Fortune Global 500 companies were among those attending.
Describing Asia-Pacific business communities as "pioneers of the global economy", Xi encouraged business leaders to take an active part in China's reform and opening-up and to "share in the opportunities created by Chinese modernization".
Xi said that China, the world's second-largest consumer market, offers businesses new opportunities in areas ranging from new energy vehicles to smart homes and from goods and services for older consumers to winter tourism.
As China accelerates the development of a big, open market and increases imports of quality products and services, more diverse demand will be unleashed, he said.
He also said that China will continue to host the "Export to China" series and other events to open efficient channels to help quality products from across the world enter the Chinese market.
China is a supersized innovation testing ground where frontier technologies are tested, improved and upgraded in real-world scenarios and are then turned into high-quality growth drivers, Xi said.
He pointed to robots at work in factories and on farms, autonomous driving and drone delivery as examples of how companies are putting new technologies to use.
Xi said that China will deepen integration between technological and industrial innovation, and support the transformation and upgrading of traditional industries, the expansion of emerging industries and the forward-looking planning of industries of the future.
Xi said China will provide global business communities with an environment that enables mutually reinforcing cooperation.
In the face of unilateralism and protectionism, China has remained committed to true multilateralism, and has steadily brought stability and predictability to world economic growth and international trade and economic cooperation.
"No matter how the international landscape may evolve, China will not waver in its pursuit of win-win cooperation with all other countries," he said.
China will not only cut tariffs and expand market access, but will also go a step further by carrying out reform in challenging areas, such as rules, regulations and standards, Xi said. It will allow full manufacturing access with an accommodating business climate and steadily expand opening-up in the services sector, he said.
For Annette Gregg, CEO of the World Trade Centers Association, the message from Xi's speech to companies is clear. "If you want to do business with China, China is open to you and ready for your business," she said.
Gregg said that China's advances in robotics, artificial intelligence and green technology hold particular appeal for the United States market.
Ramon Jara, a director of Chile's Antofagasta Minerals, welcomed Xi's invitation. "It's very good news for all of us trying to jointly develop the future of this region," he said.
The two-day CEO summit launched the Shenzhen Initiative on Wednesday, marking the first time in its 30-year history that it has published an outcome document in the form of an initiative.
China is hosting APEC for the third time, having previously hosted the leaders' meeting in Shanghai in 2001 and Beijing in 2014. Xi will chair the 33rd APEC Economic Leaders' Meeting on Thursday.
In his address on Wednesday to the CEO summit, Xi said that China is ready to work with other economies to forge ahead with resolve, jointly build an Asia-Pacific community and bring prosperity for all in the region.
"Today, we find ourselves in a changing and turbulent world," Xi said, noting the lack of momentum in global growth and rampant unilateralism and protectionism.
"Still, global trade keeps growing against headwinds, and breakthroughs are being made in emerging technologies at a faster pace, exerting a profound impact on global development," he said.
To bring prosperity for all in the Asia-Pacific region, Xi called for efforts to uphold peace and stability, build bridges for opening-up and cooperation, boost innovation to drive growth, and expand ways for mutual benefit and win-win outcomes.
"There can be no prosperity without security, and no security without stability," Xi said. He urged economies to resolve differences through dialogue and consultation and overcome challenges through solidarity and cooperation.
"History tells us that opening doors brings progress while closing doors leaves one behind," he said, calling for steady progress toward a Free Trade Area of the Asia-Pacific.
Founded in 1989, APEC now comprises 21 economies that together accounted for 61.6 percent of global real GDP, 50.1 percent of the world's total exports of goods and 48.5 percent of world total imports of goods in 2025, according to APEC figures.
Xi urged APEC members to forge science, technology and innovation partnerships, deepen cooperation in the digital economy and AI, and promote green and low-carbon transition.
He also called on members to remove trade and investment barriers to build an open Asia-Pacific economy, while promoting development that is balanced and delivers benefits to all to ensure both efficiency and fairness.
Noting that Shenzhen is part of the Guangdong-Hong Kong-Macao Greater Bay Area, Xi said China supports the establishment of a World Association for Bay Areas Economic Cooperation to deepen exchanges and interactions among bay areas across the world.
Jeffrey Sachs, director of the Center for Sustainable Development at Columbia University in the US, said: "I believe in China's leadership in the next phase of green and digital development. Shenzhen is the world-leading cluster of innovation."
He said that China wants an open, peaceful world environment where everybody can prosper, and China sees trade and investment as a path to shared prosperity.
