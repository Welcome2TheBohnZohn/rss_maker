TITLE:
Pakistani astronaut set to become first foreign crew member aboard Tiangong

ARTICLE TEXT:
China's Tiangong space station is poised to welcome its first foreign astronaut — Pakistani astronaut Muhammad Zeeshan Ali, who will also become the first citizen of his country to travel beyond Earth's atmosphere.
The wing commander in the Pakistan Air Force is set to fly to the orbital outpost with two Chinese astronauts — Major General Tang Hongbo, commander of the Shenzhou XXIV mission, and Colonel Wu Yi, the crew's spaceflight engineer. Their spacecraft is scheduled to lift off aboard a Long March 2F rocket at 8:18 am on Wednesday from the Jiuquan Satellite Launch Center in northwestern China, Zhang Jingbo, a senior engineer and spokesman for the China Manned Space Agency, said.
At a news conference on Tuesday morning at the Jiuquan spaceport, Zhang told reporters that the rocket's propellant injection was scheduled to begin at its service tower later that day and that other prelaunch preparations were proceeding in an orderly manner.
"After the Shenzhou XXIV spacecraft enters its preset orbital position, it will activate the rapid autonomous rendezvous-docking mode and use about three and a half hours to approach and connect with the front port on the Tianhe core module," he said.
