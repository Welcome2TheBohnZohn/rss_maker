Max Baucus: China, US should build more trust and maintain balance

Max Baucus, former US ambassador to China, said China is doing an excellent job in the Asia-Pacific and its offer to convene AI cooperation is positive. He made the remarks during the US-China Political Dialogue 2026, held in Beijing on Nov 12. He stated that trust comes down to US-China relations, adding that the two countries could compete but still stay balanced.
