Shenzhen library: Straight out of Interstellar?

When British vlogger Toby Symonds stepped into Shenzhen Library North, he couldn't help exclaiming, "It looks like a scene out of Interstellar!" With futuristic, multi-level book stacks and robots that find and deliver books, this library is both sci-fi spectacular and seriously practical. Who knew a library could be this cool?
