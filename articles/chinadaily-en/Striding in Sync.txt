ARTICLE TEXT:
Growing up in Sydney and now building a career in Shenzhen, Laurie Pearcey's life is the story of a cross-border bond forged across ocean expanses.
In Sydney he met his Chinese wife; in Shenzhen he has embraced the Guangdong-Hong Kong-Macao Greater Bay Area and all it has to offer.
Beyond personal ties, deep-rooted links in education, trade and technology — from laser innovation to drone applications — unite people and industries in both cities.
With Shenzhen hosting the APEC Economic Leaders' Meeting, this mutual engagement highlights openness, innovation and cooperation — the three priorities of the 2026 APEC "China Year".
APEC was established in Australia in 1989, since when member economies have striven to improve the prosperity of their peoples.
