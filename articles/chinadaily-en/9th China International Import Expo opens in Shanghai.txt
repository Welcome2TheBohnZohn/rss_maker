SHANGHAI - The ninth edition of the China International Import Expo (CIIE), the world's first national-level exhibition dedicated to imports, opened on Thursday in Shanghai in East China.
Chinese Vice-Premier Ding Xuexiang delivered a keynote speech at the opening ceremony.
