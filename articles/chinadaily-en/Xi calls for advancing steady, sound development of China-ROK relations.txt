TITLE:
Xi calls for advancing steady, sound development of China-ROK relations

ARTICLE TEXT:
SHENZHEN -- President Xi Jinping on Wednesday called for advancing the steady and sound development of relations between China and the Republic of Korea (ROK) as next year marks the 35th anniversary of diplomatic ties between the two "friendly neighbors and strategic cooperative partners."
Xi made the remarks during his meeting with ROK President Lee Jae Myung, who is in Shenzhen to attend the 33rd Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation (APEC) Economic Leaders' Meeting on Nov 18-19.
The two countries should uphold strategic independence, maintain the political foundation for bilateral relations, and further consolidate and develop the relationship, Xi stressed.
China and the ROK have substantially concluded the second phase of negotiations on the China-ROK Free Trade Agreement. The two countries should make full use of this institutional arrangement to strengthen macroeconomic policy coordination and foster a collaborative ecosystem for emerging technology industries such as artificial intelligence, Xi said.
China also stands ready to work with the ROK to facilitate people-to-people exchanges and carry out sound and beneficial cultural exchanges in a step-by-step manner, Xi said.
The two sides should further strengthen multilateral coordination, jointly uphold true multilateralism, advance the building of an Asia-Pacific community and the Free Trade Area of the Asia-Pacific, make greater contributions to regional peace and development, and maintain the stability and smooth functioning of global industrial and supply chains, Xi said.
For his part, Lee said that the two countries have seen sustained momentum in the comprehensive restoration of ROK-China relations, with bilateral economic and trade cooperation growing rapidly, people-to-people exchanges becoming increasingly frequent, and mutual perceptions steadily improving.
The ROK's commitment to the one-China principle has not changed and will not change, Lee stressed.
The ROK hopes to maintain close high-level exchanges with China, strengthen cooperation in trade, investment, and industrial and supply chains, foster closer people-to-people bonds, and pursue a more mature ROK-China strategic partnership, he said.
