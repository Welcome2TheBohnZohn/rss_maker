      From mountain terraces to ancient landmarks and island waters, these five destinations offer inspiration for a National Day holiday getaway, boasting fine mild autumn conditions in early October.
Bijie, Guizhou — Music among the terraces
In Bijie's Dafang county, the curved terraces of Shexiang Ancient Town resemble a giant fingerprint. The landscape has also become a setting for open-air concerts, with lights tracing the terraces as music fills the night.
