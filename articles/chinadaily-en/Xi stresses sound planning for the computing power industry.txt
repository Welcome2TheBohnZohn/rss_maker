Ulaanqab in the Inner Mongolia autonomous region has made significant progress in developing its green computing industry. What comes next?
During a visit to the Qahar High-tech Industrial Development Zone on Friday, President Xi Jinping stressed the need for sound planning and coordination across computing power, electricity supply and water use. Local officials and industry representatives said his guidance boosted their confidence and pledged to plan ahead and promote the orderly development of this emerging industry.
