中国国防部邀请驻华武官赴贵州、四川参观访问
MND of the PRC Invites Foreign Military Attachés to China to Visit Guizhou and Sichuan
10月29日下午，国防部举行例行记者会，国防部新闻发言人张晓刚大校发布消息。
张晓刚：应中国国防部邀请，11月2日至7日，来自50余个国家近90名驻华武官将赴贵州、四川参观访问。其间，各国驻华武官将参访军地有关单位和长征遗址遗迹，了解贵州省军区、四川省军区，以及当地经济社会建设发展有关情况，感悟伟大长征精神。
Zhang Xiaogang: Upon the invitation of the MND of the PRC, about 90 foreign military attachés to China from over 50 countries will visit Guizhou and Sichuan from November 2 to 7. During their stay, they will visit relevant military and civilian units and revolutionary sites of the Long March, know more about the military commands of Guizhou and Sichuan as well as local economic and social development, and gain a deeper understanding of the great spirit of the Long March.
