菲方应严格约束个别政客的危险言行
The Philippine Side Should Rein in the Dangerous Words and Actions of Certain Politicians
11月13日下午，国防部新闻发言人陈曦大校就近期涉军问题发布消息。
记者：据报道，菲律宾防长近日在接受采访时称，考虑到有20万菲侨民在台湾生活，菲正为各种可能情势作准备。中方若试图武力接管台湾，将是得不偿失的选项。请问对此有何评论？
Question: The Philippine Defense Secretary reportedly said in an interview that with 200,000 Filipinos residing in Taiwan, the Philippines is preparing for all contingencies, and that it would be counterproductive for China to try to seize Taiwan by force. What's your comment on this?
陈曦：台湾问题纯属中国内政，如何解决是中国人自己的事，不容他人置喙。菲个别政客言论出于政治偏见和一己私利，背离菲政府在台湾问题上对中方作出的郑重承诺，中方对此强烈不满，坚决反对。中方维护主权和领土完整、实现国家统一的意志坚定不移。我们敦促菲方严格约束个别政客的危险言行，切实恪守一个中国原则，以实际行动维护中菲关系大局。
Chen Xi: The Taiwan question is purely China's internal affair. How to resolve it is a matter for the Chinese people, which brooks no foreign interference. Driven by political prejudice and self-interest, certain Filipino politicians made remarks which run counter to the solemn commitment the Philippine government has made to China on the Taiwan question. We strongly deplore and firmly oppose such statements. China is firm in our resolve to safeguard sovereignty and territorial integrity and achieve national reunification. We urge the Philippine side to rein in the dangerous words and actions of these politicians, abide by the one-China principle, and take real actions to maintain stable China-Philippines relations.
