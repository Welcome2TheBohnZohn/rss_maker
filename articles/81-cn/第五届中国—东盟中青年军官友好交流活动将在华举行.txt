第五届中国—东盟中青年军官友好交流活动将在华举行
The 5th China-ASEAN Mid-and-junior Level Officer Exchange to be Held in China
10月29日下午，国防部举行例行记者会，国防部新闻发言人张晓刚大校发布消息。
张晓刚：11月2日至8日，第五届中国—东盟中青年军官友好交流活动将在南京、杭州、上海、昆山举行，以“互信·责任·担当：共建亚洲和平家园——纪念中国—东盟全面战略伙伴关系5周年暨建立对话关系35周年”为主题，通过专题研讨、互动交流、参观见学等，增进中国—东盟中青年军官相互了解和友谊。
Zhang Xiaogang: The 5th China-ASEAN Mid-and-junior Level Officer Exchange will be held in Nanjing, Hangzhou, Shanghai and Kunshan from November 2 to 8. Themed "Trust, Responsibility, Commitment: Jointly Build a Peaceful Home in Asia—in Commemoration of the 5th Anniversary of China-ASEAN Comprehensive Strategic Partnership & the 35th Anniversary of China-ASEAN Dialogue Relations", the event will include such activities as themed seminars, exchanges and visits, which aim to enhance mutual understanding and friendship among mid-and-junior level officers from China and ASEAN countries.
