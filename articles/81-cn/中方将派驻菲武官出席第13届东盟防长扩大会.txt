中方将派驻菲武官出席第13届东盟防长扩大会

PUBLISHED:
2026-10-27

SOURCE:

ARTICLE TEXT:
10月29日，中国驻菲律宾使馆国防武官将作为中方代表，出席在宿务举行的第13届东盟防长扩大会，同各方就坚持对话协商、增进理解互信交换意见，重申坚定支持东盟在区域架构中的中心地位，促进地区防务安全对话合作，为维护地区和平稳定贡献力量。
The Chinese Side Will Send Its DA to the Philippines to Attend the 13th ADMM-Plus
The defense attaché of the Chinese Embassy to the Philippines will represent the Chinese side to attend the 13th ASEAN Defense Ministers’ Meeting Plus in Cebu on October 29. During the event, the Chinese side will exchange views with other parties on upholding dialogue and enhancing mutual understanding and trust, and reiterate China's firm support to ASEAN centrality in the regional architecture to promote regional defense and security dialogue and cooperation, and safeguard peace and stability in the region.
