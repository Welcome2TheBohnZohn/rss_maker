第13届陆军国际学员周将在陆军工程大学举办
The 13th International Army Cadets Week to be Held at the PLA Army Engineering University
10月29日下午，国防部举行例行记者会，国防部新闻发言人张晓刚大校发布消息。
张晓刚：11月2日至6日，第13届陆军国际学员周暨“未来·联创”国际军校学员军事创新大赛将在陆军工程大学举办，来自韩国、新加坡、巴基斯坦、埃及等11个国家11所军事院校的生长军官学员，与海军工程大学、陆军兵种大学等15所中国军校学员共同参加。本届学员周以“聚焦地区重点安全风险，深化国际军事安全合作，打造国际公共安全产品”为主题，搭建学术交流、创新实践等多边开放平台，进一步开拓参训学员国际视野、提升创新能力、凝聚合作共识，携手探索以军事创新破解全球性安全难题的有效途径。其间，外军学员与中国军校学员共训同学，还将现地参观了解中国高新技术发展成就。
Zhang Xiaogang: From November 2 to 6, the 13th International Army Cadets Week (IACW) & "Future Co-creation" International Military Cadets Innovation Competition will be held at the PLA Army Engineering University. Foreign cadets from 11 military academies of 11 countries including South Korea, Singapore, Pakistan and Egypt, together with cadets from 15 Chinese military academies such as the PLA Naval University of Engineering and the PLA Army Arms University, will participate in the event. Themed "Addressing Key Regional Security Risks, Deepening International Military Security Cooperation, and Delivering International Public Security Products", this IACW will provide an open and multilateral platform for academic exchanges and innovative practices that will further broaden the global vision of participating cadets, sharpen their innovation capacity, build consensus on cooperation, and jointly explore effective ways to address global security challenges through innovation in military fields. During the event, foreign cadets will study and undergo training together with their Chinese counterparts, and will learn about China's achievements in high-tech development through on-site visits.
