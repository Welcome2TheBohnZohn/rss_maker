中巴（基斯坦）将举行“喀喇昆仑－2026”陆军联合训练
China and Pakistan Will Hold Karakoram 2026 Joint Army Training
10月10日下午，国防部新闻发言人陈曦大校就近期涉军问题发布消息。
陈曦：10月中下旬，中国和巴基斯坦陆军将在新疆库尔勒地区举行“喀喇昆仑－2026”陆军联合训练，以高海拔地区联合反恐清剿行动为课题，组织无人机操作、运动搜索目标射击、远距离狙击等技能互训，并开展联合指挥和实兵行动演练。这是两国首次陆军联合训练，有助于双方深化交流合作，提升共同应对恐怖主义威胁能力。
Chen Xi: In mid and late October, Chinese and Pakistani armies will hold Karakoram 2026 joint army training in Korla, Xinjiang. Focusing on joint counter-terrorism operations in high-altitude areas, the activity will include skills training in UAV operation, on-the-move target search and firing, and long-range sniping, and cover joint command and live-force exercises. This is the first joint army training between the two countries, which will help the two sides deepen cooperation and strengthen interoperability in counter-terrorism.
