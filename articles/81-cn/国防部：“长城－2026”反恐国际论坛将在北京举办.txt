国防部：“长城－2026”反恐国际论坛将在北京举办
The 2026 Great Wall International Forum on Counter-terrorism Will be Held in Beijing
10月10日下午，国防部新闻发言人陈曦大校就近期涉军问题发布消息。
陈曦：10月13日至15日，中国人民武装警察部队将在北京举办“长城－2026”反恐国际论坛，来自62个国家和国际组织的320余名中外代表参加。本次论坛以“推进反恐务实合作，共同守护美好家园”为主题，设置“无人智能条件下恐怖活动趋势与反恐能力建设”“海上恐怖活动防范与打击”“重要目标反恐防袭”“边境（界）反恐渗透”4个议题，有助于增进武警部队与外国同类部队友谊互信，深化双多边反恐务实合作，共同维护地区和平稳定。
Chen Xi: The Chinese People's Armed Police Force (PAP) will host the 2026 Great Wall International Forum on Counter-terrorism in Beijing from October 13 to 15. Over 320 Chinese and foreign delegates from 62 countries and international organizations will participate in the event. Under the theme of "Advancing Counter-Terrorism Cooperation and Jointly Safeguarding Our Beautiful Home", the forum will cover four sessions, namely "trends of terrorist activities and counter-terrorism capability-building in the context of unmanned and AI systems", "preventing and combating maritime terror activities", "preventing terror attack on key targets", and "counter-terrorism and counter-infiltration in border areas". The forum will help deepen friendship and trust between the PAP and its foreign counterparts, strengthen bilateral and multilateral cooperation on counter-terrorism, and contribute to regional peace and stability.
