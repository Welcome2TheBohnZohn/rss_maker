ARTICLE TEXT:
JIUQUAN, Nov. 24 (Xinhua) -- Chinese astronauts Tang Hongbo, Wu Yi and Pakistani astronaut Muhammad Zeeshan Ali, for the upcoming Shenzhou-24 spaceflight mission, met the press on Tuesday.
The Shenzhou-24 crewed spaceship is scheduled to be launched at 8:18 a.m. (Beijing Time) Wednesday from the Jiuquan Satellite Launch Center in northwest China, the China Manned Space Agency announced at a press conference earlier Tuesday. ■
