TITLE:
Hainan sees robust tourism growth during National Day holiday

PUBLISHED:
2026-10-09

SOURCE:
Xinhua English

CATEGORY:
Authoritative PRC Media

URL:

ARTICLE TEXT:
This photo taken with a mobile phone on Oct. 1, 2026 shows tourists visiting Qilou Old Street in Haikou, south China's Hainan Province. Hainan saw 4.754 million tourist trips during the National Day holiday, marking a year-on-year increase of 13.1 percent. Total tourist spending rose 14.7 percent year on year to 6.875 billion yuan (about 1.02 billion U.S. dollars), according to the Hainan provincial department of tourism, culture, radio, television and sports. (Xinhua/Guo Cheng)
