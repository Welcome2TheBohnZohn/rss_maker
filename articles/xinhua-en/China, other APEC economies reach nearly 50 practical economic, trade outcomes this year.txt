SHENZHEN, Nov. 16 (Xinhua) -- China has worked with other Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation (APEC) economies to reach nearly 50 practical outcomes in the economic and trade field this year, the Ministry of Commerce said on Monday.
Since the beginning of this year, the ministry has worked with relevant departments to promote cooperation on advancing regional economic integration, supporting the work of the World Trade Organization, deepening digital and intelligent cooperation, and developing the green economy, Li Chenggang, China international trade representative and vice minister of commerce, told a press conference. ■
