TITLE:
Wuhan sends 500th China-Europe freight train in 2026

PUBLISHED:
2026-11-20

SOURCE:
Xinhua English

CATEGORY:
Authoritative PRC Media

URL:
https://english.news.cn/20261120/3161abfc11e54c2d9731c4758e497c3a/c.html

ARTICLE TEXT:
This photo taken on Nov. 20, 2026 shows a China-Europe freight train waiting for departure at the China United International Rail Containers Co., Ltd. (CRIntermodal) Wuhan Terminal in Wuhan, central China's Hubei Province. A China-Europe freight train departed from CRIntermodal Wuhan Terminal for Moscow on Friday, marking the 500th China-Europe freight train trip departing from Wuhan. So far this year, a total of 454,800 tonnes of cargo have been dispatched through the service, up 44.2 percent year on year.
Since the start of trial operations in 2012, China-Europe freight train service from Wuhan has expanded to 64 regular cross-border routes, connecting 167 cities in 46 countries. (Xinhua/Wu Zhizun)
