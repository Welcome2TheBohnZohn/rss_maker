TITLE:
Hainan sees steady growth in offshore duty-free sales this summer

PUBLISHED:
2026-09-07

SOURCE:
Xinhua English

CATEGORY:
Authoritative PRC Media

URL:

ARTICLE TEXT:
Customers shop at a duty-free shop for daily consumer goods in Haikou, south China's Hainan Province, Aug. 29, 2026. Data from Haikou Customs showed that sales from offshore duty-free shopping across the island amounted to 3.82 billion yuan (about 569.22 million U.S. dollars) during this year's summer vacation period, up 4.1 percent from the same period last year.
