BEIJING, Nov. 21 (Xinhua) -- The 2026 edition of the International Forum on Overseas Studies of Xi Jinping Thought on Socialism with Chinese Characteristics for a New Era opened in Beijing on Saturday.
Li Shulei, a member of the Political Bureau of the Communist Party of China (CPC) Central Committee and head of the Publicity Department of the CPC Central Committee, delivered a keynote speech at the opening ceremony.
Participants said Xi Jinping Thought on Socialism with Chinese Characteristics for a New Era had taken shape and evolved through efforts to respond to questions posed by China, by the world, by the people, and by the times, and to advance China's development in tandem with that of the rest of the world.
They noted that Xi has proposed the vision of building a community with a shared future for humanity, offering China's answer to common challenges facing countries in the current era and has won broad recognition from the international community.
Participants called for stronger overseas research into and communication of the Thought, as well as joint efforts to advance global development, safeguard world peace and stability, promote human progress and improve the global governance system.
Under the theme "The Great Way: Building a Community with a Shared Future for Humanity," the forum was hosted by the Chinese Academy of Social Sciences.
It will run through Sunday, with thematic sessions also being held in Hong Kong Special Administrative Region and five countries, namely Uzbekistan, Ethiopia, Greece, Hungary and Uruguay, bringing together nearly 650 participants from more than 80 countries and regions. ■
