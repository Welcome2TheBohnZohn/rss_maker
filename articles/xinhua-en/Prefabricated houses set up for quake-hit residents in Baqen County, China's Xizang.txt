TITLE:
Prefabricated houses set up for quake-hit residents in Baqen County, China's Xizang

PUBLISHED:
2026-10-24

SOURCE:
Xinhua English

CATEGORY:
Authoritative PRC Media

URL:
https://english.news.cn/20261024/0dd61a5bdf964da8a894b4db95316e6f/c.html

ARTICLE TEXT:
An aerial drone photo taken on Oct. 24, 2026 shows workers of China Railway Group Limited building prefabricated houses at a quake-hit village in Maru Township of Baqen County, Nagqu City, southwest China's Xizang Autonomous Region.
The first batch of prefabricated houses has been set up on Saturday for the affected residents in a village of Maru Township, one of the hardest-hit areas near the epicenter, following a 7.0-magnitude earthquake that struck Baqen County of the city of Nagqu in southwest China's Xizang Autonomous Region at 10:33 p.m. on Thursday.
