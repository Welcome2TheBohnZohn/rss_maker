ARTICLE TEXT:
JIUQUAN, Nov. 17 (Xinhua) -- The combination of the Shenzhou-24 crewed spacecraft and a Long March-2F carrier rocket was transferred to the launch area on Tuesday, with the Shenzhou-24 crewed spacecraft scheduled for launch at an appropriate time, according to the China Manned Space Agency (CMSA).
All facilities and equipment at the launch site are in good condition. Various pre-launch function checks and joint tests will be carried out as planned, the CMSA noted. ■
