Building a strong Party for a strong China

ARTICLE TEXT:
"Rigorous self-governance strengthens the Party, by building the confidence of the people," said an American writer who has been living in China for over 20 years.
At a plenum that concluded Thursday, the Communist Party of China (CPC) Central Committee adopted a resolution on persevering with full and rigorous Party self-governance, stressing the need to maintain a tough stance against corruption and strengthen checks on and oversight over the exercise of power.
Since its founding in 1921, the CPC has grown from just over 50 members to the world's largest governing party.
How does a party of this scale ensure strict self-governance? What measures has it put in place, and what results has it delivered?
