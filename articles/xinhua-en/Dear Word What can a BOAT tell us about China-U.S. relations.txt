ARTICLE TEXT:
Chinese President Xi Jinping has on several occasions compared China-U.S. relations to a giant ship navigating winds and waves.
In English, we say, "We're in the same boat."
In Chinese, there's a similar expression: 同舟共济 (tóng zhōu gòng jì). But it goes one step further - not just sharing the same boat, but helping each other through the storm.
