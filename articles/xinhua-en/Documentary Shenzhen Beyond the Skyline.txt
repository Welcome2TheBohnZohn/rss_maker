Shenzhen, the host city of APEC 2026, is in many ways the embodiment of China's development miracle. But its story goes far beyond the skyline.
From mangrove wetlands to bustling electronics markets, from contemporary art to everyday life in a care home, Xinhua's Miao Xiaojuan explores the people and ideas shaping this metropolis, and what it means to live well here.
