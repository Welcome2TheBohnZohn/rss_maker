A staff member works at a workshop of Yongxing Tech in Beihai, south China's Guangxi Zhuang Autonomous Region, Oct. 16, 2026. Taking advantage of its ports, resources as well as geographical location, Beihai has in recent years focused on developing eight pillar industries such as green chemicals, metal materials, information technology, and marine engineering equipment. It is accelerating the building of a modern and ocean-oriented industrial system featuring intelligence, greenness and integration. (Xinhua/Lu Peng)
