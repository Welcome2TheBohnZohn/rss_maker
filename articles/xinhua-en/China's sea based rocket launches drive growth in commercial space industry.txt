China's sea based rocket launches drive growth in commercial space industry

ARTICLE TEXT:
China's sea-based rocket launches are becoming more frequent and routine, injecting new momentum into the country's commercial space industry.
Recently, the Gravity 1 carrier rocket successfully launched nine satellites into their planned orbits from waters off eastern China, setting new records for payload mass and orbital altitude in a single sea based launch. ■
