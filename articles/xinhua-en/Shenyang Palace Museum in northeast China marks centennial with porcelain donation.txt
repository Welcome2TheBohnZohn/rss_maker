SHENYANG, Nov. 16 (Xinhua) -- The Shenyang Palace Museum in the capital city of northeast China's Liaoning Province celebrated its centennial on Monday, and received a donation of 41 porcelain pieces as a birthday gift.
The donated collection, all export porcelain spanning three major categories, fills a blank in the museum's export porcelain holdings and further improves the collection system, said Zhang Guobin, director of the collection department of the museum.
These pieces were donated by Zhao Qiang, chairman of the Macau Association for Preservation of Cultural Relics. "These porcelain pieces once crossed mountains and seas to reach the world. Today, they have entered the museum. It is my hope that through research and display, they can tell more stories of the exchange between Chinese culture and the world," he said.
Shenyang Palace Museum, where the former imperial complex of three emperors of the Qing Dynasty (1644-1911) was located, was set up in 1926 and opened to the public in 1929. In 2004, it was added to the World Cultural Heritage List.
To mark its centennial this year, the museum is hosting multiple exhibitions highlighting its history, including an exhibition of 100 of its most precious items and another featuring photographs from the past century.
Over the years, the museum has conducted a series of activities. Through innovative exhibitions and creative cultural products, it has showcased its architectural and cultural relics, as well as the culture behind them.
By Nov. 1, Shenyang Palace Museum had received more than 6 million visitors this year, setting a new record high. ■
