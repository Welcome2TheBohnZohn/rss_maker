A drone photo taken on Oct. 30, 2026 show tourists taking a sightseeing train to enjoy rural scenery in Quanjiang Village of Xingye County, Yulin City, south China's Guangxi Zhuang Autonomous Region. Since the beginning of this year, Xingye County has been implementing a "six improvements" program in villages, focusing on the practical improvements in manufacturing, agricultural industry, green space, road access, water facilities, and streetlights. These measures are creating fresh momentum for rural revitalization. (Xinhua/Cao Yiming)
