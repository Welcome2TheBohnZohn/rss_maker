ST. PETERSBURG, Oct. 21 (Xinhua) -- Chinese Premier Li Qiang said on Wednesday that China is ready to work with Russia to deepen practical cooperation in such areas as energy resources, trade and investment, and transportation, and achieve more positive outcomes.
Li made the remarks when meeting with Russian President Vladimir Putin in St. Petersburg. ■
