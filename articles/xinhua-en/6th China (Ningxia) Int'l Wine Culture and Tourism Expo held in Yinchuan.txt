TITLE:
6th China (Ningxia) Int'l Wine Culture and Tourism Expo held in Yinchuan

PUBLISHED:
2026-10-13

SOURCE:
Xinhua English

CATEGORY:
Authoritative PRC Media

URL:
https://english.news.cn/20261013/30e074cd3d204192a39c4b28af3b6053/c.html

ARTICLE TEXT:
People visit the sixth China (Ningxia) International Wine Culture and Tourism Expo in Yinchuan, northwest China's Ningxia Hui Autonomous Region, Oct. 13, 2026.
From Oct. 12 to 14, the expo is held in Yinchuan, attracting 84 world-renowned wineries and enterprises from distinctive production areas in 16 countries. It has also drawn a total of 146 leading domestic wineries from Ningxia and other core production areas in China.
The local wine industry has also become a driver of tourism. Wineries in Ningxia receive more than 3.7 million tourist visits each year, generating combined benefits of over 50 billion yuan (around 7.4 billion U.S. dollars). (Xinhua/Yang Zhisen)
