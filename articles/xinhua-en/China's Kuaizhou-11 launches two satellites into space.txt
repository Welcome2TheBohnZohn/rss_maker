JIUQUAN, Sept. 17 (Xinhua) -- China on Thursday launched the Kuaizhou-11 Y3 carrier rocket from the Jiuquan Satellite Launch Center in northwest China.
The rocket lifted off at 10:40 a.m. (Beijing Time), successfully sending two satellites -- Tianyi-51 and Tianyi-52 -- into the planned orbits. ■
