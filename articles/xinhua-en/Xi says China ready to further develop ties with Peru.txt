SHENZHEN, Nov. 17 (Xinhua) -- Chinese President Xi Jinping on Tuesday said China is ready to work with Peru to elevate the comprehensive strategic partnership between the two countries to new heights and deliver greater benefits to the two peoples.
China has remained Peru's largest trading partner and largest export destination country for 12 consecutive years, Xi said while meeting with Peruvian President Keiko Fujimori in Shenzhen, south China's Guangdong Province. Fujimori is in Shenzhen to attend the 33rd Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation (APEC) Economic Leaders' Meeting, scheduled for Nov. 18-19.
Xi also noted that the protocol upgrading the China-Peru free trade agreement (FTA) took effect officially about two weeks ago, and the Chancay Port, built with the participation of Chinese enterprises, has given a strong boost to economic and trade cooperation between Asia and Latin America.
Xi called for closer exchanges between the two countries' governments, legislative bodies, political parties, and localities to strengthen political mutual trust. He also urged both sides to make full use of the protocol upgrading the FTA, consolidate traditional cooperation in infrastructure, energy and resources, and explore potential in emerging fields such as new energy and artificial intelligence.
He called for enhancing cooperation in culture, education, tourism, archaeology and health to carry forward the traditional friendship.
Xi also said the two sides should strengthen communication and coordination within the United Nations, APEC and other frameworks to uphold true multilateralism and free trade, and safeguard international fairness and justice.
Fujimori said Peru firmly adheres to the one-China principle, stands ready to strengthen high-level exchanges, and will use the protocol upgrading the FTA as an opportunity to enhance people-to-people exchanges and promote cooperation in trade, investment and infrastructure.
Peru welcomes further investment by Chinese companies and will provide an open and stable business environment, she said, noting that projects such as the Chancay Port have played an important role in Peru's economic development.
Fujimori also called for closer people-to-people and party-to-party exchanges, and thanked China for its support to Peru in addressing El Nino challenges. Peru is willing to strengthen multilateral coordination with China and enhance friendship between the two peoples, she said. ■
