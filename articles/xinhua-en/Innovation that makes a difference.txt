Why do we innovate?
To save lives, overcome challenges. To make a difference.
We see it in action when thermal imaging drones help locate and rescue koalas during Australian bushfires, or when an exoskeleton helps an injured French cyclist get back on the road and complete a 4,037-km journey in just 20 days.
Behind these innovations are companies based in Shenzhen, China's tech hub, also the host city for upcoming APEC 2026.
