The Red Falcon aerobatics team from the Aviation University of Air Force performs during celebrations to mark the 77th anniversary of the founding of the People's Liberation Army (PLA) Air Force in Changchun, northeast China's Jilin Province, Nov. 9, 2026. Besides that, a powered paraglider formation also staged a performance on Monday to mark the Chinese Air Force anniversary, which falls on Nov. 11. (Xinhua/Zhang Nan)
