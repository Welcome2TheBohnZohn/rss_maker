BEIJING, Aug. 24 (Xinhua) -- China and India will hold the 25th round of talks between the two countries' special representatives on the boundary question in Beijing on Aug. 25, a Chinese foreign ministry spokesperson announced on Monday.
As agreed between the two sides, Wang Yi, a member of the Political Bureau of the Communist Party of China Central Committee, director of the Office of the Central Commission for Foreign Affairs and China's Special Representative on the Boundary Question between China and India, will co-chair the talks with India's Special Representative and National Security Advisor Ajit Doval, said the spokesperson. ■
