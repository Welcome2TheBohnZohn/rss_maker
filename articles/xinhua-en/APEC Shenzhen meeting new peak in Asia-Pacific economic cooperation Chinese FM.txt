ARTICLE TEXT:
SHENZHEN, Nov. 19 (Xinhua) -- Chinese Foreign Minister Wang Yi said Thursday that the 33rd Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation (APEC) Economic Leaders' Meeting marked a new peak in Asia-Pacific economic cooperation.
It was the most important diplomatic event China hosted this year, said Wang, who is also a member of the Political Bureau of the Communist Party of China Central Committee, at a press conference held after the meeting in Shenzhen, south China's Guangdong Province.
The meeting produced the ambitious, practical and comprehensive APEC Leaders' Shenzhen Outlook, which is the most significant outcome of the APEC "China Year" and a new milestone in APEC cooperation, Wang said.
At the meeting, Chinese President Xi Jinping called for building an Asia-Pacific community characterized by solidarity and mutual assistance, openness and integration, enterprise and innovation, as well as inclusive development.
Xi elaborated on China's proposals regarding what kind of Asia-Pacific community should be built and how to build it, Wang noted.
Wang said the meeting also injected new momentum into advancing the Free Trade Area of the Asia-Pacific.
Wang highlighted the upgraded APEC Connectivity Blueprint endorsed at the meeting, which is aimed at advancing infrastructure development, strengthening supply chain connectivity, enhancing policy and regulatory cooperation, and facilitating the movement of people.
Wang hailed China's contribution to the endorsement of an APEC statement on harnessing digital and emerging technologies for jobs and prosperity. The statement helps align cooperation on AI and other new technologies to advance high-quality full employment, public services and digital infrastructure, Wang said.
The Chinese foreign minister also noted a series of measures Xi announced to boost Asia-Pacific cooperation, including setting up an Asia-Pacific community studies center, a proposal to establish a World Association for Bay Areas Economic Cooperation, and financial support for practical cooperation.
Wang said the Shenzhen meeting has mapped out the key pillars and priorities for building an Asia-Pacific community and charted a roadmap for implementation.
It is fair to say that the journey toward an Asia-Pacific community has truly begun in Shenzhen, Wang said. ■
