ARTICLE TEXT:
BEIJING, Nov. 25 (Xinhua) -- China will make greater use of emerging technologies and digital applications to promote public awareness of national defense over the next five years, according to a new plan aimed at strengthening relevant education and improving citizens' defense-related knowledge and skills.
The plan, covering the 15th Five-Year Plan period (2026-2030), was jointly issued by the Publicity Department of the Communist Party of China Central Committee and the defense mobilization department of the Central Military Commission.
It sets targets for the national defense education system and mechanisms to be further enhanced by 2030, with improved programs in relevant fields both at schools and among the public.
It also calls for stronger coordination between military and civilian sectors in relevant efforts.
The plan outlines seven key areas of work, including promoting relevant awareness among different groups of people, broadening practical educational activities, and making better use of the military's resources for educational purposes.
It also stresses the importance of upholding the Party's overall leadership and implementing a holistic approach to national security.
China's 15th Five-Year Plan calls for further raising public awareness of national defense and strengthening the unity between the military and the government, and between the military and the people.
The country in 2001 adopted its National Defense Education Law, which underwent an amendment in 2018 and a revision in 2024.
In 2023, several authorities started to jointly designate September as national defense education month.
During the 2026 campaign, relevant authorities, institutions and schools across China organized a range of activities for public awareness promotion, such as airing educational videos and shows, and arranging student visits to revolutionary bases and memorial sites.
Some military units also held open days, allowing members of the public to visit military barracks, naval ports and warships. ■
