FENGCHENG, Nov. 26 (Xinhua) -- The industrial park in Fengcheng, east China's Jiangxi Province is home to many recycling enterprises that dismantle used household appliances to recycle metals and plastics. These metals are then processed and transformed into circuit boards, metal components, and other materials, which are reassembled into new household appliances and other industrial products.
So, what happens to an old fridge after more than 15 years of service? Join our reporter as we take a closer look at how this old appliance is dismantled, recycled and given a second life.
