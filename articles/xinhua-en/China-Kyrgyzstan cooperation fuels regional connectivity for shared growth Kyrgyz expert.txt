Rooted in the ancient Silk Road, the China-Kyrgyzstan cooperation not only sets a gold standard for good neighborliness, but also actively promotes regional connectivity and people-to-people ties, said Sheradil Baktygulov, director of the Kyrgyz Institute of World Politics.
Progress on the landmark China-Kyrgyzstan-Uzbekistan railway, he noted, goes beyond #infrastructure -- it unlocks new potential for Central Asia for a true win-win outcome.
