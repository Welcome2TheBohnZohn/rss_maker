BEIJING, Sept. 2 (Xinhua) -- The Shougang Park in Beijing, capital of China, venue for the upcoming China International Fair for Trade in Services (CIFTIS), is now in the final stage of preparations to welcome the event. This year's CIFTIS, set to be held from Sept. 9 to 13 here, will bring together exhibitors from over 90 countries and international organizations, along with more than 1,800 companies.
