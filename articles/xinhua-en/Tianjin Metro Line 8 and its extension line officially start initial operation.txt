Passengers are seen at a station of the Tianjin Metro Line 8 in north China's Tianjin, Nov. 28, 2026. The 23-kilometer Tianjin Metro Line 8, together with its extension line, officially started initial operation on Saturday.
The metro line is a key rail transit line passing through the central urban area of Tianjin. It is a fully driverless line operating at Grade of Automation 4 (GoA4), the highest automation level available, with a maximum operating speed of 80 kilometers per hour among its 21 stations.
