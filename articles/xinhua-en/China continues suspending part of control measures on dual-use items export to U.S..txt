China continues suspending part of control measures on dual-use items export to U.S.

ARTICLE TEXT:
BEIJING, Nov. 24 (Xinhua) -- China will extend the suspension of part of its export control measures on dual-use items to the United States from Nov. 28, 2026, to Jan. 27, 2027, the Ministry of Commerce said Tuesday.
The suspension of the second clause of the Announcement No. 46 of 2024 was first announced in November 2025 and was originally scheduled to run through Nov. 27, 2026.
According to the clause, now suspended, the export of dual-use items related to gallium, germanium, antimony and superhard materials to the United States is not to be permitted in principle, while stricter examinations on end-users and end-use purposes are to be conducted regarding exports of the dual-use item of graphite to the United States. ■
