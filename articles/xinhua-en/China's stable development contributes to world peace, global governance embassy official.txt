LONDON, Nov. 27 (Xinhua) -- The fifth plenary session of the 20th Central Committee of the Communist Party of China (CPC) has provided a solid political guarantee for advancing Chinese modernization, while China's stable development will continue to contribute to world peace and prosperity, a Chinese diplomat said in London on Thursday.
Speaking at a press briefing, Bi Haibo, minister counsellor at the Chinese Embassy in Britain, said the plenary session had made strategic arrangements for exercising full and rigorous Party self-governance.
Bi said the session was of great significance in strengthening Party unity, ensuring that Party members remain committed to their missions and responsibilities, maintain close ties with the people and work towards the common goal of Chinese modernization and national rejuvenation.
Bi also said China's stability and development are in themselves "a contribution to world peace," adding that the country will continue to pursue stable and predictable development.
China is committed to building a community with a shared future for humanity, he said, highlighting the Global Development Initiative, the Global Security Initiative, the Global Civilization Initiative and the Global Governance Initiative as China's solutions to growing global uncertainties and instabilities.
"These are also the initiatives arranged and implemented with concrete actions, measures rather than slogans," Bi said, stressing China's commitment to multilateralism, the WTO-centered multilateral trading system and making greater contributions to global peace and development. ■
