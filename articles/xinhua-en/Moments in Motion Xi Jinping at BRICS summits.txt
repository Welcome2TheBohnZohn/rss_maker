Chinese President Xi Jinping will attend the upcoming 18th BRICS Summit in New Delhi, India from Saturday to Sunday.
In today's world, BRICS has become the most important platform for solidarity and cooperation among emerging markets and developing countries.
At the summit, Xi will work with leaders of other countries to chart a blueprint for the high-quality development of greater BRICS cooperation, generate strong momentum for the Global South to pursue strength through unity, and inject greater stability and positive energy into a turbulent and changing world.
