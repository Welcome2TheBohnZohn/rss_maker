China unveils roadmap for next-generation communication networks

ARTICLE TEXT:
BEIJING, Nov. 26 (Xinhua) -- Chinese authorities have released an implementation plan for the development of next-generation communication networks, aiming to build such networks featuring comprehensive coverage and leading performance by 2030.
The plan targets advancing 6G network development at an appropriate time and basically completing the construction of a low-Earth-orbit satellite internet system by 2030.
China also expects to promote the large-scale development of integrated networks like the Industrial Internet and the Internet of Vehicles, significantly improve smart development, and further strengthen the industrial ecosystem, according to the plan.
It sets out measures to improve basic networks, space-based networks and international networks, as well as integrated networks, while making communication networks smarter, greener, and more secure and resilient.
Regarding the industrial ecosystem, the plan calls for accelerating the development of standards for technologies such as 6G, satellite internet and ultra-high-speed optical communications, and the integration of computing resources and communication networks.
It also seeks to advance deep-space, deep-Earth, deep-sea and far-sea technological exploration, while promoting the integration of brain-computer interfaces, embodied artificial intelligence and intelligent connected vehicles with next-generation communication networks.
The plan encourages private enterprises to participate in network development in accordance with laws and regulations and explore more diverse ways to engage. Stronger support should be given to securing land, forest, grassland, sea and spectrum resources for network development, according to the plan.
Next-generation communication networks will form an intelligent, integrated digital infrastructure supported by advanced mobile, fiber-optic and satellite technologies, providing fast, secure and green connectivity.
The development of next-generation communication networks is one of six network infrastructure priorities for China's 15th Five-Year Plan period (2026-2030), alongside water networks, new-type power grids, computing power networks, urban underground pipeline networks and logistics networks.
Investment in these six networks and relevant areas is estimated to exceed 7 trillion yuan (about 1.04 trillion U.S. dollars) this year, the National Development and Reform Commission revealed. ■
