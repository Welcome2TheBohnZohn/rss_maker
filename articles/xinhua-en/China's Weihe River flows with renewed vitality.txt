Visitors trace the source of the Weihe River deep in the Niaoshu Mountains in Weiyuan County, northwest China's Gansu Province, Oct. 20, 2026. The Weihe River, the largest tributary of the Yellow River, rises in the Niaoshu Mountains in Weiyuan County, Dingxi City of northwest China's Gansu Province, and empties into the Yellow River in Tongguan County, Weinan City of northwest China's Shaanxi Province. Stretching 818 kilometers with a drainage area of over 134,000 square kilometers, the river and its basin span more than ten cities across northwest China's Shaanxi, Gansu provinces and Ningxia Hui Autonomous Region.
The Weihe River basin is an important birthplace of the Chinese civilization, home to capitals of several ancient Chinese dynasties, and a key passage along the eastern section of the ancient Silk Road.
Today, the river flows broad and mighty, flocks of waterbirds gather in large numbers, and the region's rich cultural traditions are thriving with renewed vitality. (Xinhua/Li Yibo)
