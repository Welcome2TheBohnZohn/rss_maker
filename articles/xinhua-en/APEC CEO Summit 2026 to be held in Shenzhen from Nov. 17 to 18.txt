BEIJING, Sept. 10 (Xinhua) -- The Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation (APEC) CEO Summit 2026 will be held in Shenzhen, south China's Guangdong Province, from Nov. 17 to 18, according to the China Council for the Promotion of International Trade (CCPIT) on Thursday. ■
