BISHKEK, Sept. 1 (Xinhua) -- Chinese President Xi Jinping on Tuesday called on the Shanghai Cooperation Organization (SCO) to shoulder the responsibility of the times and work together to advance a more just and equitable global governance system.
Xi made the remarks while addressing the "SCO Plus" meeting in the Kyrgyz capital of Bishkek. ■
