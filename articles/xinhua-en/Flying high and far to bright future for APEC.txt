ARTICLE TEXT:
The world's attention turns to China as the 33rd APEC Economic Leaders' Meeting in Shenzhen, the City of the Roc (named after a legendary bird of great size and strength), delivers fruitful outcomes and marks a new peak in Asia-Pacific economic cooperation. Openness, innovation and cooperation are the priorities of China's APEC 2026 . With joint efforts to meet challenges facing development through opening up, generate momentum for growth through innovation, and pursue prosperity for all through cooperation, we look forward to seeing Asia-Pacific cooperation spread its wings like the roc and fly high and far to a bright future.
