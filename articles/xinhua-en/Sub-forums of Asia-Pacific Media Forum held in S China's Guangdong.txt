Guests attend the Asia-Pacific Media Forum Shenzhen Sub-forum in Shenzhen, south China's Guangdong Province, Sept. 4, 2026.
The Asia-Pacific Media Forum will open on Saturday in Shenzhen, south China's Guangdong Province, as the country hosts a series of events under the 2026 APEC "China Year."
Multiple outcomes are expected to be unveiled at the forum, under the theme of "Building a Path to Shared Prosperity for the Asia-Pacific Community: Media Consensus and Action."
From Sept. 3 to 4, the forum held four sub-forums in Guangzhou, Dongguan, Zhuhai, and Shenzhen in Guangdong Province. (Xinhua/Dai Mingxuan)
