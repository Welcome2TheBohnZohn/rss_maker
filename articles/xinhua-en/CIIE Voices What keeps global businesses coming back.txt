ARTICLE TEXT:
Why do global businesses keep coming back to the China International Import Expo (CIIE)? And what makes the Chinese market so vital to their long-term strategies? Global companies, including Intel, Philips, and IQAir, share their perspectives at this year's CIIE. Their answers go beyond market size, they highlight China's fast-evolving innovation ecosystem, digital transformation, expanding opportunities and the value of partnering with local players.
