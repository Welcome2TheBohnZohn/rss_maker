BEIJING, Nov. 11 (Xinhua) -- At the invitation of Thai Prime Minister Anutin Charnvirakul, Chinese Premier Li Qiang will pay an official visit to Thailand from Nov. 13 to 15, a Chinese foreign ministry spokesperson announced on Wednesday.
At the invitation of President Ferdinand Romualdez Marcos Jr. of the Philippines, the rotating chair of ASEAN, Premier Li will attend the 29th China-ASEAN Summit in Manila from Nov. 15 to 16, the spokesperson added. ■
