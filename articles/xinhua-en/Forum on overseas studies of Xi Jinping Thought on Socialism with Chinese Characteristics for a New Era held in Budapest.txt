BUDAPEST, Nov. 21 (Xinhua) -- The Hungarian thematic forum of the 2026 edition of the International Forum on Overseas Studies of Xi Jinping Thought on Socialism with Chinese Characteristics for a New Era was held in Budapest on Saturday.
The forum was co-hosted by the Institute of European Studies of the Chinese Academy of Social Sciences and the China-CEE Institute under the theme "Mutual Trust, Interconnection and Shared Benefits: the Vision of a Community with a Shared Future for Humanity and China-Europe Cooperation for Mutual Benefits."
Experts and scholars from China and several European countries attended the forum, discussing topics including the global significance of Chinese modernization, the vision of a community with a shared future for humanity and China-Europe cooperation.
Hungary was the first European country to sign a cooperation document with China on the Belt and Road Initiative. Strengthening dialogue and cooperation between China and Europe is an important way to put the vision of a community with a shared future for humanity into practice and help translate the four major global initiatives into concrete results in addressing shared challenges, they said.
Connectivity is a practical means through which the vision of a community with a shared future for humanity takes tangible form, they said. ■
