ARTICLE TEXT:
BEIJING, Nov. 12 (Xinhua) -- Kazakh President Kassym-Jomart Tokayev has sent a congratulatory letter on the 95th anniversary of the founding of Xinhua News Agency, commending its important contributions to consolidating and deepening the deep-rooted friendship, good-neighborly relations and mutual trust between Kazakhstan and China.
"I would like to extend my heartfelt congratulations to you and all your colleagues on the occasion of the 95th anniversary of the founding of Xinhua News Agency," Tokayev said in the letter.
"Over nearly a century, Xinhua has established itself as one of the world's leading and most prestigious news organizations, earning widespread recognition and respect from the international community," he said.
Xinhua has chronicled major historical events and witnessed China's remarkable achievements, Tokayev said.
"The agency's highly professional editorial team, through its efficiency, coordination and creative excellence, continues to set an outstanding example of modern journalism," he added.
Thanks to the joint efforts of the two countries and the special support of Chinese President Xi Jinping, bilateral cooperation has reached an unprecedented level, culminating in a permanent comprehensive strategic partnership, Tokayev said.
"I am confident that Xinhua will continue to communicate the shared achievements of our two countries to a wider audience and serve as a bridge of friendship and mutual understanding, bringing our peoples even closer together," he said. ■
