TITLE:
China's Xinjiang builds ecological barriers to improve ecosystem and biodiversity

PUBLISHED:
2026-10-30

SOURCE:
Xinhua English

CATEGORY:
Authoritative PRC Media

Forest and grassland firefighters remove weeds while patrolling among tamarisk trees at Hoxud County, northwest China's Xinjiang Uygur Autonomous Region, Oct. 26, 2026.
In recent years, various parts of Xinjiang have taken a range of measures to build ecological barriers in both urban and rural areas. While efforts are being coordinated to green barren land and restore wetlands, advanced technologies have been adopted to safeguard forests and grasslands.
These initiatives prove highly effective in securing and improving the ecosystem and biodiversity across the region. Specialty fruit planting and cultural tourism services are also burgeoning here. (Xinhua/Ding Lei)
