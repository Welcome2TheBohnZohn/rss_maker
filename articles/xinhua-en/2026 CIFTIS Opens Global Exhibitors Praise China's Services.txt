The 2026 China International Fair for Trade in Services (CIFTIS) has kicked off at Beijing's Shougang Park!
From green energy and digital technology to overseas-expansion services, this year's fair has brought together exhibitors from over 90 countries, regions and international organizations, with Norway featured as the Guest Country of Honor. CIFTIS has been a vital bridge for global cooperation in trade in services!
