ARTICLE TEXT:
SHENZHEN, Nov. 18 (Xinhua) -- The Shenzhen initiative of the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation (APEC) CEO Summit 2026 was released on Wednesday, calling for deepening an open regional economy, driving inclusive growth through technological innovation, strengthening regional connectivity, and working together to achieve shared prosperity.
This marks the first time in the 30-year history of the summit that an outcome document has been issued in the form of an initiative.
The initiative states that, guided by four priority areas -- openness and inclusiveness, innovation-driven growth, greater connectivity and mutually beneficial cooperation -- the Asia-Pacific business community stands ready to work with all economies to fully implement the Putrajaya Vision 2040 and build an open, dynamic, resilient and peaceful Asia-Pacific community.
It also calls for translating this year's APEC theme, "Building an Asia-Pacific Community to Prosper Together," into concrete actions, so that the Asia-Pacific region continues to serve as the world's most dynamic engine of growth and a community of shared prosperity. ■
