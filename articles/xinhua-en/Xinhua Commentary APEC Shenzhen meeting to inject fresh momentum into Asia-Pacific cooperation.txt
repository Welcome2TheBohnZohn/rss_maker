ARTICLE TEXT:
SHENZHEN, Nov. 16 (Xinhua) -- Asia-Pacific cooperation now stands at a crossroads amid mounting economic uncertainty and rising protectionism. The upcoming 33rd Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation (APEC) Economic Leaders' Meeting offers a timely opportunity to reaffirm the region's commitment to openness and cooperation, and inject much-needed certainty and fresh momentum into regional and global growth.
The Nov. 18-19 meeting in Shenzhen, south China, is expected to lay the main pillars of an Asia-Pacific community, coordinate different pathways toward the Free Trade Area of the Asia-Pacific, make new arrangements to boost regional connectivity, and advance digital, smart and green transformation for the region to prosper together.
Against the backdrop of a sluggish global economy, there could be no better occasion than the Shenzhen meeting to send a resounding message of championing free trade, deepening regional economic integration, opposing protectionism and "decoupling," and enhancing the resilience of industrial and supply chains.
Interactions among leaders of APEC economies are also expected to help enhance mutual trust and build consensus -- a positive signal for global peace and development.
The 2026 APEC "China Year" has seen more than 300 meetings and activities held across Chinese cities, laying a solid foundation for fruitful outcomes during the Shenzhen meeting.
The meeting is expected to adopt an APEC leaders' declaration, with practical outcomes in key areas such as trade, connectivity, the digital economy and artificial intelligence (AI).
Business leaders from across the region and beyond will also gather in Shenzhen for the APEC CEO Summit, where cooperation in AI, the digital economy and green development will be high on the agenda and is expected to create new drivers of growth for the Asia-Pacific economy.
The prosperity of the Asia-Pacific over the past few decades has been built on open cooperation and industrial complementarity, rather than bloc confrontation and economic fragmentation. This is the underlying logic behind the region's remarkable growth and its role as a major engine of the world economy.
Over the years, China has played a leading role in advancing Asia-Pacific cooperation, offering ideas and solutions to promote common development. Meanwhile, China-proposed international public goods, such as the Belt and Road Initiative, have remarkably boosted economic and trade cooperation in the region.
China will remain committed to promoting high-standard opening up, and continuously provide new opportunities for shared growth via its modernization drive. Its super-large market, booming AI, digital economy, green technologies and wider opening up will unlock new potential of cooperation in the region.
APEC serves as both an incubator of ideas and a platform for building consensus and advancing cooperation. In today's complex and volatile world, the value of its flexible, pragmatic and results-oriented approach is clearer than ever.
China's leader has urged staying true to APEC's founding mission to promote economic growth and improve people's lives, and championing open development where everyone shares opportunities and emerges a winner.
As leaders of APEC member economies gather in Shenzhen, a coastal city at the forefront of China's opening up and innovation drive, they have an opportunity to demonstrate that openness, inclusiveness and mutually beneficial cooperation remain the foundations of regional prosperity.
The Shenzhen meeting is set to help build an Asia-Pacific community to prosper together, and chart an open, innovative and cooperative future for the Asia-Pacific and the wider world. ■
