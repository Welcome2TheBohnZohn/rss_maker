This photo taken on Nov. 3, 2026 shows a view of the venue of the 140th China Import and Export Fair in Guangzhou, south China's Guangdong Province. The 140th China Import and Export Fair, also known as the Canton Fair, concluded here on Wednesday. As of November 3, the fair has attracted 352,000 overseas buyers from 219 countries and regions, a 12.2 percent increase from last year and a new high, according to the China Foreign Trade Centre (CFTC), the organizer of the fair. (Xinhua/Deng Hua)
