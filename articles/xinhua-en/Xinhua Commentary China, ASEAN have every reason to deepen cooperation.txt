ARTICLE TEXT:
MANILA, Nov. 16 (Xinhua) -- The Chinese proverb "a close neighbor is better than a distant relative" also applies to the relations between China and ASEAN, who share an enduring bond and see their cooperation bring tangible benefits to people across the region.
At a time of growing global uncertainties, the two sides have every reason to deepen their partnership, focus on shared interests and keep their cooperation moving forward.
The strength of China-ASEAN relations lies in their shared interests and the practical benefits they deliver. This partnership is too important to be distracted by occasional differences, political rhetoric or external attempts to sow discord.
There is a clear reason for keeping this partnership on track: its benefits are real and far-reaching.
China has remained ASEAN's largest trading partner for 17 consecutive years, while ASEAN has been China's biggest trading partner for six successive years. This year also marks the fifth anniversary of the establishment of the China-ASEAN Comprehensive Strategic Partnership and the 35th anniversary of China-ASEAN dialogue relations, making it an important milestone for taking stock of achievements and charting the way forward.
China has consistently demonstrated its commitment to building a closer China-ASEAN community with a shared future, backing its pledges with concrete actions. With China's participation, the China-Laos Railway has turned Laos from a landlocked to a land-linked nation. Under the China-proposed Belt and Road Initiative, the Sihanoukville Special Economic Zone has made Cambodia a rising manufacturing and export hub. Across Southeast Asia, infrastructure connectivity, industrial cooperation and people-to-people exchanges are creating opportunities that extend well beyond national borders.
Cooperation is also expanding into emerging fields. The implementation of the China-ASEAN Free Trade Area 3.0 Upgrade Protocol and closer coordination with the ASEAN Digital Economy Framework Agreement will open new avenues for trade and investment. In electric vehicles, digital technologies, renewable energy and green agriculture, Chinese companies are increasingly working with ASEAN partners to develop solutions tailored to regional needs.
Such cooperation is particularly valuable as ASEAN countries seek to upgrade their industries, strengthen economic resilience and move up global value chains. By combining China's industrial strengths, technological capabilities and vast market with ASEAN's dynamic economies and development potential, both sides can create more opportunities for businesses, especially micro, small and medium-sized enterprises.
China's commitment to deeper cooperation with ASEAN also goes beyond the economic arena. It firmly supports ASEAN centrality and ASEAN-led regional mechanisms, and stands ready to work with regional countries to build a peaceful, stable and inclusive regional community.
This year marks the 50th anniversary of the signing of the Treaty of Amity and Cooperation in Southeast Asia. China was the first major country to accede to the treaty, demonstrating its support for ASEAN's principles.
The development of China-ASEAN relations has never been without challenges. Differences over certain issues, including those concerning the South China Sea, should be addressed through dialogue and consultation in a constructive manner.
The South China Sea, meanwhile, should remain a sea of peace, friendship and cooperation. China and ASEAN members have a common interest in maintaining stability, promoting practical maritime cooperation and ensuring the safety of shipping and trade. Differences should be properly handled through dialogue and consultation, while efforts to advance cooperation in areas such as environmental protection, marine research and sustainable development deserve greater attention.
As China and ASEAN look to the future, their shared interests and common aspirations provide a strong foundation for closer cooperation. By staying committed to good-neighborliness, mutual benefit and dialogue, both sides can further strengthen their partnership, deliver greater benefits to their peoples and contribute more to peace, stability and prosperity in the region.■
