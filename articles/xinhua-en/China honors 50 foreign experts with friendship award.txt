ARTICLE TEXT:
BEIJING, Sept. 30 (Xinhua) -- China on Wednesday honored 50 foreign experts with the 2026 Chinese Government Friendship Award for their outstanding contributions to the country's development.
Chinese State Councilor Shen Yiqin extended congratulations to the experts at a ceremony held at the Great Hall of the People in Beijing.
China's economy is moving toward innovation-driven and high-quality growth, injecting much-needed certainty into the world, Shen said, noting that foreign experts play an important role in promoting China's economic and social development and enhancing friendly exchanges and cooperation between China and other countries.
More foreign experts are welcome to work with the Chinese people to create a better future during China's modernization drive, reach new heights in science, and strive for the well-being of humanity, Shen said.
She added that China will provide solid support for foreign talent seeking long-term, stable development in the country. ■
