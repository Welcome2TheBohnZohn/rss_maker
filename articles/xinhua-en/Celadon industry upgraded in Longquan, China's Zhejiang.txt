A staff member livestreams at a studio of the Longquan celadon in Longquan, east China's Zhejiang Province, Oct. 14, 2026. Located in southwestern Zhejiang Province, Longquan City boasts abundant ceramic clay, dense forests, and the Longquan River, providing ideal conditions for its celadon industry.
Longquan celadon firing technique was listed as a national intangible cultural heritage in 2006 and was inscribed on UNESCO's Representative List of the Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity in 2009.
In recent years, Longquan has upgraded its celadon industry by improving the quality of raw material, boosting industrial intelligence and empowering innovation. Today, the city has over 3,000 celadon-related businesses with more than 23,000 workers involved. (Xinhua/Jiang Han)
