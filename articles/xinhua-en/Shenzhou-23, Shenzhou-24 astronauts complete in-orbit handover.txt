ARTICLE TEXT:
JIUQUAN, Nov. 28 (Xinhua) -- The Shenzhou-23 and Shenzhou-24 astronauts held an in-orbit handover ceremony in China's Tiangong space station on Saturday, according to the China Manned Space Agency (CMSA).
Pakistani astronaut Muhammad Zeeshan Ali, the first foreign national entering Tiangong, will return to Earth with the Shenzhou-23 astronauts Zhang Zhiyuan and Li Jiaying, the agency said.
Shenzhou-23 astronaut Zhu Yangzhu, who has already spent six months in orbit for a year-long stay experiment, will remain aboard the space station for the Shenzhou-24 mission together with commander Tang Hongbo and astronaut Wu Yi.
The returning astronauts have finished all their scheduled tasks. They are expected to return to the Dongfeng landing site in north China aboard the Shenzhou-23 spacecraft in the coming days, and Zhang will serve as commander of the return mission, the CMSA said.
They will bring back Earth "mysterious gifts" from Tiangong, the agency added.
At present, the landing site and all systems involved are busy putting the final touches on preparations to welcome the astronauts home. ■
