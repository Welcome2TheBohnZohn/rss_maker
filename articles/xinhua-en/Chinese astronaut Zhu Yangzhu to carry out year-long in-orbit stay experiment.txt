JIUQUAN, Nov. 24 (Xinhua) -- Chinese astronaut Zhu Yangzhu, who has already spent six months in orbit, will remain aboard the Tiangong space station for another six months to complete a year-long stay experiment, the China Manned Space Agency announced on Tuesday. ■
