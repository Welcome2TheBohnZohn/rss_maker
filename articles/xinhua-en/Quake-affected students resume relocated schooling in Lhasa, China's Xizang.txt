TITLE:
Quake-affected students resume relocated schooling in Lhasa, China's Xizang

PUBLISHED:
2026-11-05

SOURCE:
Xinhua English

CATEGORY:
Authoritative PRC Media

URL:

ARTICLE TEXT:
A teacher gives a lesson to students from Nagqu at a school in Lhasa, southwest China's Xizang Autonomous Region, Nov. 4, 2026. Over a thousand students from two primary schools in Baqen County of Nagqu City, southwest China's Xizang Autonomous Region, resumed classes at a school in Lhasa on Wednesday.
A 7.0-magnitude earthquake caused severe damage to the two primary schools on Oct. 22, 2026. To ensure relocated schooling, teachers and students from the two schools were transferred to a school in Lhasa, minimizing the disruption to the teaching caused by the earthquake. (Xinhua/Jigme Dorje)
