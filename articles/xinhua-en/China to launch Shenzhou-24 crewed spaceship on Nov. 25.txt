JIUQUAN, Nov. 24 (Xinhua) -- China's Shenzhou-24 crewed spaceship is scheduled to be launched at 8:18 a.m. (Beijing Time) Wednesday from the Jiuquan Satellite Launch Center in northwest China, the China Manned Space Agency (CMSA) announced on Tuesday.
A Long March-2F carrier rocket, which will be used for the launch, will soon be filled with propellant, said Zhang Jingbo, the CMSA spokesperson, at a press conference.
Shenzhou-24 is the eighth crewed mission during the application and development stage of China's space station, and the 41st flight mission of China's manned space program. ■
