ARTICLE TEXT:
SHANGHAI, Nov. 20 (Xinhua) -- The Chinese and U.S. militaries held their second working group meeting of 2026 and the annual session under the Military Maritime Consultative Agreement in east China's Shanghai from Wednesday to Friday this week, the Chinese navy said.
These meetings aimed to implement the important consensus reached by the two heads of state and promote the healthy, stable and sustainable development of ties between the two militaries, the navy noted in a statement.
The two sides had "candid, practical and constructive discussions" on the current maritime and air safety situation of China and the United States, according to the statement.
They reviewed the implementation of the Rules of Behavior for Safety of Air and Maritime Encounters over the past year, and discussed measures to improve military maritime security between the two countries.
The two sides also agreed on the agenda for the 2027 working group meetings. ■
