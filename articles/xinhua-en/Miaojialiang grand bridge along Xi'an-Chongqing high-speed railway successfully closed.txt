TITLE:
Miaojialiang grand bridge along Xi'an-Chongqing high-speed railway successfully closed

PUBLISHED:
2026-10-12

SOURCE:
Xinhua English

CATEGORY:
Authoritative PRC Media

URL:
https://english.news.cn/20261012/fdc88a7b6a9e46cfb4e44eade6bc45bc/c.html

ARTICLE TEXT:
An aerial drone photo taken on Oct. 12, 2026 shows the construction site of the Miaojialiang grand bridge in southwest China's Chongqing Municipality. Located in Chengkou County of Chongqing, the 941.6-meter-long Miaojialiang grand bridge, a key project along the Xi'an-Chongqing high-speed railway, was successfully closed here on Monday. (Xinhua/Tang Yi)
