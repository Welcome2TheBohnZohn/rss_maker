ARTICLE TEXT:
More than 80 years ago during World War II, ordinary Chinese villagers in east China's Zhejiang Province risked their lives to shelter and rescue downed American airmen. Decades later, traces of those wartime encounters still linger. What began in the midst of war has endured across generations, leaving behind a lasting legacy of friendship between the people of China and the United States.
