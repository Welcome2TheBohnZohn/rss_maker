BEIJING, Nov. 6 (Xinhua) -- China has built 54,000 water conservancy projects in the Yellow River Basin over the past 80 years, while advancing water conservancy infrastructure and the scientific development and utilization of water and hydropower resources, according to a press conference held on Friday. ■
