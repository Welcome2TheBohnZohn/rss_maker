ARTICLE TEXT:
Chinese President Xi Jinping concluded a packed trip to New Delhi for the 18th BRICS Summit.
He called on BRICS countries to be pioneers in innovation, peace, mutual learning and global governance reform, and put forward five proposals to deepen practical cooperation.
China will assume the BRICS chairmanship in 2027 and host the 19th BRICS Summit.
