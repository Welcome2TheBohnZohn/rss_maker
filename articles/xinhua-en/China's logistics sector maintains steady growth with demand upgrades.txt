China's logistics sector maintains steady growth with demand upgrades

ARTICLE TEXT:
BEIJING, Nov. 28 (Xinhua) -- China's logistics sector maintained steady growth in the first 10 months of 2026, with demand increasingly driven by emerging and high-end industries, official data showed Saturday.
The total value of national social logistics reached 312.4 trillion yuan (about 46.6 trillion U.S. dollars) from January to October, up 4.8 percent year on year, according to data from the China Federation of Logistics and Purchasing.
Industrial logistics demand showed a shift toward higher-end manufacturing. From January to October, the value of industrial goods logistics rose 5.3 percent year on year. In October alone, logistics demand from high-tech manufacturing grew 17.6 percent year on year, marking its fastest monthly expansion in nearly five years.
"The growth in logistics demand is increasingly concentrated in equipment manufacturing and high-tech manufacturing, reflecting upgrades in the structure used to transport goods," said Liu Yuhang, chief engineer of the federation.
On the import side, while the total value of import goods logistics declined 0.6 percent year on year in the first 10 months, that of imports of mechanical and electrical products and high-tech products rose 35 percent and 44.4 percent, respectively.
In the January-October period, China's logistics industry generated 12.3 trillion yuan in revenue, up 4.2 percent year on year. Revenue at major logistics enterprises grew 6.5 percent, while their operating costs per 100 yuan of revenue fell to 94.5 yuan, down 0.5 yuan from the first three quarters.
Looking ahead, Liu said logistics demand is expected to receive a seasonal boost toward the end of the year, driven by e-commerce promotions and increased year-end production activities.
With policy measures to develop the logistics networks taking effect and digital and green transformation gathering pace, China's logistics sector is expected to maintain steady growth while further improving quality and efficiency throughout the year, providing strong support for the recovery of the real economy, Liu said.
The logistics networks are one of the six networks prioritized during China's 15th Five-Year Plan period (2026-2030), alongside water networks, new-type power grids, computing power networks, next-generation communication networks and urban underground pipeline networks.
In a plan to develop the logistics networks unveiled earlier this year, China set goals to advance the development of the country's logistics network, including bringing total social logistics costs to 13.1 percent of GDP by 2030, down 0.8 percentage points from the end of 2025. ■
