TASHKENT, Nov. 22 (Xinhua) -- A thematic forum of the 2026 edition of the International Forum on Overseas Studies of Xi Jinping Thought on Socialism with Chinese Characteristics for a New Era was held Saturday in Tashkent, capital of Uzbekistan.
Co-organized by the Institute of Russian, Eastern European and Central Asian Studies of the Chinese Academy of Social Sciences and the University of World Economy and Diplomacy of Uzbekistan, the forum was themed "China-Central Asia: High-Quality Belt and Road Cooperation and Green, Innovation-Driven Development."
Participants noted that the "Book House for Xi Jinping's Works," which was inaugurated in January this year, serves as an important platform for China and Uzbekistan to deepen research on Xi Jinping Thought on Socialism with Chinese Characteristics for a New Era and exchange experience in state governance.
They added that China's innovative proposal of the Belt and Road Initiative helps strengthen policy and strategic coordination among countries.
The participants also said the forum's theme has strong practical significance. They stressed that when proposing new infrastructure and technical solutions for Central Asia, all parties should take full consideration of regional characteristics, the actual needs of each country and future development priorities, in which universities, research institutions and think tank experts play an important role.
At the seminar, think tank experts and university scholars from China and Central Asian countries conducted in-depth discussions on topics including the Green Silk Road and the pursuit of sustainable development. ■
