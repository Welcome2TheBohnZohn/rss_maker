Head of the Pumaqangtang border police station arranges police equipment in Pumaqangtang Township in Shannan, southwest China's Xizang Autonomous Region, Aug. 19, 2026. Pumaqangtang, China's highest township, has an average altitude of 5,373 meters, with oxygen levels less than a half of that at sea level.
Here stands the Pumaqangtang border police station, the highest-altitude border police station in China. Police and auxiliary police officers here, with an average age of just over 30, hold their posts on the frigid, oxygen-thin plateau. (Xinhua/Tenzin Nyida)
