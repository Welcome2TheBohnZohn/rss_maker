SHANGHAI, Nov. 5 (Xinhua) -- The China International Import Expo (CIIE), the world's first national-level exhibition dedicated to imports, opened in east China's Shanghai on Thursday.
Themed on "New Advances for a Bright, Win-win Future," the China Pavilion presents a forward-looking perspective, global vision and openness in line with the 15th Five-Year Plan (2026-2030) blueprint.
