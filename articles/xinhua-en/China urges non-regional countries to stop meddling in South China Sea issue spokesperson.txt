China urges non-regional countries to stop meddling in South China Sea issue: spokesperson

ARTICLE TEXT:
BEIJING, Nov. 26 (Xinhua) -- China on Thursday urged certain non-regional countries to respect its sovereignty and rights and interests and stop interfering in the South China Sea issue.
The Philippines and Australia recently signed a defense cooperation agreement, which the Philippine side claimed would bolster the Philippines' credible deterrent posture in the South China Sea to counter China's actions.
Asked to make comment at a regular press conference on Thursday, Chen Xi, a spokesperson for China's Ministry of National Defense, said certain Filipino politicians, driven by personal interests, have been spreading false narratives about China, stirring up trouble, and seeking to rope in outside forces to back them up and make waves.
Such moves will only escalate tensions and undermine regional stability, Chen said.
"The South China Sea should be a sea of peace, friendship and cooperation. Stoking troubles and provoking confrontation are not welcome here," he said.
The spokesperson urged relevant non-regional countries to earnestly respect China's sovereignty and rights and interests in the South China Sea and cease meddling in the issue.
China's actions to safeguard its territorial sovereignty and maritime rights and interests are legitimate and justified, Chen said, adding that China will continue to take necessary measures to respond firmly to provocations and infringement activities.
China will resolutely thwart any attempt to destabilize the South China Sea, Chen said. ■
