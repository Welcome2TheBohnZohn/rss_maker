JIUQUAN, Nov. 25 (Xinhua) -- The China Manned Space Agency (CMSA) announced on Wednesday that the launch of the Shenzhou-24 crewed spaceship was "a complete success."
The spaceship, atop a Long March-2F carrier rocket, blasted off at 8:18 a.m. (Beijing Time) from the Jiuquan Satellite Launch Center in northwest China, sending Chinese astronauts Tang Hongbo, Wu Yi and Pakistani astronaut Muhammad Zeeshan Ali to China's orbiting Tiangong space station, according to the CMSA.
About 10 minutes after the launch, the spaceship separated from the rocket and entered its designated orbit, the CMSA said, adding that the astronauts are in good condition. ■
