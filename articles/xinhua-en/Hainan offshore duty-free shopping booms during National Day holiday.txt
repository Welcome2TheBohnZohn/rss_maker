TITLE:
Hainan offshore duty-free shopping booms during National Day holiday

PUBLISHED:
2026-10-08

SOURCE:
Xinhua English

CATEGORY:
Authoritative PRC Media

URL:

ARTICLE TEXT:
Customers select goods at a duty-free shop in Haikou, south China's Hainan Province, Oct. 7, 2026. According to Haikou customs, the customs authority supervised offshore duty-free purchases worth 855 million yuan (about 126.8 million U.S. dollars) during the seven-day National Day holiday from Oct. 1 to 7, involving 123,000 shopping visits and 696,800 items. (Xinhua/Yang Guanyu)
