A drone photo taken on Oct. 27, 2026 shows a vessel, assisted by tugboats, approaching the ore terminal of Caofeidian Port Area in Tangshan Port in Tangshan, north China's Hebei Province. Tangshan Port handled a total cargo throughput of 665.89 million tonnes in the first three quarters of 2026, marking a 2.01 percent year-on-year growth, according to the local port administration.
