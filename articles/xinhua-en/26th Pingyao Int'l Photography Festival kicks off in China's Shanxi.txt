People visit the 26th Pingyao International Photography Festival (PIP) in Pingyao ancient town in Jinzhong, north China's Shanxi Province, Sept. 19, 2026. The 26th PIP kicked off here on Saturday, featuring works by more than 2,600 photographers from over 30 countries and regions. The event will run until Sept. 23. (Xinhua/Yang Chenguang)
