TITLE:
Farmers busy with apple harvest in Zhongwei, China's Ningxia

PUBLISHED:
2026-10-19

SOURCE:
Xinhua English

CATEGORY:
Authoritative PRC Media

URL:

ARTICLE TEXT:
A farmer works at a cold chain distribution center in Yongkang Town of Shapotou District, Zhongwei City, northwest China's Ningxia Hui Autonomous Region, Oct. 18, 2026. Spreading along the southern edge of the Tengger Desert and the Yellow River, Shapotou District of Zhongwei City has ushered in a bumper harvest season for its drought-enduring apples recently.
Apple plantation now covers 80,200 mu (about 5346.67 hectares) in the district, with an estimated yield of 144,000 tonnes at a worth of over 1 billion yuan (about 150 million U.S. dollars) this season. (Xinhua/Wang Peng)
