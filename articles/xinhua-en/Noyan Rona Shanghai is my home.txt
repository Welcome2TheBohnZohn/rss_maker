For 40 years, Shanghai has been home to Noyan Rona. From an international student to a diplomat and banker, and later an active community participant, his journey offers a unique perspective on China's whole-process people's democracy.
In China, foreign residents have opportunities to take part in community affairs, voice their views and contribute to the legislative process, turning participation into a meaningful sense of connection and belonging.■
