What is the BRICS family looking for in China? And where are the biggest opportunities?
At the 26th China International Fair for Investment and Trade (CIFIT) in east China's coastal city Xiamen, CIFIT participants from Brazil, South Africa, Indonesia, Uganda and Thailand had a clear answer: bigger markets, investment, technology, supply chains and new business partnerships.
