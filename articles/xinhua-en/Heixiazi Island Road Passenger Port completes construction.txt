TITLE:

PUBLISHED:

SOURCE:

A drone photo shows the passenger inspection building of the Heixiazi Island Road Port in northeast China's Heilongjiang Province, Oct. 30, 2026. A completion ceremony for the construction of the Heixiazi Island Road Passenger Port was held in Fuyuan City on Friday.
The Heixiazi Island Road Port will provide efficient round-the-clock customs clearance, effectively easing the clearance pressure on the Fuyuan Port, and deepening economic and trade cooperation and cultural exchanges between China and Russia. (Xinhua/Zhang Tao)
