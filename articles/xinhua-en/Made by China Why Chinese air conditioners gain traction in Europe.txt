BEIJING, Oct. 15 (Xinhua) -- Chinese-made air conditioners (ACs) flew off shelves across Europe, offering local families much-needed respite from this year's blistering summer. Xinhua correspondent Fu Tian visited Chinese AC makers to explore the rapid innovation, robust supply chains, and strong engineering capabilities behind these hot-selling products that benefit the world.
