An aerial drone photo taken on Nov. 25, 2026 shows a scene during the opening of a joint exercise involving the Chinese and Cambodian militaries at a Cambodia-China Joint Support and Training Center at Port Ream, Cambodia. The Chinese and Cambodian militaries have launched a joint exercise in Cambodia on Wednesday.
The exercise, codenamed "Golden Dragon-2026," will focus on "joint counterterrorism and anti-piracy operations and humanitarian relief," according to the statement from China's Ministry of National Defense. (Photo by Jia Siyu/Xinhua)
