BAOSHAN, Sept. 23 (Xinhua) -- In recent years, Baoshan City in southwest China's Yunnan Province has developed distinctive industries such as homestays, camping, dendrobium cultivation and tea.
The city is leveraging ecological tourism and cultural resources to advance rural revitalization, injecting new vitality into the high-quality development of its cultural and tourism industries.
