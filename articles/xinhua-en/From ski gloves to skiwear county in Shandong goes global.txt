JINAN, Nov. 26 (Xinhua) -- Jiaxiang County in east China's Shandong Province has developed an integrated winter sportswear industry, expanding from ski gloves to a full range of apparel. With products exported to over 60 countries and regions, the industry generates an annual output value of about 2.5 billion yuan (about 380 million U.S. dollars) and provides employment for nearly 50,000 people.
