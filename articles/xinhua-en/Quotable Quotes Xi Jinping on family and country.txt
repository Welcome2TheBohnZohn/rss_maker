ARTICLE TEXT:
Friday marks the Mid-Autumn Festival, also known as the Moon Festival, a traditional Chinese holiday when families gather to celebrate.
Under the full moon, people share mooncakes and savor the joy of reunion. The moon goddess Chang'e, the Jade Rabbit, the osmanthus tree and glowing lanterns are among the timeless symbols of the festival.
In Chinese culture, love for one's family is closely tied to a sense of responsibility for the country. President Xi Jinping has often spoken about this connection. Let's revisit his words and the values they reflect.
