MANILA, Nov. 16 (Xinhua) -- Chinese Premier Li Qiang on Monday delivered a speech at the 29th China-ASEAN Summit.
The following is the full text of the speech:
Remarks by H.E. Li Qiang
Premier of the State Council of the People's Republic of China
At the 29th China-ASEAN Summit
Manila, November 16, 2026
Dear Colleagues,
It is my great pleasure to join you at this meeting.
This year marks the fifth anniversary of the China-ASEAN Comprehensive Strategic Partnership. Over the past five years, we have witnessed a growing countercurrent of unilateralism and protectionism and recurring geopolitical conflicts on the global stage. Despite such a challenging environment, China-ASEAN cooperation has maintained strong momentum and scored encouraging achievements. First, we have elevated our cooperation in both output and quality. Trade between the two sides exceeded US$1 trillion last year, up by 20.1 percent compared with 2021. In the first 10 months of this year, our trade increased by 29.6 percent. To date, two-way investment has exceeded US$500 billion in cumulative terms, an increase of over 50 percent compared with five years ago. Cooperation in new areas such as digital economy, green economy and artificial intelligence has made rapid advances. Second, we have further consolidated cooperation mechanisms. We signed the China-ASEAN Free Trade Area 3.0 Upgrade Protocol, creating the institutional foundation for building a mega-sized regional market. We also adopted and delivered on the Plan of Action to Implement the ASEAN-China Comprehensive Strategic Partnership (2026-2030), drawing a promising blueprint for common development in the next five years. Third, we have continued to strengthen connectivity. Major projects such as the Jakarta-Bandung high-speed railway and the China-Laos railway were turned from plans into reality, the Pinglu Canal was officially opened, and the "ASEAN Visa" program was implemented. The flow of personnel, capital, technology and other factors has become more efficient across the region, and generated greater economic returns.
The past five years have been truly extraordinary, and the achievements we scored have not come easily. From our shared journey, we can draw some valuable experience and inspirations, particularly the following three points. First, promoting the tradition of friendship and mutual assistance. China and ASEAN countries share a long history of friendly exchanges. Instead of losing its shine in the turbulent world, this fine tradition has grown more mature and resilient as we work together to tackle one challenge after another. Second, upholding the fundamental principles of openness and mutual benefit. China and ASEAN are both long-standing advocates and contributors of economic globalization and regional integration. Although economic and trade restrictions have substantially increased around the world, we remain firmly committed to safeguarding multilateralism and promoting free trade, and endeavor to make the pie of cooperation bigger. Third, embracing the trend of innovation-driven development. Amid rapid advances in science and technology, we have kept abreast of the times, and worked together to strengthen innovation capabilities and open up new vistas for economic growth. These experience and inspirations are a valuable asset for us all. We must carry them forward and harness them to the fullest so as to keep writing new chapters of China-ASEAN cooperation.
Colleagues,
Five years ago, President Xi Jinping put forward the vision of China and ASEAN jointly building a peaceful, safe and secure, prosperous, beautiful and amicable home. China and ASEAN countries both value the concept of family. We enjoy geographical proximity and our people feel close to each other. Our profound friendship, which has been nurtured over generations, shall not and will not be swayed by any temporary or isolated issue. As we now live in a volatile world, it is all the more important for China and ASEAN to stand united and remove disruptions. We should jointly build an even closer China-ASEAN community with a shared future, preserve and build our home well, and provide greater stability and certainty for the development and prosperity of the entire global village.
First, we should strengthen the safeguards for peace and tranquility. The more complex and severe the international landscape is, the more imperative it is for us to build up strategic trust, properly handle differences through dialogue and negotiation, jointly oppose foreign interference, and uphold regional peace and stability. China looks forward to the early adoption of a Code of Conduct in the South China Sea, so as to provide solid institutional safeguards for lasting peace and stability in the South China Sea.
Second, we should promote regional development and prosperity. We should take the entry into force of the China-ASEAN Free Trade Area 3.0 Upgrade Protocol as an opportunity to deepen industrial and supply chain cooperation, accelerate the development of "hard connectivity" of infrastructure including railways and highways, and strengthen "soft connectivity" in areas such as mechanisms, standards and rules. China welcomes the establishment of the China-ASEAN energy cooperation partnership and the mechanism of energy ministerial meeting. China will work with ASEAN to host a successful Year of AI Cooperation in the next year, carry out "AI plus" cooperation, and explore the alignment with the ASEAN's Digital Economy Framework Agreement, so as to cultivate powerful engines for the high-quality development of the regional economy.
Third, we should increase the benefits to the people. Making life better for the people is an important task for all of us. China will continue to replenish the China-ASEAN Cooperation Fund, and stands ready to step up cooperation with ASEAN on poverty reduction, disaster prevention and mitigation and food security to promote sustainable development of the region. The China-ASEAN Digital Academy will provide 100 full scholarships for ASEAN countries next year and the China-ASEAN Women and Children's Health Cooperation Center is ready to work with ASEAN countries to train 1,000 professionals in the coming three years. We welcome your active participation in these programs. China is willing to strengthen law enforcement cooperation with ASEAN to jointly combat transnational crimes and ensure people's safety.
When the family is in harmony, everything thrives; when people are in harmony, everything goes well. I believe that with the close collaboration between our two sides, our shared home will stay vibrant and prosperous, and embrace a brighter future.
Thank you. ■
