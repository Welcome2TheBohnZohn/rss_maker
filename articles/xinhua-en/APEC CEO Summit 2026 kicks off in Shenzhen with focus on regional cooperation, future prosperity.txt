SHENZHEN, Nov. 17 (Xinhua) -- The Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation (APEC) CEO Summit 2026 opened Tuesday in Shenzhen, south China's Guangdong Province, bringing together business and political leaders from across the Asia-Pacific and beyond to discuss regional economic cooperation and future development.
Under the theme "To Prosper and Win the Future Together," the two-day event has attracted more than 2,000 representatives from 21 APEC economies and 13 non-APEC economies, including Britain, France, Germany and Switzerland.
Participants include representatives from 115 Fortune Global 500 companies, as well as heads of several international organizations and representatives of business associations from Russia, the United States and other economies.
U.S. companies make up the largest group among participating companies from economies other than China, accounting for nearly 20 percent of the total.
As an important part of the 2026 APEC Economic Leaders' Week, the summit is expected to launch a Shenzhen initiative this year, marking the first time in the summit's 30-year history that an outcome document will be issued in the form of an initiative.
In his welcome address, Ren Hongbin, chairman of the China Council for the Promotion of International Trade and chair of the APEC CEO Summit 2026, expressed the hope that the dialogue will spark new ideas and forge broad consensus, injecting fresh momentum into the Asia-Pacific community and global growth.
Attendees at the summit are discussing the new landscape of the Asia-Pacific economy and opportunities for cooperation. Key topics include crafting a new blueprint for connectivity, building an open and inclusive AI innovation ecosystem, and setting a new paradigm for sustainable development in the Asia-Pacific.
Other sessions will focus on advancing the Free Trade Area of the Asia-Pacific, exploring new paths for digital economy cooperation, the Guangdong-Hong Kong-Macao Greater Bay Area and Asia-Pacific development opportunities, and promoting synergized digital and green transformation.
An important event in the APEC calendar, the APEC CEO Summit has been held annually since 1996, serving as the highest-level platform for APEC economy leaders and business representatives to discuss open cooperation and advance regional economic development.
As the host of APEC in 2026, China will hold the 33rd APEC Economic Leaders' Meeting in Shenzhen from Nov. 18 to 19. This year's gathering, under the theme "Building an Asia-Pacific Community to Prosper Together," will focus on three priority areas identified by China -- openness, innovation, and cooperation.
These priorities are "particularly relevant today," according to Antonio Basilio, director of the secretariat of the APEC Business Advisory Council, a mechanism that allows the regional business community to participate in APEC cooperation and submit policy recommendations to APEC economy leaders.
Basilio expects all sides to hold constructive dialogues during the APEC meeting, and enable businesses to conduct trade and investment activities under more coherent, transparent and predictable rules.
Working together with other APEC member economies, China has hosted more than 300 APEC meetings and events this year, drawing a cumulative attendance of over 10,000 participants. In the economic and trade field, China and other APEC economies have reached nearly 50 practical outcomes. ■
