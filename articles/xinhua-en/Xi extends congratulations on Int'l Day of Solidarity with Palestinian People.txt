ARTICLE TEXT:
BEIJING, Nov. 24 (Xinhua) -- Chinese President Xi Jinping extended congratulations to a UN meeting held on Monday to commemorate the International Day of Solidarity with the Palestinian People.
In his message, Xi said that the Palestinian question lies at the heart of the Middle East issue, adding that the current tensions in the Middle East should not divert the international community's attention from the Palestinian issue. The key to resolving the Palestinian question is to stay on course toward the two-state solution and promote the resumption of peace talks between Palestine and Israel, Xi said.
He stressed that the governance of the West Bank and post-war reconstruction in Gaza should be advanced in accordance with the principle of "Palestinians governing Palestine," and opposed any land-grabbing or rights-infringing actions targeting the Palestinian people.
The international community should translate its consensus and commitments in support of Palestine into concrete actions, promote a lasting ceasefire in Gaza and protect the safety of civilians, Xi said.
Xi said that justice will prevail and must not be absent. China has always firmly supported the just cause of the Palestinian people to restore their legitimate national rights, he said.
China will continue to work with all countries that cherish peace and uphold justice to promote the early realization of a comprehensive, just and lasting solution of the Palestinian question, based on the rule of international law and with a view to addressing both its symptoms and root causes, Xi said.
Such efforts will contribute to lasting peace and stability in the Middle East and make positive contributions to world peace, stability, development and prosperity, Xi added. ■
