ARTICLE TEXT:
SHANGHAI, Nov. 24 (Xinhua) -- China is among the world's top countries in 6G patent applications and has developed more than 300 key 6G technology assets, according to the 2026 Global 6G Development Conference held in Shanghai on Tuesday.
"Our 5G networks have been operating for years, laying a solid foundation for 6G evolution, and this foundation has also benefited from cooperation across the global industry," said Wen Ku, chairman of the China Communications Standards Association. As of the end of August, China had built about 5.19 million 5G base stations.
From 3G to 5G, globally unified standards have delivered economies of scale that significantly lowered the cost of communications equipment and connected billions of people to the digital world, and 6G should continue on the path of cooperative development, Wen said.
Zhong Zhihong, chief engineer of the Ministry of Industry and Information Technology, said 6G technology is now at a critical stage of deepening technological breakthroughs and advancing comprehensive standards development, making it all the more necessary for global industry, academia and research parties to build consensus and cooperate closely.
At the conference, the IMT-2030 (6G) promotion group, together with major international organizations, industry alliances and leading Chinese and international enterprises, released the Global 6G Development Philosophy Declaration. The declaration sets out five concepts, including cross-domain integrated innovation with intrinsic intelligence at its core, green and sustainable development throughout the entire chain, and a secure and trustworthy system based on all-domain security, among others.
The conference also launched a pilot program for 6G innovative development jointly by the ministry and provincial governments. Eight provincial-level regions, namely Beijing, Hebei in north China, Shanghai, Jiangsu, Zhejiang and Anhui in east China, Hubei in central China and Guangdong in south China, are participating to jointly build a coordinated national framework for 6G innovation. ■
