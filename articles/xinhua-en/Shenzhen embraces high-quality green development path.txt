A drone photo taken on Oct. 23, 2026 shows a view of Shenzhen Bay Park in Shenzhen, south China's Guangdong Province.
In recent years, Shenzhen has embraced a high-quality green development path.
According to the white paper titled "A City of Symbiosis: the Shenzhen Practice of Harmonious Coexistence between Humanity and Nature," released in August 2026, the city has a forest coverage rate of 39 percent, ecological shorelines of rivers and lakes reaching 65 percent, and a natural shoreline conservation rate of 43.8 percent. To date, more than 6,870 species have been recorded across the city, with the number of nationally protected key species reaching 208. It also boasts 1,350 parks, with per capita green space reaching 14.8 square meters. (Xinhua/Mao Siqian)
