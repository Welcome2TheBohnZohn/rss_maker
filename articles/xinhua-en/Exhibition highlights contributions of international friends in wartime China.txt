ARTICLE TEXT:
A special exhibition revisits a pivotal chapter in China's revolutionary history -- through the eyes of Western journalists who witnessed it. "The Great Bridge-Builder -- Edgar Snow and International Friends in Yan'an" opened in Beijing, as this year marks the 90th anniversary of the Long March victory and of Snow's first trip to northern Shaanxi. ■
