As Chinese President Xi Jinping prepares for his state visit to the United States on Sept. 23-25, his old American friends are sharing fond memories, and their expectations for the upcoming visit.
"Xi Jinping is a friend who respects history, values people-to-people bonds, and keeps his word," one of Xi's old U.S. friends said.
