Beyond the Long March: Wuqi's green transformation

ARTICLE TEXT:
From a historic destination of the Long March to a model of ecological restoration, Wuqi County in northwest China has undergone a remarkable transformation over the past decades.
An American scholar recently visited Wuqi to explore how the determination that carried the Red Army through hardship, lives on in the county's decades-long effort to turn barren hills into thriving green landscapes.
