In commemoration of the 90th anniversary of the victory of the Long March, Xinhua presents this video, bringing to life the voices and memories of the Red Army veterans who lived through it.
Though they have passed away, their voices live on. Through their memories, we revisit a journey marked by unimaginable hardship, sacrifice, and unwavering faith. From fierce battles to snow-covered mountains and treacherous grasslands, their stories bring the Long March to life and remind us of a spirit that continues to echo across generations.■
