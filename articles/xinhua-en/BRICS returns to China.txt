BRICS is coming back to China. At the ongoing BRICS summit in New Delhi, China was announced as the host of next year's BRICS gathering.
But this won't be China's first BRICS moment. Over the past two decades, China has hosted three BRICS summits, and each marked a new milestone in the group's evolution.
