A staff member introduces an age-friendly elevator at the Otis booth during the ninth China International Import Expo (CIIE) in Shanghai, east China, Nov. 7, 2026. A range of age-friendly products and services were showcased at the ninth CIIE as part of efforts to meet the needs of older adults, expand the use of age-friendly solutions in new settings, and promote the high-quality development of the silver economy.
Running from Nov. 5 to 10, this year's CIIE has attracted 4,142 overseas exhibitors, including more than 300 Fortune Global 500 companies and industry leaders. (Xinhua/Du Xiaoyi)
