Openness. Innovation. Cooperation. APEC 2026's priorities are also woven into the story of its host city, Shenzhen.
From a border town to a global metropolis in less than half a century, Shenzhen offers a window into Chinese modernization.
Ahead of the APEC Economic Leaders' Meeting, Xinhua's Miao Xiaojuan invites the people who call Shenzhen home to spell out what makes their city special.
