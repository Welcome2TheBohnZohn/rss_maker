TITLE:
First campus low-altitude drone delivery route launched in China's Guizhou

PUBLISHED:
2026-10-31

SOURCE:
Xinhua English

CATEGORY:
Authoritative PRC Media

URL:
https://english.news.cn/20261031/a3459f737e8247fc928d14b61f54104a/c.html

ARTICLE TEXT:
A drone carrying takeout food takes off from a control center for low-altitude logistics service in Qingzhen City, southwest China's Guizhou Province, Oct. 30, 2026. Recently, the mountainous southwestern Chinese province of Guizhou has officially launched its first campus low-altitude drone delivery route in Qingzhen vocational education town. The aerial route connects local commercial districts directly to campuses, cutting the delivery time for teachers and students to less than 20 minutes with delivery fees starting at 3.8 yuan (about 0.57 U.S. dollars). The service also helps build a training platform integrating low-altitude industry and education to support talent cultivation. (Photo by Yuan Fuhong/Xinhua)
