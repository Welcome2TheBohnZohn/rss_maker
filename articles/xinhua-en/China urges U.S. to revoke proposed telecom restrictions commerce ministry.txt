China urges U.S. to revoke proposed telecom restrictions: commerce ministry

ARTICLE TEXT:
BEIJING, Nov. 22 (Xinhua) -- China on Sunday called on the United States to revoke proposed restrictions on Chinese telecom operators' direct access to U.S. phone numbers, saying the move violates the principles of non-discrimination and fair competition.
The U.S. Federal Communications Commission (FCC) abused its regulatory powers and overstretched the concept of national security, while making arbitrary accusations against Chinese telecom companies without factual basis and maliciously undermining their operations in the United States, a commerce ministry spokesperson noted in response to a media query.
The FCC has recently continued to push to decouple and sever supply chains between China and the United States in telecommunications, communications, testing and certification, the spokesperson added.
Such moves run counter to the important consensus reached by the two heads of state and undermine the stability of China-U.S. economic and trade relations, the spokesperson said.
China urges the United States to immediately rescind the measures and return to the right way of resolving issues through consultations, the spokesperson said, noting that China will take necessary measures to firmly safeguard its own interests. ■
