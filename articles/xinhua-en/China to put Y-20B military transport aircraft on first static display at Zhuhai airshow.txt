ARTICLE TEXT:
BEIJING, Nov. 26 (Xinhua) -- China's Air Force will put its domestically developed Y-20B large military transport aircraft in a static display for the first time at the 16th China International Aviation and Aerospace Exhibition, also known as Airshow China, showcasing the latest advances in the country's military aviation capabilities.
The Air Force will present more than 60 pieces of equipment across 33 types at the exhibition, scheduled to take place in Zhuhai, Guangdong Province, from Dec. 7 to 13, the Air Force said on Thursday.
The exhibition will feature aerial demonstrations and static displays, showcasing the Air Force's latest equipment and modernization achievements.
In the flying displays, the J-35A and J-20A stealth fighters, J-16 multirole fighter, Y-20B, and YY-20A tanker plane will demonstrate their capabilities.
The Air Force's Bayi (August 1) Aerobatic Team will also perform alongside two foreign aerobatic teams, the Russian Aerospace Forces' Russian Knights and the United Arab Emirates Air Force's Al Fursan.
In addition to the Y-20B, the Air Force will display a new-generation early-warning radar system and other equipment on the ground for the first time.
Visitors will get the chance to tour the Y-20B's cargo hold and take part in simulated flight experiences. The Air Force military band will also stage performances and tours at the exhibition.
Interactive activities will be organized across recruitment, talent development, military education, air traffic control and equipment maintenance exhibition areas, including presentations, consultations, simulated operations and immersive experiences.
The exhibition coincides with the 30th anniversary of Airshow China. Air force leaders and representatives from multiple countries will attend international exchanges on military flight training and a forum on unmanned intelligent systems hosted by the Chinese Air Force.
Distinguished representatives of Air Force units and test pilots will also meet with visitors, the Air Force said. ■
