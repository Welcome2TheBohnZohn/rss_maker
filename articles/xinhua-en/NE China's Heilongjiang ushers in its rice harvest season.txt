SHUANGYASHAN, Sept. 9 (Xinhua) -- As the farm's 550,000 mu (about 36,667 hectares) of rice paddies in the hinterland of the Sanjiang Plain enter the harvest season, the Heilongjiang Reclamation Area is advancing all autumn harvest operations in an orderly manner.
