SHENZHEN, Nov. 19 (Xinhua) -- The China-Russia-U.S. summit was the first-ever face-to-face meeting between the heads of state of the three countries and is of great significance with a far-reaching impact, Chinese Foreign Minister Wang Yi said on Thursday, calling it a landmark event that will go down in history.
Wang, also a member of the Political Bureau of the Communist Party of China Central Committee, made the remarks when meeting the press after the 33rd Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation (APEC) Economic Leaders' Meeting in Shenzhen, south China's Guangdong Province.
The meeting between Chinese President Xi Jinping, Russian President Vladimir Putin and U.S. President Donald Trump reached important consensus. The China-Russia-U.S. Heads of State Declaration toward Peace issued after the meeting is instrumental to addressing global challenges and maintaining world peace and stability, Wang said. "You could certainly call it a peace summit."
Noting that the top leaders of the three countries exchanged views on the international situation and global challenges, Wang said the summit sent a positive message to the world.
Calling the meeting a historic one, Wang said it created a paradigm for interaction between major countries in the new era, and represented a new step in building a new type of major-country relations. It was indeed a major pioneering move in the history of international relations, he added.
It was also a landmark meeting showing a sense of responsibility of major countries, Wang noted. Articulating their respective strategic intentions face-to-face, the three leaders agreed to prevent conflict and confrontation and build a paradigm of major-country relations featuring peaceful coexistence, overall stability and balanced development.
"This is not only good for the three countries, but also benefits the whole world," Wang said. ■
