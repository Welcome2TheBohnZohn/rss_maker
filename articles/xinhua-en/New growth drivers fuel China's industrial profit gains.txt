New growth drivers fuel China's industrial profit gains

ARTICLE TEXT:
BEIJING, Nov. 27 (Xinhua) -- Profits of China's major industrial firms rose 16 percent year on year in the first 10 months of 2026, official data showed Friday, as new growth drivers gathered momentum.
Profits of industrial firms with an annual main business revenue of at least 20 million yuan (about 2.98 million U.S. dollars) totaled 6.74 trillion yuan during the January-October period, according to the National Bureau of Statistics (NBS).
The growth rate accelerated 0.5 percentage points from the January-September period, maintaining double-digit growth since the beginning of the year, NBS statistician Yu Weining said. In October alone, profits surged 20.1 percent year on year, accelerating 5.7 percentage points from September.
By sector, mining profits jumped 38.8 percent and manufacturing profits rose 17.5 percent, while profits in the production and supply of electricity, heat, gas and water fell 14.8 percent in the first 10 months.
The electronics sector was a standout driver, with profits increasing 100 percent year on year in the first 10 months and contributing 9.7 percentage points to overall industrial profit growth, as surging demand for AI computing power fueled profits in memory chips and related manufacturing, according to Yu. Within computer manufacturing, profits of computer makers surged 410 percent and those of computer peripheral makers jumped 200 percent.
High-tech manufacturing also shone, with profits up 50.9 percent year on year in the first 10 months, outpacing overall industrial growth by 34.9 percentage points. Driven by large-scale construction of intelligent computing centers, profits of optical fiber and optical cable makers surged 670 percent and 150 percent, respectively, Yu said.
The profit margin on operating revenue stood at 5.69 percent, up 0.45 percentage points year on year.
Yu noted that profits in the non-ferrous metals and petrochemical industry chains have grown rapidly. From January to October, the quick development of new energy and AI industries drove strong demand and price increases for basic metal products such as copper and aluminum.
Profits in the non-ferrous metal mining and non-ferrous metal industries increased by 60.2 percent and 71.7 percent year on year, respectively, collectively contributing 4.6 percentage points to the overall industrial profit growth.
During the same period, the petroleum processing industry reversed losses and achieved a total profit of 106.67 billion yuan. Profits in the oil and gas extraction, chemical, and chemical fiber industries increased by 22.8 percent, 50.3 percent, and 87 percent, respectively.
From January to October, costs per 100 yuan of operating income for major industrial enterprises stood at 85.06 yuan, down 0.42 yuan year on year, maintaining a year-on-year downward trend since the beginning of this year.
Looking ahead, efforts should be strengthened to optimize macroeconomic policies, further smooth economic circulation, continuously foster and expand new growth drivers in the industrial sector, consolidate the foundation for stable industrial operation, and promote high-quality development of the industrial economy, Yu added.
Wen Bin, chief economist at China Minsheng Bank, said profit growth in the fourth quarter is expected to remain stable.
Wen noted that, on the one hand, the sharp decline in industrial enterprises' monthly profits in November last year had lowered the comparison base for the fourth quarter. On the other hand, industrial production remained generally steady, the producer price index rose at a faster pace, and the improvement in profit margins stayed stable, further strengthening price support for corporate earnings.
Wei Zheng, an analyst at LC Securities, said in a research report that energy prices and the AI industry chain remain the two main pillars for industrial profits in the short term. ■
