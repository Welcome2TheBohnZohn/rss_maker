Tariff and trade wars serve no one's interests: Chinese FM spokesperson

ARTICLE TEXT:
BEIJING, Nov. 25 (Xinhua) -- Tariff wars and trade wars serve no one's interests, Chinese Foreign Ministry spokesperson Guo Jiakun said on Wednesday.
Guo made the remarks at a regular news briefing when asked to comment on reports that the U.S. is set to announce tariffs on goods from China and some other countries.
Guo stated that when it comes to handling the economic and trade issues between China and the United States, China's position is consistent and clear. Both sides should resolve relevant issues through consultation on the basis of equality, respect and mutual benefit.
The so-called issue of overcapacity is a false proposition, and China opposes using it as a pretext for political manipulation. The two sides should work together to deliver on the important common understandings between the two heads of state and maintain the steady growth momentum of China-U.S. economic and trade relations, he added. ■
