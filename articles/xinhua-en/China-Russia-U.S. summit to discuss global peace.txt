BEIJING, Nov. 18 (Xinhua) -- The heads of state of China, Russia and the United States will have an in-depth exchange of views on important issues concerning global peace in their upcoming summit, Chinese foreign ministry spokesperson Guo Jiakun said on Wednesday.
Guo made the remarks at a regular news briefing when asked about the trilateral leaders' summit in China's Shenzhen on Nov. 19. ■
