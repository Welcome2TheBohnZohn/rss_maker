This photo taken on Sept. 22, 2026 shows the Sino-German Forum on Cooperation and Development of New Energy Vehicles held during the 2026 World New Energy Vehicle Congress in Haikou, south China's Hainan Province. The 2026 World New Energy Vehicle Congress (WNEVC) opened here on Tuesday. Under the theme "Transformation & Upgrading, Resilient Growth," the congress is expected to attract delegates from more than 20 countries and regions, including executives from multinational automakers, representatives of international industry organizations and leading researchers.
Discussions will cover technological innovation, industrial and supply chain resilience, low-carbon development and global cooperation in the new energy vehicle (NEV) sector. (Xinhua/Guo Cheng)
