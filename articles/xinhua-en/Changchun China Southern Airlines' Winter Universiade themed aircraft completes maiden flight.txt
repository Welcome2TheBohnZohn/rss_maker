ARTICLE TEXT:
China Southern Airlines unveiled a Changchun 2027 Winter Universiade-themed aircraft on Nov. 20, completing its maiden flight from Changchun to Beijing. Featuring the Games' mascots and emblem, the special livery celebrates the city's winter sports culture, while themed cabin activities, souvenirs and refreshments bring the Winter Universiade spirit to passengers at 10,000 meters.
