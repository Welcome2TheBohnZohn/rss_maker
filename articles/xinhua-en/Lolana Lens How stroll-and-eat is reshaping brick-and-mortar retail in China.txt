ARTICLE TEXT:
Across China, going out to shop is becoming about more than what you put in your shopping cart. From pop-up markets and reinvented supermarkets to new neighborhood commercial spaces, brick-and-mortar retail is finding new ways to bring shopping, leisure and everyday life closer together. What looks like a simple "stroll-and-eat" routine is part of a broader shift in how people shop, spend time and live in cities.
