A Taiping drumming dance is staged during a grand parade in Chaoyang, northeast China's Liaoning Province, Oct. 17, 2026. A grand parade of intangible cultural heritage dances was held here on Saturday, as part of the 4th Annual Conference on Intangible Cultural Heritage Safeguarding in China. The event brought together over 20 performing groups, presenting an array of folk dances and drumming performances. (Xinhua/Li Gang)
