China’s New Economic Growth Drivers Index Increased by 12.5% in 2025
To continuously monitor the changes in new drivers of China’s economic development, the Institute of Statistical Sciences of the National Bureau of Statistics of China calculated the new economic growth drivers index for 2025 by employing the fixed-base index method, according to the Statistical Monitoring System for New Industries, New Business Forms and New Business Models. The results showed that China’s new economic growth drivers index in 2025 (taking 2022 as 100) was 153.0, an increase of 12.5% over the previous year. In 2025, all sub-indexes increased compared to the previous year, among which the transformation and upgrading index and the innovation-driven index contributed the most to the growth of the total index.
I. Transformation and upgrading index. The transformation and upgrading index in 2025 was 156.8, an increase of 22.7% over the previous year, contributing 34.1% to the growth of the overall index.
II. Innovation-driven index. The innovation-driven index in 2025 was 156.3, an increase of 12.9% over the previous year, contributing 31.4% to the growth of the overall index.
III. Network economy index. The network economy index in 2025 was 157.8, an increase of 10.8% over the previous year, contributing 27.2% to the growth of the overall index.
IV. Economic vitality index. The economic vitality index in 2025 was 137.2, an increase of 4.8% over the previous year, contributing 7.3% to the growth of the overall index.

II. Calculation Method for the New Economic Growth Drivers Index
The New Economic Growth Drivers Index is a composite index calculated using a linear weighted comprehensive evaluation method. The calculation is based on data from surveys on the “three new” economy (namely, new industries, new business forms, and new business models). The year 2022 is taken as the base period (taking 2022 as 100) in this calculation. The index value represents the variation trend of new economic growth drivers compared to that of 2022.
