Industrial Producer Price Indexes in October 2026
In October 2026, China’s producer price index for industrial products (PPI) increased by 5.7% year on year and 0.7% month on month. The purchasing price index for industrial producers increased by 7.9% year on year and 1.1% month on month. From January to October, on average, the producer price index for industrial products increased by 2.7% year on year, and the purchasing price index for industrial producers increased by 4.0%.
I. Year-on-Year Changes in Industrial Producer Price Indexes
In October, among producer price indexes for industrial products, the price index for means of production increased by 7.4% year on year, pushing the overall producer price index for industrial products up by about 5.77 percentage points. Specifically, the price index for mining and quarrying increased by 27.9%, that for raw materials increased by 11.6%, and that for the processing industry increased by 3.5%. The price index for consumer goods decreased by 0.1%, bringing the overall producer price index for industrial products down by about 0.03 percentage points. Specifically, the price index for food decreased by 1.6%, that for clothing decreased by 1.3%, that for articles for daily use decreased by 0.7%, and that for durable consumer goods increased by 1.4%.
Among purchasing price indexes for industrial producers, the price index for fuel and power increased by 18.6%, that for raw chemical materials increased by 16.8%, that for non-ferrous metals and cables increased by 16.7%, that for textile materials increased by 5.3%, that for agricultural and sideline products increased by 0.5%, that for building materials and non-metals decreased by 1.9%, and that for ferrous metals decreased by 1.1%.
II. Month-on-Month Changes in Industrial Producer Price Indexes
In October, among producer price indexes for industrial products, the price index for means of production increased by 0.9% month on month, pushing the overall producer price index for industrial products up by about 0.75 percentage points. Specifically, the price index for mining and quarrying increased by 2.8%, that for raw materials increased by 2.0%, and that for the processing industry increased by 0.2%. The price index for consumer goods decreased by 0.1%, bringing the overall producer price index for industrial products down by about 0.01 percentage points. Specifically, the price index for food remained flat, that for clothing decreased by 0.2%, and that for articles for daily use and durable consumer goods both decreased by 0.1%.
Among purchasing price indexes for industrial producers, the price index for fuel and power increased by 4.1%, that for raw chemical materials increased by 3.2%, that for textile materials increased by 0.6%, that for building materials and non-metals increased by 0.4%, that for ferrous metals decreased by 0.6%, and that for non-ferrous metals and cables, and agricultural and sideline products both decreased by 0.3%.

Annotations:
1. Index Interpretation
The industrial producer price index comprises the producer price index for industrial products (PPI) and the purchasing price index for industrial producers.
The PPI reflects the trend and magnitude of changes in producer prices for products when they are sold for the first time by industrial enterprises.
The purchasing price index for industrial producers reflects the trend and magnitude of changes in prices for intermediate inputs purchased by industrial enterprises.
2. Statistical Coverage
The statistical survey of the PPI covers prices for 41 major industrial categories and more than 1,300 basic categories of industrial products; the statistical survey of the purchasing price index for industrial producers covers prices for 9 major categories and more than 800 basic categories of industrial products.
3. Survey Method
The survey of the industrial producer price index adopts an approach that combines surveys of main units and typical surveys, covering more than 40,000 industrial enterprises across China.
4. Statistical Standard
The industries are classified based on the Industrial Classification for National Economic Activities (GB/T 4754-2017).
5. Data Description
Due to rounding, the aggregate data may be equal to the high or low value of the classified figures. For some categories or industries where price changes are minor, the growth rate becomes zero after being rounded to one decimal place. In such cases, the price change is described as “flat”.
6. Base Period Rotation
According to the statistical system arrangement, starting from January 2026, China’s PPI has been compiled and published with 2025 as the base period. Compared with the previous base period, the survey categories, surveyed enterprises and representative specification items, and the category weights have been adjusted to reflect the latest changes in the industrial production structure. According to calculations, the impact of the base period rotation on the monthly year-on-year PPI is approximately 0.08 percentage points on average.
