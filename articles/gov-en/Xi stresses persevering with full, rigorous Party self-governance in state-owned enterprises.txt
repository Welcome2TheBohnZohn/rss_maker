TITLE:
Xi stresses persevering with full, rigorous Party self-governance in state-owned enterprises

PUBLISHED:

CATEGORY:
Authoritative PRC Media

ARTICLE TEXT:
BEIJING, Nov. 13 -- Chinese President Xi Jinping has emphasized the need to persevere with full and rigorous Party self-governance in state-owned enterprises (SOEs) to provide strong guarantees for SOEs and state capital to get stronger, do better, and grow bigger.
Xi, also general secretary of the Communist Party of China (CPC) Central Committee and chairman of the Central Military Commission, made the remarks in a recent instruction on Party building in SOEs.
He noted that Party building in SOEs has been strengthened and new progress has been made across the board since the 18th CPC National Congress in 2012.
Xi urged further efforts to uphold and strengthen the Party's overall leadership of SOEs, better integrate the Party's leadership into all links of corporate governance, advance in-depth integration of Party building with production and operation, foster high-caliber, professional teams, and build good conduct, enforce discipline and fight corruption.
The instruction was conveyed at a meeting on Party building in SOEs held in Beijing on Friday. Cai Qi, a member of the Standing Committee of the Political Bureau of the CPC Central Committee and a member of the Secretariat of the CPC Central Committee, attended the event.
Cai said Xi's important instruction has charted the course for further strengthening Party building in SOEs. Cai emphasized the need to accurately grasp the new requirements for Party building in SOEs arising from the evolving global, national and Party conditions. He called for efforts to resolutely combat corruption and further institutionalize and standardize the Party's leadership over SOEs.
