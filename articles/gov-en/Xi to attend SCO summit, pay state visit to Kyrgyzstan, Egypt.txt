TITLE:
Xi to attend SCO summit, pay state visit to Kyrgyzstan, Egypt

PUBLISHED:
2026-08-26

SOURCE:
PRC Government English

CATEGORY:
Authoritative PRC Media

URL:
https://english.www.gov.cn/news/202608/26/content_WS6a8e9ec1c6d00ca5f9a0ccc6.html

ARTICLE TEXT:
BEIJING, Aug. 26 -- From August 30 to September 3, Chinese President Xi Jinping will attend the Shanghai Cooperation Organization Summit 2026 in Bishkek, and pay a state visit to Kyrgyzstan and Egypt upon the invitation of President Sadyr Japarov of Kyrgyzstan and President Abdel Fattah El-Sisi of Egypt, a Chinese foreign ministry spokesperson announced on Wednesday.
