TITLE:
Xi says China, Singapore should strengthen strategic communication, enhance coordination

ARTICLE TEXT:
SHENZHEN, Nov. 20 -- Chinese President Xi Jinping said on Friday that China and Singapore should strengthen strategic communication and enhance solidarity and coordination as the world is undergoing accelerated changes unseen in a century.
The two countries should be sincere friends who trust each other and support each other in pursuing development paths suited to their respective national conditions, said Xi when meeting with Singaporean Prime Minister Lawrence Wong in Shenzhen, south China's Guangdong Province.
Wong attended the 33rd APEC Economic Leaders' Meeting held from Wednesday to Thursday in Shenzhen.
During the meeting, Xi called for advancing the all-round high-quality future-oriented partnership between China and Singapore to new heights, and stressed the need for both sides to advance high-quality Belt and Road cooperation.
The two countries should launch negotiations for upgraded Version 4.0 of their free trade agreement, and deliver signature cooperation outcomes in areas such as the digital economy, green development, and artificial intelligence, Xi added.
Noting that both countries should be "a progressive force" for championing international fairness and justice, Xi called for safeguarding regional peace and stability while jointly building an Asia-Pacific community and a community with a shared future for humanity.
For his part, Wong stated that Singapore firmly adheres to the one-China policy and opposes "Taiwan independence."
Singapore stands ready to work with China to enhance political mutual trust, deepen cooperation in areas such as trade, industrial and supply chains, and artificial intelligence, and promote people-to-people exchanges, the prime minister noted.
Wong said Singapore is willing to strengthen communication and cooperation with the Chinese side on international affairs to jointly address global challenges.
