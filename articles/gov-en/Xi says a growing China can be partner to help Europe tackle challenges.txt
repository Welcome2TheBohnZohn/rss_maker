TITLE:
Xi says a growing China can be partner to help Europe tackle challenges

CATEGORY:

ARTICLE TEXT:
BEIJING, Nov. 6 -- A growing and developing China is not the source of Europe's problems, but a partner that can help Europe address its challenges, said Chinese President Xi Jinping during his meeting with Austrian Chancellor Christian Stocker in Beijing on Friday.
Xi stressed that China and Europe should focus on areas of cooperation, resolve economic and trade frictions through dialogue and consultation, and achieve win-win results and common development.
