Chinese vice premier urges pragmatic cooperation in global mining development

ARTICLE TEXT:
TIANJIN, Sept. 10 -- Chinese Vice Premier He Lifeng on Thursday called for pragmatic cooperation to build a win-win, green and prosperous future for the global mining industry.
He, also a member of the Political Bureau of the Communist Party of China Central Committee, made the remarks while addressing the opening ceremony of the 2026 China Mining Conference and Exhibition in Tianjin, north China.
China has deepened global mining cooperation and forged a high-quality development path featuring openness, inclusiveness and win-win cooperation, He said.
The vice premier noted that international mining cooperation is an important vehicle for countries to complement each other's resource advantages and promote economic and social development, and is also a practical necessity for safeguarding the security and stability of global industrial and supply chains.
China is willing to deepen all-round pragmatic cooperation with all countries, share mining development opportunities, and jointly create a green future of mutual benefit and prosperity for the global mining industry, He added.
During his inspection in Tianjin, He stressed the need to further implement the strategy for the coordinated development of the Beijing-Tianjin-Hebei region, accelerate urban renewal, and support the development of new business forms, including cross-border e-commerce, financial leasing, and logistics and warehouse services.
