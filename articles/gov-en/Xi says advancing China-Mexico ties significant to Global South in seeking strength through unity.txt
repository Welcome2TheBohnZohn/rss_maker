Xi says advancing China-Mexico ties significant to Global South in seeking strength through unity

ARTICLE TEXT:
SHENZHEN, Nov. 17 -- Chinese President Xi Jinping said Tuesday that advancing China-Mexico relations to new heights, across broader areas and in greater depth is of great significance to implementing their respective national development strategies and supporting the Global South in seeking strength through unity.
Xi made the remarks while meeting with Mexican President Claudia Sheinbaum Pardo, who is in Shenzhen to attend the 33rd Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation (APEC) Economic Leaders' Meeting, scheduled for Nov. 18-19.
China is ready to work with Mexico to steer bilateral relations toward high-quality development, promote the building of an Asia Pacific that is prosperous, stable, open, interconnected, universally beneficial, inclusive, united and mutually supportive, as well as a more just and equitable global governance system, and contribute to world peace and common development, Xi added.
Xi called on China and Mexico to deepen political mutual trust, strengthen exchanges and cooperation at all levels, stay committed to mutual benefit and win-win results, strengthen strategic alignment, and deepen cooperation on infrastructure, science and technology, clean energy, culture and tourism.
He stressed the need for both sides to uphold fairness and justice, boost multilateral cooperation, enhance coordination at multilateral platforms such as the United Nations, and safeguard the basic norms governing international relations.
Xi also called for deepening cooperation between China and Latin American and Caribbean (LAC) countries by advancing the five programs for the China-LAC community with a shared future.
Noting that Mexico and China enjoy deep-rooted friendship, share similar development philosophies and have witnessed fruitful cooperation across various fields, Sheinbaum said Mexico is ready to expand cooperation with China in areas such as trade, investment, science and technology, manufacturing and artificial intelligence, while promoting exchanges in health, education, culture and tourism.
She said China, as this year's APEC host, has played an important role in advancing Asia-Pacific cooperation, expressing confidence that the upcoming APEC Economic Leaders' Meeting would be a complete success and lay a solid foundation for future regional cooperation.
Mexico stands ready to work with China to strengthen multilateral coordination, uphold multilateralism, and safeguard the common interests of the Global South, said Sheinbaum.
