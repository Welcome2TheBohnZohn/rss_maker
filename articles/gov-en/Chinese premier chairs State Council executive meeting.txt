Chinese premier chairs State Council executive meeting

ARTICLE TEXT:
BEIJING, Nov. 27 -- Chinese Premier Li Qiang on Friday chaired a State Council executive meeting, which heard a report on the comprehensive inspection of efforts to promote high-quality development and studied work on further deepening comprehensive investment and financing reforms in the capital market.
The meeting also heard a report on the development of the national standards system, and reviewed and approved a draft regulation on the investigation and rectification of potential workplace safety hazards.
Solid efforts should be made to rectify problems discovered in the inspection, the meeting said, urging action to ensure that all goals and tasks for the year are accomplished to a high standard.
It is imperative to adjust and improve policies and measures that no longer meet the requirements of high-quality development, optimize the incentive and constraint mechanisms, and ensure better coordination of policies, the meeting said.
Stressing that the capital market is vital to overall economic development, the meeting called for efforts to strengthen counter-cyclical adjustments and tackle critical reform tasks at the same time.
The meeting stressed the need to improve market-stabilizing mechanisms, give full play to existing policies, and mull and introduce new measures to promote steady and sound development of the capital market.
According to the meeting, efforts should be made to encourage listed companies to pay more dividends and step up share repurchases, give greater play to state-backed funds in steadying the market, and optimize the quasi-"stabilization fund" function of the state-owned Central Huijin Investment Ltd. to provide sufficient liquidity.
The meeting also underlined the need to improve policies to support and guide medium- and long-term funds such as insurance funds, enterprise annuities, and social security funds into the market, and strengthen the capacity to guard against and respond to imported risks.
It also underlined efforts to boost the internal momentum for high-quality development of the capital market by deepening reform, and to move faster to develop a multi-tiered capital market.
It is imperative to strengthen law-based whole-chain supervision and regulation of the capital market, take firm action against financial fraud, non-compliant share reductions and other violations, and effectively protect the lawful rights and interests of investors.
The meeting also stressed efforts to systematically improve the standards system, with measures including abolishing local standards that do not meet the requirements of building a unified national market, strengthening the supply of high-quality standards, and taking a forward-looking approach to planning standards for emerging industries and future industries.
In terms of workplace safety, the meeting stressed that efforts should focus on key industries and sectors, and that firm measures should be taken to prevent serious and major accidents.
