ARTICLE TEXT:
BEIJING, Nov. 20 -- China is willing to work with Australia to focus on common interests and boost cooperation in areas including green development and artificial intelligence (AI), Chinese Premier Li Qiang said on Friday.
Li made the remarks during the 11th China-Australia Annual Leaders' Meeting with Australian Prime Minister Anthony Albanese in Beijing.
Noting next year marks the 55th anniversary of China-Australia diplomatic relations, Li said China stands ready to work with Australia to seize this opportunity to focus on common interests, properly manage differences, and deliver more outcomes in the comprehensive strategic partnership.
China is willing to expand trade volume and optimize its structure with Australia, develop cooperation highlights in emerging areas such as green and low-carbon development, new energy vehicles and AI, and further enhance trade and investment liberalization and facilitation, Li said.
The two sides should make next year's China-Australia Year of Tourism a success and deepen exchanges and cooperation in culture, youth, local areas, media and think tanks, the premier noted.
China will strengthen communication and coordination with Australia within the UN, APEC and other frameworks, jointly uphold the multilateral system and free trade, and safeguard international fairness and justice, he said.
Albanese congratulated China on successfully hosting the 33rd APEC Economic Leaders' Meeting in Shenzhen in south China. He said Australia and China should take a long-term perspective, not let differences disrupt bilateral relations, and actively advance cooperation.
Albanese said Australia is willing to deepen cooperation with China in economy and trade, agriculture, energy and other fields, and accelerate cooperation in emerging fields like clean energy, AI, electric vehicles and digital economy.
