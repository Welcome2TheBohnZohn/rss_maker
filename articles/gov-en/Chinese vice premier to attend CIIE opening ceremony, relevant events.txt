ARTICLE TEXT:
BEIJING, Nov. 3 -- Chinese Vice Premier Ding Xuexiang will attend the opening ceremony of the ninth China International Import Expo (CIIE) and relevant events, and deliver a keynote speech, a Ministry of Commerce spokesperson announced Tuesday.
The ninth CIIE will be held from Nov. 5 to 10 in Shanghai, said spokesperson He Yadong.
