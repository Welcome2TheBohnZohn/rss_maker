Chinese vice premier, Singapore's senior minister meet on expanding cooperation

ARTICLE TEXT:
SINGAPORE, Nov. 23 -- Chinese Vice Premier Ding Xuexiang met on Monday with Singapore's Senior Minister Lee Hsien Loong on expanding bilateral cooperation.
Ding, also a member of the Standing Committee of the Political Bureau of the Communist Party of China Central Committee, said that under the care and guidance of the leaders of the two countries, the bilateral cooperation mechanisms between China and Singapore have been operating smoothly, with fruitful outcomes achieved across various fields.
Ding said he co-chaired bilateral cooperation mechanism meetings with Singaporean Deputy Prime Minister Gan Kim Yong on Monday morning to discuss, in line with the strategic guidance of the leaders of the two countries, new pathways and approaches for further expanding future cooperation and better promoting the well-being of the two peoples.
China stands ready to work with Singapore to translate the leaders' aspiration and consensus on deepening cooperation into concrete actions and outcomes, fully demonstrating the comprehensive, high-quality and forward-looking nature of bilateral relations, he said.
China has remained firmly committed to deepening reform comprehensively, pursuing opening up, and promoting independent innovation, so as to firmly hold the future of the country's development in its own hands, Ding said.
In the face of the complex and turbulent international situation, the two sides should continue to follow the right strategic direction, maintain close communication and coordination, and promote steady and sustained progress in bilateral cooperation, he said.
China upholds the principles of putting people first and developing artificial intelligence (AI) for the positive and for good, and is committed to advancing AI development so as to empower various sectors and industries and to improve people's well-being, he said.
China stands ready to expand cooperation with Singapore in relevant fields, Ding said.
For his part, Lee Hsien Loong congratulated the successful convening of the bilateral cooperation mechanism meetings, which yielded many new outcomes, and said that bilateral relations enjoy sound development, with extensive cooperation across a wide range of fields.
Singaporean Prime Minister Lawrence Wong's visit to China to attend the APEC Economic Leaders' Meeting, as well as his fruitful meetings and talks with Chinese President Xi Jinping and Premier Li Qiang, have identified the priorities and direction for bilateral cooperation in the next stage, he said.
Noting the profound changes taking place in the world and in both China and Singapore, Lee expressed the hope that the two sides would maintain communication and sustain the momentum of bilateral relations.
He called on both sides to actively explore new areas of cooperation and work together to achieve further tangible outcomes.
