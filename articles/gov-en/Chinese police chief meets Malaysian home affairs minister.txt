ARTICLE TEXT:
SHENZHEN, Nov. 18 -- Chinese State Councilor and Minister of Public Security Wang Xiaohong on Wednesday met with Minister of Home Affairs of Malaysia Saifuddin Nasution Ismail in Shenzhen, south China's Guangdong Province.
Wang noted that the Chinese side stands ready to work with Malaysia to step up joint efforts to combat transnational crimes and advance the development of the International Alliance Combating Telecom and Cyber Fraud.
The alliance was inaugurated in China in September this year.
He also called for stronger cooperation in key areas to further enhance bilateral law enforcement and security cooperation.
Saifuddin expressed his willingness to maintain close communication with the Chinese side and further advance law enforcement and security cooperation between the two countries.
