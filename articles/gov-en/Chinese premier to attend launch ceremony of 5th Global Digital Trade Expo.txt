ARTICLE TEXT:
BEIJING, Sept. 21 -- Chinese Premier Li Qiang will attend the launch ceremony of the 5th Global Digital Trade Expo and related activities, and deliver a speech, according to Chinese commerce ministry spokesperson He Yadong.
The event will be held in Hangzhou, capital of east China's Zhejiang Province from Sept. 23 to 27.
