ARTICLE TEXT:
BEIJING, Nov. 25 -- Chinese State Councilor and Minister of Public Security Wang Xiaohong met with Monica Juma, executive director of the United Nations Office on Drugs and Crime (UNODC), in Beijing on Wednesday.
Wang said China will resolutely safeguard the core position of the United Nations, deepen practical cooperation with the UNODC in the field of drug control, join hands to address the threat of transnational organized crime, and jointly create a new model of international cooperation in combating telecom fraud.
Juma said she highly appreciated China's firm determination and pragmatic achievements in combating transnational crimes such as drugs, and expressed willingness to further strengthen cooperation to jointly address new challenges to global security.
