Chinese vice premier stresses high-quality development in east China's Jiangxi

ARTICLE TEXT:
NANCHANG, Nov. 18 -- Chinese Vice Premier Ding Xuexiang has called on Jiangxi Province in east China to build on its distinctive strengths, foster new growth drivers and achieve new progress in high-quality development.
Ding, also a member of the Standing Committee of the Political Bureau of the Communist Party of China Central Committee, made the comments during a research tour of the province from Monday to Wednesday this week.
He first visited a site in Yudu County, where the Central Red Army had assembled ahead of the Long March. Noting that this year marks the 90th anniversary of the victory of the Red Army's Long March, Ding urged carrying forward the great spirit of the Long March, steadfastly advancing high-quality development, and continually meeting the people's growing aspirations for a better life.
Ding visited a primary school and a local company in Yudu to learn about efforts to improve basic education and the development of key local industries, and heard suggestions on promoting the revitalization of old revolutionary base areas.
He called for ensuring that old revolutionary base areas are not left behind in the modernization process, developing county-level economies, expanding employment opportunities and increasing local residents' incomes, consolidating and building on the gains made in poverty alleviation, addressing gaps in infrastructure and public services, and improving the quality of basic education, so as to steadily improve people's lives.
He also visited a university laboratory and local companies in Jiangxi to learn about scientific research and industrial development.
High-level sci-tech innovation platforms should be established and resources pooled to advance basic research and original innovation to meet the country's urgent needs and long-term priorities, Ding said.
He called for building on local research capabilities and industrial foundations, leveraging comparative advantages, developing new quality productive forces in light of local conditions, and fostering new drivers of economic growth.
He stressed the need to deepen the integration of sci-tech innovation and industrial innovation, and strengthen innovation capabilities to support the steady and sound development of China's sci-tech finance sector.
