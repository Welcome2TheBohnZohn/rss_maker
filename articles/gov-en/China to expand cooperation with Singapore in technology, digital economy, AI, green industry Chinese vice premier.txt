China to expand cooperation with Singapore in technology, digital economy, AI, green industry: Chinese vice premier

ARTICLE TEXT:
SINGAPORE, Nov. 23 -- China is willing to work with Singapore to upgrade cooperation in traditional fields, and expand cooperation in emerging fields such as technological innovation, the digital economy, artificial intelligence (AI), and green industries, Chinese Vice Premier Ding Xuexiang said Monday.
Ding, also a member of the Standing Committee of the Political Bureau of the Communist Party of China Central Committee, met with Singaporean Deputy Prime Minister Gan Kim Yong in Singapore.
They also co-chaired the 22nd China-Singapore Joint Council for Bilateral Cooperation Meeting, the 27th China-Singapore Suzhou Industrial Park Joint Steering Council Meeting, the 18th China-Singapore Tianjin Eco-City Joint Steering Council Meeting, and the 10th China-Singapore (Chongqing) Demonstration Initiative on Strategic Connectivity Joint Steering Council Meeting.
Ding said that the practical cooperation between China and Singapore has achieved remarkable results, reflecting the all-round high-quality future-oriented nature of bilateral relations.
Against a complex international backdrop, both sides should build on past successes, adapt to changing circumstances, seize new opportunities and explore new avenues for cooperation to support their respective modernization efforts, he said.
China stands ready to work with Singapore to implement the important consensus reached by the two countries' leaders, make full use of the role of intergovernmental cooperation in driving broader collaboration, and explore ways to better align China's 15th Five-Year Plan with Singapore's national development priorities, Ding said.
Gan said that Prime Minister Lawrence Wong had just visited China, during which he had productive exchanges with Chinese leaders on Singapore-China relations, providing direction for bilateral cooperation.
Singapore is willing to work with China to explore ways to make better use of mechanisms and intergovernmental projects, deepen cooperation in such fields as the digital economy, AI, the services sector and connectivity, and deliver more practical results under the all-round high-quality future-oriented partnership, said Gan.
The meetings yielded 29 outcomes in emerging and frontier areas, financial cooperation, health and other fields.
