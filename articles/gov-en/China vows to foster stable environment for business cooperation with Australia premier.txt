TITLE:
China vows to foster stable environment for business cooperation with Australia: premier

PUBLISHED:
2026-11-21

SOURCE:

CATEGORY:
Authoritative PRC Media

ARTICLE TEXT:
BEIJING, Nov. 20 -- China stands ready to work with Australia to eliminate disruptions to normal economic and trade exchanges, and provide a favorable environment and stable expectations for business cooperation between the two countries, Chinese Premier Li Qiang said on Friday.
Li made the remarks while jointly attending the 9th China-Australia CEO Roundtable with Australian Prime Minister Anthony Albanese in Beijing.
Li said China-Australia relations and cooperation have made considerable new progress over the past year under the strategic guidance of the leaders of the two countries.
These hard-won achievements came amid severe disruptions to international trade and sluggish global economic growth, fully demonstrating the strong inherent momentum of bilateral cooperation, he said.
Practice has repeatedly shown that China and Australia have highly complementary economic strengths and extensive common interests, making them natural partners for cooperation and opportunities for each other's development, Li added.
Noting that global development still faces many uncertainties, Li said that stable and reliable partners are all the more important in times of uncertainty. China and Australia should further strengthen economic and trade ties to achieve broader and higher-level mutually beneficial cooperation, he said.
Li called on the two sides to deepen cooperation in three key areas.
First, the two countries should focus on consumption upgrading to improve people's quality of life. While consolidating and expanding traditional trade in agricultural and food products, they should tap the potential for cooperation in tourism, education, healthcare and elderly care, biomedicine and high-end medical services, and develop more high-quality products and services to meet people's needs throughout their lives.
Second, the two sides should strengthen innovation cooperation, particularly in digital and intelligent technologies. They should better integrate their respective strengths in innovation, enhance mutual empowerment and deepen cooperation in the application and industrialization of new technologies, so as to accelerate the transformation of research achievements into new products and orders for businesses in both countries and better share the benefits of innovation.
Third, the two countries should deepen cooperation in green and low-carbon transition for sustainable development. They should jointly develop competitive green products, build green steel supply chains and new energy vehicle industrial and supply chains, explore third-party markets together, and move up the global green value chain.
Li stressed that the Chinese government stands ready to work with the Australian government to keep bilateral relations on the right direction, maintain win-win cooperation as the prevailing trend, and eliminate disruptions to normal economic and trade exchanges, so as to provide a favorable environment and stable expectations for business cooperation.
China's door to the outside world will only open wider, and more Australian companies are welcome to deepen their presence in the Chinese market, Li said.
He expressed hope that the business communities of both countries would continue to serve as pioneers of mutually beneficial cooperation and promoters of bilateral friendship, and help achieve new results in China-Australia economic and trade cooperation.
Albanese said China is Australia's largest trading partner, and that bilateral economic and trade cooperation contributes to the economic development of both countries and brings tangible benefits to their peoples.
Noting that next year marks the 55th anniversary of the establishment of diplomatic relations between Australia and China, Albanese said Australia stands ready to take this opportunity to strengthen mutually beneficial cooperation with China in areas such as artificial intelligence, green transition and modern agriculture, and deepen people-to-people and cultural exchanges.
He expressed hope that the business communities of the two countries would learn from each other, draw on each other's experiences, expand cooperation, and make greater contributions to the development of Australia-China relations.
