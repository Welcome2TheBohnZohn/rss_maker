ARTICLE TEXT:
BEIJING, Nov. 16 -- Chinese Premier Li Qiang returned to Beijing on Monday after attending the 29th China-ASEAN Summit in Manila, the Philippines.
When leaving Manila, Li was seen off at the airport by Philippine Finance Secretary Frederick Go, Chinese Ambassador to the Philippines Jing Quan and Chinese Ambassador to ASEAN Wang Qing.
