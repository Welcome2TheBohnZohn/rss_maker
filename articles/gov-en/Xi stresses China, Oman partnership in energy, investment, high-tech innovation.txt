Xi stresses China, Oman partnership in energy, investment, high-tech innovation
Updated: November 2, 2026 15:55Xinhua
BEIJING, Nov. 2 -- Chinese President Xi Jinping said on Monday that China stands ready to deepen the alignment of development strategies with Oman, turning the two countries into stable and reliable partners in energy, partners with great potential in investment, and partners in high-tech innovation.
Xi made the remarks during talks with Sultan Haitham Bin Tarik Al Said of Oman, who is on a state visit to China.
This marks the first visit to China by Oman's head of state since the establishment of diplomatic relations between the two countries 48 years ago.
