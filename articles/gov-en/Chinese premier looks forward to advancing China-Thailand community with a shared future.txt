Chinese premier looks forward to advancing China-Thailand community with a shared future

ARTICLE TEXT:
BANGKOK, Nov. 13 -- Chinese Premier Li Qiang said on Friday that he looks forward to in-depth exchanges of views with Thai leaders on deepening China-Thailand comprehensive strategic cooperation and making further substantive progress in the building of a China-Thailand community with a shared future.
Li arrived in Bangkok for an official visit to Thailand at the invitation of Thai Prime Minister Anutin Charnvirakul.
