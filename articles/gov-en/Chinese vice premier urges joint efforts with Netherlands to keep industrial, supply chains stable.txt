TITLE:
Chinese vice premier urges joint efforts with Netherlands to keep industrial, supply chains stable

ARTICLE TEXT:
BEIJING, Nov. 25 -- Chinese Vice Premier Ding Xuexiang on Wednesday called for joint efforts with the Netherlands to ensure that industrial and supply chains remain stable and unobstructed.
Ding, also a member of the Standing Committee of the Political Bureau of the Communist Party of China Central Committee, made the remarks when meeting with Minister of Foreign Affairs of the Netherlands Tom Berendsen in Beijing.
Ding said China and the Netherlands have an open and pragmatic partnership for comprehensive cooperation, and their bilateral cooperation is at the forefront of China-EU cooperation.
Noting next year marks the 55th anniversary of the establishment of ambassadorial diplomatic relations between the two countries, Ding said China stands ready to work with the Netherlands to implement the important consensus reached by the two heads of state, strengthen exchanges and cooperation in all fields, and promote the sound and steady growth of bilateral relations.
As major economies in the world, China and the Netherlands should also work together to continuously inject new momentum into the common development of both countries and the world at large, Ding pointed out.
Berendsen congratulated China on the successful hosting of the APEC Economic Leaders' Meeting in Shenzhen and spoke highly of China's development achievements.
He said the Dutch side stands ready to strengthen high-level exchanges with China, deepen practical cooperation across all fields, properly handle differences through dialogue and consultation, and achieve common development and prosperity.
