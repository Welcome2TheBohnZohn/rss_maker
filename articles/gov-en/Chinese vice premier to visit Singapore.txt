ARTICLE TEXT:
BEIJING, Nov. 20 -- Chinese Vice Premier Ding Xuexiang will visit Singapore from November 22 to 23 at the invitation of Deputy Prime Minister of Singapore Gan Kim Yong, a Chinese foreign ministry spokesperson announced on Friday.
Ding, member of the Standing Committee of the Political Bureau of the Communist Party of China (CPC) Central Committee, will co-chair with Gan bilateral cooperation mechanism meetings, the spokesperson said.
The meetings include the 22nd meeting of the China-Singapore Joint Council for Bilateral Cooperation, the 27th meeting of the China-Singapore Suzhou Industrial Park Joint Steering Council, the 18th meeting of the China-Singapore Tianjin Eco-City Joint Steering Council, and the 10th meeting of the China-Singapore (Chongqing) Demonstration Initiative on Strategic Connectivity Joint Steering Council, according to the spokesperson.
