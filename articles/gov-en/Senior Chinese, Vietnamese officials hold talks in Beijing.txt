Senior Chinese, Vietnamese officials hold talks in Beijing

ARTICLE TEXT:
BEIJING, Nov. 3 -- Senior Chinese official He Lifeng held talks with Nguyen Thanh Nghi, a member of the Political Bureau of the Communist Party of Vietnam (CPV) Central Committee and secretary of the CPV Central Committee, in Beijing on Monday.
The two sides exchanged views on topics such as economic relations between China and Vietnam, and bilateral economic and trade exchanges and cooperation.
He, a member of the Political Bureau of the Communist Party of China Central Committee, said China is ready to work with Vietnam to jointly implement the important common understandings reached between the general secretaries of the two parties.
He, also director of the Office of the Central Commission for Financial and Economic Affairs, said China is willing to maintain close strategic communication with Vietnam and deepen economic exchanges and mutual learning from each other's experience to advance the building of a higher-level China-Vietnam community with a shared future.
Nguyen Thanh Nghi, also chairman of the CPV Central Committee's Commission for Policies and Strategies, said Vietnam is willing to work with China to strengthen the synergy of development strategies and cooperation in economy, trade and other fields, and push for greater progress in the relations between the two parties and the two countries.
