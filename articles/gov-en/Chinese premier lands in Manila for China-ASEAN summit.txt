ARTICLE TEXT:
MANILA, Nov. 15 -- Chinese Premier Li Qiang arrived in Manila on Sunday for the 29th China-ASEAN Summit at the invitation of President Ferdinand Romualdez Marcos Jr. of the Philippines, the rotating chair of ASEAN.
Li arrived in the Philippine capital after concluding an official visit to Thailand and will attend the China-ASEAN Summit on Monday.
