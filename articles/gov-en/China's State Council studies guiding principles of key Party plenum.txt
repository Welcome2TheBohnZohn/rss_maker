TITLE:
China's State Council studies guiding principles of key Party plenum

ARTICLE TEXT:
BEIJING, Oct. 30 -- The leading Party members group of China's State Council met on Friday to study and discuss plans to implement the guiding principles of the fifth plenary session of the 20th Central Committee of the Communist Party of China (CPC).
Chinese Premier Li Qiang, who is also secretary of the leading Party members group of the State Council, presided over the meeting.
The meeting noted that the resolution on persevering with full and rigorous Party self-governance, adopted at the plenary session, analyzes challenges and tasks in supervising, governing, developing and strengthening the Party at present and in the period to come, and sets out the overall requirements and key priorities for persevering with full and rigorous Party self-governance.
The meeting emphasized that the leading Party members group of the State Council and all departments under the State Council should make studying and implementing the guiding principles of the plenary session a major political priority at present and in the period ahead.
The meeting stressed that the requirements of full and rigorous Party self-governance should be applied throughout every aspect and at every stage of government work.
The meeting pledged that the State Council and its departments will remain steadfast in their role as effective implementers in carrying out the decisions and plans of the CPC Central Committee.
They will integrate the implementation of the requirements of full and rigorous Party self-governance into the planning, deployment and execution of economic and social development initiatives. They will also maintain an unwavering commitment to improving Party and government conduct, enforcing Party discipline and combating corruption, and ensure that administrative conduct is strictly regulated, the meeting noted.
The meeting was attended by Ding Xuexiang, He Lifeng, Zhang Guoqing, Liu Guozhong, Wang Xiaohong, Wu Zhenglong and Shen Yiqin.
