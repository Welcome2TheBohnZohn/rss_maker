ARTICLE TEXT:
SHENZHEN, Nov. 17 -- Chinese Minister of Public Security Wang Xiaohong on Tuesday met with Luong Tam Quang, a member of the Political Bureau of the Communist Party of Vietnam Central Committee and Vietnamese Minister of Public Security, in Shenzhen, south China's Guangdong Province.
Wang, also a member of the Secretariat of the Communist Party of China Central Committee, expressed the hope that both sides will fully implement the important consensus reached by the top leaders of the two parties and the two countries.
He called for sustained efforts to deepen practical cooperation in law enforcement and security, with a focus on safeguarding political security and combating telecom and online fraud.
Quang voiced the willingness to strengthen solidarity and coordination with the Chinese side to achieve further progress in law enforcement and security cooperation.
