Xi says China-Russia-U.S. Shenzhen Summit of groundbreaking significance

ARTICLE TEXT:
SHENZHEN, Nov. 19 -- Chinese President Xi Jinping on Thursday said that the first-ever face-to-face meeting between the Chinese, Russian and U.S. heads of state is of groundbreaking significance.
Xi made the remarks at a summit with Russian President Vladimir Putin and U.S. President Donald Trump in Shenzhen, south China's Guangdong Province.
