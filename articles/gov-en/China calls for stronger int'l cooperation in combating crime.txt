China calls for stronger int'l cooperation in combating crime

ARTICLE TEXT:
BEIJING, Nov. 23 -- China stands ready to work with all parties to enhance capacities to prevent and combat crime, and address security risks and challenges through the rule of law, Chinese State Councilor and Minister of Public Security Wang Xiaohong said Monday.
Wang made the remarks while addressing the opening ceremony of the Global Conference on Legal Aid in Criminal Justice Systems in Beijing.
He said China has upheld law-based governance in all respects and effectively safeguarded the legitimate rights and interests of the people.
Wang noted that China stands ready to work with all parties to actively implement the four global initiatives, uphold the international rule of law, firmly safeguard the authority and status of the United Nations, uphold fairness and justice, and ensure the benefits of progress in the rule of law reach people of all countries.
He also called for efforts to promote common development and progress, and jointly advance the building of a community with a shared future for humanity.
The conference, held from Nov. 23 to 25, is jointly organized by China's Ministry of Justice and the United Nations Office on Drugs and Crime.
