TITLE:
Xi calls for joint efforts to develop China-Chile ties

ARTICLE TEXT:
SHENZHEN, Nov. 17 -- Chinese President Xi Jinping said Tuesday that he is ready to work with Chilean President Jose Antonio Kast to strengthen strategic guidance, keep to the right direction, and jointly draw a blueprint for the growth of bilateral relations, so as to support the development and prosperity of the two countries.
Xi made the remarks during his meeting with Kast in Shenzhen. The Chilean president is in the southern Chinese city to attend the 33rd Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation (APEC) Economic Leaders' Meeting, scheduled for Nov. 18-19.
Noting that Chile was the first South American country to establish diplomatic relations with the People's Republic of China, Xi said the bilateral ties had withstood the test of a changing international landscape and maintained sound and steady development for a long time.
The most valuable experience is that the two sides respect each other, treat each other as equals, and pursue win-win outcomes, he said.
As this year marks the 10th anniversary of the establishment of the China-Chile comprehensive strategic partnership and the 20th anniversary of the entry into force of the China-Chile Free Trade Agreement, the bilateral relations have reached an important juncture for building on past achievements and forging ahead into the future, Xi said.
Xi noted that China and Chile should continue to support each other on issues concerning their respective core interests and major concerns, strengthen exchanges across multiple sectors and at various levels, and share experience in state governance.
Given the complementarity of their economies and the solid foundation of their cooperation, the two countries should better align their development strategies through high-quality Belt and Road cooperation, promote traditional cooperation in trade, energy, minerals, infrastructure and astronomic research, and expand into emerging fields such as artificial intelligence (AI), polar scientific expeditions, and deep-sea exploration, Xi said.
He stressed that China stands ready to enhance communication and coordination with Chile within multilateral frameworks such as APEC, jointly uphold true multilateralism, and act as defenders of the common interests of developing countries.
China is willing to work with Latin American and Caribbean (LAC) countries to inject greater certainty into peace and development in the region, and push forward China-LAC cooperation, Xi said.
Kast stated that since the establishment of diplomatic relations between Chile and China, their bilateral ties have developed steadily, regardless of changes in the international landscape.
For many years, China has remained Chile's largest trading partner and the leading destination for its agricultural and mineral exports. Cooperation with China has provided important opportunities for Chile's economic and social development, and strengthening ties with China represents a consensus across all sectors of Chilean society, the president added.
Chile will unswervingly adhere to the one-China policy, deepen its good relations with China, expand the scale of trade and investment cooperation, broaden collaboration in agriculture, mining, new energy vehicles, infrastructure development and AI, and promote exchanges in education and culture, Kast noted.
Chile stands ready to strengthen coordination with China within multilateral frameworks such as APEC, uphold multilateralism, remain committed to free trade, and promote peace and development worldwide, he said.
