Xi urges breaking new ground in high-quality development of Greater Bay Area
Updated: November 16, 2026 18:37Xinhua
ZHONGSHAN, Guangdong, Nov. 16 -- Chinese President Xi Jinping on Monday urged efforts to thoroughly study and implement the guiding principles of the fifth plenary session of the 20th Communist Party of China (CPC) Central Committee, and to strive to break new ground in the high-quality development of the Guangdong-Hong Kong-Macao Greater Bay Area.
Xi, also general secretary of the CPC Central Committee and chairman of the Central Military Commission, made the remarks during an inspection tour in Zhongshan, south China's Guangdong Province.
Stressing fully and faithfully implementing the decisions and arrangements made at the plenary session and leveraging new progress in persevering with full and rigorous Party self-governance, Xi urged a better job in advancing integrated development along the Pearl River Estuary.
Visiting an artificial island of the Shenzhen-Zhongshan Link, a sea-crossing project connecting the cities of Shenzhen, Zhongshan and Guangzhou, Xi demanded efforts to further enhance the management and operation of this mega transport project to better serve the development of the Greater Bay Area.
At an industrial park in Zhongshan, Xi learned about local efforts to absorb industrial transfers, transform and upgrade traditional industries, and develop emerging industries.
Building the Greater Bay Area is a major strategic plan made by the CPC Central Committee, Xi said, calling on cities along the Pearl River Estuary including Guangzhou, Shenzhen, Zhuhai, Dongguan and Zhongshan to play a pioneering role.
Xi expressed the hope that Zhongshan would seize the opportunities, take reform and innovation as the fundamental driving force, improve regional cooperation mechanisms, foster a sound business environment, and vigorously promote the deep integration of technological innovation and industrial innovation.
Speaking to representatives of small and medium-sized enterprises and sci-tech personnel, Xi encouraged them to make greater contributions to the development of Guangdong and the Greater Bay Area and China's greater self-reliance and strength in science and technology.
Xi also visited the Museum of Dr. Sun Yat-sen and laid a floral basket before his bronze statue. This year marks the 160th anniversary of Sun's birth.
On the new journey, it is necessary to emulate, inherit and promote his invaluable spirit, and inspire all Chinese people at home and abroad to unite and strive to build a great country and advance national rejuvenation through Chinese modernization, Xi said.
During the inspection tour, Xi also stressed the need to continuously steer the economy toward innovation-driven and high-quality development, ensure that major targets and tasks for the year are fulfilled, and get the 15th Five-Year Plan (2026-2030) off to a good start.
