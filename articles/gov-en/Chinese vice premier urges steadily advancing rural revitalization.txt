TITLE:
Chinese vice premier urges steadily advancing rural revitalization

ARTICLE TEXT:
LANZHOU, Nov. 19 -- Chinese Vice Premier Liu Guozhong has called for consolidating poverty alleviation achievements, preventing large-scale relapse into poverty, and steadily advancing rural revitalization across the board.
Liu, who is also a member of the Political Bureau of the Communist Party of China Central Committee, made the remarks during a research tour from Monday to Thursday in northwest China's Gansu Province. He chaired a symposium on regular assistance work in five provinces and autonomous regions in northwest China.
In Longnan's Tanchang County, Wenxian County and Wudu District, Liu inspected the monitoring system to prevent relapse into poverty, industrial and employment assistance, east-west collaboration, and targeted assistance by central government agencies.
This year marks the first year of implementing regular assistance. Efforts must be made to carry out all related policies and measures in a meticulous and thorough manner, and continue to improve the lives of rural residents, he said.
It is necessary to promote the upgrading of assistance industries in accordance with local conditions, strengthen skills training and labor cooperation, and spare no effort to expand employment channels, Liu noted.
Also, work should be done to develop the county-level economy and help accelerate the development of old revolutionary base areas, ethnic minority areas and border areas, he said.
The vice premier, in addition, stressed applying the experience gained from the Green Rural Revival Program in Zhejiang Province in east China to build a beautiful and harmonious countryside that is desirable to live and work in.
