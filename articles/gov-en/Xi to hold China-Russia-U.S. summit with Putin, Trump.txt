Xi to hold China-Russia-U.S. summit with Putin, Trump
Updated: November 16, 2026 10:07Xinhua
BEIJING, Nov. 16 -- Chinese President Xi Jinping will hold a China-Russia-U.S. summit with Russian President Vladimir Putin and U.S. President Donald Trump in China's Shenzhen on Nov. 19, a Chinese foreign ministry spokesperson announced on Monday.
