China says no gray zone on Taiwan question
        Source: XinhuaUpdated: 2026-10-22
		
    
BEIJING, Oct. 22 (Xinhua) -- There is no gray area on the Taiwan question, and the Philippine side should abide by the one-China principle with concrete actions, Chinese foreign ministry spokesperson Mao Ning said Thursday.
Mao made the remarks at a regular news briefing when asked to comment on attempts by the Philippines to explore deeper cooperation with Taiwan region to address so-called "gray zone" activities despite Manila's one-China policy.
"Any external exchanges conducted by China's Taiwan region must be handled in strict accordance with the one-China principle," Mao stressed.
