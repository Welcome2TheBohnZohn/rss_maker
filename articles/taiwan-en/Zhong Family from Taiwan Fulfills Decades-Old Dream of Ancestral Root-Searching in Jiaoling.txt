Zhong Family from Taiwan Fulfills Decades-Old Dream of Ancestral Root-Searching in Jiaoling
Aug 14(Taiwan.cn)—In August 2026, Zhong Ronghe and his son Zhong Binghan, a father and son from Kaohsiung, Taiwan, crossed the Strait to visit their ancestral home in Jiaoling County, Meizhou, Guangdong, and worship their ancestors. They fulfilled their family’s decades-long aspiration to trace their origins and pay homage to their forebears.
In April this year, Zhong Binghan, who had just returned from studies in the United Kingdom, made his first solo trip to Jiaoling, following his father’s request to trace their ancestral roots. With assistance from the local Taiwan, Hong Kong and Macao Affairs Office and clan relatives, cross-checking of genealogical records confirmed Guangfu Town as their ancestral hometown. Zhong Binghan then offered incense and paid homage at Yixuan Ancestral Hall and Yangting Ancestral Hall. He shared the good news via video call with his father in Taiwan, fulfilling Zhong Ronghe’s long-cherished wish to trace his roots.
