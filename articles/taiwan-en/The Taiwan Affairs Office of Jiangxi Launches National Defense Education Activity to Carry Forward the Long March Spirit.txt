The Taiwan Affairs Office of Jiangxi Launches National Defense Education Activity to Carry Forward the Long March Spirit
        Source: Taiwan.cnUpdated: 2026-10-13
		
    
　　Oct 13(Taiwan.cn)—Recently, the Taiwan Affairs Office of the CPC Jiangxi Provincial Committee organized all Party members and cadres of the office to visit the Jiangxi Provincial Museum for a national defense education activity. The group toured the exhibitions The Great Departure, the Eternal Long March: Exhibition on the History of the Central Red Army’s Departure for the Long March and Red Cradle: Exhibition of Jiangxi Revolutionary History. The activity aimed to guide cadres and staff in studying revolutionary traditions and enhancing their awareness of national defense.
　　During the visit, the participants followed in the footsteps of history. They deeply reflected on the firm convictions and fearless sacrifice of revolutionary predecessors, as well as their arduous journey marked by perseverance and courage in overcoming hardships. The activity helped them gain a deep understanding of the paramount importance of national defense and national security, offering profound ideological refinement and spiritual edification.
　　After the visit and study, Party members and cadres stated that the tour was both an in-depth education in revolutionary history and a vivid lesson in national defense. As cadres engaged in Taiwan-related work in the new era, they will inherit and carry forward the great Long March Spirit, uphold the glorious traditions and fine conduct of Communists, firmly embrace the holistic approach to national security, and continuously strengthen their awareness of national defense and potential risks.
　　They will translate their insights into concrete actions to tackle challenges and deliver results. In line with their duties, they will deepen cross-strait exchanges and cooperation across various fields in Jiangxi. With high morale and pragmatic measures, they will promote high-quality development of Taiwan-related work in the province, actively foster spiritual affinity between compatriots on both sides of the Taiwan Strait, contribute to writing the Jiangxi chapter of Chinese modernization, and advance the great cause of national reunification.(Jointly reported by Taiwan.cn and the Taiwan Affairs Office of Jiangxi)
