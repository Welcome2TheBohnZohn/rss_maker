　　Sep 24(Taiwan.cn)—On the evening of September 23, the "e Blooms Together" Straits Concert, produced by China Media Group, was held in Beijing. Performing guests and youth representatives from both sides of the Taiwan Strait gathered at the venue. With singing as the mainstay and incorporating diverse art forms such as dance, traditional opera, instrumental music, and situational performance, they presented a warm and heartfelt musical gala through co-creation, joint singing, joint performance, and mutual integration, demonstrating the shared aspiration of compatriots on both sides of the Strait to walk side by side and embrace the future together.
