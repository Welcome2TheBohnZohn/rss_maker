ARTICLE TEXT:
GUANGZHOU, Oct. 28 -- The opening ceremony for the China-Indonesia "Sharp Knife 2026" joint army counter-terrorism training was held in Guangzhou City, south China's Guangdong Province, on October 27, 2026.
Focusing on technical and tactical aspects of joint army counter-terrorism operations, the training brings together participating troops from both sides in mixed formations to undergo three modules: specialized training, tactical coordination, and comprehensive exercises. During the event, the two sides will also organize thematic seminars, weapon and equipment displays, sports competitions, and cultural exchanges.
