TITLE:
China urges Japan to correct its remarks and actions on Taiwan question

PUBLISHED:
2026-11-20

SOURCE:
China Military English

CATEGORY:
Military

URL:

ARTICLE TEXT:
China's Foreign Ministry on Friday urged Japan to correct its erroneous remarks and actions on the Taiwan question as soon as possible, stressing that Tokyo must honor its political commitments in both words and deeds.
Foreign Ministry spokesperson Guo Jiakun made the remarks at a regular press briefing in response to a question from a reporter about Japanese Prime Minister Sanae Takaichi's comments about Taiwan following the APEC Economic Leaders' Meeting in Shenzhen on Thursday.
Takaichi said Japan's basic position on the Taiwan question remained consistent with the 1972 Japan-China Joint Communique, and that the positioning of the four political documents between the two countries as the foundation of bilateral relations had not changed.
In response, Guo said Japan's adherence to the four political documents between China and Japan was a political responsibility and legal obligation that it must fulfill. This commitment should be reflected not only in its statements but also in its actions, he added.
"For Japan, the pressing task now is to correct its erroneous remarks and actions on the Taiwan question as soon as possible. What is important is to adopt a correct attitude toward historical issues, and what is fundamental is to develop a truly objective and rational understanding of China," Guo said.
