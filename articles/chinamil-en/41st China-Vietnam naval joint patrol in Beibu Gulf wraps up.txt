ARTICLE TEXT:
By Zhang Bin, Tong Jiajun, and Ruan Jian
BEIJING, Nov. 23 -- The Chinese and Vietnamese navies successfully completed their 41st joint patrol in the Beibu Gulf on November 21, 2026. Spanning two days with 32 hours of navigation over a total distance of 351 nautical miles, the joint patrol involved the Chinese PLA Navy's vessels Wuzhou (Hull 626) and Enshi (Hull 627), alongside two naval vessels from the Vietnam People's Navy.
As a friendly exchange in accordance with relevant agreements and annual routine plans between the two militaries, this naval joint patrol holds great significance for enhancing bilateral friendship and deepening mutual trust. It has also effectively strengthened both sides' capabilities to jointly respond to maritime emergencies and safeguard regional maritime security and stability.
