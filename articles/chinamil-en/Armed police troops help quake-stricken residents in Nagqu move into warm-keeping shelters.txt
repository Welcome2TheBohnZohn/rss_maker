TITLE:
Armed police troops help quake-stricken residents in Nagqu move into warm-keeping shelters

PUBLISHED:

SOURCE:
China Military English

CATEGORY:
Military

ARTICLE TEXT:
LHASA, Oct. 30 -- The temperature in the earthquake-hit area of Nagqu City, south west China's Xizang Autonomous Region, will drop soon. Troops assigned to the Chinese People's Armed Police Force (PAP) Nagqu detachment are working against time to help the affected residents move into warm-keeping prefabricated houses, ensuring they can safely get through the winter.
