ARTICLE TEXT:
BEIJING, Nov. 25 -- The China-Vietnam "Cooperation-2026" joint counter-terrorism training successfully concluded in Hanoi, Vietnam, in recent days. Against the backdrop of jointly addressing terrorist threats and themed on jointly combating terrorist crimes, the joint training incorporated elements such as individual combat skills training, tactical coordination training, and comprehensive counter-terrorism exercises.
Both sides conducted joint live-force drills including explosive ordnance disposal (EOD), battlefield first-aid operations, and counter-terrorism strikes in mountainous terrain.
