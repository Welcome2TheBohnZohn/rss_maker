TITLE:
China Coast Guard conducts patrol in the territorial waters of Diaoyu Dao

PUBLISHED:
2026-10-10

SOURCE:
China Military English

CATEGORY:
Military

URL:
http://eng.chinamil.com.cn/2025xb/H_251454/L_251456/16491993.html

ARTICLE TEXT:
The China Coast Guard (CCG) task group led by CCGS Xiamen patrolled the territorial waters of China's Diaoyu Dao and its affiliated islands on Saturday, the CCG said in a statement.
The patrol was a rights-protection operation carried out by the CCG in accordance with the law.
