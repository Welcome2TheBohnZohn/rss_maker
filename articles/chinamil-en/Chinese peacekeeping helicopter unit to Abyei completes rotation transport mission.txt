The 6th Chinese Peacekeeping Military Utility Helicopter Unit to UNISFA carries out a rotation transport mission for the Vietnamese Military Construction Engineering Company of UNISFA.
ABYEI, Sept. 28 -- The 6th Chinese Peacekeeping Military Utility Helicopter Unit to the United Nations Interim Security Force for Abyei (UNISFA) successfully completed a rotation transport mission for the Vietnamese Military Construction Engineering Company of UNISFA on Sept. 27, 2026, after three days of consecutive operations.
During the mission, the unit conducted 20 helicopter sorties, logging a total of 25 flight hours and covering more than 4,200 kilometers. It safely transported 220 rotating peacekeeping personnel and 4.3 tons of various supplies and equipment, successfully completing the transportation support task assigned by the United Nations.
Vietnamese peacekeepers who completed their peacekeeping mission board a helicopter for their return home.
