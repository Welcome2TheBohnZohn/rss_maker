ARTICLE TEXT:
KORLA, Oct. 21-- The China-Pakistan "Karakoram-2026" joint army training exercise has entered the multi-subject live-force training exercise phase.
Throughout the exercise, both sides' participating troops were divided into multiple mixed teams for coordinated actions, shared weapons and equipment, and achieved unified command. During the assault operations, the mixed teams conducted tactical drills including providing security, bounding, and the handling of emerging threats.
In addition, military medics from both sides conducted joint training on high-altitude tactical combat casualty care, sharing practical first-aid experience.
