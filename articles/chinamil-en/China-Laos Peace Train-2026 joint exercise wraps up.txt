By Zhou Na, Liu Xiaowei, Chi Xuedong, Zhang Tianyu, Yang Chao, Yin Chuandong, Tan Yuzhou and Tan Youming
VIENTIANE, Sept. 15 -- The China-Laos "Peace Train-2026" joint humanitarian medical rescue exercise concluded in Vientiane, Laos, on September 14, 2026, after completing all scheduled training activities.
Leaders from both sides presented representatives of the participating troops with the "Third-Class Medal of Valor" and the "Friendship Medal" conferred by the Lao Ministry of National Defense, as well as "Certificates of Participation" issued by the medical service commanders of the two militaries. Chinese representatives also donated medical supplies, including pharmaceuticals, to the Lao People's Army.
After the exercise, the "Peace Train" medical team will provide specialized diagnosis and treatment, surgery demonstrations, physical examinations, academic lectures, and other medical services in Vientiane, Pakse in Champasak Province, and other locations.
