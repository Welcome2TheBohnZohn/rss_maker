ARTICLE TEXT:
It was the arduous and perilous expedition that forged the great Long March spirit. Back then, some "Little Red Army Soldiers" also embarked on the hard journey. Following the Red Army ranks, they crossed snow-capped mountains and trekked across boundless grasslands, overcoming severe hardships and countless life-or-death trials. Going through the flames of war, they finally grew into resolute and faithful Red Army fighters with indomitable courage and will of steel.
This year marks the 90th anniversary of the victory of the Long March. Let us follow the footsteps of those young Red Army soldiers and look back on their legendary stories.
