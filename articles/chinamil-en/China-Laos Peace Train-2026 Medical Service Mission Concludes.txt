By Zhou Na, Liu Xiaowei, Yang Chao, Tan Yuzhou and Wang Yong
BEIJING, Sept. 23 -- On September 21, the Chinese medical team participating in the China-Laos "Peace Train-2026" medical service mission successfully completed its seven-day mission at four locations in Laos, namely Vientiane, Pakse, Savannakhet and Attapeu, providing medical services during a total of 6,275 patient visits.
The "Peace Train" medical team selected more than 70 key medical experts and formed four specialized medical detachments covering 31 specialties, including cardiovascular medicine, orthopedics, emergency medicine, respiratory medicine, ophthalmology and traditional Chinese medicine. The team provided free medical consultations, health checkups, multidisciplinary consultations, medical science education and other services benefiting the local residents.
On the evening of September 22, the Chinese medical team boarded a medical train and departed from Vientiane Station via the China-Laos Railway on its journey home, marking the successful conclusion of the 17-day China-Laos "Peace Train-2026" joint humanitarian medical rescue exercise and medical service mission.
