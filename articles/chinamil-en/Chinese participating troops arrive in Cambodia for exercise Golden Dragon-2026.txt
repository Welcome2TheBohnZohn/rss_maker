ARTICLE TEXT:
BEIJING, Nov. 25 -- China-Cambodia "Golden Dragon 2026" will be held from late November to mid-December in Cambodia.
The Chinese participating troops and equipment have arrived in Cambodia in the last few days.
The exercise focuses on joint counter-terrorism, anti-piracy, and humanitarian assistance and disaster relief (HADR) operations, and consists of adaptive training, command post exercises, and live-troop comprehensive drills.
