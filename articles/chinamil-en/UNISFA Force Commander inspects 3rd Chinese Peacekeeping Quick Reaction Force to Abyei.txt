ABYEI, Oct. 8 -- Lieutenant General Ganesh Kumar Shrestha , Acting Head of Mission and Force Commander of the United Nations Interim Security Force for Abyei (UNISFA), recently inspected and reviewed the 3rd Chinese Peacekeeping Quick Reaction Force to UNISFA. He spoke highly of the Chinese peacekeepers' conduct and discipline, combat readiness, capability to fulfill mandated tasks, and emergency response capabilities, expressing gratitude for the force's contributions since its deployment to the mission area.
