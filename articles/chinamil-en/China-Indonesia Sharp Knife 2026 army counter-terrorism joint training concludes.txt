GUANGZHOU, Nov. 3 -- The China-Indonesia "Sharp Knife 2026" army counter-terrorism joint training concluded in Guangzhou on November 2. At the closing ceremony, commanders from both sides delivered remarks and awarded certificates to outstanding participating personnel.
During the live-force phase, the participating troops conducted joint mountain counter-terrorism rescue operation, completing subjects including covert infiltration, coordinated rescue, and search and clearance.
Since the training started on October 27, participating troops from both sides were organized into mixed formations to undergo specialized training, tactical coordination, and comprehensive exercises. The two sides also conducted thematic seminars, weapon and equipment displays, sports competitions, and cultural exchanges.
As the third edition of this series of joint training, the Sharp Knife 2026 is of great significance for enhancing traditional friendship between the two countries, promoting bilateral defense cooperation, and safeguarding regional peace and stability.
