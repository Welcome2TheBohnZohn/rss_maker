SHENZHEN, Nov. 19 (Xinhua) -- Chinese President Xi Jinping on Thursday called for further efforts to build an Asia-Pacific community and write a new chapter of the "Asia-Pacific miracle."
"We must strive to build an Asia-Pacific community characterized by solidarity and mutual assistance, openness and integration, enterprise and innovation, as well as inclusive development," Xi said as he chaired the 33rd Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation (APEC) Economic Leaders' Meeting, held in Shenzhen, south China's Guangdong Province, on Nov. 18-19.
Xi noted that for more than three decades, the Asia-Pacific region has undergone tremendous changes, creating the "Asia-Pacific miracle" of rapid economic growth.
In the face of new opportunities and challenges, the region must adapt to the new realities and open up new prospects for Asia-Pacific cooperation, he added.
Xi called for solidarity and mutual assistance in building an Asia-Pacific community, urging APEC economies to expand their shared interests, meet the difficulties and challenges together, and steadily foster a shared sense of community.
"Members of APEC are like passengers riding the same ship. To navigate the waves of the Pacific, we must unite as one and look out for each other," he said.
Xi highlighted the importance of openness and integration, calling for efforts to uphold open regionalism, steadily advance economic integration, and champion free and open trade and investment, while developing a cooperation pattern that is open and integrated rather than exclusive or fragmented.
Building an Asia-Pacific community requires enterprise and innovation, Xi said, urging stronger scientific and technological cooperation, the commercialization of innovation outcomes, and an open, fair, just and nondiscriminatory environment for cooperation.
He also underscored the importance of inclusive development, stressing the need to ensure that development benefits all, deliver for the people, and foster favorable conditions for turning the vision of an Asia-Pacific community into reality.
FOUR PILLARS
Founded in 1989, APEC now brings together 21 member economies, including China, Russia and the United States. China joined in 1991. The forum has helped expand trade and investment and strengthen economic ties across the region.
"China has been an advocate, promoter and forerunner in Asia-Pacific economic cooperation," Xi said at the Shenzhen meeting, adding that the country is ready to work with all parties to build the four key pillars of an Asia-Pacific community -- economy and trade, connectivity, innovation, and development.
The Chinese president called for stronger economy and trade as a pillar underpinning such a community. Efforts should be made to align various regional economic and trade agreements, boost trade and investment facilitation and digitalization, and reduce barriers to trade and investment, he said.
Xi underscored the importance of upgrading connectivity in building the Asia-Pacific community. He called for measures to improve infrastructure network, harmonize policies and regulations, advance people-to-people exchanges, and promote mutual understanding and closer bonds among peoples.
Innovation should be fostered as another pillar of the Asia-Pacific community, Xi said. Greater efforts need to be made to develop digital infrastructure and encourage the broad application of artificial intelligence, he added.
Xi also stressed the importance of development in building the Asia-Pacific community. He called on regional economies to advance cooperation on energy and food security, anti-corruption governance across borders, and green and low-carbon transition. He also expressed support for improving the people's well-being and called for increased investment in human capital.
TURNING CONSENSUS INTO ACTION
China is rooted in the Asia-Pacific and will continue to support Asia-Pacific cooperation, Xi said.
He announced that China would contribute 20 million U.S. dollars to support APEC practical cooperation.
China will also establish a Center for Asia-Pacific Community Studies in Shenzhen to conduct policy research and exchanges, and set up cooperation institutions in such areas as digital technology, cross-border data flow, clean energy and the development of small and medium-sized enterprises, according to Xi.
Leaders and representatives of APEC member economies shared their views at the meeting on building an Asia-Pacific community to prosper together.
They spoke highly of the achievements of China's APEC host year and expressed their readiness to step up cooperation to advance APEC's development.
In his closing remarks, Xi called for strengthening confidence to translate the consensus on building an Asia-Pacific community into concrete action, and to turn the vision into reality.
He stressed the need to focus on development as the broadest common ground for cooperation and deliver more tangible benefits through Asia-Pacific cooperation.
The meeting adopted by consensus the 2026 APEC Leaders' Declaration, which sets out the Shenzhen Outlook on building an Asia-Pacific community, and three outcome documents on trade, connectivity and innovation.
Vietnam took over as APEC host for 2027, with President To Lam inviting leaders and representatives of member economies to attend next year's meeting in Vietnam.
