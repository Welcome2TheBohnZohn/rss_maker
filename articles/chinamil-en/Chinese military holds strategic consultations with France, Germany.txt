Chinese military holds strategic consultations with France, Germany

ARTICLE TEXT:
BEIJING, Oct. 28 -- A strategic consultation delegation of the Chinese People's Liberation Army (PLA) visited France and Germany from October 21 to 28, 2026.
During the visits, the PLA delegation had in-depth exchanges of views with the two countries on bilateral relations, international and regional security situation, and other issues of common concern.
All parties agreed to continue strengthening strategic communication, enhancing mutual understanding and trust, and expanding exchanges and cooperation in the defense field.
