TITLE:
China Coast Guard conducts drills in waters near China's Huangyan Dao

PUBLISHED:
2026-09-27

SOURCE:
China Military English

CATEGORY:
Military

URL:

ARTICLE TEXT:
BEIJING, Sept. 27 -- Jiang Lue, spokesperson for the China Coast Guard (CCG), stated that on September 27, 2026, the China Coast Guard (CCG) conducted drills on maritime rights-protection, law enforcement, and management and control in the waters near China's Huangyan Dao, focusing on operations such as boarding and inspection, interdiction of intrusion, and forced towing. The CCG will step up efforts in rights-protection, law enforcement, and management and control in the waters near China's Huangyan Dao, and resolutely safeguard China's territorial sovereignty and maritime rights and interests.
