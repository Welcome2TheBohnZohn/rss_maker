ARTICLE TEXT:
BEIJING, Nov. 17 -- Chinese Defense Minister Admiral Dong Jun visited Italy upon the invitation of Italian Defense Minister Guido Crosetto on November 16, 2026.
The two sides held an in-depth exchange of views on bilateral relations and military-to-military ties, the international and regional situation, and issues of common interest.
They agreed to further promote military-to-military relations and jointly safeguard regional and global peace and stability.
Prior to the talks, Minister Crosetto hosted a welcome ceremony for Minister Dong.
