TITLE:
Japan should be urged to abide by Three Non-Nuclear Principles: Chinese spokesperson

PUBLISHED:
2026-09-30

SOURCE:
China Military English

CATEGORY:
Military

URL:

ARTICLE TEXT:
BEIJING, Sept. 30 (Xinhua) -- The international community should urge Japan to abide by the Three Non-Nuclear Principles, faithfully fulfill its obligations under the Treaty on the Non-Proliferation of Nuclear Weapons, and completely abandon its attempts at "remilitarization" and nuclear possession, a Chinese foreign ministry spokesperson said on Wednesday.
Spokesperson Guo Jiakun made the remarks at a daily press briefing after the incumbent head of Japan's defense department said that discussions on Japan's possession of nuclear weapons and nuclear submarines should be made without taboo.
