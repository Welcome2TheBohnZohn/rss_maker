ARTICLE TEXT:
By Qu Fatong, Liao Yun, Zhou Jie, and Zhang Lei
BEIJING, Nov. 11 -- The 24th Chinese Peacekeeping Contingent to the United Nations Interim Force in Lebanon (UNIFIL), jointly with the Chinese Embassy in Lebanon, recently conducted humanitarian assistance activities in Tyre, southern Lebanon.
Chinese peacekeepers visited Bazouriye Official Secondary School, the Mosan Center for Special Needs, and the headquarters of the South Litani Sector of the Lebanese Armed Forces, to donate supplies and provide free medical services.
Valued at over $34,000, the donated supplies address the needs of vulnerable groups, support education and public livelihoods, and meet the medical service and support needs of the Lebanese Armed Forces units.
