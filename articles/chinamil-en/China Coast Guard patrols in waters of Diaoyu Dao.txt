TITLE:
China Coast Guard patrols in waters of Diaoyu Dao

ARTICLE TEXT:
BEIJING, Sept. 10 (Xinhua) -- A vessel formation of the China Coast Guard (CCG) on Thursday conducted a patrol in China's territorial waters of Diaoyu Dao and its affiliated islands, according to a statement by the CCG.
The patrol was carried out in accordance with the law to protect China's legal rights in the area, the statement said.
