BEIJING, Nov. 20 -- During his visit to Britain, Chinese Defense Minister Admiral Dong Jun held talks with British Defense Secretary Wes Streeting on November 19, 2026.
The two sides had an in-depth exchange of views on bilateral relations between the two countries and the two militaries, the international and regional situation, as well as issues of common concern.
They agreed to deliver on the important consensus reached by the two heads of state and promote steady, sound and sustainable development of the mil-to-mil relationship.
Prior to the talks, Defense Secretary Streeting hosted a welcome ceremony for Defense Minister Dong.
