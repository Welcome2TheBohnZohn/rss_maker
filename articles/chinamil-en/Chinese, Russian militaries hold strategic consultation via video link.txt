ARTICLE TEXT:
BEIJING, Nov. 26 (Xinhua) -- The Chinese and Russian militaries held a strategic consultation via video conference on Thursday, pledging to strengthen communication and coordination to jointly safeguard international and regional security and stability.
The two sides exchanged views on implementing the consensus reached by the two countries' heads of state and on advancing the sustained development of military-to-military relations, according to a statement released by China's Ministry of National Defense.
They also held an in-depth exchange of views on the international and regional situation, according to the statement.
