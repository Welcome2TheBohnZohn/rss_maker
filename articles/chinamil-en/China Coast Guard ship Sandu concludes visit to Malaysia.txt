ARTICLE TEXT:
BEIJING, Nov. 16--The China Coast Guard (CCG) ship Sandu (Hull 5308) concluded its four-day friendly visit to Penang, Malaysia, on November 13, and set sail for home.
During the transit, vessels from China and Malaysia conducted maritime formation training and a communications exercise. In the formation training, the CCG ship Sandu and the Malaysian Maritime Enforcement Agency (MMEA) ship KM Tun Fatimah (Hull 8305) shifted between column and line-abreast formations.
During the visit, the Chinese and Malaysian sides held high-level meetings and conducted multiple training subjects, including a maritime search and rescue tabletop exercise, formation training, and a communications exercise. The two sides also hosted activities such as open-ship days, cultural and sports exchanges, and a deck receptionevent.
