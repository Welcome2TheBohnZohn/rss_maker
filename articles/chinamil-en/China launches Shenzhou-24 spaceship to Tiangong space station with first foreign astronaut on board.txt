JIUQUAN, Nov. 25 (Xinhua) -- China launched the Shenzhou-24 crewed spaceship on Wednesday, sending three astronauts, including a Pakistani payload specialist, to its orbiting Tiangong space station.
The spaceship, atop a Long March-2F carrier rocket, blasted off from the Jiuquan Satellite Launch Center in northwest China.
The astronauts aboard the spaceship are mission commander and spacecraft pilot Tang Hongbo, flight engineer Wu Yi, and Pakistani astronaut Muhammad Zeeshan Ali, the first-ever foreign astronaut to visit Tiangong.
The Shenzhou-24 mission marks the first joint flight consisting of Chinese and foreign astronauts in the history of China's manned space program.
