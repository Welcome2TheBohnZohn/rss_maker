BEIJING, Oct. 23 -- The nine-day China-Pakistan "Karakoram-2026" joint army training concluded at Korla in northwest China's Xinjiang Uygur Autonomous Region on October 22, 2026.
During the final live-force training exercise phase, the Chinese and Pakistani participating troops executed multiple combat subjects based on the principles of joint command, mixed formation, task-based grouping, and coordinated action.
