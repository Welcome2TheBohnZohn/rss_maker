ARTICLE TEXT:
BEIJING, Nov. 24 (Xinhua) -- The Chinese and Cambodian militaries will hold a joint exercise in Cambodia from late November to mid-December, according to a statement from China's Ministry of National Defense released on Tuesday.
The exercise, codenamed "Golden Dragon-2026," will focus on "joint counterterrorism and anti-piracy operations and humanitarian relief," said the statement.
This is the eighth edition of the "Golden Dragon" series involving these two militaries.
It is expected to contribute positively to building an all-weather China-Cambodia community with a shared future in the new era, the ministry noted.
The "Golden Dragon" series is a regular joint exercise between the Chinese and Cambodian militaries, aimed at strengthening political mutual trust and expanding military exchanges.
China first sent troops to participate in the exercise in 2016.
