BEIJING, Sept. 17 -- The 10th China-Australia defense coordination dialogue was held in Beijing on September 17, 2026.
The two sides had in-depth exchange of views on military-to-military relations, maritime and air security, as well as international and regional issues of common concern, enhancing mutual understanding and trust.
