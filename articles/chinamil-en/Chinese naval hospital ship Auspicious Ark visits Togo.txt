ARTICLE TEXT:
LOME, Nov. 2 -- The Chinese PLA Navy's hospital ship Auspicious Ark (Hull 868), which is on "Mission Harmony-2026," arrived at the Port of Lomé, Togo, on November 1, 2026, embarking on a seven-day goodwill visit.
During the visit, the ship will provide medical services aboard its deck while dispatching medical teams to visit and carry out exchanges with Togolese naval bases and military hospitals.
