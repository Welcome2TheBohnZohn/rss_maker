ARTICLE TEXT:
PENANG, Nov. 11 -- The China Coast Guard (CCG) ship Sandu arrived in Penang, Malaysia, on November 10, 2026, for a four-day goodwill visit. The two sides will engage in exchanges on multiple subjects, including maritime search and rescue, seminars, and formation training, to further deepen pragmatic maritime cooperation and jointly safeguard regional maritime security and stability.
