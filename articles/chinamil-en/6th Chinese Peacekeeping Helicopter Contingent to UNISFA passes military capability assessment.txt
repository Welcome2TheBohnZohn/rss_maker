ABYEI, Sept. 30 -- The 6th Chinese Peacekeeping Helicopter Contingent to the United Nations Interim Security Force for Abyei (UNISFA) successfully passed the UNISFA annual military capability assessment on September 28, 2026.
Aligned with the complex situation in the Abyei mission area and tailored to the core functions of the helicopter contingent, the assessment covered key areas including the implementation of combat readiness system and flight mission, equipment maintenance, capacity building, emergency response, logistical support, and compliance in duty performance.
Moving forward, the contingent will continue to contribute Chinese peacekeeping force to maintaining peace and stability in the Abyei region.
