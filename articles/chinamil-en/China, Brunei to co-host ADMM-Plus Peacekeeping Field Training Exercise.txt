TITLE:
China, Brunei to co-host ADMM-Plus Peacekeeping Field Training Exercise

ARTICLE TEXT:
BEIJING, Nov. 26 -- China and Brunei will co-host the ADMM-Plus Peacekeeping Field Training Exercise in Bandar Seri Begawan, Brunei, from November 27 to December 12, said Senior Colonel Chen Xi, spokesperson for China's Ministry of National Defense (MND), at a press conference on Thursday.
"The exercise, codenamed Aman Damai Shared Future 2026, will involve personnel from different ADMM-Plus countries," Chen said, adding that the drill would focus on such subjects as battlefield reconnaissance, security patrol, response to terrorist attacks, and medical rescue.
The spokesperson said that this event will help enhance the interoperability and technical and tactical competence of participating forces in peacekeeping operations, deepen substantive cooperation under the ADMM-Plus framework, and safeguard regional peace and stability.
