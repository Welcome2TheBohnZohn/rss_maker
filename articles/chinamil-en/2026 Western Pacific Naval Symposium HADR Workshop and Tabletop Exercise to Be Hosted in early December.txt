2026 Western Pacific Naval Symposium HADR Workshop and Tabletop Exercise to Be Hosted in early December

ARTICLE TEXT:
BEIJING, Nov.26 -- The PLA Navy will host the 2026 Western Pacific Naval Symposium (WPNS) Humanitarian Assistance and Disaster Relief (HADR) Seminar and Tabletop Exercise in Nanjing in early December, said Chen Xi, spokesperson for China's Ministry of National Defense (MND), at a regular press conference on Thursday.
"Representatives from member and observer states are invited to attend," Chen said, adding that under the theme "Multi-navy HADR in the Aftermath of an Earthquake", the exercise will focus on command chain integration, information sharing and operational coordination.
According to the spokesperson, seminars will be held on topics including rule compliance, command organization and operational patterns for HADR activities.
"The event will further deepen substantive cooperation on HADR in the Western Pacific region and enhance interoperability in crisis prevention and response," the spokesperson stressed.
