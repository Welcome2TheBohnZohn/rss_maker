YANTAI, Aug. 25 -- On August 24, 2026, the "Future International Midshipmen Leadership Camp" was kicked off in Yantai City, east China's Shandong Province.
Hosted by the Chinese PLA Navy and organized by the Chinese PLA Naval Aviation University, the event brings together military cadets from naval academies in Pakistan, Indonesia, Singapore, Vietnam, and other countries. They will train in mixed groups with Chinese naval cadets and take part in a week of interactive and immersive learning and training.
The event features over 20 activities, including leadership training, seminars and exchanges, study visits, and traditional Chinese cultural experiences.
