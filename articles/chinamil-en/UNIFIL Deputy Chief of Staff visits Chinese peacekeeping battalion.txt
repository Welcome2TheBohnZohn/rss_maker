ARTICLE TEXT:
By Qi Pengfei, Qu Fatong, Luo Sen and Cheng Yuda
BEIJING, Sept. 22 -- Colonel José Luis López-Rubio, the newly appointed Deputy Chief of Staff for Operations of the United Nations Interim Force in Lebanon (UNIFIL), recently led a working group on inspection visit to the 24th Chinese peacekeeping contingent to Lebanon.
The working group inspected the Chinese peacekeeping troops' operational readiness facilities and observed demonstrations of mine clearance, engineering equipment operation, and unexploded ordnance disposal, comprehensively assessing the Chinese peacekeepers' ability to perform mandated tasks.
Colonel Rubio spoke highly of the Chinese peacekeepers' performance and commended their outstanding contributions.
The Chinese peacekeeping contingent is currently making steady progress on various tasks, including construction work at Camp Green Hill, the detection and clearance of unexploded ordnance, and road repair along the Blue Line.
