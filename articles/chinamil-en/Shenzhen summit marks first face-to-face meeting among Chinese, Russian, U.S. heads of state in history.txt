Shenzhen summit marks first face-to-face meeting among Chinese, Russian, U.S. heads of state in history

BEIJING, Nov. 16 (Xinhua) -- The meeting of the Chinese, Russian and U.S. heads of state in south China's Shenzhen will be the first face-to-face meeting in history involving the top leaders of the three countries, Chinese Foreign Ministry spokesperson Mao Ning said on Monday.
Chinese President Xi Jinping will hold a China-Russia-U.S. summit with Russian President Vladimir Putin and U.S. President Donald Trump in Shenzhen on Nov. 19, according to the ministry.
Mao said the heads of state will have an in-depth exchange of views on maintaining world peace and promoting strategic stability in major country relations, which is of groundbreaking significance.
Noting that China, Russia and the United States are all founding members of the UN and permanent members of the UN Security Council, Mao said the three countries have fought together to achieve the victory of the World Anti-Fascist War.
Under current circumstances, the three countries shoulder the important responsibility of cooperating to address global challenges and promote world peace and stability, said Mao.
Strengthening communication, managing and controlling differences, and stabilizing relations among China, Russia and the United States serve the interests of the three countries, meet the expectations of the international community, and are conducive to stabilizing the world situation and promoting dialogue and cooperation, she said.
