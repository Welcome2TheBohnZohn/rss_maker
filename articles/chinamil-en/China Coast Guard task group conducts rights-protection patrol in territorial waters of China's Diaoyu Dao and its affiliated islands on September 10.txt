TITLE:
China Coast Guard task group conducts rights-protection patrol in territorial waters of China's Diaoyu Dao and its affiliated islands on September 10

PUBLISHED:
2026-09-10

SOURCE:
China Military English

CATEGORY:
Military

URL:

ARTICLE TEXT:
On September 10, the China Coast Guard (CCG) task group led by CCGS Dengbu conducted a rights-protection patrol in the territorial waters of China's Diaoyu Dao and its affiliated islands, according to a statement released by CCG.
The patrol was a rights-protection operation carried out by the CCG in accordance with the law.
