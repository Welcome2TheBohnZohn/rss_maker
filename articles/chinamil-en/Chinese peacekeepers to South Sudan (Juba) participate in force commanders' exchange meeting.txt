By Lu Jinfang, Wang Shuai, LyuJunqi, Yang Shiwei, Zhou Fei and Wang Liguo
JUBA, Nov. 16 -- The United Nations Mission in South Sudan (UNMISS) recently convened a force commanders' conference and on-site exchange demonstration. The 12th Chinese Peacekeeping Infantry Battalion to UNMISS participated in the event. Focusing on comprehensive defense, Chinese peacekeepers joined forces with the United Nations Police (UNPOL) and the Nepal Armed Police Force (APF) to conduct an emergency response exercise for an influx of displaced people as well as an emergency combat assault demonstration, showcasing its rapid and efficient emergency response capabilities to all participating parties.
Participants also visited the Chinese camp for an on-site observation, gaining a detailed understanding of Chinese peacekeepers' daily combat readiness management, duty system operations, and comprehensive defense system construction.
