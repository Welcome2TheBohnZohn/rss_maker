ARTICLE TEXT:
BEIJING, Nov. 13 -- The Chinese PLA Air Force Engineering University hosted a three-week United Nations peacekeeping drone training course in late October 2026, with instructors and trainees from the UN and eight countries including Algeria, Malaysia, and South Africa.
A comprehensive drill was held during the training course, centering on peacekeepers operating UAVs, unmanned robots and counter-UAV systems to handle tasks such as fire suppression, delivery of medicines and food, as well as terrain surveying and low-altitude surveillance.
The training course helped international participants sharpen their ability to employ unmanned and counter-unmanned equipment for peacekeeping missions and civilian protection.
