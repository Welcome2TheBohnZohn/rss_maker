CAIRO, Sept. 2 (Xinhua) -- Chinese President Xi Jinping on Wednesday urged China and Egypt to work together to oppose unilateralism and acts of bullying.
He made the remarks during his talks with Egyptian President Abdel Fattah El-Sisi, calling on the two sides to work together for a more just and equitable global governance system.
