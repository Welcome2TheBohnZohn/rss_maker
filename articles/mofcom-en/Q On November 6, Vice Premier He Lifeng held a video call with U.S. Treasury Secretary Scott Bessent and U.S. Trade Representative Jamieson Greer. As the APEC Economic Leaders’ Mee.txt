MOFCOM BJT
Q: On November 6, Vice Premier He Lifeng held a video call with U.S. Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent and U.S. Trade Representative Jamieson Greer. As the APEC Economic Leaders’ Meeting is due next week in Shenzhen, how does MOFCOM assess the differences and cooperation progress in the current China-U.S. economic and trade relations? (MOFCOM Regular Press Conference November 12, 2026)
A: Since May 2025, China and the U.S. have held eight rounds of economic and trade consultations, and China has fully implemented the consensus reached in previous consultations. I would like to emphasize that China-U.S. economic and trade relations are mutually beneficial and win-win in nature, and cooperation is the only correct path. China is ready to work with the U.S. under the economic and trade consultation mechanism to keep strengthening cooperation and properly manage differences, so as to promote the sustained, stable and sound development of China-U.S. economic and trade relations.
