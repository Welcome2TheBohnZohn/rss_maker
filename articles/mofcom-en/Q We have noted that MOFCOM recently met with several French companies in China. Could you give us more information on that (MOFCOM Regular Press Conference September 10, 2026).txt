MOFCOM BJT
Q: We have noted that MOFCOM recently met with several French companies in China. Could you give us more information on that? (MOFCOM Regular Press Conference September 10, 2026)
A: Public reports show that some French companies have received relevant subsidies from the EU and its member states, the effects of which may, to some extent, be passed on to their entities in China. In order to assess the impact, MOFCOM, in accordance with the law, asked them for relevant information and requested explanations.
