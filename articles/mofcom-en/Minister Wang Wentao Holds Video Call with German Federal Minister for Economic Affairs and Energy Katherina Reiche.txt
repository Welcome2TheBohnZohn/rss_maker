Minister Wang Wentao Holds Video Call with German Federal Minister for Economic Affairs and Energy Katherina Reiche
On September 28, Minister of Commerce Wang Wentao held a video call with Katherina Reiche, Federal Minister for Economic Affairs and Energy of Germany. The two sides exchanged views on China-Germany and China-EU economic and trade relations. (Released on September 28)
