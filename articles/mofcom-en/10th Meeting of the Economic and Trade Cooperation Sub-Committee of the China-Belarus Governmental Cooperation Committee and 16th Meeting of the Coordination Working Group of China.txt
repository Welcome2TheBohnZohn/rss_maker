10th Meeting of the Economic and Trade Cooperation Sub-Committee of the China-Belarus Governmental Cooperation Committee and 16th Meeting of the Coordination Working Group of China-Belarus Industrial Park Held in Beijing
On October 14, Ling Ji, Vice Minister of Commerce and Deputy International Trade Representative of the People’s Republic of China, and Belarusian Economic Minister Yuri Chebotar co-chaired in Beijing the 10th meeting of the Economic and Trade Cooperation Sub-Committee of the China-Belarus Governmental Cooperation Committee and the 16th meeting of the China-Belarus Industrial Park Coordination Working Group. (Released on October 14)
