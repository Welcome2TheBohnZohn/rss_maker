MOFCOM BJT
On November 3, Minister of Commerce Wang Wentao and Maninder Sidhu, Minister of International Trade of Canada, co-chaired in Beijing the 29th meeting of the China-Canada Joint Economic and Trade Commission (JETC), the Commission’s first ministerial-level meeting. The two sides had a candid and in-depth exchange of views on China-Canada economic and trade relations, cooperation in traditional and emerging fields, and each other’s economic and trade concerns. Li Chenggang, China International Trade Representative (full minister rank) and Vice Minister of Commerce, attended the meeting.
Wang Wentao said that under the strategic guidance of the leaders of the two countries, recent China-Canada economic and trade consultations have made positive progress, including the further extension of relevant remission measures under the preliminary joint arrangement. China is ready to continue to work with Canada to give full play to JETC’s role as the main channel for resolving economic and trade differences and promoting economic and trade cooperation, and to continue to shorten the list of problems and lengthen the list of cooperation. At the same time, the two sides should continue to implement the China-Canada Economic and Trade Cooperation Roadmap, consolidate cooperation in traditional fields such as oil and gas, agricultural and aquatic products, and consumer goods, and further expand cooperation in emerging fields such as new materials, advanced manufacturing, clean energy, and smart agriculture, so as to provide strong support for the development of China-Canada economic and trade relations.
Maninder Sidhu said that the current momentum of Canada-China relations is encouraging. The two sides have actively implemented the consensus reached by the leaders of the two countries, with fruitful outcomes in cooperation across various fields. Canada’s quality agricultural and aquatic products have resumed exports to China, and bilateral trade has grown rapidly. The Canada-China Joint Economic and Trade Commission is the most important economic and trade exchange mechanism between the two countries. Canada is ready, within the JETC framework of the commission, to continue to properly handle issues and advance cooperation with China, and to encourage enterprises from both countries to further expand trade and investment cooperation. In particular, Canada welcomes active participation by Chinese enterprises in cooperation in Canada’s modern agriculture, manufacturing, financial services, energy, and other fields.
Before the meeting, Wang Wentao and Maninder Sidhu jointly attended the China-Canada Investment and Trade Roundtable. After the meeting, the two sides signed the Memorandum of Understanding on the Establishment of the China-Canada CEO Roundtable. (Released on November 4)
