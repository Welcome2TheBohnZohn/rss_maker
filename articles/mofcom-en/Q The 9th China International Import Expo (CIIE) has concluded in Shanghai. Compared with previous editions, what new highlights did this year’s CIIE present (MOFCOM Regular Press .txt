MOFCOM BJT
A: The CIIE is a microcosm of China’s continued expansion of high-standard opening up. This year’s CIIE delivered the dividends of opening up and, as it marked its ninth edition, showed renewed vitality and numerous highlights. The exhibition area of the Enterprise & Business Exhibition exceeded 370,000 square meters. For the first time, a Dedicated Showcase for National Brands Worldwide was set up, featuring about 100 time-honored brands from guest countries of honor and theme countries, including 30 centenary-old time-honored brands. The “circle of friends” of the Country Exhibition expanded further, demonstrating the strong “magnetic pull” of open cooperation, with 69 countries and international organizations showcasing their overall images. With the release of its first pair of dual flagship reports, the Hongqiao International Economic Forum has become an important window for observing China’s opening up and understanding the global economy.
The CIIE has become a snapshot of “China opportunity” and sent a strong message about China’s firm determination to share development opportunities with the world. Preparations for the 10th CIIE have already begun. We welcome continued attention and coverage from our friends in the media.
