MOFCOM BJT
Q: According to reports, in accordance with the consensus reached at the eighth round of China-U.S. economic and trade consultations, China will adjust a number of non-tariff measures targeting the U.S. What are the considerations regarding the Unreliable Entity List?
A: In accordance with the Anti-Foreign Sanctions Law of the People’s Republic of China, the Provisions on the Unreliable Entity List and other relevant provisions, the Unreliable Entity List Working Mechanism issued an announcement on April 4, 2025, adding 11 U.S. entities to the list and taking corresponding measures. To implement the consensus reached at the eighth round of China-U.S. economic and trade consultations, China has decided to continue to suspend the relevant measures under the Announcement of April 4, 2025 (Unreliable Entity List Working Mechanism [2025] No. 7) for another two months, starting from November 10, 2026. In accordance with the Provisions on the Unreliable Entity List, domestic enterprises may apply to engage in transactions with the above-mentioned entities. The Working Mechanism will review such applications in accordance with the law and approve those that meet the relevant conditions. (Released on November 9)
