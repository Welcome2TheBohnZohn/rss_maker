MOFCOM BJT
Q: On September 16, Ursula von der Leyen, President of the European Commission, delivered the 2026 State of the Union Address, in which she mentioned China-EU trade imbalances and “China Shock 2.0,” stating that the EU would use all tools at its disposal to rebalance EU-China relations. What is China’s comment on this? (MOFCOM Regular Press Conference September 17, 2026)
A: China has noted the relevant remarks. I would like to emphasize that China’s position and attitude are have been consistent and clear. At the same time, we do not engage in “microphone diplomacy” or “a war of words.”
