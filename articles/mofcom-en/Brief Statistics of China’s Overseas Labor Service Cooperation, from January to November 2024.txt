Brief Statistics of China’s Overseas Labor Service Cooperation, from January to November 2024
From January to November 2024, various kinds of workers dispatched abroad amounted to 358,000, an increase of 50,000 year on year. The number of workers dispatched for contracted projects was 130,000, and that for labor cooperation was 228,000. By the end of November 2024, 582,000 Chinese workers were working overseas.
