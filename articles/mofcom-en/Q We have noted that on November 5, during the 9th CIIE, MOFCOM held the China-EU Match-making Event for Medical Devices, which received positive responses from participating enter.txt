Q: We have noted that on November 5, during the 9th CIIE, MOFCOM held the China-EU Match-making Event for Medical Devices, which received positive responses from participating enterprises. Could you brief us on the relevant developments? (MOFCOM Regular Press Conference November 12, 2026)

ARTICLE TEXT:
MOFCOM BJT
A: Holding the China-EU Match-making Event for Medical Devices during the CIIE is an important outcome and consensus of the second meeting of the China-EU Trade and Investment Consultations (TIC). MOFCOM, together with the Ministry of Finance, the National Health Commission and other departments, organized more than 160 domestic medical institutions and enterprises to participate in market-based procurement. The EU side organized more than 40 medical device enterprises to attend the event, and more than 100 cooperation intentions were reached, benefiting EU member states such as Germany, Ireland, the Netherlands and Sweden. Overall, the event effectively boosted China-EU trade in medical devices and is a vivid example of promoting upward balancing and forward development in China-EU trade. China is ready to continue to work with the EU under the China-EU TIC to tap cooperation potential and create new opportunities.
