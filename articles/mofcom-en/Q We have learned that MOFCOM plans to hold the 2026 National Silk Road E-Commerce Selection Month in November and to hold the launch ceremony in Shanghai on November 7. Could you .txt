ARTICLE TEXT:
MOFCOM BJT
A: China has been the world’s largest online retail market for 13 consecutive years. We have worked with other countries to share the dividends of e-commerce development and provide Chinese consumers with rich choices of goods. To implement the strategic arrangements of the CPC Central Committee and the State Council on Belt and Road cooperation, we plan to hold the 2026 National Silk Road E-Commerce Selection Month from November 7 to 30.
During this period, we will organize local governments and multiple e-commerce enterprises to make good use of open platforms such as the CIIE and the pilot zones for Silk Road e-commerce cooperation, leverage the matrix effect of brands such as “Silk Road E-Commerce,” and hold themed consumption promotion activities both online and offline, bringing more quality overseas goods to consumers and helping Silk Road e-commerce partner countries share in China’s vast e-commerce market.
On November 7, during the CIIE, the launch ceremony and the Silk Road E-Commerce Promotion Conference on Cooperation, Innovation and Development will be held in Shanghai. At that time, a collection of typical practices and a collection of stories on Silk Road e-commerce will be released, and several localities will promote their supporting activities. We will also hold a Silk Road e-commerce supply-demand matchmaking meeting and invite overseas enterprises to conduct precise matchmaking with Chinese e-commerce platforms, livestreaming e-commerce institutions and supporting service enterprises. We welcome your coverage.
