MOFCOM BJT
Q: Since November 1, the EU has imposed a 2-euro handling fee on low-value imported goods, after imposing a 3-euro tariff in July. How does MOFCOM assess the impact of these two overlapping measures on Chinese cross-border e-commerce exporters? Has China expressed concerns to the EU on this matter? (MOFCOM Regular Press Conference November 5, 2026)
A: China is highly concerned about the EU’s new rules. As a new business type and model of international trade, cross-border e-commerce provides diversified choices for global enterprises and consumers. It is hoped the EU will carefully assess the impact of relevant policies and create favorable conditions for China-EU cooperation in e-commerce and other fields.
