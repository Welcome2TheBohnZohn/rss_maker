TITLE:
PLA Southern Theater Command spokesperson slams Philippines for co-opting outside countries in so-called 'joint patrols' that disrupt regional peace, stability

2026-08-26 11:25:00

SOURCE:
Global Times

CATEGORY:

ARTICLE TEXT:
South China Sea Photo: VCG
Senior Captain Zhai Shichen, spokesperson for the PLA Southern Theater Command (STC)，stated that from August 21 to 25, naval and air units of the PLA STC conducted a routine patrol in the South China Sea. In an attempt to stir up troubles in the South China Sea, the Philippines co-opted countries outside the region to conduct so-called joint patrols, disrupting peace and stability in the region.
