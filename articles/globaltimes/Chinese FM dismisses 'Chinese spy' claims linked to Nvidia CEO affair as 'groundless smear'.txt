TITLE:
Chinese FM dismisses 'Chinese spy' claims linked to Nvidia CEO affair as 'groundless smear'

PUBLISHED:
2026-11-09 16:01:00

SOURCE:
Global Times

CATEGORY:
PRC Non-Authoritative Media

URL:

ARTICLE TEXT:
Chinese Foreign Ministry spokesperson Guo Jiakun
When asked to comment on claims that the woman linked to Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang, who admitted to an extramarital affair on Sunday, works for Chinese intelligence, Chinese Foreign Ministry spokesperson Guo Jiakun said that "we have already responded to this. The so-called claim of 'Chinese spies' is completely groundless and is nothing but a smear against China."
Global Times
