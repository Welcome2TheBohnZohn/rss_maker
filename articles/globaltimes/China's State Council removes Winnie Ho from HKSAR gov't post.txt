China's State Council removes Winnie Ho from HKSAR gov't post
                            By Xinhua Published: Nov 03, 2026 11:36 AM 
                    
                    
                         China's State Council has decided to remove Winnie Ho from the post of housing secretary of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region (HKSAR) government.
The decision, made on Tuesday, was in accordance with the HKSAR Basic Law, and based on the suggestion of HKSAR Chief Executive John Lee.
