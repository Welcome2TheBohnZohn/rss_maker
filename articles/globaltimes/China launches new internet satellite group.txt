China launches new internet satellite group
                        
                        
                            By Xinhua Published: Sep 17, 2026 10:54 AM 
                            
                                
                            
                         
                     
                    
                         China launched a Long March-12 carrier rocket from the southern island province of Hainan Thursday, sending a batch of internet satellites into space.
The rocket lifted off at 8:32 a.m. (Beijing Time) from the Hainan commercial spacecraft launch site. The payloads, the 25th group of low-orbit internet satellites, entered the designated orbit successfully.
