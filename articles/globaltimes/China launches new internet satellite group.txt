TITLE:
China launches new internet satellite group

PUBLISHED:
2026-11-21 10:31:00

SOURCE:
Global Times

CATEGORY:
PRC Non-Authoritative Media

URL:

ARTICLE TEXT:
A Long March-8A carrier rocket carrying the 30th group of low-orbit internet satellites blasts off from the Hainan commercial spacecraft launch site in south China's Hainan Province, Nov. 20, 2026. China launched a Long March-8A carrier rocket on Friday, sending a new batch of internet satellites into space. The rocket lifted off at 7:59 p.m. (Beijing Time) from the Hainan commercial spacecraft launch site. The payloads, the 30th group of low-orbit internet satellites, successfully entered their designated orbits. (Xinhua/Yang Guanyu) 
China launched a Long March-8A carrier rocket from the southern island province of Hainan on Friday, sending a new batch of internet satellites into space.
The rocket lifted off at 7:59 p.m. (Beijing Time) from the Hainan commercial spacecraft launch site. The payloads, the 30th group of low-orbit internet satellites, successfully entered their designated orbits.
