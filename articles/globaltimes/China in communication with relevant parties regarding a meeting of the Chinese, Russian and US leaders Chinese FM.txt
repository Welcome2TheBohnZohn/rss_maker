TITLE:
China in communication with relevant parties regarding a meeting of the Chinese, Russian and US leaders: Chinese FM

PUBLISHED:
2026-11-04 17:38:00

SOURCE:
Global Times

CATEGORY:
PRC Non-Authoritative Media

URL:

ARTICLE TEXT:
Chinese foreign ministry spokesperson Mao Ning
China is in communication with relevant parties regarding a meeting of the Chinese, Russian and US leaders, Chinese foreign ministry spokesperson Mao Ning said on Wednesday, when asked to confirm a Financial Times report that officials from the three countries are coordinating arrangements for such a meeting on the sidelines of the APEC meeting in Shenzhen.
"As for your specific question, I have no information to share at this time," Mao said.
