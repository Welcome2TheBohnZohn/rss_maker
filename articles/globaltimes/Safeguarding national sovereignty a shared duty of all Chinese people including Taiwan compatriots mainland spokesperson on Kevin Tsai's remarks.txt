TITLE:
Safeguarding national sovereignty a shared duty of all Chinese people including Taiwan compatriots: mainland spokesperson on Kevin Tsai's remarks

ARTICLE TEXT:
Photo: Chen Binhua, spokesperson for the State Council Taiwan Affairs Office
Asked about a recent social media post in which Kevin Tsai Kang-yong said he had "never supported Taiwan independence," Chen Binhua, a spokesperson for the State Council Taiwan Affairs Office, said on Wednesday that safeguarding national sovereignty and territorial integrity is a shared obligation of all Chinese people, including compatriots in the Taiwan region.
The spokesperson said that China has always respected and protected the legitimate freedom of speech and expression of Taiwan compatriots. However, there is no room for ambiguity on fundamental issues involving national sovereignty, security, and development interests. Chen also expressed the hope that members of Taiwan's entertainment industry would take pride in being Chinese, do more to deepen the bonds of kinship between compatriots on both sides of the Taiwan Straits, and promote cross-Straits exchanges and cooperation.
China has always welcomed Taiwan compatriots to visit the mainland for sightseeing, to see family and friends, and to participate in exchange activities. China will continue to support greater exchanges and cooperation between entertainment industry professionals on both sides of the Taiwan Straits, so they can share the cultural markets on both sides and jointly promote and pass on Chinese culture.
Previously, Tsai's appearance at a campaign event for a diehard "Taiwan independence" separatist sparked widespread criticism. Tsai attended the campaign headquarters launch of Shen Pao-yang, a Democratic Progressive Party candidate for Taipei mayor, who has been designated a diehard "Taiwan independence" separatist by mainland authorities.
Several brands operating in the Chinese mainland have cut ties with Tsai recently following the incident. For example, Chinese clothing brand Inner Mongolia Erdos Garment Co said in a statement on October 14 that it remains firmly committed to the one-China principle and Taiwan is an inalienable part of China's territory.
Noting that Tsai had only served as a host at a previous one-off event, had no long-term partnership and cannot represent any stance of the company, ERDOS said that the company and its brand took immediate action to remove all relevant materials, and it also reserves the right to pursue legal action.
His actions have also prompted widespread criticism from netizens. Some netizens have urged brands to step up scrutiny of the political motives of relevant entertainers.
On October 14, Chen, the spokesperson for the State Council Taiwan Affairs Office responded to Tsai's appearance at the campaign event for a diehard "Taiwan independence" separatist, saying that safeguarding national reunification and opposing "Taiwan independence" represent the shared will of all Chinese people, and no individual or force can benefit from the mainland's development while colluding with "Taiwan independence" separatist forces and being two-faced.
