Russian Presidential Aide and Chairman of the Maritime Board Nikolai Patrushev to visit China
                            By Chinese Foreign Ministry Published: Oct 07, 2026 03:14 PM 
                    
                    
                         Foreign Ministry Spokesperson announces:
Russian Presidential Aide and Chairman of the Maritime Board Nikolai Patrushev will visit China from October 8 to 13. Member of the Political Bureau of the CPC Central Committee and Director of the Office of the Central Commission for Foreign Affairs Wang Yi will hold talks and exchanges with him.
