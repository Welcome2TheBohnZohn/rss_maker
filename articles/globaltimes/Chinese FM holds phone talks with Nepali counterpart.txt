TITLE:
Chinese FM holds phone talks with Nepali counterpart

SOURCE:

URL:

ARTICLE TEXT:
Chinese Foreign Minister Wang Yi on Saturday said that China's package of assistance to Nepal is being implemented, including emergency cash assistance and humanitarian aid.
Wang, also a member of the Political Bureau of the Communist Party of China Central Committee, made the remarks while holding a phone conversation with his Nepali counterpart Shisir Khanal.
