TITLE:
China opposes US blacklisting of three Chinese firms over so-called 'forced labor' accusations, vows to safeguard interests

PUBLISHED:
2026-10-29 15:36:00

SOURCE:
Global Times

CATEGORY:
PRC Non-Authoritative Media

URL:
https://www.globaltimes.cn/page/202610/1373211.shtml

ARTICLE TEXT:
Chinese Foreign Ministry spokesperson Guo Jiakun
Two Chinese ministries on Thursday expressed strong dissatisfaction with and firm opposition to the US decision to add three Chinese genomics entities to the so-called "Uyghur Forced Labor Prevention Act Entity List," vowing to take necessary measures to resolutely safeguard the lawful rights and interests of Chinese enterprises.
"The US has been spreading disinformation about Xinjiang and using so-called "forced labor" as false pretext to suppress Chinese enterprises. China deplores and firmly opposes the move," Chinese Foreign Ministry spokesperson Guo Jiakun said on Thursday.
The remarks came as the US added BGI Genomics Co Ltd, MGI Tech Co Ltd, and Shenzhen BGI Technology Co Ltd to the "Uyghur Forced Labor Prevention Act Entity List," a move that bans imports from the three companies, according to a statement posted by the US Department of Homeland Security on its website on Wednesday.
The so-called "forced labor" is a huge lie propagated by anti-China forces to destabilize Xinjiang, smear China and curb its development, Guo said. He noted that China urges the US to stop interfering in China's internal affairs and curbing China's development in the name of human rights. China will take necessary measures to firmly safeguard the lawful rights and interests of Chinese enterprises.
"There is no 'forced labor' in China, and people of all ethnic groups in Xinjiang enjoy freedom and equality in employment, with their labor rights and interests effectively protected by law," a spokesperson for the Ministry of Commerce (MOFCOM) said Thursday.
The MOFCOM spokesperson noted that the US had recently added a large number of Chinese entities to the same list. China has lodged solemn representations with the US side and taken resolute countermeasures. Disregarding China's firm opposition, the US has continued to impose new sanctions, this time targeting Chinese biomedical technology companies, the spokesperson said.
Such actions run counter to the consensus reached by the two countries' heads of state, seriously undermine the stability of China-US economic and trade relations, and are contrary to humanitarian principles, the spokesperson added.
The three companies involved have also stated their positions on the matter. They strongly opposed the US government's decision.
"We firmly reject all allegations. BGI Group has not engaged in any form of forced labor. BGI Group strictly complies with applicable laws and regulations in China and the countries and regions where it operates, and respects internationally recognized principles concerning human rights and labor standards. We firmly oppose all forms of forced labor," BGI Group said in a statement sent to the Global Times on Thursday.
BGI Genomics expressed strong opposition to the US allegation in a similar statement on Thursday. MGI Tech Co Ltd stressed in a statement sent to the Global Times that "We firmly reject the allegations, which we believe are unfounded and inconsistent with the facts."
Jia Chunyang, research fellow at Development Research Center of the State Council, told the Global Times on Thursday that the "forced labor" accusation serves merely as a pretext: The true purpose is to destabilize the Xinjiang region and disrupt its development.
"The US moves may seem high-sounding, but in essence are underhanded tricks that cannot stand up to scrutiny. They will only create obstacles for China-US economic and trade relations, undermine the strategic consensus reached between the two countries' leaders, and create new uncertainties in the bilateral relationship," Jia said.
