TITLE:
China launches new satellite into space

PUBLISHED:
2026-11-12 09:44:00

SOURCE:
Global Times

CATEGORY:
PRC Non-Authoritative Media

URL:
https://www.globaltimes.cn/page/202611/1374133.shtml

ARTICLE TEXT:
A Long March-2D carrier rocket with a satellite onboard blasts off from the Jiuquan Satellite Launch Center in northwest China, Nov. 11, 2026. The satellite was launched at 7:41 p.m. (Beijing Time) aboard a Long March-2D carrier rocket, and it entered the preset orbit successfully. The launch marked the 677th mission of the Long March carrier rockets. (Photo by Wang Jiangbo/Xinhua)
