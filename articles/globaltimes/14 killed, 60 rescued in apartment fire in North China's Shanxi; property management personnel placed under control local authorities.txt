TITLE:
14 killed, 60 rescued in apartment fire in North China's Shanxi; property management personnel placed under control: local authorities

Firefighters work to extinguish a blaze at a high-rise building in Yingze district, Taiyuan, North China's Shanxi Province, on November 8, 2026. Firefighting operations were still underway as of 7 pm. Photo: VCG
A fire at an apartment building in Taiyuan, North China's Shanxi Province, has killed 14 people, while 60 others were evacuated or rescued, with management personnel of the property owner and personnel in charge of the property management company been placed under control measures, local authorities said at a press briefing on Monday afternoon, Xinhua reported.
The briefing, held by the Publicity Department of the Taiyuan Municipal Committee of the Communist Party of China, provided an update on the fire at Haosheng International Apartment.
The fire broke out at an apartment building in Yingze district, Taiyuan, at 4:59 pm on Sunday. The open flames were extinguished by 7 pm, and search and rescue operations concluded at 8:30 pm, CCTV News reported earlier.
At around 7:30 pm on Sunday, firefighters were using high-altitude firefighting equipment to spray water on the fire-hit area near the top of the building to prevent the flames from reigniting, while smoke continued to billow from some floors, a reporter from China National Radio (CNR) reported from the scene.
Flashlights could be seen moving behind the windows of the burned floors as rescue personnel searched for people inside. Signboards of several training institutions were visible on the building's exterior. Local residents told the reporter that the building housed several training institutions and was adjacent to a hotel.
Following the incident, Taiyuan immediately activated its emergency response plan and set up a task force to oversee rescue and response efforts, with dedicated teams for medical treatment, post-accident arrangements, accident investigation and accountability, and overall coordination.
The city has mobilized medical resources across the province and municipality to treat the injured, adopting an individual treatment plan and dedicated support team for each patient, according to the briefing. Authorities are also providing assistance to the families of those killed, offering emotional support to the injured and handling compensation matters.
Management personnel of the property owner and personnel in charge of the property management company have been placed under control measures, the briefing said.
In response to the accident, Taiyuan will conduct a citywide campaign to comprehensively inspect, identify and address workplace safety risks, with a particular focus on fire safety and high-risk industries, in a bid to prevent similar incidents and protect people's lives and property.
