China, India arrange bilateral meeting between President Xi Jinping, Indian PM Modi: FM
                        
                        
                            By Global Times Published: Sep 11, 2026 03:36 PM 
                            
                                
                            
                         
                     
                    
                         China and India are arranging a bilateral meeting between President Xi Jinping and Indian Prime Minister Narendra Modi, Chinese Foreign Ministry spokesperson Mao Ning said at a regular press conference on Friday. 
It will mark the third consecutive year that the two leaders would meet, following their meetings in Kazan and Tianjin, according to Xinhua.
