Cooperation between countries should help safeguard regional peace, stability: FM on US-NZ Cook Islands port plan

ARTICLE TEXT:
Photo: Chinese Foreign Ministry spokesperson Guo Jiakun
China has always maintained that cooperation between countries should be conducive to safeguarding regional peace and stability, Guo Jiakun, a spokesperson for Chinese Foreign Ministry, said on Tuesday, in response to reports that the US and New Zealand plan to jointly develop a deep-water port in the Cook Islands at a site that previously served as a military base.
