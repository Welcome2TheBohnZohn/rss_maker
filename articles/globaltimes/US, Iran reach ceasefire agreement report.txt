US, Iran reach ceasefire agreement: report
                        
                        
                            By Xinhua Published: Aug 26, 2026 11:07 AM 
                            
                                
                            
                         
                     
                    
                         The United States and Iran have reportedly reached a ceasefire agreement, including free navigation in the Strait of Hormuz.
Russian news agency RIA Novosti reported this Tuesday, citing Pakistani military and Iranian security sources.
The agreement is expected to be formally announced in the coming days, the sources said.
