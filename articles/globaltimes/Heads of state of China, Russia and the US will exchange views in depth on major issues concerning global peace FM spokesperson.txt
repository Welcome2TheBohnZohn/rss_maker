Heads of state of China, Russia and the US will exchange views in depth on major issues concerning global peace: FM spokesperson

PUBLISHED:

SOURCE:

ARTICLE TEXT:
Chinese Foreign Ministry spokesperson Guo Jiakun
The heads of state of China, Russia and the US will have an in-depth exchange of views on major issues concerning global peace, Chinese Foreign Ministry spokesperson Guo Jiakun said at a regular press conference on Wednesday when asked about the China-Russia-US summit, according to Xinhua News Agency.
