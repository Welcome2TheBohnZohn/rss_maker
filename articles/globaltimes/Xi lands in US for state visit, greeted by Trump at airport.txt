Xi lands in US for state visit, greeted by Trump at airport
                        
                        
                            By Xinhua Published: Sep 24, 2026 06:42 AM 
                            
                                
                            
                         
                     
                    
                         Chinese President Xi Jinping arrived at Joint Base Andrews on Wednesday afternoon for a state visit to the United States.
Xi and his wife, Peng Liyuan, were warmly greeted by US President Donald Trump and his wife, Melania Trump, at the airport in a rare planeside welcome.
The welcome ceremony also included gun salute and the playing of the national anthems of both countries.
