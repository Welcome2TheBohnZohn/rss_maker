China passes law to prevent, punish cross-border corruption
                            By Xinhua Published: Nov 06, 2026 09:55 AM Updated: Nov 06, 2026 10:46 AM 
                    
                    
                         China's top legislature on Friday adopted a law to combat cross-border corruption.
The law aims to prevent and punish cross-border corruption, deepen international anti-corruption cooperation, safeguard China's sovereignty, security and development interests, and promote the building of a fair and equitable global anti-corruption governance system.
With 47 articles in six chapters, the law covers mechanisms and responsibilities, case handling and international cooperation, integrity and compliance, legal liability, among other areas.
The law was passed at a legislative session of the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress.
