Xi to attend SCO summit, pay state visits to Kyrgyzstan, Egypt
                        
                        
                            By Xinhua Published: Aug 26, 2026 03:07 PM 
                            
                                
                            
                         
                     
                    
                         From August 30 to September 3, Chinese President Xi Jinping will attend the Shanghai Cooperation Organization Summit 2026 in Bishkek, and pay a state visit to Kyrgyzstan and Egypt upon the invitation of President Sadyr Japarov of Kyrgyzstan and President Abdel Fattah El-Sisi of Egypt, a Chinese foreign ministry spokesperson announced on Wednesday.
