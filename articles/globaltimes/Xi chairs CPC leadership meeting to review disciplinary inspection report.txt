Xi chairs CPC leadership meeting to review disciplinary inspection report
                            By Xinhua Published: Oct 23, 2026 01:23 PM 
                    
                    
                         The Political Bureau of the Communist Party of China (CPC) Central Committee held a meeting on Friday to review a report on the seventh round of disciplinary inspection launched by the 20th CPC Central Committee.
Xi Jinping, general secretary of the CPC Central Committee, chaired the meeting.
